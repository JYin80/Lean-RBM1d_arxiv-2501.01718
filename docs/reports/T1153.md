# T1153 — fixed-order transfer from the full k0 moment to the good k1 moment

## Verdict: mathematical preflight PASS; exact conditional theorem proved

`RBM1D/Gauss/APrimeGeneralMovingHighQK1GoodFixedOrder.lean`.

For fixed `δ > 0` and `q ≥ 1`, assume the exact full Gaussian expectation at
`k=0`, with the literal order-`q` `widenedW`, `cutTrunc`, endpoint, and
`Gauss.P`, is eventually at most `C₀ N^(δq/2)`. The theorem returns `C₁ > 0`
such that the same order-`q` integral at `k=1`, restricted to the literal
`APrimeGeneralMovingGoodMesh.good N`, is eventually at most `C₁ N^(δq/2)`
whenever `k=1` is active. No order, loss, mesh, endpoint sequence, Gaussian
sample, cutoff, weight, or probability law is changed.

## Mathematical preflight

I checked the actual public signatures and definitions used by the transfer:

- T1097's `eventually_selected_high_k0` has the full order-`q` Gaussian
  expectation at `k=0` with the exact target-mesh `canonicalR`, `widenedW`,
  `cutTrunc`, and `jSnorm`. Its selected-order form returns `q ≥ p` before the
  eventual `N`; the new theorem instead accepts that exact fixed-order bound
  as its premise.
- T475's `eventually_jSnorm_modulus` is uniform on the literal event
  `‖Xmat‖ ≤ N` and over both endpoints in `[s_N,t_N]`. Its coefficient is
  `N^(2D+7)`. The public target-mesh estimate gives
  `N^(2D+7) mesh_N^(-1/2) ≤ N^(-2)`, yielding a same-sample endpoint change
  of at most `N^-2` for the active `k=1` point.
- `cutTrunc` is globally `19/4`-Lipschitz; the source reproves this from the
  public cutoff profile. The actual order-`q` `widenedW` is measurable,
  nonnegative, and at most one. At `k=0`, it is exactly one for `N ≥ 1`
  because the prefix is empty.
- The initial and positive endpoints use the same `Dims.exampleGrow`
  Gaussian sample space, `B.P`, `jSnorm`, moving `s,t`, target mesh,
  `canonicalR`, `δ`, `q`, and cutoff level. The endpoint perturbation is
  pointwise on the same `ω ∈ good N`.

The pointwise estimate uses
`(a+b)^(2q) ≤ 2^(2q)(a^(2q)+b^(2q))`. Integrating the initial term is bounded
by the full `k=0` premise. The cutoff error is at most `(19/4)^(2q)` for
`N ≥ 1`, and `1 ≤ N^(δq/2)` eventually. One valid returned constant is
`C₁ = 2^(2q) (C₀ + (19/4)^(2q))`.

The nondegenerate witness `fixed_order_premise_with_positive_k1_witness`
combines T1097's exact `k=0` moment producer with T995's scheduled
`E=0, D=60` model. On this same model and window it supplies `Cond272Reg`,
`BoundsCore`, a positive interval, and eventually an active first positive
cell with a common-event sample whose scheduled actual smooth weight is
positive. Thus the premise is satisfiable alongside a genuine `k=1` cell.

I visually checked the authorized paper PDF at formulas (5.43)–(5.44), page
58. Formula (5.43) defines the stopping threshold for `J*`; (5.44) bounds the
quadratic variation of the stopped martingale. Those formulas concern the
stopped process. This theorem is only the static, conditional cutoff-moment
transfer above and does not claim the stopped quadratic-variation result or
an all-index family estimate.

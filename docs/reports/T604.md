# T604 — post-T598 drift-integration preflight

## Scope and verdict

This was a read-only audit of the route from the pointwise general-moving
drift estimate in T598 to a time-integrated, actual-smooth-weight moment
budget.  No Lean source or project bookkeeping was changed.

**Verdict: the proposed integration is not yet dispatchable as one theorem.**
The deterministic time-integrability and the positive-support transfer are
available, but the common-event complement cannot presently be paid.  The
smallest missing implication is an all-sample polynomial envelope for the
actual general-moving `driftAt`, uniform over the active closed prefix.  The
first dispatch should prove that envelope.  Only after it lands should the
actual-smooth-weight integral theorem be dispatched.

This is a genuine missing implication rather than a cosmetic library gap.
The event-splitting lemma requires an `Env` valid for every sample, while the
available T598 estimate is restricted to the common event and positive
widened support.  The only current polynomial drift envelope is the
first-cell result with `E = 0`, `D = 60`, `s = 0`, and endpoint at most
`1/2`; it cannot be reused for the general-moving consumer.

## What T598 supplies, exactly

All of the following restrictions are semantically active and must be
preserved:

- `d` is fixed to `Dims.exampleGrow`; the event and sample are the literal
  T579 `commonEvent` and `sample d`.
- The theorem is for positive cells only: `1 <= k`, with
  `k <= cutNetTop ...`.
- The endpoint is exactly
  `v := cutNetPt s (APrimeGeneralMovingMesh.targetMesh D) N k`.
- Time is quantified on the closed interval `u ∈ Set.Icc (s N) v`.
- The moment order `p` and `hp : 1 <= p` occur before the eventual `N`.
- The support assumption is positivity of the canonical widened weight at
  the same `N`, `k`, sample, mesh, and event resident.
- The transport factor is exactly
  `xiK * ratR(E,s,N,u)^(-2) * ratR(E,s,N,v)^(-2)`.
- The linear source is the literal sum of T586's `nearSourceCoeff` and
  `farSourceCoeff`, including the residual/tail terms.  These may not be
  replaced by only the leading paper monomials.
- The quadratic source is
  `exp 1 * (Step2.jS (sample d) E D N u omega)^2` times the exact (5.34)
  bracket.  The random quantity here is the actual `Step2.jS`; it is not
  `Jbar`.

The exact T598 coefficient ledger is therefore

```text
nearSourceCoeff
  = 4 N^(zetaCtr+zetaSrc) eta_u^-1 ratR_u^3 cNear(W,ell_u)
    + [4 N^zetaCtr * (L Jbar/(ell_u eta_u ratR_u^2))
       * (W ell_u eta_u)^2 * exp((log W)^(3/4))
       * tailT(W,ell_u,eta_u,D,gap(W,ell_u))]
      * [eta_u^-1 ratR_u^3]

farSourceCoeff
  = eta_u^-1 * (4 N^zetaCtr ratR_u)
    * [cFar(W,ell_u) Jbar (flowDelta + W^-1)
       + Jbar sqrt(Jbar)
         * (168 (W ell_u eta_u)^-1 + L sqrt(W^-D)/ell_u)]

quadCoeff
  = exp(1) * (Step2.jS(sample d,E,D,N,u,omega))^2
    * [36 eta_u^-1 (W ell_u eta_u)^-1 + W L W^(-D)].
```

Here T598's `Jbar` is

```text
1 + N^tauG * (9 exp(sqrt 3) * ((4 exp(1) + 2) N^(2 delta) ratR_u^4) + 2).
```

The later deterministic cap on the quadratic row must instead come from
`APrimeGeneralMovingCommonSources.eventually_block_cap_on_widened_support`:

```text
Step2.jS(sample d,E,D,N,u,omega)
  <= (4 exp(1) + 2) N^(2 deltaCap) ratR_u^4.
```

In particular, no proof step may identify `Step2.jS` with `Jbar`.

## Support and integrability audit

### Weight direction

`APrimeSmoothWeightActual.widenedW_le_weight_canonical` states

```text
widenedW(delta) <= actualSmoothWeight(delta).
```

Consequently, positive widened support implies positive actual-smooth
support, not conversely.  Nor does a widened-weight norm estimate transfer
to the larger actual weight by monotonicity.  Thus a same-`delta`,
widened-only budget would not feed the current Duhamel consumer.

The available repair is exactly T599:
`eventually_actualWeight_pos_implies_widenedW_pos`.  For every fixed
`xi > 0`, positive actual weight at `deltaWeight` implies positive widened
weight at

```text
deltaCap := deltaWeight + xi,
```

uniformly in the active `k` and sample.  The support loss is precisely the
buffer `N^(2 xi)`.  Every use of `Jbar`, the common-event block cap, and the
quadratic cap must therefore use `deltaCap`, not `deltaWeight`.

The strict-transition cap from T597 is insufficient: actual smooth support
also includes the plateau, whereas T597 only covers the strict transition.

### Time integrability

`APrimeDriftTimeFamily.intervalIntegrable_momNormW_driftAt` already proves
closed-interval time integrability for every measurable weight with values
in `[0,1]`.  For the literal actual smooth weight, measurability follows
from `APrimeSmoothWeightActual.weightC1(...).cont.measurable`; nonnegativity
and the upper bound are `weight_nonneg` and `weight_le_one`.  The hypotheses
needed by `weightC1` are routine consequences of the active net and stored
window assumptions.  Hence finiteness of the time integral is not the
blocker.

The proposed deterministic profile is assembled from continuous positive
finite-size functions on the same closed interval.  Proving its
`IntervalIntegrable` instance is implementation work, but no singularity or
endpoint obstruction was found.

### Event complement

`APrimeFirstCellDriftNormSplit.drift_norm_le_of_weighted_event` is the right
event-splitting lemma.  It needs:

1. the favorable weighted pointwise estimate on the event;
2. an all-sample pointwise envelope `|G omega| <= Env`;
3. sample integrability;
4. the common-event probability estimate.

Items 1 and 4 are available from T598/T579 after the buffered support
transfer.  Sample integrability follows from sample continuity/measurability
of `driftAt` and a finite all-sample envelope.  The missing item is a
polynomial `Env` uniform in `N`, the active prefix, closed time, charge, and
sample.  A merely finite, fixed-`N` constant from
`Gauss.exists_bdd_uker_driftF` cannot be combined with the eventual
high-probability estimate, because its growth in `N` is unspecified.

Once a polynomial envelope is available,
`Gauss.eventually_env_mul_prob_rpow_le_moment` pays its complement by any
prescribed `N^(-beta)` for fixed `p`.  As in T591, `p` and `beta` must be
quantified before the eventual cutoff.

### Exact theorem schema and quantifier order

```lean
theorem eventually_abs_driftAt_le_rpow
    {E D c : Real} {s t : Nat -> Real}
    (hE : |E| < 2) (hD : 60 <= D)
    (hs0 : forall N, 0 <= s N)
    (hst : forall N, s N <= t N)
    (ht1 : forall N, t N < 1)
    (hc : 0 < c)
    (hreg : Cond272Reg B E s t c) :
    ∀ᶠ N : Nat in Filter.atTop, forall k : Nat,
      k <= cutNetTop s t (APrimeGeneralMovingMesh.targetMesh D) N ->
      let v := cutNetPt s (APrimeGeneralMovingMesh.targetMesh D) N k
      forall u, u ∈ Set.Icc (s N) v ->
      forall a : LoopArg (d.L N) 2, forall omega : Gauss.Ω d,
        |APrimeDriftTimeFamily.driftAt
            d E D N Step2.sigPM a (s N) v u omega|
          <= (N : Real) ^ (D + 8)
```

Important features of this schema:

- it remains fixed at `Dims.exampleGrow`;
- it is all-sample and has no event, moment-order, or support hypothesis;
- it covers every active `k`, including `k = 0`;
- it retains the exact target mesh and the closed interval;
- it introduces no unnecessary `BoundsCore`, `zeta`, `tauG`, or `delta`;
- it states the absolute-value form required by the event split.

### Proof dependency plan

The exponent `D + 8` has a direct conservative derivation:

1. Unfold `Cond272Reg` only to obtain the eventual scale inequality.  Apply
   `Gauss.rpow_neg_one_le_etaT_of_scale_ge` to get
   `N^(-1) <= etaT E (t N)`.
2. By monotonicity of `etaT` on the window, obtain
   `etaT E v` and `etaT E u` inverse bounds by `N`.
3. Use `Gauss.exists_norm_Kval_le_win B hE 2` for a constant `CK`
   independent of `N`, `u`, and the charge.
4. Bound every required Gaussian loop by the standard resolvent bound.  For
   the `n = 0` source, take `MG = etaT(E,v)^(-3)` and
   `MK = CK * etaT(E,v)^(-2)`.
5. Apply `Gauss.norm_driftF_le_crude` with `n = 0`.  Since `W * L <= N`,
   the three counted source blocks are bounded by a fixed `E`-dependent
   constant times `N^7`.
6. Use `Gauss.norm_Uker_apply_le_ukerRow` and
   `APrimeQVGlobalPoly.inv_driftScale_mul_ukerRow_le`.  The latter gives the
   endpoint-normalized propagation factor `<= N^D`.
7. Prove `driftScale > 0` and absorb the fixed constant into one additional
   power of `N` eventually.  This yields `N^(D+8)`.

No stochastic hypothesis is used in this proof.  In particular, it should
not be proved by weakening the T598 common-event theorem.

### Boundary plan

The global envelope naturally handles `k = 0`.  The later time-integral
theorem should split cells as follows:

- `k = 0`: use `cutNetPt_zero` and `integral_same` for both sides.  Do not
  claim that `driftAt d E D N ... (s N) (s N) (s N) omega = 0`; that
  pointwise assertion is generally false for a moving start.
- `1 <= k`: use T599 to pass from positive actual-smooth support at
  `deltaWeight` to positive widened support at `deltaCap`, then apply T598
  on the same common-event resident, sample, cell, endpoint, and time.

This is why the integral theorem may quantify all `k`, even though T598
itself correctly remains positive-cell only.

### Non-degeneracy

The theorem is not vacuous.  T598's
`positive_cell_full_drift_hypotheses_witness` supplies `E = 0`, `D = 60`, a
positive-length moving-window instance, `Cond272Reg`, and a resident of the
same common event at `k = 1`.  For the later buffered consumer, instantiate
that resident's widened weight `= 1` at `deltaWeight`; domination gives
positive actual smooth weight there, and T599 transfers it on the identical
sample to positive widened support at `deltaCap = deltaWeight + xi`.  The
global envelope itself is stronger: it
requires no event resident and applies to every sample, including the zero
sample and arbitrary nonzero samples.

## The actual-smooth integral theorem unlocked by the envelope

This should be a separate subsequent ticket, tentatively in

```text
RBM1D/Gauss/APrimeGeneralMovingSmoothDriftNormBudget.lean
```

with direct imports

```lean
import RBM1D.Gauss.APrimeGeneralMovingDriftGlobalPoly
import RBM1D.Gauss.APrimeSmoothToWidenedSupport
import RBM1D.Gauss.APrimeFirstCellDriftNormSplit
```

Define, rather than abbreviate away, the following exact objects:

```text
deltaCap := deltaWeight + xi

weight := APrimeSmoothWeightActual.weight
  d E D deltaWeight s t targetMesh 2 p N k
  (APrimeSmoothWeightActual.canonicalM d s t targetMesh N)

jSCap(u) := (4 exp(1) + 2) * N^(2 deltaCap)
  * (Step2Moment.ratR E s N u)^4

quadCap(u) := exp(1) * jSCap(u)^2
  * [36 eta_u^-1 (W ell_u eta_u)^-1 + W L W^(-D)]

profile(u) := xiK * ratR_u^-2 * ratR_v^-2
  * (nearSourceCoeff(...,deltaCap,N,u)
     + farSourceCoeff(...,deltaCap,N,u)
     + quadCap(u)).
```

The target theorem should have this quantifier order and conclusion:

```lean
theorem eventually_actual_smooth_drift_integral_le_exact_profile
    {E D c : Real} {s t : Nat -> Real}
    (hE : |E| < 2) (hD : 60 <= D)
    (hs0 : forall N, 0 <= s N)
    (hst : forall N, s N <= t N)
    (ht1 : forall N, t N < 1)
    (hc : 0 < c)
    (hreg : Cond272Reg B E s t c)
    (hB : BoundsCore (Gauss.sample d) E s)
    {zetaSrc zetaCtr tauG deltaWeight xi : Real}
    (hzetaSrc : 0 < zetaSrc) (hzetaCtr : 0 < zetaCtr)
    (htauG : 0 < tauG) (hdeltaWeight : 0 < deltaWeight)
    (hxi : 0 < xi)
    (p : Nat) (hp : 1 <= p)
    (beta : Real) (hbeta : 0 < beta) :
    ∀ᶠ N : Nat in Filter.atTop, forall k : Nat,
      k <= cutNetTop s t (APrimeGeneralMovingMesh.targetMesh D) N ->
      let v := cutNetPt s (APrimeGeneralMovingMesh.targetMesh D) N k
      forall a : LoopArg (d.L N) 2,
        MeasureTheory.IntervalIntegrable
          (fun u => MomentDuhamel.momNormW (Gauss.P d)
            (weight E D deltaWeight s t p N k)
            p (APrimeDriftTimeFamily.driftAt
              d E D N Step2.sigPM a (s N) v u))
          MeasureTheory.volume (s N) v
        /\
        MeasureTheory.IntervalIntegrable
          (fun u => profile E D s t zetaSrc zetaCtr tauG
            (deltaWeight + xi) N v u)
          MeasureTheory.volume (s N) v
        /\
        (∫ u in s N..v,
            MomentDuhamel.momNormW (Gauss.P d)
              (weight E D deltaWeight s t p N k)
              p (APrimeDriftTimeFamily.driftAt
                d E D N Step2.sigPM a (s N) v u))
          <=
        (∫ u in s N..v,
            profile E D s t zetaSrc zetaCtr tauG
              (deltaWeight + xi) N v u)
          + (v - s N) * (N : Real)^(-beta)
```

The displayed helper argument lists are schematic only where their actual
definitions have not yet been introduced; the theorem's semantic
quantifier order and conclusion are the dispatch requirement.  In the Lean
implementation, prefer the standard interval-integral notation used by the
surrounding modules and keep both endpoints closed in every pointwise
lemma.

Proof route for positive cells:

1. Split each sample by whether the actual smooth weight is zero.
2. On positive actual support, use T599 with
   `deltaCap = deltaWeight + xi`.
3. On the same common-event resident, apply T598 with `deltaCap`.
4. Apply `eventually_block_cap_on_widened_support` separately to the actual
   `Step2.jS` in `quadCoeff`, producing `quadCap`; do not route this step
   through `Jbar`.
5. Use `eventually_abs_driftAt_le_rpow` as the all-sample envelope in
   `drift_norm_le_of_weighted_event`.
6. Pay the T579 complement by `N^(-beta)` and integrate the pointwise moment
   estimate over `[s N, v]`.

No exponent-room hypothesis is needed for this exact, unabsorbed profile.
Such a hypothesis belongs only to a later theorem that deliberately
compresses the exact rows into a single monomial.

The assumptions of this future theorem are also non-degenerate.  In the
T598 witness choose, for example, `xi = deltaCap / 2` and
`deltaWeight = deltaCap / 2`.  The witness has widened weight equal to one
at `k = 1` for every moment order.  Since
`widenedW <= actualSmoothWeight <= 1`, the same resident has actual smooth
weight equal to one.  Thus the favorable event intersected with positive
actual support is inhabited.

## Strongest safe nonclaims

This audit does **not** establish any of the following:

- the actual-smooth drift integral theorem itself;
- a same-`delta` implication from positive actual support to positive
  widened support;
- a norm comparison from a widened weight to the larger actual smooth
  weight;
- `driftAt = 0` on the degenerate `k = 0` cell;
- replacement of the actual `Step2.jS` by T598's `Jbar`;
- deletion of T586's residual/tail terms or alteration of any `N`, `W`,
  `eta`, `ell`, `ratR`, or `xiK` power;
- an all-dimensions theorem;
- or closure of the full Duhamel argument.

The safe conclusion is narrower: after one deterministic all-sample
polynomial-envelope ticket, the existing T579/T598/T599/event-split/time-
integrability APIs are sufficient to dispatch an honest actual-smooth,
all-cell integral drift budget with an exact unabsorbed profile.

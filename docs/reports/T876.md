## Claim and dependency audit

`semicircleMeasure_cdf_reflection` states for **every** `x : ℝ` that
`cdf semicircleMeasure (-x) = 1 - cdf semicircleMeasure x`.
`semicircleMeasure_cdf_zero` states `cdf semicircleMeasure 0 = 1/2`.
There are no new hypotheses or exceptional x values.

The proof imports only the accepted `SemicircleDensityMeasure` module and
Mathlib's CDF module. Its measure inputs are the proved reflection invariance
`semicircleMeasure_map_neg`, singleton nullity
`semicircleMeasure_singleton`, and normalization `semicircleMeasure_univ`.
There is no import edge to T863/T867/T869, T871/T873, or a quantile/Q0 module.

The map direction is correct: the inverse image of `Iic (-x)` under `y ↦ -y`
is `Ici x`. Reflection therefore gives `μ(Iic (-x)) = μ(Ici x)`.
The latter set is the complement of `Iio x`, so finite measureReal complementation
gives `1 - μ.real(Iio x)`. The private lemma proves
`μ.real(Iio x) = μ.real(Iic x)` by the disjoint union
`Iic x = Iio x ∪ {x}` and `μ({x})=0`. This is the necessary atom correction;
without it the CDF identity could fail at an atom. The normalization used in the
complement formula is exactly `μ(univ)=1`. At x=0, the all-real identity yields
`F(0)=1-F(0)`, hence `F(0)=1/2`.

This is a CDF fact for the accepted semicircle probability measure. It does not
establish equal-mass quantiles, Q0, a Stieltjes integral, a Gaussian local law,
or a paper-level theorem.

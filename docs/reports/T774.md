## Result

For all `W k : ℕ` and actual `π : PermΩ W`, `uniformPerm_prefixCell_factorial_mass` proves

```lean
uniformPerm W {ρ | ρ ∈ prefixCell W k π} =
  ((W - min k W).factorial : ENNReal) / (W.factorial : ENNReal)
```

It composes T736's exact finite counting-measure formula with T738's free-completion factorial cardinality and `Fintype.card_perm`/`Fintype.card_fin`. `factorial_ennreal_pos` records that the denominator is strictly positive for every `W`, including `W = 0`, so the formula never relies on a zero denominator.

The edge cases are included: zero revealed coordinates give mass one; for `W ≤ k` the mass is `1 / W!`; and width zero gives mass one for all `k` (hence also at `k = 0`). At width two and `k = 1`, identity and swap have distinct actual-prefix cells, both of mass `1/2`, by `uniformPerm_prefixCell_factorial_two_one_witness`.

The only inputs are natural numbers and an actual permutation. No arbitrary prescribed prefix or consistency hypothesis is used: the actual `π` realizes its own cell. No additional assumptions were introduced, so no separate satisfiability witness is needed. The result is a finite combinatorial/probability identity relevant downstream of paper formulas (2.3)–(2.4); it does not claim those Green-function local laws or introduce `N`, time, first-cell/shared-event, conditional-law, or P2 conclusions. No paper delta is needed.

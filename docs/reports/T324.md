# T324 — pre-A′ audit of general-window (4.5)

## Verdict

**The current internal producer does not derive general-window `Eq45Flow` from `Step1.weakLaw` alone.** The transfer theorem itself is proved, and the finite-word conditional-IBP machinery is genuine, but its fixed-threshold end-to-end Gaussian route needs a local-law scale `Ψ` with `W Ψ² ≤ N^τ` eventually for **every** `τ>0`. Step 1 supplies `A_u^(-1/4)`; even on a positive first adjacent grid cell with `A_u ≍ W`, substituting that scale gives `W Ψ² ≍ W^(1/2)`, a polynomial loss. The route also needs `LocalLawUnifIcc` at its selected `Ψ=2δ`, which Step 1's weaker domination does not establish at `δ ≍ W^(-1/2)`. The first-cell `Eq45FlowBudget` proof uses a *family of thresholds chosen after the requested exponent* and must not be conflated with the fixed-threshold theorem.

This is an audit of the available implication, not a counterexample to paper (4.5). A stronger pre-A′ entry law on the first cell is being checked separately by T323. No Step 2 `LocalLawFlow` or A′ conclusion was used as a source here.

## Positive-cell boundary and the first gap

Take `d=Dims.exampleGrow`, `E=0`, and the positive first adjacent cell `s=firstCellS τ'=0<t=firstCellT τ'≤1/2`. `first_cell_window_nondegenerate` and `first_cell_joint_grid_scales` certify nonzero length, positive bandwidth, and positive selected threshold; on this cell `W/2≤A_u≤W`. The Gaussian growing-bandwidth witness has `W_N≥N^(5/8)` eventually (`Dims.bandwidth_grow`). A direct use of Step 1's weak law to obtain the good event takes, for fixed `θ>0`, a threshold of order `δ_N=N^θ W_N^(-1/4)` (constant factors are harmless). But the current `eq45Flow_of_goodSetFlow_highProb` asks, with `Ψ=2δ`,

```text
∀ τ>0, eventually 4 W_N Ψ_N² = 16 W_N δ_N² ≤ N^τ.
```

The left side is of order `N^(2θ) W_N^(1/2) ≥ N^(2θ+5/16)`, so the requested clause fails already at `τ=1/4`. This shows that **this direct Step 1 substitution** is invalid even on a nondegenerate early cell. It does not assert that the actual Gaussian good event at a smaller threshold is false; Step 1 simply has no theorem giving it.

The successful first-cell route does **not** pass a fixed `δ=N^θ W^(-1/2)` to `eq45Flow_of_goodSetFlow_highProb`: its universal `hΨW` would fail for `τ<2θ`. Instead, `firstCellFlucAvg_budget_family` (`Gauss/Eq45FlowBudget.lean:366`) takes each `θ>0` separately; `firstCellBudgetFamily_absorb` chooses `θ≤τ/16` *after* the requested domination exponent and converts the movable `4δ_θ²` bound to the base `firstCellPsi²`. `first_cell_eq45FlowInputs_of_localLaw'` then uses the base-scale condition `W firstCellPsi²=O(1)`. This internal mechanism is sound but still takes the sharper first-cell `LocalLawUnifIcc` as an input.

For a general later adjacent cell, `Step1.apriori` gives `Φ_u=(ell_u/ell_s)/A_u`; a possible entry scale from Lemma 4.1 is `sqrt(Φ_u+W⁻¹)`. The current `IBPFlow`/row/block producers target the **random** `Lmax` using `W Ψ²=N^{o(1)}` to compare deterministic `Ψ²` with `Lmax≳W⁻¹` (`unifDomIcc_condExpDiag_flow`, `CondStableFlow.lean:970–1080`). No general grid theorem in the inspected chain proves that comparison for this `Φ_u`. The paper's (4.12) instead controls the fluctuation average directly by the two-loop size; the existing deterministic good-event tower gives only `O_p(δ²)`, which at Step 1's `δ≈A^(-1/4)` is `A^(-1/2)`, not the desired `Φ≈A⁻¹` on an early cell.

The first unsupplied probabilistic input for the **existing first-cell family** is a pre-A′ time-uniform `LocalLawUnifIcc` at `firstCellPsi=W^(-1/2)/2`. The existing `firstCellBudgetDelta_highProb` then constructs each needed good event at the movable `δ_θ`, and the budget-family argument absorbs the margin. For a **general-window** proof at the paper's `(4.4)` strength, the next mathematical theorem must instead internalize (4.12) at the two-loop/`Φ` scale for the row and block weights on the same positive cell, together with a `Φ`-scale conditional-IBP remainder, without the `W Ψ²=N^{o(1)}` specialization. The current `FlucGainUpTo'` is a valid component under a sufficiently sharp good event; it is not the missing (4.12) estimate under only the weak law.

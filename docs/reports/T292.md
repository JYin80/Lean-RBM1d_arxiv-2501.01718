`‖Uker ... (DriftDef.driftF (Gauss.band d) E N r ((Gauss.sample d).H N r ω) σ) a‖`.

The theorem concerns the **unnormalized** `Gauss.momentObsT` and `Gauss.ukerObsT`. `APrimeDuhamelModel.momFlowDeriv_le`, however, consumes a test function `Ψ`, its inner coordinate `Ψ₁`, a sample drift `G`, a sample QV `Q`, and a scalar cross budget `Bc` in one and the same pointwise inequality. ## Compiled time regularity

`continuousOn_momNormW_of_envelope` proves weighted finite-size moment continuity by dominated convergence for a fixed measurable weight `0≤w≤1`. The actual Gaussian producer `Gauss.exists_bdd_uker_driftF`, together with samplewise time continuity `Gauss.continuousOn_uker_driftF_path` and sample measurability `Gauss.continuous_uker_driftF_omega`, supplies its premises for `driftAt`. The resulting `continuousOn_momNormW_driftAt` and `intervalIntegrable_momNormW_driftAt` apply on every closed window `0≤s≤v<1`, including a first cell with `s=0`. The normalization denominator is strictly positive by `driftScale_pos`.

The deterministic candidate `crossAt E s v κ r := APrimeModel.crossInt κ (APrimeTimeInt.qHatNear E s v) r` is genuinely interval integrable on the same closed window by `intervalIntegrable_crossAt`. The proof extends T291's first-cell argument: `qHatNear` is continuous on the window, while `(√r)⁻¹` is integrable from zero and hence on its subinterval. `intervalIntegrable_drift_add_cross` gives the required **time-integrability statement for these two chosen functions**. This statement alone does not certify that `crossAt` bounds the actual cross part.

`first_cell_witness` compiles a positive-length window `s=0`, `v=1/2` with positive endpoint normalization, an integrable `crossAt` for `E=0`, `κ=1`, and `crossAt(1/4)>0`. Its Gaussian dimension, finite size, and coordinate are arbitrary.

## Exact remaining (G) gaps

Three model-pinned propositions in the new file type-check the exact missing interfaces:

1. `NormalizedTestFunBridge d E D N p σ a s v` is the `Gauss.TestFunT₁` premise of T275 for `momentAt`. The available Gaussian constructor treats unnormalized `momentObsT`; a fixed positive real scaling of that constructor is needed here.
2. `NormalizedGeneratorBridge d E D N p σ a s v r` is the samplewise `hle` of T275 with `Ψ=momentAt`, `Ψ₁=coordAt`, `G=driftAt`, and `Q=qvAt`. The existing `timeD1_add_genMomentPt_le_driftF_flow` gives this only for the unnormalized `momentObsT`. The noncircular repair is a constant-in-`r` scaling lemma for `timeD1` and `genPt` (equivalently for `momentFun`), followed by that existing raw Gaussian theorem. The new definitions and `flowY_coordAt` pin the scaling factor exactly; no free stochastic `G` is introduced.
3. `NearCrossBridge d E D κ N p σ a s v r w wD` is the exact `hcross` of T275 with `Bc=crossAt`. T275's `crossPart_le_of_S5` and `crossBudget_le_crossInt_model` can feed it only after a **same-family** (S5) bound and an evolved-QV envelope on the **same Good event** show that the resulting cross budget is at most the chosen near `qHatNear` rate (including the required fit inequality). `qHatNear` is a deterministic near-field rate, while `qvAt` is the full evolved QV. Their identification or domination is not supplied by T291's integrability theorem or by the T280g near-remainder estimate. If far contributions remain, the cross budget must include an additional integrable far rate; selecting the near term alone would be unjustified. Moreover, an event-restricted QV cannot replace the `Q` in the pointwise `hle` off Good without a weight-support statement or a full-rate envelope.

None of these propositions is asserted as a theorem or installed as a hypothesis of a purported closure. Consequently this ticket does **not** claim the same-family (G) derivative inequality, `hfamily_of_coord_budgets`, or `APrimeSlot'`. The first missing regularity edge is `NormalizedTestFunBridge`; the pointwise model edges are `NormalizedGeneratorBridge` and `NearCrossBridge`. T294's time regularity of the evolved QV remains separate.

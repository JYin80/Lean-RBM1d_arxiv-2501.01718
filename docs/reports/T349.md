## Exact live premises and quantifiers

Put `m=n+2`, `A_N=B.scale E N (v_N)>0`, `R_N=(1-s_N)/(1-v_N)`, `I_N=∫[s_N,v_N] ρ_N(u)du`, and `c_N=√Λ_N+Φ_N` (Case 1 uses `max c_N 1`). In `rhs514QAt_of_kernel_inputs'''` (`Lemma514QAssembly.lean:1738,1870`) and `rhsNonAltAt_of_kernel_inputs'''` (`Lemma514NonAlt.lean:1460,1541`), `η>0` is bound **before** `Kd,ζ,δ,δ',Msz,Pb,esz`, the event, the moment envelopes, and `C₀`. Their `hnum` is `∀ᶠ N in atTop, ∀ q : LoopData (B.L N) m, ... ≤ C₀ N^(η/2)c_N A_N^(-m)`. The preceding `hMψI`, `hMψ`, `hMψE` each quantify `∀ ε>0, ∀ p≥1, ∃ C>0, ∀ᶠ N`; interior bounds are uniform over `s_N≤u≤v_N` and all `q`. An `N`-dependent constant, a bound for just one `q`, or one fixed moment order does not meet these signatures.

The final `lemma514_of_momentDuhamelQ_of_scaled_families` (`Lemma514QAssembly.lean:479`) quantifies `Λ,Φ` and their four `Lemma514Premises` fields, then **every endpoint** `v_N∈[s_N,t_N]`, then every `η>0`, and asks for `∃ C₀>0` and the scaled Q and non-alternating RHS bounds. `rhs514QAt_of_scaled_family` and its short analogue choose `η=ε/2` after a requested stochastic-domination tolerance `ε`; the kernel's `N^(η/2)=N^(ε/4)` and the RHS moment allowance `N^(ε/4)` together yield `N^(ε/2)`. Hence a first-cell-only numeric result cannot be substituted for the stated all-endpoint family without an endpoint reduction theorem.

For a **fixed** loop offset `n`, grid step `τ'>0`, decay-loss exponent `τ₁>0`, requested `η>0`, and target residual decay `d>0`, choose a small kernel-radius exponent `θ>0` to pay the main coefficient, then choose the raw decay exponent `D` sufficiently large **after** `n,τ₁,τ',η,d` and the fixed polynomial size exponents. For example `0<θ<η/(16m)` leaves positive exponent room for the Q coefficient and the logarithmic time integral. Finally pass to sufficiently large `N`. In stochastic-domination applications, the requested moment order `p` and tail exponent are fixed before their high-probability event and its `D` are chosen. There is no legitimate choice of one fixed `D` before arbitrary `n,τ₁,d`; nor may `D` be hidden inside a constant `C₀` that must be independent of `N,q` and the grid index. This is the parameter order needed for the paper's `W^{-D}` remainder in (7.16) and for T253's nonexponential error. Translating a paper `W^{-D}` target into `N^{-d}` additionally uses the growing-model lower bound `W_N≥N^a` for some fixed `a>0`; `W_N≤N` alone gives the wrong inequality direction for this translation.

## Normalized weighted `hnum` rows

Write `e^Q_r=errKer716(L_N,r,4Kd_N,ζ_N,δ'_N,s_N,v_N)` and `e^S_r=errKer716Short(r,√κ,Kd_N,ζ_N,δ_N,s_N,v_N)`. Multiplying the exact Lean inequalities by `A_N^m` gives, for every `q` eventually:

```text
Q: [cKer716(m,4Kd) Phi_N(q) + A_N^m(e^Q_m+cE_N)](1+6I_N)
     + sqrt(I_N [cKer716(2m,4Kd) PhiE_N(q)
                    + A_N^(2m)(e^Q_(2m)+cE_N)])
   ≤ C₀ N^(η/2)c_N;

short: [cKer716Short(m,√κ,Kd) Phi_N(q) + A_N^m(e^S_m+cE_N)](1+2I_N)
     + sqrt(I_N [cKer716Short(2m,√κ,Kd) PhiE_N(q)
                    + A_N^(2m)(e^S_(2m)+cE_N)])
   ≤ C₀ N^(η/2)max(c_N,1).
```

This normalization uses `A_N>0` and the square-root scaling `sqrt(A_N^(-2m)X)=A_N^(-m)sqrt(X)` for nonnegative `X`. The older `''` rows (`Q:1723`, short `:1319`) have `I_N` replaced by `v_N-s_N`; they do not preserve the needed inverse-time weight. For `ρ_N(u)=etaT(E,u)^(-1)`, `integral_etaT_inv_gridS_succ` gives exactly `I_N=τ' log W_N / Im(m_E)` on adjacent p.24 cells, and `integral_etaT_inv_le_rpow` absorbs this logarithm into any positive `N` power. There is no `R_N^m` multiplier on the **main** (7.16) term, consistent with the no-`η_s/η_v` assertion after (5.92). The ratio remains in the additive errors.

On `s_N=gridS(W_N,τ',k)`, `v_N=gridS(W_N,τ',k+1)`, `R_N=W_N^τ'`. If `W_N≤N`, then `R_N≤N^τ'`; for a finite grid `k≤k₀`, `(1-v_N)^(-1)=W_N^((k+1)τ')≤N^((k₀+1)τ')`. The grid's `k₀` is fixed by the endpoint regime, not allowed to grow silently with `N`. The Gaussian scale satisfies `A_N≤W_N L_N≤N` eventually, and `L_N≤N`. These are the polynomial comparison inputs needed below, not consequences of either abstract `hnum` signature for arbitrary `v`.

The leading terms have existing exponent proofs: `cKer716_four_le_rpow` (`Q:674`) costs `N^(2mθ)` at length `m`, and its length-`2m` term costs `N^(4mθ)` before the square root, hence again `N^(2mθ)` after it. `cKer716Short_le_rpow` (`short:578`) costs `N^(mθ)` at length `m` and `N^(2mθ)` before the square root, hence `N^(mθ)` after it. Constants depend on fixed `m,κ`, not on `N,k,q`. The factors `1+6I_N`, `1+2I_N`, and `sqrt(I_N)` cost only a logarithm on adjacent cells. If the *actual* moment envelopes obey `Phi_N(q)≤C_m(1+Φ_N)` and `PhiE_N(q)≤C_mΛ_N`, then `Λ_N≥1` gives `1+Φ_N≤2c_N` and `sqrt(PhiE_N(q))≤√C_m c_N`; this arithmetic is available. It does not create the moment-envelope hypotheses.

## Positive error ledger and T253's exact scope

The definitions at `Lemma514QAssembly.lean:662` and `Lemma514NonAlt.lean:569` give, for `r=m,2m`,

```text
A_N^r e^Q_r = A_N^r R_N^r [cKerSumZero(r)(4Kd_N)^(2r)ζ_N
                              + cKerSumZeroErr(r)L_N^r δ'_N],
A_N^r e^S_r = A_N^r R_N^r [cKerShort(r,√κ)Kd_N^r ζ_N + δ_N].
```

Both rows also contain `A_N^r cE_N`. `FastDecayFlow.exists_uniform_errBudget` (`FastDecayFlow.lean:1464`) returns the explicit **nonnegative** `δ'_N=errBudget(L_N,n,v_N,Kd_N,Msz_N,δ_N,Pb_N,esz_N)` and the four exact `hErrQ/C/D/E` inequalities on the entire window. It assumes only size/error nonnegativity and `v_N<1`; it gives no asymptotic smallness. Its `qqErrBd` contains nonexponential nested `L_N^{2(n+1)}δ_N` terms (safely bounded by `C_m L_N^{2m}δ_N`). Thus `δ'_N` has a polynomial-times-`exp(-c Kd_N/2)` part **and** a polynomial-times-`δ_N` part. Taking `Kd_N≈N^θ`, polynomial `Msz_N,Pb_N,esz_N`, and `δ_N≤C N^(max(τ₁,2)-D)` makes the exponential part negligible at any fixed power, but the other part requires the late choice of `D`.

For a conservative visible exponent ledger, assume `A_N,L_N≤N`, `R_N≤N^τ'`, `Kd_N≤N^θ`, and `δ'_N≤C N^(2m+max(τ₁,2)-D)+poly(N)exp(-cN^θ/2)`. Then the worst Q decay-error contribution, at `r=2m`, is bounded by

```text
C N^[6m+2mτ'+max(τ₁,2)-D] + polynomial × exp(-cN^θ/2).
```

The Q envelope-offset term at `r=2m` costs at most `C N^(2m+2mτ'+4mθ)ζ_N`; its bad-event payment costs `N^(2m)cE_N`. Short-side costs are at most `C N^(2m+2mτ'+2mθ)ζ_N`, `C N^(2m+2mτ')δ_N`, and `N^(2m)cE_N`. Fixed polynomial factors from `(1-v_N)^(-1)` enter the exponential branch of T253 and do not obstruct a fixed `θ>0`; they must still be stated. Taking positive `ζ_N,δ_N,cE_N` with sufficiently strong proved decay would pay all these terms, including the `I_N` logarithm and its square root. **No current theorem establishes all these bounds from the actual model event and moment envelopes.** In particular, setting `δ'_N=0` contradicts its positive input decay budget, and the zero-error identities cannot be used here.

## The Φ budget and noncircularity

`Lemma514Premises` (`Lemma514Moment.lean:388`) allows arbitrary nonnegative `Φ_N`; it does not assert `Φ≺1`. Its lower-loop and quadratic fields give `SumZeroDyn.xiRhs_stochDom` (`SumZeroDyn.lean:3012`) at target length `m=n+2` with control **`(2n+3)Φ_N`**. This fixed-in-`n` coefficient belongs in `Phi_N(q)` for the drift's `hMψ` row and is absorbed by `C₀`, while the `Φ_N` factor remains in `c_N=√Λ_N+Φ_N`. At `n=0`, the three terms of `xiRhs_2=XiLK_1+XiLK_2²/A_u+XiL_3` yield `3Φ`, not `1`; `Φ_N=N^β` is permitted. The existing `psiF_stochDom_etaT_inv_of_drift_inputs''` (`Lemma514FBridge.lean:24`) is noncircular in its additive-error branch but explicitly needs `flowXiLK_1≺1` and `xiRhs_m≺1` as inputs. Substituting the arbitrary four `Lemma514Premises` fields for these is invalid. The older unprimed `psiF` theorem also demanded top-length `flowXiLK_m≺1`; deriving that from `Lemma514 m` would cycle at the base case. T290/T296 remove that *additive* cycle, not the main `Φ` dependence. A correct general moment producer must carry `(2n+3)Φ` through the projected drift (and separately source the initial-data and `E⊗E` moment rows) without consuming Lemma 5.14 itself.

The paper's (7.16) supplies a sum-zero kernel bound with `W^{C_mθ}` coefficient and `W^{-D+C_m}` remainder; (5.92) is a bound at control `√Λ+Φ` with no `η_s/η_v` prefactor. Neither statement upgrades `Φ` to `1`, and neither supplies the Lean producer's all-`q`, every-`p`, uniform-time moment hypotheses. This is the decisive dependency gap, separate from the deterministic arithmetic.

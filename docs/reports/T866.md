## Boundaries and a nonconstant witness

* `W=0`: `Fin 0` has no reveal index. The unique permutation gives singleton actual cells. Do not form `8/W` or `64/W²`; any width-zero statement is a separate trivial case.
* `W=1`: the permutation space is a singleton, `M_g(0)=M_g(1)=g(π)`, and the only increment is zero. No nonzero Fourier character exists. The `W≥2` tail theorem avoids a fictitious positive-width MGF parameter here.
* `W=2`: with `x=(2,-2)`, `χ=(1,-1)`, identity `e` and swap `s`, direct evaluation gives `F(e)=2`, `F(s)=-2`, `M_0=0`, `M_1(e)=2`, `M_1(s)=-2`, and `M_2=F`. Thus `D_Re(0)=±2`, `D_Re(1)=0`; both permutations have mass `1/2`. The process and the cancellation assumption are jointly nondegenerate. The first increments are strictly below the safe `8/W=4` bound.
* Last reveal `k=W-1`: only one unused image remains, so its next-image fiber is the whole current cell and `M_g(W)=M_g(W-1)` pointwise. Keeping the positive parameter `64/W²` at this step is conservative and makes the denominator `64/W` exact for the stated proof. One may later sharpen it, but no sharpening is needed for T668's constant.

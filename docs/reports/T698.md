## Statement and proof check

If `a` and `b` are absent from the revealed prefix, the swap fixes each previously revealed value. It sends `π k = a` to `π k = b` and conversely. The proved involution makes `permutationPrefixFiberSwap` an actual equivalence, with no assumption that either subtype is inhabited. The theorem quantifies over every `W ≥ 2`, `k : Fin W`, prefix table `p`, and distinct unused `a,b`; the inequality and distinctness parameters are not used by the construction, which is mathematically harmless.

The completion claim is not empty in general: at `W=2`, `k=0`, `p i=i`, `a=0`, `b=1`, the theorem constructs the identity in the first fiber and the transposition in the second. An arbitrary `p` can repeat prefix values, leaving both fibers empty; the equivalence remains correct and T694 does not assert their unconditional nonemptiness. Thus this example is the required nondegenerate witness, while any later conditional law must establish its own admissible-prefix support.

The codomain swap equals `(Equiv.swap (π.symm a) (π.symm b)).trans π`, with the correct composition order. The Fourier corollary rewrites by that identity and applies T676's domain-swap estimate.

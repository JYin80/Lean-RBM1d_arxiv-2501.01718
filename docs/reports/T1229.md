## Mathematical preflight

I checked formulas (5.21), (5.24), and (5.40)–(5.43) in the authorized
`paper/250520-YinJun-v2.pdf`. Formula (5.21) is the stopped integrated loop
hierarchy, (5.24) is the martingale moment estimate in terms of integrated
quadratic variation, (5.40)–(5.42) are the propagated second-order, drift,
and covariance estimates, and (5.43) defines the stopping time. This theorem
does not assert those stochastic or stopped-process estimates; it composes
the accepted Lean auxiliary producers.

I inspected the actual declarations and definitions of the accepted
producers:

- **T1073**, `eventually_actual_weighted_g_integral_le_small_slot_anyD`,
  accepts arbitrary fixed `p ≥ 1`, the T995 schedule, the same `E,D,c,s,t`,
  `Cond272Reg`, and `BoundsCore` premises, and is eventually uniform over all
  active target-mesh cells and outputs. Its integrand is the literal
  actual-smooth weighted drift `g`, with coefficient
  `11 / (mE E).im + 3` and the requested endpoint/terminal ratio.
- **T1227**, `eventually_integral_positiveTimeCrossBudget_allOrders_le_small_slot`,
  uses the same fixed `p`, schedule, sample, band, endpoint and active-cell
  range. It estimates the literal full `positiveTimeCrossBudget` at the same
  `N,k,a`, by `Ccross * N^(5δ/32) * ratR(v)^(-2)`.
- **T1123**, `eventually_intervalIntegrable_positive_time_cross_budget`,
  proves interval integrability of that same full cross budget for each fixed
  `p ≥ 1`, including the closed endpoint `r=0` and `k=0`. At `r=0`, its
  literal budget is zero. T1073 separately supplies drift integrability.
- **T1219** supplies the accepted p=1 proof pattern: intersect the producer
  eventual sets before `k,a`, add the integrals using interval integrability,
  absorb the possibly signed drift coefficient by `|q|+1`, and retain the
  outer factor `2` and terminal ratio. The new proof repeats that exact
  composition with the all-orders T1227 producer and fixed arbitrary `p`.

For the composition, `p` is fixed before both producer cutoffs and before
choosing `C = 2 (|q| + Ccross + 1)`, where `q = 11 / Im(m_E) + 3`.
Consequently `C` may depend on fixed `p` but not on `N,k,a`. The common target
is nonnegative on every active endpoint, so the `+1` safely absorbs the drift
coefficient whether or not `q` is positive. The producer integrability
statements justify `integral_add`; no restriction removes the zero cell.

T995's `scheduled_positive_cell_witness` (re-exported by T1073) supplies a
nondegenerate shared premise instance at `E=0,D=60`, with an eventually active
positive cell and a same-sample point in the common event having positive
actual smooth weight. This witnesses satisfiability of the inherited premise
regime; it does not assert transition residence or positive cross mass. No
new hypotheses were introduced.

The result is this literal Lean drift-plus-cross integral slot only. It does
not claim a fit to `APrimeOneStep.driftTerm`, a universal `N1`, an `hfamily`,
general A′ closure, or the paper's stopping-time conclusion. No paper-delta
was added because the theorem is explicitly an auxiliary composition of the
accepted Lean budgets, not a formalization claim for (5.21), (5.24), or
(5.40)–(5.43).

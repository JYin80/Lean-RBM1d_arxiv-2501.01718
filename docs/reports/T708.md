## Statement and proof audit

`permutationPrefixFiber W k p v` consists exactly of permutations `π` with
`π i = p i` for every `i < k` and `π k = v`. T694's equivalence sends an
`a`-fiber element to the `b`-fiber by
`π.trans (Equiv.swap a b)`. Since both `a` and `b` are absent from the
prescribed prefix, this postcomposition fixes all earlier images. The same
postcomposition is its inverse, so the map is a genuine equivalence even when
both fibers are empty.

T703 quantifies over every `W` with `2 ≤ W`, every `k : Fin W`, every
`p : Fin W → Fin W`, distinct unused `a,b : Fin W`, and every complex test
function `F : Equiv.Perm (Fin W) → ℂ`. Its sum identity has the correct
direction: `Fintype.sum_equiv` sums `F (π.trans (Equiv.swap a b))` over the
`a`-fiber and `F ρ` over the `b`-fiber; the integrand compatibility is
definitionally `rfl`. `Fintype.card_congr` yields equality of the two finite
cardinalities. Neither theorem asserts that a cardinality is positive.

The `Fintype` instance is substantive: it obtains `Finite` for permutations
by their injective embedding into the finite function type `Fin W → Fin W`,
then uses the induced finite subtype and `Fintype.ofFinite`. It does not
assume that the fiber is inhabited. For example, at `W=3`, `k=2`, with
`p 0=p 1=0`, `a=1`, and `b=2`, both unused-value hypotheses hold, but no
permutation can realize the repeated prefix; both fibers are empty.

The required nondegenerate case is proved separately. At `W=2`, `k=0`,
`p=id`, `a=0`, `b=1`, T694 constructs the identity in the first fiber and
the transposition in the second. T703 turns these `Nonempty` witnesses into
strictly positive cardinalities. The unused-value conditions are vacuous
for the empty prefix. At `W=1` there cannot be distinct `a,b : Fin 1`, and
the `2 ≤ W` hypothesis excludes that case.

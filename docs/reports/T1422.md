# T1422 report — higher smooth-norm finite-cube calculus

**Result: INCOMPLETE (the stated dimension-free arrow is false without a dimension condition).**

## Exact obstruction

The ticket asks for constants depending only on the fixed order and polynomially on the smooth exponent `q`, with no coordinate-cardinality factor. Its stated hypotheses impose `q ≥ 4M` but do not relate `q` to the finite coordinate type's cardinality.

Fix any even `q ≥ 4M`, let the coordinate type be `Fin d`, and use a one-dimensional deletion cube with vertex vectors

```text
a(0)_i = 1,     a(1)_i = 2       for every i : Fin d.
```

Both vertices have a strictly positive floor coordinate (indeed every coordinate is at least one). Each coordinate's first cube difference is `1`, so it satisfies the requested order-one input bound with `Γ = 1` (and `N = 1`). By the definition of `Cutoff.smoothMax`,

```text
smoothMax q (a(0)) = d^(1/q),
smoothMax q (a(1)) = 2 d^(1/q).
```

Thus the order-one output difference is `d^(1/q)`. With fixed `q`, this is unbounded as `d` varies. Therefore no bound with constants depending only on `M`, `q` (including a factor `q^(C_M)`), and the stated `N, Γ` scales can hold uniformly over finite coordinate types. This already disproves the norm half of the required pair, irrespective of reciprocal positivity.

## Exact missing condition

The existing weight-sum route in `RBM1D/Gauss/CutoffBounds.lean` has exactly the needed dimension control when one assumes

```text
Real.log (Fintype.card ι) ≤ (q : ℝ).
```

Then `Cutoff.rpow_card_le_exp_one` bounds `(Fintype.card ι)^(q⁻¹)` by `e`. Equivalently, the ticket could impose direct `ℓ_q` bounds on vector-valued cube differences instead of coordinatewise bounds. The present ticket states neither condition. An eventual application may also supply the logarithmic cardinality relation for its chosen `q_N`, but that relation is not present in the assigned specification and cannot be silently inferred here.

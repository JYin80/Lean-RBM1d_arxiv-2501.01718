## Result

Added the following public declarations in `RBM.Gauss`:

- `prefixCell_eq_at_width_of_le`: for all `W k : ℕ`, `W ≤ k` and
  `π : PermΩ W`, `prefixCell W k π = prefixCell W W π`.
- `prefixCell_card_eq_one_of_width_le`: under the same hypotheses,
  `(prefixCell W k π).card = 1`.
- `prefixCell_stable_boundary_cases`: checks `W=0,k=0`, `W=1,k=1`, and
  `W=2,k≥2`, including the singleton and cardinality conclusions. It also
  includes T711's `W=2,k=0` identity/swap witness with distinct permutations,
  so the full-reveal singleton is not confused with a trivial sample space.

The proofs reuse T711's singleton result. For the boundary witness the new
module directly uses T711's `prefixCell_two_support_witness`. No new hypotheses
or paper-delta were introduced. No conditional law, local law, probability
estimate, first-cell/shared event, or chain closure is asserted.

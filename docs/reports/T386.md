## One literal candidate and the four pointwise rows

Fix `m=n+2≥2`, the actual Gaussian sample `X=sample d`, `s_N=0`, `0<t_N<1`, and an actual Gaussian `H : MomentDuhamel.Hyp X E s t n` (the Gaussian `gaussHypOfMoments` producer still takes `MomentIneq` and `MomentIneqQ` as inputs). Write `A_u=B.scale E N u`, `L=B.L N`, and `T_u=SumZeroDyn.lkT X E N u ω σ`. For `q=(σ,a)` let

```
F_b = ‖Qop L u (H.F N u (X.H N u ω) σ) b‖
C_b = ‖SumZeroDyn.commS L (xiOf (mSigma E) σ) u T_u b‖
D_b = ‖Psum L T_u (b 0) * SumZeroDyn.varthetaDot L u b‖
ψ(N,u,q,ω) = if 0 ≤ u ∧ u ≤ t_N then
                A_u^m * ∑ b : LoopArg L m, (F_b + C_b + D_b)
              else 0.
```

All scalar times in `Qop`/`commS` are cast to `ℂ`. The finite sum avoids choosing a maximizing argument. On the window, `A_u>0`, so `A_u^{-m}ψ` dominates each of `F_b,C_b,D_b` pointwise for **all** `N,u,q,ω,b`; any `ζ_N≥0` can be added. This gives the literal shapes of `hEnvF`, `hEnvC`, and `hEnvD` at `Lemma514QAssembly.lean:1754–1776`, independently of a good event. The time cutoff makes `ψ` zero outside the physical window, as needed to address `hψint`'s quantifier over *every real* `u` (`:1748`); its definition does not select a stochastic event or depend on a moment order.

At `u=0`, `SumZeroDyn.lkT X E N 0 ω σ=0` by `Sample.lkErr_zero` and `SumZeroDyn.norm_lkT` (T381), hence both `C_b` and `D_b` vanish. `RBM.Gauss.Fpath_zero_at_initial` and `Qop_Fpath_zero_at_initial` (`Lemma514FpathZero.lean:172,184`, T383) make `F_b=0` for the actual `H`, under its displayed `s_N=0≤t_N` conditions. Consequently `ψ(N,0,q,ω)=0` for every `N,q,ω`. The `hEnvI` left side is also exactly zero. Taking `Phi_N(q)≥0`, `C=1` gives the literal `hMψI` order

`∀ ε>0, ∀ p≥1, ∃ C>0, ∀ᶠ N, ∀ q, momNorm B.P (2*p) (ψ N 0 q) ≤ C N^(ε/2) Phi_N(q)`

with no large-`N` or event argument. This is compatible with a strictly positive first-cell interval and positive control: `RBM.Gauss.driftF_zero_first_cell_exampleGrow` (`Lemma514FpathZero.lean:220`) uses `Dims.exampleGrow`, `E=0`, `τ′=1`, positive `A_0`, and eventually `0<t_N`. The zero value reflects the exact initial condition (2.67), not an empty window. The actual `H` remains conditional on its two moment-inequality inputs; no witness for them is inferred from T383.

## Positive-time moment budget and noncircularity

The live `hMψ` row (`QAssembly.lean:1863`) is, for **every** `ε>0` and `p≥1`, `∃ C>0` then eventually uniformly in `u∈[0,v_N]` and **all** `q`, with control `Phi_N(q) ρ_N(u)`. In the intended model budget `Phi_N(q)≤C_m(1+Φ_N)` and `ρ_N(u)=(etaT E u)^{-1}`; `C` may depend on fixed `m,p,ε`, but neither on `N,u,q` nor on a chosen event. An all-sample polynomial envelope plus a suitable stochastic-domination estimate would convert to these all-order moments, but cannot itself supply the estimate.

* **Projected drift.** `psiF`/`norm_Qop_F_le_psiF` (`QAssembly.lean:2596,2612`) provide its literal pointwise envelope. The bound `psiF_le_of_drift_inputs''` (`:2789`) has a main term proportional to `xiRhs_m`, so its correct stochastic input is `SumZeroDyn.xiRhs_stochDom` (`SumZeroDyn.lean:3012`) with **`(2n+3)Φ_N`**, plus the displayed length-one and decay inputs. The compiled `psiF_stochDom_etaT_inv_of_drift_inputs''` (`Lemma514FBridge.lean:24`) instead assumes `flowXiLK_1≺1` and `xiRhs_m≺1`; `Lemma514Premises` permits growing `Φ_N`, and these stronger inputs do not follow. The older `SumZeroDyn.F_stochDom` (`SumZeroDyn.lean:3051`) retains `(2n+3)Φ` but assumes `0<s_N` and bounds the unprojected drift; it does not fill the first-cell projected moment row.
* **Commutator and dot.** Both literal `C_b,D_b` contain the *current target length* `T_u=lkT_m`. Bounding them by a current-cell Lemma 5.14 estimate would be circular. `SumZeroDyn.norm_commS_le` (`SumZeroDyn.lean:1046`) reduces the commutator to a `Psum T_u` bound. `SumZeroDyn.norm_Psum_lkT_le` (`:3209`) derives that bound from lower length `xiLK_{m-1}` and decay, hence noncircular, **only under `QGood σ`**. `SumZeroDyn.hgood_commDot` (`:3243`) uses the guarded `commTerm`/`dotTerm` (`:3234,3239`), which are zero for non-`QGood`; it additionally assumes `0<s_N`, so it cannot be applied literally to the first cell. In contrast, `hEnvC`, `hEnvD`, their integrand rows, and `hMψ` in `rhs514QAt_of_kernel_inputs'''` are unguarded and quantify over all `q`. Its proof invokes the raw commutator and dot tensors at `:2072,2100`. The final Q/P assembly needs the Q route only for `QGood`, and the non-`QGood` cases have their separate route; the unguarded `'''` interface is stronger than that use.

This is a real quantifier gap. The generic unguarded slot-sum estimate loses an optimal `L^n` factor (`Lemma514QAssembly.lean:289`, T218), which cannot be silently absorbed into `N^ε` or `C_m(1+Φ_N)` for arbitrarily small `ε`. It does **not** prove that the actual Gaussian `lkT` violates the desired bound off `QGood`; no such counterexample is claimed. It proves that the existing guarded Ward producer cannot justify the literal all-charge `hMψ` hypothesis. Setting `ψ=0` off `QGood` likewise fails the raw `hEnvC/D` rows unless a new small `ζ_N` bound is proved there.

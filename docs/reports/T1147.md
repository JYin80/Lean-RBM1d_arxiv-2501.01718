## Exact statement proved

For each fixed `p ≥ 1` and admissible `E,D,c,s,t,δ`, eventually, uniformly in
every active cell `k` and output `a`, the actual weighted-drift integrand is
interval-integrable and

```text
∫_[s_N,v] g(E,D,s,t,deltaWeight(δ),p,N,k,a)(u) du
+ ∫_[s_N,v] crossProfile(E,D,s,t,zetaSrc(δ),tauG(δ),
                         deltaWeight(δ),deltaCap(δ),N,k,a)(u) du
≤ (11 / (mE E).im + 3 + crossProfileBudgetConst E)
    * N^(5δ/32) * ratR(E,s,N,v)^(-2),
```

The coefficient is the sum of `11 / (mE E).im + 3` and the
cross-profile constant.

The common eventual set is the intersection of the two producer cutoffs.
The proof adds their inequalities directly; no new analytic or probabilistic
assumption is introduced. The theorem includes `k = 0`; at that cell both
integrals use the same zero-length endpoint convention from the producers.

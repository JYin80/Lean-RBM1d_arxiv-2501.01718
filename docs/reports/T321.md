The older `psiF_stochDom_etaT_inv_of_drift_inputs` requires `flowXiLK_m ≺ 1` through `m=n+2`, and using `Step45.xiLK_le_one_of_hyp` there is circular at `n=0,m=2` (T288). The four `Lemma514Premises` alone provide the latter two only with control `Φ`, so the main projected-drift term cannot be discharged at control `1` from those premises.

## Polynomial envelope and exponent ledger

For fixed `M=n+2`, `exists_norm_Kval_le_upto_one` bounds `Kval` at every length `1,…,M` by one fixed `CK`. On an eventual `Cond272` window, `SumZeroDyn.flow_crude` gives `1≤A_u≤N`; the resolvent/window estimate gives `η_u^{-1}≤N²`. The deterministic Hermitian resolvent and `Kval` bounds then give, for **every** window time and sample point,

```text
XiLK_j ≤ (1+CK) N^(3j+1),                 1≤j≤M,
xiSum_M ≤ Cpoly N^(3M+1),                 Cpoly=M(1+CK).
```

In `psiF_le_of_drift_inputs''`, the relevant additive term is

```text
A_u^M [1+(24e cTwo52 K)^(n+1)] W L δ
  [(2n+1)M² xiSum_M + M C1]
  + A_u^M (2 cTwo52)^(n+1) L^(n+1) δF.
```

With `K=C1=N^θ`, `δ=δF=N^{-D1}`, `WL≤N`, `L≤N`, and `A_u≤N`, its top-length component is at most a fixed constant times `N^(4n+10+(n+1)θ-D1)` (the `xiSum_M` factor contributes `N^(3n+7)`). The `C1` component costs at most `N^(n+3+(n+2)θ-D1)`; the projection tail costs at most `N^(2n+3-D1)`. Thus `D1` may be chosen after fixed `n,θ,D` to make each at most `N^{-D}`. The compiled proof deliberately uses a larger, simpler envelope: `k=3n+7`, `e=k(2n+6)`, `Rbig=(1+Cpoly)N^k`, and `D1=D+e+1`; `eventually_psiF_poly_error_bound` absorbs the fixed coefficients with the last `N^{-1}`. The enlarged `Rbig` is used only in the error branch; the main branch retains `K=N^θ`.

At `n=0,m=2`, `xiSum_2≤Cpoly N^7`. The top-length error is `O(N^(10+θ-D1))`; the other two errors are `O(N^(3+2θ-D1))` and `O(N^(3-D1))`. The compiled choice is `e=42`, `D1=D+43`, more than enough for its chosen small `θ`. No estimate `XiLK_2≺1` is invoked.

## Event and parameter order

The outer `StochDom` proof fixes a tolerance `τ>0` and a tail exponent `D>0` after `n`. It obtains a small radius exponent `θ>0` from the main coefficient bound, then chooses `D1=D+(3n+7)(2n+6)+1` for the additive branch. `FlowInputs` supplies the joint `K`, `L-K`, and `L` decay event at this `θ,D1`; `highProb_Fpath_fastDecay` supplies `FastDecay(H.F)` at the same `θ,D1`, window, tensor length, and radius `4 ellHat·N^θ`. The length-one and `xiRhs` stochastic events are intersected with these events. The polynomial `xiSum` estimate holds for all `ω` and `u` eventually, so it adds no random event. On the intersection the bound is uniform in `u` and the length-`n+2` loop coordinate. T289's decay budget independently chooses its raw decay exponent after `n,θ,D1`; its fixed-length polynomial sizes are absorbed by that choice.

This matches the arbitrary fixed decay exponents in paper Lemma 5.9, (5.75), and the fast-decay assertion following (5.77). The paper's Lemma 5.14 target is (5.92). The repository's `FlowInputs` is an explicit Step-1/2 decay/local-law interface, not a hidden Lemma-5.14 statement; this audit does not claim its model-level production.

For the strict base window `s_N=0`, `v_N=1/2`, one has `0<v_N<1`, `η_{v_N}>0`, and `A_{v_N}` grows with `W_N` in the growing-band model. Hence the `Cond272` inequality is eventually compatible with this positive-duration window; the deterministic polynomial bound has no `s_N>0` requirement. The compiled p.24 grid witness is a separate genuine first cell: `gridT_zero` and `eventually_gridT_zero_lt_gridT_one` give `u_0=0<u_1`, while `cond272Reg_grid_step_domain` supplies its scale condition. `eventually_Fpath_fastDecay_forward_nonempty` certifies nonempty decay events on such forward windows **conditional on `FlowInputs`**. No assertion of joint satisfiability of the still independent `flowXiLK_1≺1` and `xiRhs≺1` inputs is made.

## Narrow next theorem and limits

The narrow additive-row theorem is the existing `psiF_stochDom_etaT_inv_of_drift_inputs'`, or T296's `''` version when the T289 event is desired as a supplied theorem. No new top-length estimate is needed for that row. The remaining main term is

```text
η_u^{-1} [1+(24e cTwo52 K)^(n+1)] (K+2)
  cDrift(n,CK,C1) · xiRhs_{n+2}.
```

`Lemma514Premises` yields `xiRhs_{n+2} ≺ (2n+3)Φ` via `SumZeroDyn.xiRhs_stochDom`, rather than `≺1`; its length-one control is likewise `Φ`. Any full fourth-slot theorem must carry `Φ` through this main term (toward the intended `Λ^{1/2}+Φ` control), or obtain stronger noncircular model inputs. The polynomial additive-row argument alone cannot close the slot.

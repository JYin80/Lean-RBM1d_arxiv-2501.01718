## Exact consumer and proof

The theorem
`APrimeGeneralMovingWeightedDriftProductIntegrable.eventually_actual_weighted_drift_product_integrable`
proves integrability of exactly

```text
w(ω) · |flowY(coordAt Step2.sigPM)(r,ω)|^(2p−1) · |driftAt Step2.sigPM(r,ω)|,
```

where `w` is T615's `APrimeSmoothWeightActual.weight` at the same `N`,
prefix `k`, smoothing order, `deltaWeight`, endpoint
`cutNetPt s (targetMesh D) N k`, starting time `s N`, and running time `r`.
The drift is the normalized `APrimeDriftTimeFamily.driftAt` with charge
`Step2.sigPM` and that same endpoint/time/output. No event restriction or
replacement weight is introduced.

The proof applies accepted T1067's weighted `2p`-moment integrability for
the same `flowY`, and accepted T1069's weighted `2p`-moment integrability for
the same normalized drift. On an active cell with `N ≥ 2`, T615's weight is
the actual cutoff to power `2p`. Writing `Y = cutoff · flowY` and
`G = cutoff · |drift|`, the two inputs give `Integrable(|Y|^(2p))` and
`Integrable(|G|^(2p))`, and the target integrand is pointwise
`|Y|^(2p−1) · |G|`. Hölder with conjugate exponents
`2p/(2p−1)` and `2p` proves the result. At `p = 1`, these are both `2`;
the exponent `2p−1` is positive and no zero-power or division-by-zero branch
is used.

Measurability is supplied by T1043 for the actual coordinate observable and
by continuity in the Gaussian sample for the normalized drift. The interval
membership is used on its closed form, so both endpoints are covered. No
assumption excludes `k = 0`.

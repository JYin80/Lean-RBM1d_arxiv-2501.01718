# T291 — A′ rate regularity at the Gaussian coordinate level

## Step 0: the actual interfaces

`APrimeDuhamelModel.momFlowDeriv_le` is pointwise in a time `u`. Its `G : Gauss.Ω d → ℝ`, `Q : Gauss.Ω d → ℝ`, and scalar cross budget `Bc` are free parameters at that time. No declaration in the inspected A′ assembly fixes a common family `r ↦ G r` or `r ↦ B r` for one endpoint `v` and coordinate `a`. In particular, `APrimeAssembly.lean` has no instantiation of `weightedMinkowski_of_deriv_le` for those rates. This is the first structural gap, before a time-integrability proof can be stated for the actual drift rate `A r = momNormW (Gauss.P d) w p (G r)`.

The evolved QV random-variable family **is** named: `APrimeDuhamelModel.qvRateEvolvedOn d N Ψ₁ Good r`. `rateNormW_qvRateEvolvedOn_le` gives a pointwise scalar upper bound when `EvolvedQVBound` holds. It gives neither time measurability nor `IntervalIntegrable` for the actual scalar rate `g r = APrimeModel.rateNormW (Gauss.P d) w p (qvRateEvolvedOn … r)`. The `Good` indicator is fixed in the sample variable, so a producer can prove time continuity of the underlying evolved QV, then use finite-size domination; that producer is not present in the inspected A′ files.

`Gauss.continuousOn_driftELK_time` and `continuousOn_driftEG_time` in `FlowContInt` concern **expectations** of drift expressions, and `intervalIntegrable_uker_driftELK`/`intervalIntegrable_uker_driftEG` concern their kernel propagation. They do not identify the sample-dependent `G r` consumed by `momFlowDeriv_le`, and therefore cannot be substituted for the weighted norm of that `G r`.

## Compiled positive result

`intervalIntegrable_crossInt_qHatNear` proves genuine first-cell time integrability of the deterministic candidate cross budget

`r ↦ APrimeModel.crossInt κ (APrimeTimeInt.qHatNear E 0 t) r`.

It uses `APrimeTimeInt.nearInt_continuousOn` for the near QV envelope and `intervalIntegrable_sqrt_inv` for the integrable `r^{-1/2}` singularity at zero. It assumes only `|E|<2`, `0≤t<1`; it does not infer integrability from an integral budget. `crossInt_qHatNear_first_cell_witness` compiles a joint nondegenerate instance: `E=0`, `κ=1`, `t=1/2`, with the cross rate **strictly positive** at `r=1/4`.

This theorem does **not** identify the planned `B r` with this candidate. `crossBudget_le_crossInt_model` is an inequality; an upper bound alone cannot make an unmeasurable `B` integrable. A noncircular route is to select the explicit deterministic `crossInt` itself as the `B` in the derivative inequality, using S5 to bound the cross part by it. The near QV envelope then supplies its time integrability. If the final `B` includes far-field terms, those terms require their own continuous or dominated time-rate theorem.

## Minimum remaining producer and assembly status

For each fixed finite `N`, endpoint `v`, coordinate `a`, moment order `p`, and fixed sample weight `w`, the next model theorem must:

1. define one time family `G_{v,a}(r,ω)` for the normalized evolved coordinate and prove the pointwise (G) generator inequality with that **same** family;
2. prove time measurability and an integrable finite-size envelope, or closed-window continuity, for `r ↦ momNormW (Gauss.P d) w p (G_{v,a} r)`;
3. prove the analogous time regularity of `r ↦ rateNormW (Gauss.P d) w p (qvRateEvolvedOn … r)`, or explicitly choose a continuous integrable envelope as the `g` in the derivative inequality and prove the pointwise replacement;
4. select a concrete `B r` in the same derivative inequality, with time integrability (the compiled near-field cross budget is one available component).

Uniform finite-size bounds must hold on the closed window `s_N≤r≤v≤t_N≤t₀<1`; the endpoint restriction supplies a positive lower bound for `η_r` at each fixed `N`. Pointwise sample moments and N1/N2 numerical integral budgets cannot replace the time-regularity steps, as T285's Gaussian rate certificates and T280e's budget counterexample demonstrate.

When the first two scalar rates are supplied, `APrimeTimeIntegrability.intervalIntegrable_momNormW_mul_flowY` supplies the third prefix premise automatically. Only then can `weightedMinkowski_of_deriv_le` be instantiated with the same concrete rate families. **No `hfamily_of_coord_budgets` or `APrimeSlot'` is claimed here.**

## Result

`RBM.Gauss.RandomLmaxSmoothNormCubeComposition.alternatingSmoothNormPower_cube_bound`
accepts a finite nonempty coordinate type, `1 ≤ r ≤ M`, even `q ≥ max 2 (4*M)`, either
`s = 1` or `s = -1`, nonnegative vertex vectors, a common vertexwise lower comparison
`c*Gamma ≤ qNorm q (a v)` with `c, Gamma > 0`, and nonnegative `B_j`. For every nonempty
duplicate-free coordinate list and every complementary bit assignment (the bits on the
listed coordinates are false), it assumes the shifted-face bound
`qNorm q (Delta_coords a b) ≤ B_coords.length * Gamma^(1+coords.length/2)`.

The conclusion is

```text
|alternatingVertexSum r (fun v => smoothNormPower q s (a v))|
  ≤ Gamma^(s+r/2) * Σ_{d : OrderedFinpartition r}
      outerDerivativeConstant d.length * q^(d.length-1)
        * rho^(s-d.length) * ∏_{m : Fin d.length} B_(d.partSize m),
rho = 2^(-M) * min(c, 1).
```

Thus the two sign instances expose exactly `Gamma^(1+r/2)` and
`Gamma^(-1+r/2)`. The partition terms retain `q^(k-1)`, the accepted T1450 constant, and
the product loss for all blocks; there is no dimension factor or order-zero upper-bound
assumption.

The proof establishes `qNorm q (A(t)) ≥ rho*Gamma` throughout the unit cube for the
multiaffine interpolation `A`. This places the cube inside the open preimage of the nonzero
vectors, where the reciprocal power is smooth. Repeated cube FTC then reduces the alternating
sum to an integral. The Faà di Bruno ordered-partition sum is bounded block by block using the
all-shifted-face hypothesis and the accepted outer derivative estimate. The block Gamma
exponents sum to `k+r/2`, yielding the stated common `Gamma^(s+r/2)` factor.

The theorem is parameterized by every `r` satisfying `1 ≤ r ≤ M`. Hence `r=1` is covered
whenever `M ≥ 1`, and the full-order case `r=M` is covered whenever `M ≥ 1`; the proof uses
only the weak inequality `r ≤ M` and does not require a strict gap.

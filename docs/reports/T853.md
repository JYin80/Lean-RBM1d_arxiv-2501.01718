# T853 — finite prefix-cell conditional expectation

## Result

**PASS within the finite permutation model.** For every `W k : ℕ` and every real-valued `F : PermΩ W → ℝ`, `RBM.Gauss.uniformPerm_prefixCell_condExp` proves

```text
(uniformPerm W)[F | prefixSigma W k]
  =ᵐ[uniformPerm W]
    (fun π => (∑ ρ ∈ prefixCell W k π, F ρ) / (prefixCell W k π).card).
```

The mean uses the actual prefix cell containing `π`. Its denominator is positive by `prefixCell_card_pos`; the proof uses the generated `prefixSigma W k`, the cell partition, and the established cell-integral uniqueness criterion. The right-hand side is measurable because it is constant on actual cells. Both functions are integrable on the finite space. The proof does not posit or select a pointwise representative of the conditional expectation.

## Boundary and nondegeneracy checks

- `prefixCellMean_zero_width` and `prefixCellMean_one` show the cell average equals `F π` at every prefix for `W = 0` and `W = 1`.
- `prefixCellMean_zero` identifies the `k = 0` mean with the full-space mean. `uniformPerm_prefixCell_condExp_zero` gives the corresponding a.e. conditional-expectation statement.
- `prefixCellMean_eq_of_width_le` is the pointwise `F π` identity whenever `W ≤ k`; `uniformPerm_prefixCell_condExp_of_width_le` gives the a.e. conditional-expectation specialization.
- `prefixCellMean_two_zero_indicator` checks `W = 2`, `k = 0`, and the identity-permutation indicator: its actual cell is the whole two-element permutation space, it contains both identity and swap, the two are distinct, each singleton has positive `uniformPerm` mass, and the mean is `1/2`. `prefixCellMean_two_zero_indicator_all` and `uniformPerm_prefixCell_condExp_two_zero_indicator` give the mean and conditional expectation for every sample point.

There are no new assumptions and no vacuous premises. The positive singleton masses at `W = 2` provide a nondegenerate support witness.

## Proof and public supporting results

`eventually_block_card_le` is a direct application of
`Gauss.card_ZMod_L_le d`. Thus the block count uses exponent one and introduces
no additional index or cardinality factor.

`window_length_bounds` proves both `0 <= t_N-s_N` and
`t_N-s_N <= 1` from the window hypotheses.

`net_exponent_eq` proves the exact arithmetic identity

```text
(6+1+1)/(1/2) = 16.
```

For N at least one, `net_spacing_eq` proves

```text
1/N^((6+1+1)/(1/2)) = N^(-16).
```

Its eventual corollary supplies `hdelta` at equality, with no slack exponent
or constant. The spacing is nonnegative for every N and positive for N at
least one, by `meshSpacing_nonneg`, `meshSpacing_pos`, and
`eventually_meshSpacing_pos`. Strict positivity at N=0 is neither required
by the consumer nor asserted.

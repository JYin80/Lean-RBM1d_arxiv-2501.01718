## Installed APIs

### Generated measurable spaces and finite observations

* `MeasurableSpace.generateFrom_mono (h : s ⊆ t) : generateFrom s ≤ generateFrom t` and `MeasurableSpace.measurableSet_generateFrom (ht : t ∈ s) : MeasurableSet t` are the basic generated-sigma tools used by T711/T732.
* `MeasurableSpace.comap_generateFrom` has type
  `comap f (generateFrom s) = generateFrom (preimage f '' s)`.
  `MeasurableSpace.comap_eq_generateFrom m f` gives the general preimage-generator presentation of a comap; `MeasurableSpace.measurableSet_comap` characterizes its measurable sets as preimages of measurable codomain sets.
* `MeasurableSpace.pi` equips a dependent function space with the product sigma algebra. The theorem `generateFrom_pi` (root namespace) states, for `[Finite ι]` and measurable component spaces, that this sigma algebra is generated by measurable boxes. Together these APIs support presenting prefix revelation as the comap of a finite coordinate-observation map
  `π ↦ (fun i : {i : Fin W // i.val < k} => π i.1)` into a dependent function space. T711's coordinate-cylinder generators are exactly the one-coordinate observations in that map. The corresponding equality with `prefixSigma W k` is a small generator-identification lemma, not an existing named theorem located in this search.
* For generic finite partitions, `MeasurableSpace.generateFrom_memPartition_le` gives `generateFrom (memPartition t n) ≤ m` when each `t n` is measurable, and `MeasurableSpace.measurableSet_generateFrom_memPartition_iff` characterizes its measurable sets as finite unions of partition cells. `generateFrom_iUnion_memPartition` identifies the sigma algebra generated by all levels with the one generated by the sequence. `ProbabilityTheory.partitionFiltration` and `measurableSet_partitionFiltration_memPartitionSet` expose the associated filtration and cell measurability. These are available if a proof first encodes the finite coordinate observations as a sequence; they are generic partition machinery, not a direct theorem about T711's `prefixSigma`.

### Measurable atoms

* `measurableAtom x` is the intersection of every measurable set containing `x`, for the currently inferred measurable-space instance.
* `measurableAtom_subset hs hx` says the atom is contained in any measurable set containing the point. `measurableAtom_eq_of_mem` identifies atoms when one point belongs to the other's atom.
* `MeasurableSet.measurableAtom_of_countable x` proves atom measurability from a `Countable` carrier. Alternatively, `measurableSet_measurableAtom` assumes `[CountablyGenerated α]`. `measurableAtom_of_measurableSingletonClass` reduces atoms to singletons when singleton sets are measurable; that is the wrong reduction for a coarse prefix sigma algebra until the full-prefix boundary.
* In the proposed bridge the atom must explicitly use the prefix sigma algebra instance, e.g. `@measurableAtom (PermΩ W) (prefixSigma W k) π`. T711's ambient instance on `PermΩ W` is `⊤`, so an unqualified `measurableAtom π` under that default instance would give the full-information singleton rather than the prefix atom. The instance distinction is material.

### Conditional expectation on a single measurable set

* `ProbabilityTheory.condExp_generateFrom_singleton hs hf` applies to `generateFrom {s}`. It states that the conditional expectation is a.e. equal on `μ.restrict s` to the integral against `μ[|s]`; it requires a finite measure, a measurable `s`, and integrability of `f` (plus the normed-space assumptions in the target). `condExp_set_generateFrom_singleton` is the indicator-set version.
* `ProbabilityTheory.condExpKernel_singleton_ae_eq_cond` gives the corresponding conditional-kernel formula for `generateFrom {s}` under `[StandardBorelSpace Ω]` and measurability of both sets. It is not a prefix-partition theorem.
* `MeasureTheory.condExp_indicator` says conditional expectation commutes a.e. with restricting an integrand to a conditioning-measurable set. `condExp_restrict_ae_eq_restrict` compares conditional expectations under a restricted measure and the original measure on that measurable set, with the sigma-finiteness and integrability assumptions shown by `#check`.
* In the searched installed sources I found no direct theorem that writes `condExp` with respect to an arbitrary finite partition as the normalized average on each atom. The singleton-generator result should not be silently generalized to a sigma algebra with many cells. A later proof can use finite-partition generator characterization and conditional-expectation integral characterization, or formulate the needed finite conditional-average interface directly.

## Smallest recommended next interface

Use a single explicit, noncircular atom identity as the next finite bridge:

```lean
theorem measurableAtom_prefixSigma_eq_prefixCell
    (W k : ℕ) (π : PermΩ W) :
    @measurableAtom (PermΩ W) (prefixSigma W k) π =
      {ρ | ρ ∈ prefixCell W k π}
```

This is a proposed signature only, not proved here. T732 already supplies measurability of the right-hand event in exactly `prefixSigma W k`; T711 supplies that the realized cell contains `π`; T733 supplies the actual-cell equality/disjointness/cover properties. A proof can characterize prefix-measurable sets as unions of the finite observation fibers, or first establish the finite-observation `comap` identity above and identify its fiber at `π`. This avoids assuming a conditional law or using such a law to establish the atom itself. For the zero-width and `k=0` boundaries the cell is `univ`; for `W ≤ k` it is `{π}`. At `W=2,k=0`, id and swap remain distinct members of the same atom/cell; at `W=2,k=1`, their cells separate. Thus the proposed interface respects the existing nondegenerate witnesses.

This identity only identifies the conditioning atom. It does not state a conditional distribution, a conditional expectation formula on the prefix partition, or any paper local law. In particular, equations (2.3)–(2.4) and their `N/W/ell/eta/time` scales remain downstream. No first-cell or shared event, P2 assertion, or chain closure is included.

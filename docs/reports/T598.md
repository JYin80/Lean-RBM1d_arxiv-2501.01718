## Formula bookkeeping

### (5.35): linear source

The far coefficient is T586's literal row:

```text

  [cFar(W,ell_u) Jbar_u (flowDelta + W^-1)

        + L sqrt(W^-D) / ell_u)].
```

the exact T586 cap

```text

```

The near coefficient is the principal T586 term


plus the exact conversion of T586's residual by
`APrimeDriftNearAbsorb.residual_ratio_le` with `C=2`:

```text

   * (W ell_u eta_u)^2
   * exp((log W)^(3/4)) * T_u(gap)]
 * [eta_u^-1 ratR_u^3].
```

The raw length-three loss `zetaSrc` and centered loss `zetaCtr` are not
identified or absorbed.

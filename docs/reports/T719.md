## Statements and proof

For every `W ≥ 1`, table bounds `‖x i‖ ≤ 2` and `‖χ i‖ ≤ 1`, and permutation `π`, `norm_permutationFourierSum_le_two` proves

```text
‖permutationFourierSum W x χ π‖ ≤ 2.
```

It applies the norm triangle inequality to the finite sum, bounds each summand by `‖χ i‖ * ‖x (π i)‖ ≤ 2`, then divides by the positive real normalization `W`. The positivity premise excludes `W = 0` before division and includes `W = 1`.

`permutationFourierSum_sharp_two` gives the explicit witness `W=2`, `π=id`, `x=(2,-2)`, and `χ=(1,-1)`. Both tables are nonconstant, satisfy the required pointwise bounds, and their normalized sum is exactly `2` in norm.

## Claim and dependency check

`D^j (∑ i, x i ^ q)[v] = (q.descFactorial j) * ∑ i, x i ^ (q-j) * ∏ m : Fin j, v m i`.

The public `abs_iteratedFDeriv_powerSum_le` proves both the falling-factorial and `q^j` estimates with `qNorm q u = (∑ i, |u i|^q)^((q : ℝ)⁻¹)`. Its quantifiers cover every `x` and every tuple of directions; no coordinate positivity, nonzero-vector, dimension, or cardinality assumption appears.

The derivative formula follows from the Mathlib continuous-linear projection composition formula, `iteratedDeriv_pow`, and finite-sum differentiation. The norm bound follows from the source's proved `qNorm_pow`, zero-norm characterization, normalization, weighted finite AM–GM, and scaling identity. The weights are `(q-j)/q` and `j` copies of `1/q`, which sum to one. Triangle inequality and `Nat.descFactorial_le_pow` finish the two public estimates. There is no assumed Hölder estimate or circular call to the public bound.

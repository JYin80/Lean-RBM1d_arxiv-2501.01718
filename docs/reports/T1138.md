## Claim and verdict

**PASS on the narrow T1137 mathematical preflight FAIL.** The requested
Gaussian estimate remains **incomplete**, not refuted. For each fixed
`δ₀`, `0 < δ ≤ δ₀`, and `p ≥ 1`, the T999 interface selects `q ≥ p` and
`C > 0` before `∀ᶠ N`. It then needs, uniformly for every active
`N^4 < k ≤ cutNetTop s t (targetMesh D) N`, the norm-good-event integral

```text
∫ ω in {ω | ‖Xmat exampleGrow N ω‖ ≤ N},
  widenedW(canonicalR s t (targetMesh D), 1, jSnorm,
           s, t, targetMesh D, δ, q, N, k, ω)
```

The weight uses exactly the earlier indices `j < k`; the observable is the
same sample at index `k`, with the same `δ` and order `q`. T999's
`eventually_actual_widened_family_of_selected_high` assumes the corresponding
all-index high-order integral and transfers it to lower orders. It does not
produce the high-order Gaussian estimate. I found no supplied producer of the
displayed far-index good-event implication.

## Independent arithmetic and nonvacuity

T475 gives
`|jSnorm(v,ω)-jSnorm(w,ω)| ≤ N^(2D+7)|v-w|^(1/2)` on `good N`.
The target mesh is `N^(4D+18)` for positive `N`; hence comparison of index
`k` with index zero costs `N^-2 √k`. The global `19/4` Lipschitz bound for
`cutTrunc` makes the resulting `2q`-power error at most a `q`-dependent
constant times `N^-4q k^q`. Paying that error from `C N^(δq/2)` by this
route requires `k` of order at most `N^(4+δ/2)`. This is a limit of the
available **upper bound**, not a lower bound on the true random increment.

On positive widened-weight support, the preceding values are at most
`4e N^(2δ)`; the last mesh step adds at most `N^-2`. This gives only an
endpoint cap of order `N^(2δ)`, whose `2q` power has order `N^(4δq)`.
Neither support nor the deterministic truncation cap supplies the missing
tail probability needed for `N^(δq/2)`.

The range is substantive. T995's first-cell model has `E=0`, `D=60`,
`s_N=0`, and eventually `t_N=1/2`, with `Cond272Reg` and `BoundsCore` jointly
satisfied. There `targetMesh=N^258` and
`cutNetTop=⌊N^258/2⌋`, so `k=N^5` and much larger far indices are active
eventually. The norm-good event is nonempty (also supplied by the accepted
norm-event witness). These facts certify the hypotheses and index range;
they do not certify the desired integral inequality.

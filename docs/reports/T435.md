## Exact producer ledger

### 1. `IntervalIntegrable (Adr + Bcr)`

`APrimeDriftTimeFamily.intervalIntegrable_drift_add_cross` (lines 265--273) has exactly the
displayed `Adr` and `Bcr`.  Its hypotheses are

```text
|E| < 2,  0 ≤ s,  s ≤ v,  v < 1,
Measurable w,  0 ≤ w,  w ≤ 1.
```

The drift part is proved by
`continuousOn_momNormW_driftAt` and
`intervalIntegrable_momNormW_driftAt` (lines 186--238).  The proof uses the actual
`driftAt`, positivity of the fixed endpoint normalization `driftScale`, the finite-size
all-sample envelope `Gauss.exists_bdd_uker_driftF`, sample measurability, and samplewise
continuity of the `driftF` path.  The expectation is handled by dominated convergence with
a constant integrable envelope under `Gauss.P d`.  Thus this is genuine time continuity and
interval integrability, rather than an a.e. event bound or a value assigned to an undefined
interval integral.

The cross part is proved by `intervalIntegrable_crossAt` (lines 242--261).  It writes

```text
crossAt E s v κ r
  = sqrt(r)⁻¹ * sqrt(κ * qHatNear E s v r)
```

in Lean's `crossInt` form.  The second factor is continuous on `[s,v]`; the first factor is
genuinely interval integrable on a nonnegative window, including `s=0`.  This is the only
rate here that can retain a singularity at the first-cell left endpoint.  Its size is
`r⁻¹/²`, which is integrable; continuity at `r=0` is neither claimed nor needed.

For the actual smooth weight, the required weight facts come from
`APrimeSmoothWeightActual.weightC1` (lines 1191--1230), whose `cont` field gives
measurability, together with `weight_nonneg` and `weight_le_one` (lines 412--425).
The first-cell side conditions for `weightC1` are deterministic: `0<N` on the eventual
branch, `canonicalM_pos`, and
`cutNetPt ... j < 1` for `j<k`, obtained from
`MomentDuhamelCut.netFinset_subset_Icc`, `k≤cutNetTop`, and
`APrimeSupportRunning.firstT_bounds`, whose upper bound is `firstCellT τ' N≤1/2`.

### 2. `IntervalIntegrable g`

measurability of the parameterized Gaussian integral, and combines this with the

## The left endpoint `r=0`

For the actual first cell, `E=0` and

```text
etaT 0 r = 1-r,
0 ≤ r ≤ v ≤ firstCellT τ' N ≤ 1/2,
```

so `etaT 0 r≥1/2`.  Consequently all resolvent and fixed-endpoint normalization envelopes
used in the drift, coordinate, and QV regularity results remain finite uniformly on the
closed cell.  The Gaussian space is finite dimensional for every fixed `N`; the relevant
observables have deterministic polynomial/all-sample envelopes, so all fixed-`N` Gaussian
moments used by the dominated-convergence arguments are finite.

The map `r↦Hflow r ω=√r X(ω)` is continuous at zero.  Hence `Y_r` and the normalized drift
moment have no continuity obstruction there.  The QV proof only needs joint measurability
and a bounded finite-size envelope.  The explicit Brownian cross factor has the genuine
`r⁻¹/²` singularity described above, and its dedicated theorem proves integrability.  Thus
there is no hidden `r⁻¹` rate at this gate.

The parameterized-integral steps are already implemented: dominated convergence is used
for the two moment-continuity results, and
`StronglyMeasurable.integral_prod_right'` is used for QV-rate measurability.  No new Fubini
or dominated-convergence lemma is required in the assembly theorem.

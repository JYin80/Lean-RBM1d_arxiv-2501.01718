# T485 — general moving `hfamily` interface and mathematics audit

## Verdict

The preflight **passes**, with one necessary clarification of the low-order
argument.  The exact general-window `hfamily` remains mathematically
consistent and has the required quantifier order, but it is not yet produced
in Lean.

For a requested positive order `p`, one must not apply Lyapunov between
`widenedW P` and `widenedW p`.  If `P >= p`, their common base cutoff `b` gives
`widenedW P = b^(2P) <= b^(2p) = widenedW p`, which is the wrong direction for
deducing the requested moment.  The valid route is:

1. choose a fixed high order `P >= p` before `N`;
2. prove the high coordinate and family moment under one actual smooth weight
   `V_P` of order `P`;
3. apply weighted Lyapunov from `P` to `p` under this same `V_P`;
4. use the pointwise cross-order comparison
   `widenedW p <= V_P` to transfer the resulting low moment.

The generic theorem
`APrimeFirstCellCrossOrderWeight.widenedW_le_actualWeight_cross_order`
already supplies step 4 for arbitrary `d,E,D,s,t,mesh`; despite its module
name, its theorem statement is not restricted to the first cell.  Its
`exampleGrow_firstCell_cross_order` specialization and the first-cell rate
producers must not be used for the general moving window.

After the T484 support adapter, the first missing actual stochastic producer
is a general moving-endpoint coordinate moment estimate under `V_P`, uniform
in every active `k` and coordinate.  Its earliest independently bounded leaf
is the moving-window initial `hinit` without a fixed terminal `t0 < 1`.
`eventually_initial_hinit_gauss'` is endpoint-uniform but still assumes
`t_N <= t0` for one fixed `t0`; an arbitrary T483 window may approach one and
does not satisfy that premise.  No conditional `hfamily` or slot closure is
claimed here.

## Exact consumer and quantifier order

The premise of `APrimeAssembly.weightedMoment_of_widened_family` is literally

```text
forall delta, 0 < delta -> delta <= delta0 -> forall p : Nat,
  exists C > 0, eventually N, forall k <= cutNetTop s t mesh N,
    integral [ Wp p delta N k
      * |cutTrunc (N^(2 delta)) (J_N(u_k))|^(2p) ]
      <= C * N^((delta/2) p),
```

where

```text
J = jSnorm(sample exampleGrow,E,D,s),
u_k = cutNetPt s mesh N k,
Wp p = APrimeWeight.widenedW(canonicalR s t mesh,1,J,s,t,mesh,delta,p).
```

The order is

```text
delta, p, C, eventually N, forall active k.
```

The auxiliary high order `P`, source-event failure powers, and all small
source losses may depend on the fixed `delta,p`, but must be selected before
the eventual `N`.  The constant and eventual threshold must be uniform in
`k`, the moving endpoint `u_k`, the output coordinate, and the running time.

`weightedMoment_of_widened_family` then compares the integrands pointwise and
produces `WeightedMoment` for the single order-independent interface weight
`piecewiseW`.  It introduces no extra power of `N`, bandwidth, scale ratio, or
mesh cardinality.

## The three weights

Let `Lambda_N = N^(2 delta)` and let `b` denote the widened prefix cutoff.

- `piecewiseW` uses threshold `exp(1) Lambda_N` and has no moment-order
  parameter.
- `widenedW p` uses threshold `2 exp(1) Lambda_N` and equals `b^(2p)` on the
  active branch.
- `V_P` is the actual smooth weight used by the Gaussian generator at high
  order `P`; its nested cutoff is wider than the literal target cutoff.

The compiled comparisons have the required directions:

```text
piecewiseW <= widenedW p,
widenedW p <= V_P,             p >= 1, P >= 1.
```

The second statement follows by a support split.  Where `widenedW p = 0` it
is trivial.  On positive target support, the actual nested cutoff is exactly
one, so `V_P = 1` at every positive high order `P`.  This is pointwise on the
same Gaussian sample and does not compare two probability spaces.

The correct high-to-low calculation is therefore

```text
E[V_P |Z|^(2P)] <= C_P N^(delta P/2)
  -> same-weight Lyapunov under V_P
E[V_P |Z|^(2p)] <= C'_p N^(delta p/2)
  -> widenedW p <= V_P
E[widenedW p |Z|^(2p)] <= C'_p N^(delta p/2).
```

Here `Z` is the bounded truncated endpoint observable when the final family
moment is taken.  The explicit integrability premises of
`APrimeInit.momNormW_le_momNormW_of_exponent_le` follow from measurability and
boundedness of the smooth weight and `cutTrunc`; they must still be supplied
in the eventual implementation.

## Support, event, and boundary cases

T483 fixes

```text
Good_N = {omega | ||Xmat_N(omega)|| <= N}
```

and packages its measurability, high probability, and all-`N` nonemptiness.
T484's specified conclusion uses that same event and, for `p >= 1`, active
`k >= 1`, and positive widened weight, propagates

```text
jSnorm_N(u,omega) <= (4 exp(1)+2) N^(2 delta),
u in [s_N,u_k].
```

The threshold is exactly `2 exp(1) N^(2 delta)`.  Prefix interpolation gives
`4 exp(1) N^(2 delta)+1`, and `N^(2 delta) >= 1` yields the displayed safe
constant.  There is no `N`, bandwidth, or time-ratio loss.

The fixed structural `Good` and auxiliary source events have distinct jobs.
The modulus and support propagation use the literal T483 `Good`.  Sharp
drift/QV/source estimates may hold on another measurable high-probability
event `Gsrc`; the coordinate moment proof uses sharp bounds on
`Good intersect Gsrc intersect {V_P>0}` and pays the complement inside the
unconditional integral.  It may not replace the `APrimeHypOn` field `Good`
with `Gsrc` silently.  Neither `Good intersect {V_P>0}` nor target support is
assumed high probability.

The boundary branches are compulsory:

- `p = 0`: `widenedW 0 = 1` in both piecewise branches and every zeroth power
  of the observable is one.  Since the base measure is a probability measure,
  `hfamily` holds with `C = 1`, for every `N,k`.  No support or Lyapunov lemma
  is used.
- `p >= 1`: positive widened weight implies positive base cutoff and activates
  the support argument.
- `k = 0`: the prefix is empty and `u_0 = s_N`.  The weight derivative and all
  time integrals vanish.  This branch is discharged by the left-endpoint
  initial moment, not by the T484 `k >= 1` propagation theorem.
- `s_N = u_k`: the same initial branch applies because every time integral is
  zero.
- `s_N = 0`: the cross term has an integrable `r^(-1/2)` majorant; introducing
  `log(1/s_N)` would be invalid.
- A last partial interval after the final net point is a later first-failure
  interpolation issue and is covered by the T483 modulus.  It is not part of
  `hfamily`, whose endpoints are the `u_k` themselves.

T483 already gives a positive-length admissible `Cond272Reg` window and an
explicit member of `Good`.  T484 is required to give or cite a same-event
positive target-support witness for some active `k >= 1`; otherwise its
support implication remains logically usable but fails the repository's
nonvacuity audit.  Such a witness is not a substitute for the unconditional
moment proof.

## Family cardinality and same-measure high order

At `v = u_k`, the needed endpoint comparison is

```text
jSnorm_N(v) <= 1 + max_a |Y_(v,a)|.
```

The compiled endpoint theorem currently present in
`APrimeFirstCellEndpointCoord` is specialized to `E=0`, `D=60`, and `s=0`.
Its general moving analogue is still required; the first-cell specialization
cannot be reused as a general theorem.

At high order `P`, each coordinate must have norm at most

```text
C_P N^(5 delta/32)
```

under the same actual smooth weight `V_P`.  Thus its `2P` moment costs
`N^(5 delta P/16)`.  The coordinate family has at most
`L_N^2 <= N^2` members, and

```text
N^2 N^(5 delta P/16) <= N^(delta P/2)
```

if and only if

```text
32 <= 3 delta P.
```

The remaining exponent is exactly `3 delta P/16 - 2`.  For every fixed
`delta>0` and requested `p>=1`, an integer

```text
P >= p,  P >= 32/(3 delta),  1/P <= delta/8
```

exists and is selected before `N`.  The family reduction is performed at
this `P` under `V_P`; weighted Lyapunov then lowers only the observable order
while keeping `V_P` fixed.  Finally `widenedW p <= V_P` gives the requested
target moment.  No assertion involving `widenedW P <= widenedW p` is used.

## N, bandwidth, and time exponent audit

Write

```text
A_u = W_N ell_u eta_u,
x_u = eta_s/eta_u.
```

After the support cap, the positive drift rows are

```text
N^(4 delta) A_u^(-1) x_u^6,
N^(2 delta+tau) A_u^(-1/2) x_u^(11/4),
N^(3 delta+3 tau/2) A_u^(-1) x_u^(9/2),
```

the current QV rows are

```text
N^(4 delta+2 tau) A_u^(-1/2) x_u^(19/4),
N^(6 delta+3 tau) A_u^(-1) x_u^8,
```

and the early gradient rows have scale exponents

```text
N^(4 delta+2 tau) A_u^(-1/2) x_u^(7/4),
N^(6 delta+3 tau) A_u^(-1) x_u^5.
```

The quadratic and cubic costs are respectively `N^(4 delta)` and
`N^(6 delta)`, as required by the `N^(2 delta)` cap applied to the powers two
and three in paper (5.42).  The band tail `W_N^(-D)` remains in the source
estimates until it is absorbed there; no first-cell bandwidth growth or
first-cell `jG` cap may be imported.

For the concrete choice

```text
delta0 = c/1000,  tau = delta/16,  nu = delta/100,
```

the criterion for a row `N^e A^(-a) x^b` is

```text
(e+nu)/c + b/30 < a.
```

At the largest `delta=delta0`, the independently recomputed consumed and
remaining powers are:

| row | consumed | remaining |
|---|---:|---:|
| quadratic drift | `20401/100000` | `79599/100000` |
| linear far drift | `112487/1200000` | `487513/1200000` |
| three-halves far drift | `122483/800000` | `677517/800000` |
| current quadratic QV | `97481/600000` | `202519/600000` |
| current cubic QV | `327437/1200000` | `872563/1200000` |
| early quadratic gradient | `37481/600000` | `262519/600000` |
| early cubic gradient | `207437/1200000` | `992563/1200000` |

Every margin is positive, and it only improves for smaller `delta`.  The
absorption uses the two separate consequences of `Cond272Reg`,

```text
N^c <= A_t,
x_t^30 <= A_t.
```

It never assumes the rejected product inequality `N^c x_t^30 <= A_t`.
Time integration uses `du/eta_u = dx/(m_E x)` for the displayed rows and the
integrability of `r^(-1/2)` for the prefix-gradient cross term.  The final
coordinate bound has no residual bandwidth or time factor beyond the intended
endpoint normalization.

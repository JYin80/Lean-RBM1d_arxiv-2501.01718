# T822 — two successive unused images on an actual prefix cell

## Result

Added `RBM1D/Gauss/PermutationTwoNextImagesCell.lean`, imported it once from `RBM1D.lean`, and recorded the auxiliary statement boundary in `docs/paper-deltas.md` as `T822a`.

The module proves the exact frozen proposition from T821:

```lean
uniformPerm W {ρ | ρ ∈ prefixCell W k.val π ∧ ρ k = v ∧ ρ j = w} /
  uniformPerm W {ρ | ρ ∈ prefixCell W k.val π} =
    (1 : ENNReal) /
      (((W - k.val : ℕ) : ENNReal) *
        ((W - k.val - 1 : ℕ) : ENNReal))
```

Its assumptions are exactly `j.val = k.val + 1`, `v,w ∈ unusedImages W k π`, and `v ≠ w`.

## Proof route

The proof uses only the one-image actual-cell quotient and positive unused-image fiber from `PermutationNextImageUniformCell.lean` (T795), which imports the existing T776 quotient and T785 unused-image characterization. Positivity of actual prefix-cell mass comes from T776/T711. T738's factorial cell count was inspected as an independent counting route; it is not needed by the proof.

First, the positive fiber for `v` supplies an actual continuation `π₁` agreeing with `π` below `k` and satisfying `π₁ k = v`. Extensionality identifies `prefixCell W j.val π₁` with the first-image event inside the original cell. Since `w` was unused before `k` and differs from `v`, it remains unused before `j` under `π₁`. Applying T795 at `(k,π,v)` and `(j,π₁,w)` gives the two one-image quotients. Their product cancels the positive, finite intermediate cell mass; `j=k+1` rewrites the second denominator to `W-k-1`.

No conditional expectation, conditional distribution, or theorem 2.3 local-law assertion is used.

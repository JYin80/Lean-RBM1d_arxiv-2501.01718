# T614 — cutoff-aware all-charge length-two drift preflight

## Verdict

### Independent-audit correction (supersedes inconsistent details below)

The structural verdict and the four-field localizer remain valid, but the independent audit
found six dispatch-critical corrections. They govern the implementation even where an older
sentence below says otherwise.

1. `APrimeSmoothPrefix` is not an admissible import: its transitive closure reaches
   `APrimeModel` and `Flow.Step345Producer`. The regularized soft maximum and its
   differentiability facts must be defined locally from lower-level `Step2Bootstrap`, with
   `APrimeGronwall` supplying `WeightC1`.
2. The support localizer itself may use the norm event for the time modulus, but the later
   quadratic-drift lemma must work on the intersection of that event with the pre-Step-3
   Lemma-5.9 decay event that yields
   `Decay.LoopDecay ... (lkPath ...)`. The norm event alone cannot discharge the `hDd`
   hypothesis of `norm_primBil_lkPath_le`.
3. The moment order `P` is fixed before eventual `N`; the smooth-max order `r` is chosen
   after `N` (or supplied as `r N`). A single fixed `r` cannot calibrate the growing family
   size `k * 4 * L_N^2`.
4. The localizer covers `v=s_N` separately and the positive target-net endpoints `w_k`.
   It does not cover `t_N` unless `t_N` is itself the terminal net point; the terminal
   remainder requires a later modulus step or an added terminal cell.
5. Every stored coordinate is evaluated at `((sqrt u_j : ℂ) • M)`. Hence at
   `M=Xmat d N omega` it is the same-sample flow coordinate at `u_j`; omitting this scaling
   would give the wrong random field.
6. `R_(u,v)^2` is the retained upper-bound factor supplied by the kernel estimate, not an
   equality for the propagator norm.

T596's raw Duhamel right-hand side cannot be repaired after the fact.  Its proof uses
`cutTrunc_le_self` at the terminal time `v`; this leaves no information about
`xiLK E N u omega 2` at the integration times `u < v`.  In particular, the terminal statement

```text
cutTrunc Theta_N (xiLK_2(v)) != 0
```

does not imply an upper bound for `xiLK_2(u)`.  Consequently it cannot linearize the raw
self-square

```text
A_u^(-3) * eta_u^(-1) * xiLK_2(u)^2.
```

The first noncircular route is a **fixed smooth prefix weight on the same Gaussian sample**.
For a net cell with right index `k`, form one regularized smooth maximum over every earlier
pair

```text
(j,q),  j < k,  q : LoopData (L_N) 2,
```

where the coordinate is the literal normalized all-charge value

```text
A_(u_j)^2 * |(L-K)_(u_j,q)|.
```

Let `kappa_k` be a smooth cutoff of that maximum and use
`w_k = kappa_k^(2*P)` in the Gaussian moment identity.  This preserves the prefix support
inside the drift norm.  It necessarily also produces the already formalized Stein cross term
containing `D w_k`; that term must remain explicit.  On the support of `w_k`, and separately
on the support of `D w_k`, the mesh modulus gives

```text
xiLK_2(u) <= (4*exp(1)+2) * Theta_N,
Theta_N = N^(2*delta) * flowAs(E,s,N)^(1/2),
```

throughout the cell.  Only then may the square be replaced by this cutoff level times one
copy of `xiLK_2(u)`.

This is a genuine replacement for the raw T596 drift row, not a postprocessing of it.  The
smallest next implementation theorem is the all-charge smooth-prefix localizer in §8.  It
must provide `WeightC1`, plateau, ordinary support, and derivative support in one actual
Gaussian theorem.  A pointwise inequality that merely assumes an abstract favorable weight
would not break the obstruction.

## 2. Why the two tempting localizations are invalid

### Terminal truncation is too late

T596 proves, for positive net points, a bound by the unweighted coordinate Duhamel family.
The monotonicity step is pointwise only at `v`:

```text
cutTrunc Theta_N (Y_q(v)) <= Y_q(v).
```

The drift integral subsequently contains values at every `u in [s_N,v]`.  Without a prefix
modulus or stopping weight there is no implication from a small terminal value to a small
intermediate value.  Thus a proof that applies `cutTrunc_le_self`, estimates the raw Duhamel
RHS, and truncates again at the end is circular at the self-square.

### A hard prefix indicator is not a free multiplier

The Gaussian flow is represented by one static base sample `omega`; values at all times are
functions of that same sample.  The Gaussian moment identity is proved by Stein integration
by parts in `omega`.  Multiplying it by a sample-dependent prefix indicator is not neutral:
the indicator is not differentiable, while a differentiable weight contributes

```text
(2*sqrt(u))^(-1) * sum_r gvar(r) * E[(D_r w) * (D_r Psi_u)].
```

This is exactly `RBM.Gauss.hasDerivAt_integral_weighted`.  Therefore a correct localization
must use `Gauss.WeightC1` and retain the cross term.  Treating a hard event as if it were a
constant in the unweighted T596 identity is invalid.

## 3. The actual all-charge smooth prefix

For `q : LoopData (d.L N) 2`, define the matrix coordinate

```text
twoCoordFun(d,E,N,u,q,M)
  = scale(E,N,u)^2
      * (loopObs(d,N,zt(E,u),q.idx,M) - Kval(E,N,u,q.idx)).
```

At the common base sample,

```text
|twoCoordFun(..., ((sqrt u : C) • Xmat(d,N,omega)))|
  = twoXiCoord(d,E,N,u,omega,q).
```

This uses every charge word, not `Step2.sigPM`.  The stored random field is literally
`twoCoordFun(...,u_j,q,((sqrt u_j : C) • M))`; at `M=Xmat(d,N,omega)` it is therefore the
same-sample flow coordinate at `u_j`.  Its `C^1` proof for `u_j<1` must be rebuilt locally
from lower-level resolvent facts; no downstream smooth-prefix module may be imported.

For a positive cell index `k`, use the single finite type

```text
Fin k x LoopData (d.L N) 2.
```

Regularize each modulus by `sqrt(|F|^2 + eps_N^2)` and apply one `2*r` smooth maximum, with
`r` selected after `N` (or supplied as `r N`).  A
single maximum, rather than nested time and label maxima, gives the clean calibration

```text
(k * 4*L_N^2)^(1/(2*r)) <= exp(1).
```

Put

```text
Theta_N = twoCutLevel d E s delta N
        = N^(2*delta) * flowAs (band d) E s N^(1/2),

prefix_N,k = smoothMax_{j<k,q}
  sqrt(|twoCoordFun(u_j,q)|^2 + eps_N^2),

kappa_N,k = cutChi(prefix_N,k / (2*exp(1)*Theta_N)),
w_N,k,P   = kappa_N,k^(2*P).
```

Choose the already standard positive polynomially small regularizer `eps_N` and prove
eventually `eps_N <= Theta_N`.  Then:

1. if every earlier hard coordinate is at most `Theta_N`, the regularized coordinate is at
   most `2*Theta_N`, the smooth maximum is at most `2*exp(1)*Theta_N`, and `kappa_N,k = 1`;
2. if `kappa_N,k != 0`, every earlier coordinate is at most
   `4*exp(1)*Theta_N`;
3. the same bound holds when a coordinate derivative of `w_N,k,P` is nonzero, because the
   cutoff derivative vanishes outside its transition support;
4. on the actual norm event, the `Kmod=21`, `gamma=1/2` modulus and the
   `meshK 21 (1/2)` fineness add at most `Theta_N` between a preceding net point and a running
   time in its next cell.

Thus both relevant supports give the explicit running cap

```text
xiLK E N u omega 2
  <= (4*exp(1)+1) * Theta_N
  <= (4*exp(1)+2) * Theta_N.
```

The terminal truncation level remains **exactly** `Theta_N`.  The constants `2*exp(1)` and
`4*exp(1)+2` belong only to the internal smooth prefix.  They must not be folded into the
definition of `twoCutLevel`.

## 4. Exact localized quadratic ledger

Write

```text
J_u = (sample d).xiLK E N u omega 2,
A_u = (band d).scale E N u = W_N * ell_u * eta_u,
R_(u,v) = (1-u)/(1-v),
Cwt = 4*exp(1)+2.
```

`RBM.norm_primBil_lkPath_le` is arbitrary in
`sigma : Fin 2 -> Bool` and `a : LoopArg (L_N) 2`; in particular it includes the independent
`(+,+)` word.  Its exact main and leakage powers are

```text
|primBil_u(sigma,a)|
  <= 8*exp(1)*(Krad+2) * J_u^2 * W_N*ell_u*A_u^(-4)
     + 4*W_N*L_N*delta_decay * J_u

   = 8*exp(1)*(Krad+2) * J_u^2 * eta_u^(-1)*A_u^(-3)
     + 4*W_N*L_N*delta_decay * J_u.
```

No `N`, `W`, `A`, `eta`, or time-ratio factor is suppressed in this identity.  On either
active support from §3,

```text
kappa_k * |primBil_u(sigma,a)|
  <= 8*exp(1)*(Krad+2)*Cwt*Theta_N
       * eta_u^(-1)*A_u^(-3) * (kappa_k*J_u)
     + 4*W_N*L_N*delta_decay * (kappa_k*J_u).
```

The length-two kernel estimate retains the upper-bound factor `R_(u,v)^2`; this is not an
equality for the propagator norm.  Including the drift coefficient `2`
and T596's endpoint normalization `A_v^2`, the localized quadratic row is therefore

```text
16*exp(1)*(Krad+2)*Cwt*Theta_N
  * integral_[s_N,v] A_v^2*R_(u,v)^2*eta_u^(-1)*A_u^(-3)
      * ||kappa_k*J_u||_(2P) du

+ 8*W_N*L_N*delta_decay
  * integral_[s_N,v] A_v^2*R_(u,v)^2
      * ||kappa_k*J_u||_(2P) du.
```

Equivalently these are weighted norms with weight `w_k=kappa_k^(2P)`.  This is the first
noncircular linear row.  It is not yet an absorption theorem: the time integral, the other
drift summands, and the weight-gradient cross term still need quantitative bounds.

## 5. Charges, family size, and quantifier order

The prefix and the Duhamel observable must both quantify over

```text
q : LoopData (d.L N) 2
```

without reducing to `Step2.sigPM`.  The charge family has four words, the label family has
`L_N^2` elements, and T596 proves the exact identity

```text
Fintype.card (LoopData (d.L N) 2) = 4 * (d.L N)^2.
```

Thus `(+,+)` is present independently; it is not obtained from `(+,-)` by cyclicity or
conjugation.

There are two different high orders and they must not be conflated:

- `r=r_N` is the smooth-maximum order, chosen logarithmically so that the prefix family
  factor is at most `exp(1)`;
- `P` is the moment order, chosen after the requested `epsilon,p`, with
  `P >= p` and `P >= 4/epsilon`, so that descending by Lyapunov turns the final exact
  `4*L_N^2` cost into `(4*L_N^2)^(p/P)`.

The target order remains

```text
delta0;
delta, 0 < delta <= delta0;
epsilon > 0;
p : Nat;
choose P;
choose C;
eventually N;
choose r=r_N;
every net point (equivalently every valid cell index k).
```

`P` is not fixed before `epsilon,p`.  The norm event `Good_X={‖X‖≤N}` is independent of that
choice, while the Lemma-5.9 decay event and its tail exponent are selected after `P`.  The
case `p=0` is split first and uses T596's exact zero-moment identity.

## 6. Same sample, events, complements, and boundaries

All times use the same `omega : Gauss.Omega d` and the same probability measure
`(band d).P`.  The weight is a function of the same base matrix `Xmat d N omega`; it does not
introduce an independent sample or a conditional law.

Use the actual event

```text
Good_X(N) = {omega | ‖Xmat d N omega‖ <= N}.
```

It is measurable and high probability.  On `Good_X(N)`, `Gauss.hHol_flow`, the endpoint
floor from `Cond272Reg`, and the length-two deterministic `Kval` envelope give the all-charge
`Kmod=21`, `gamma=1/2` modulus recorded by T589.  The finite maximum transfer from individual
`q` to `xiLK` costs no family factor.

For the later quadratic-drift lemma, enlarge this to the intersection with the pre-Step-3
Lemma-5.9 event that supplies
`Decay.LoopDecay ... (lkPath ... u omega)` at every running time.  The norm event alone does
not imply the `hDd` hypothesis of `norm_primBil_lkPath_le`.  This decay input must be traced
to the Step-2 `LKDecayQuant.FlowInputs`, never to a Step-3 `xiLK` conclusion.

The indicator of this good intersection must **not** be inserted into Stein's identity.
After obtaining
the weighted identity on the whole Gaussian space, split each nonnegative drift integral
with `G(N)=Good_X(N) ∩ Good_decay(N)`.  The good part uses both the prefix-support
linearization and the `lkPath` decay clause.  The complement keeps the weight, pays both
the norm-event and decay-event complement tails using deterministic polynomial envelopes,
and chooses their tail exponents after `P`.  There is no change of measure or hidden
conditioning.

The prefix complement is different from `Good_X(N)^c`.  It is handled by the usual narrow /
widened-weight one-step induction (first-exit bookkeeping); it is not declared negligible by
fiat.  The derivative support of the widened weight must receive the same running cap as the
ordinary support.

Boundary plan:

- `v=s_N`: no time integral; use the transported initial branch, not the localized drift;
- `s_N<v`: index the point by a positive cell `k` and use the fixed prefix weight for that
  cell;
- `s_N=0`: apply the generator identity only for `u>0`; its cross coefficient is
  `u^(-1/2)`, which is integrable at zero.  The prefix coordinate at time zero is harmless
  because the pullback by `sqrt(0)` is constant and its Gaussian derivative is zero;
- every positive target-net endpoint `w_k` is covered through `netFinset_subset_Icc` and the
  closed-interval modulus; `t_N` is covered only if it equals the terminal net point, and
  otherwise its final mesh remainder needs a later modulus step or an explicit terminal cell;
- `p=0` is independent of all positive-order weights.

## 7. Acyclicity and satisfiability

The permitted dependency chain is:

```text
actual loop coordinates, scale, and Kval
  + locally constructed regularized smooth maximum
  + actual Gaussian norm event and hHol_flow modulus
        |
        v
all-charge prefix WeightC1 + ordinary/derivative support
        |
        +--> weighted Gaussian generator identity
        |       (drift + QV + explicit cross term)
        v
support-localized norm_primBil_lkPath_le
        |
        v
linear weighted drift row
        |
        v
future one-step estimates and high-moment 4*L_N^2 aggregation
```

There is no arrow from (2.77), `CutHypEvOnSlot`, `hfamily`, `APrimeSlot'`, `jSnorm`, the
target all-charge estimate, or a first-cell producer.  The current `APrimeSmoothWeightActual`
is useful only as a proof pattern: its actual model definitions hard-code
`Step2.sigPM`/`jSnorm` and are not producers for this ticket.

The localizer's domain is jointly nonempty.  One may take `d=Dims.exampleGrow`, `E=0`,
`s_N=0`, `t_N=1/2`, a positive first cell of `meshK 21 (1/2)`, `P=1`, and then choose `r`
large enough for the displayed finite-cardinality calibration (eventually one uses the
standard logarithmic `r_N`).  The zero base sample lies in `Good_X(N)`.  At the initial net point every
actual two-loop error is zero, so the positive regularizer gives a defined smooth maximum and
the prefix cutoff equals one eventually.  Hence neither the weight, the good set, nor the
time cell is empty.  This witness is only for the localization geometry; it is not used as a
zero-functional proof of the desired moment estimate.

For the later quadratic-drift intersection, nonemptiness must instead be obtained from the
two actual high-probability producers (the norm event and the Lemma-5.9 `lkPath` decay event),
choosing their complement bounds with sum strictly below one.  Separate witnesses for the
two events are not enough, and the zero sample is not asserted to satisfy the full-window
decay clause.

```lean

        1 ≤ k →
        k ≤ CutHypTheta.cutNetTop s t (meshK 21 (1 / 2)) N →
```

The important point is the content of the conjunction: `WeightC1` alone is insufficient,
ordinary support alone is insufficient, and a
support theorem that omits `twoPrefixWeightD` leaves the Stein cross term uncontrolled.
Inside the prefix maximum, the matrix argument must be
`((sqrt u_j : ℂ) • M)` for each stored time `u_j`; this is what makes evaluation at
`M=Xmat d N omega` the literal same-sample flow field.

Once this theorem exists, the next drift lemma is short: combine its running cap with
`RBM.norm_primBil_lkPath_le`, split
`G=(Good_X ∩ Good_decay)/G^c` after the weighted identity while paying both complement tails,
and obtain
the exact linear row of §4 for every `q`.  That later lemma may use
`APrimeDuhamelModel.momFlowDeriv_le`; it must not return to T596's unweighted
`twoCoordDuhamelRhs`.

## 10. Strongest safe non-claim

This ticket does **not** prove the all-charge cutoff moment, the localized drift absorption,
the QV row, the Stein cross-term budget, or `CutHypEvOnSlot`.  It also does not claim that
T596's raw Duhamel family can be reused unchanged.  What is established by the audit is the
first logically valid interface: a smooth all-charge prefix weight, on the same Gaussian
sample, whose ordinary and derivative supports retain the exact cutoff scale before the
self-square is estimated.  Until `eventually_twoPrefix_localizer` (or an equivalent theorem
with all four fields) is compiled, the arbitrary-charge drift obstruction remains open.

# T1043 — general-moving cross-observable sample regularity

## Verdict

**Mathematical preflight PASS; exact `hYm` and `hYi` fields proved.** For each
fixed `p ≥ 1`, eventually in `N`, every active target-net index `k` (including
`k = 0`), every `σ` and output `a`, and every time `r ∈ [s_N,v_k]`, the literal
observable

```text
Y(ω) = cutoff(d,E,D,deltaWeight,s,targetMesh(D),N,k,canonicalM,ω)
       * ‖coordAt(d,E,D,N,σ,a,s_N,v_k,r,Hflow(d,N,r,ω))‖
```

is `AEStronglyMeasurable` under the full Gaussian probability measure and
`|Y|^(2p)` is integrable. The proof in fact works at every `N > 0` satisfying
the stated moving-window hypotheses. Its finite-size bound can depend on the
size, endpoint, charge and output; no bound uniform in `N` is claimed.

The target is exactly the two unconditioned fields in
`APrimeCrossJointSplit.crossPart_active_le_jointEvent`:

```lean
(hYm : AEStronglyMeasurable Y (Gauss.P d))
(hYi : Integrable (fun ω => |Y ω| ^ (2 * p)) (Gauss.P d))
```

The consumer's separate favorable-event hypotheses do not enter either
field. Its `hBgood` and `hQgood` are restricted to `Good ∩ transition`, while
`hAll` and `hP` are separate inputs to the later cross estimate. This result
proves no event intersection, `hProdInt`, cross small-slot bound, N1, or A′
claim.

## Dependency and obligation audit

1. **Closed cell and active prefix — passed.** From `hst`, target-mesh
   positivity, and `k ≤ cutNetTop`, the endpoint `v_k` lies in `[s_N,t_N]`;
   `t_N < 1` gives `v_k < 1`. Every stored prefix time is also below one.
   `k = 0` is included: its stored-time condition is vacuous and its time cell
   may be degenerate. The conclusion quantifies over the entire closed cell,
   so both endpoints are included.
2. **Actual cutoff — passed.**
   `APrimeSmoothWeightActual.continuous_cutoff` gives continuity for the
   canonical order. `Cutoff.cutChi_nonneg` and `Cutoff.cutChi_le_one` give the
   pointwise bounds `0 ≤ cutoff ≤ 1` for this literal cutoff.
3. **Coordinate continuity — passed.** The proof follows the first-cell T462
   route in `APrimeCrossJointSplit.contDiff_coordAt`: on `[s_N,v_k]`,
   `Gauss.exists_bdd_Kval_Kprim` bounds the primitive data; then
   `Gauss.bddC2C_ukerObsT` gives `ContDiff ℝ 1` for `coordAt`. Composition with
   the continuous `Hflow`, norm, and cutoff makes `Y` continuous, hence
   strongly and almost-everywhere strongly measurable.
4. **Finite-size raw bound and integrability — passed.** The same
   `exists_bdd_Kval_Kprim` output feeds
   `Gauss.exists_bdd₀_ukerObsT`, whose bound is uniform in the matrix and over
   all `r ∈ [s_N,v_k]`. The positive `driftScale` divides this into a finite
   bound for the normalized coordinate. Since `0 ≤ cutoff ≤ 1`, `|Y|` is
   pointwise bounded. The Gaussian law `Gauss.P d` is a probability measure,
   so the measurable bounded `2p`-power is integrable.
5. **Quantifiers and events — passed.** The theorem fixes `p` before taking
   `∀ᶠ N`; then it quantifies over all active `k`, all `σ`, all outputs `a`,
   and every closed-cell time. No transition membership, common event, or
   favorable-event assumption is hidden in `hYm` or `hYi`.
6. **T995 nondegeneracy — passed with exact scope.** The module exports an
   alias of the accepted T995 `scheduled_positive_cell_witness`. It supplies
   a genuine `E = 0`, `D = 60` moving-window model and, eventually, a sample
   on a positive active cell with strictly positive actual smooth weight.
   This witnesses nonempty cutoff support; it is not used as a premise in the
   regularity proof and does not assert that `Y` is nonzero at that sample.

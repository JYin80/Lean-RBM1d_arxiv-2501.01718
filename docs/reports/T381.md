# T381 — first-cell projected `ψ` moment source, mathematical preflight

## Verdict

**The isolated projected initial datum is exactly zero on the first cell.** Its pointwise envelope `hEnvI` and all-order `hMψI` have a direct, uniform proof; the stochastic-domination-to-moment reverse bridge and a random polynomial envelope are unnecessary for this one datum. T374's designation of `hMψI` as the first missing *Gaussian initial-data* leaf was too pessimistic.

**The weighted `'''` producer is still open.** It uses one `ψ(N,u,q,ω)` for the initial datum **and** projected drift, commutator, and `ϑ̇` terms. The repository does not define a concrete common `ψ` with all four pointwise envelopes and all-time moments. The first interface issue is to show that a common `ψ` can be chosen with a harmless value at `u=0`; this requires the drift's actual time-zero identity (and then the later-time projected moment bound), not another proof of `Bounds_zero`.

This report audits the first cell `s_N=0`, `m=n+2≥2`, `|E|<2`, (5.92), and the weighted Q signature `rhs514QAt_of_kernel_inputs'''` (`Gauss/Lemma514QAssembly.lean:1738–1880`). It is read-only except for this report. No Lean, shared/root, or Git file was changed.

## Literal datum and exact source chain

Let `L_N=B.L N`, `A_0=B.scale E N 0`, and for `q=(σ,a):LoopData L_N m` put

`T^I_{N,q,ω}(b) := SumZeroDyn.lkT X E N 0 ω σ b`,
`ψ^I(N,q,ω) := A_0^m · max_b ‖Qop L_N (0:ℂ) T^I_{N,q,ω}(b)‖`.

This is the literal normalized projected initial tensor; it is *not* an abstract moment variable. The stronger existing chain is:

1. `RBM.Sample.Lval_zero_eq_Kval` and `Sample.lkErr_zero` (`Flow/Iteration.lean:130–145`) give `X.lkErr E N 0 ω I=0` for every well-formed loop `I` of positive length, **all** `N,ω`.
2. `RBM.SumZeroDyn.norm_lkT` (`Hierarchy/SumZeroDyn.lean:1443`) identifies `‖T^I(b)‖` with that error. Each entry is therefore zero; function extensionality gives `T^I=0` uniformly in `σ,b,ω`.
3. `RBM.FastDecayFlow.Qop_zero` (`Gauss/FastDecayFlow.lean:187`) sends the zero tensor to zero. Hence `ψ^I=0` for all `N,q,ω`. `Bounds_zero` (`Flow/Iteration.lean:165`) packages the same pointwise identity as the initial `BoundsCore.LmK` stochastic field; its `≺` conclusion is weaker than the equality used here.

Thus `hEnvI` has left side zero. For any nonnegative `ζ_N` and any nonnegative proposed common `ψ(N,0,q,ω)`, the right side `A_0^{-m} ψ(N,0,q,ω)+ζ_N` is nonnegative. Positivity of `A_0` follows from `B.scale_pos'` (`Flow/Hypotheses.lean:207`) at `u=0`. No `η_s/η_v` factor appears: the producer normalizes at the **initial** `A_0` in `hEnvI`, while the later `hnum` compares to `A_v^{-m}` and T354 pays that scale ratio.

If the common `ψ` can be arranged so that `ψ(N,0,q,ω)=0`, then its `hMψI` is exactly `momNorm B.P (2p) 0=0` for every `p≥1`. The required order `∀ε>0 ∀p≥1 ∃C>0 ∀ᶠN ∀q` is met with `C=1`, indeed without waiting for large `N`, whenever `Phi_N(q)≥0`. Measurability, every power's integrability, and a deterministic all-sample polynomial envelope at this *time* are immediate for the zero observable: one may take `Env_N=1`, exponent `Kenv=0`. `RBM.Gauss.hinit_of_stochDom` (`Gauss/MomentDuhamelRhs.lean:540`) is therefore unnecessary here. Its positive lower-control hypothesis would be met by `A_0^{-m}>0`, but using it would conceal the exact zero.

## The shared-envelope obstruction and first missing declaration

The Q `'''` signature has `hEnvI` at `:1750`, `hEnvF/C/D` at `:1754–1776`, and only one `hMψI` at `:1860`. It also asks `hψint` at *all* times, not just at zero. Taking `ψ=ψ^I≡0` for the entire window would therefore force the drift/commutator/`ϑ̇` envelopes to be paid only by `ζ_N`; no existing theorem proves those pointwise claims. Conversely, taking a time-independent `ψ` large enough for the interior terms may make `ψ(0)` nonzero, so the exact initial-tensor identity alone would no longer discharge `hMψI` for that choice. This is a quantifier/interface issue, not a counterexample to the initial estimate.

The commutator and `ϑ̇` terms are linear in `lkT`; their time-zero values vanish after the exact `lkT_0=0` identity. For the drift, `RBM.DriftDef.Fpath_eq_driftF_of_lt_one` (`Hierarchy/DriftDef.lean:335`) identifies the actual `H.Fpath` on `[0,v_N]`. The definition of `driftF` (`:205`) has three pieces: the `eGterm`, a coupling with `gloop-K`, and `primBil(gloop-K,gloop-K)`. At `u=0`, `X.H_zero`, `Gsig_zero_zt_zero_sub` (`Flow/Initial.lean:112`), and `gloop_zero_zt_zero_eq_Kgen` (`:215`) make these pieces zero on the relevant well-formed loops. This strongly indicates `H.Fpath N 0 ω σ a=0` and hence `Q_0 F_0=0`, but **no named theorem with that complete signature was found**. In particular `driftF_envelope_critical_zero` (`Gauss/DriftEnvelope.lean:511`) proves only a finite upper envelope at the sample `ω=0`; it does not assert vanishing for every `ω`.

The smallest corrected route is therefore: first prove the actual `Fpath_zero` identity on first-cell data, preserving the well-formedness and positive-length side conditions in the coupling sums; then define a single nonnegative `ψ` from the normalized maximum of the four required Q tensors (or an equivalent explicit sum). All four are zero at `u=0`, so that shared `ψ(0)=0`, closing `hEnvI` and the full all-`p`, all-`q` `hMψI` immediately. Its measurability, all-order integrability, and polynomial all-sample envelope for **interior** `u>0`, plus `hMψ` with `ρ_u(2n+3)Φ`, remain separate substantive obligations. The present audit does not promote the indicated time-zero drift calculation into a compiled theorem and does not claim those interior obligations.

## Powers, witness, and circularity

At `u=0`, `η_0=(mE E).im>0` and `A_0=W_N·ellHat(L_N,0)·η_0>0`; for `E=0`, `η_0=1`. The initial projected tensor has scale **exactly zero** and needs no `Φ` factor. For the weighted numeric interface one may keep a positive deterministic moment budget, e.g. `Phi_N(q)=C_m(1+Φ_N)` with `C_m≥1`, `Φ_N≥0`, and `Λ_N≥1`; zero is below it for every `ε,p,N,q`. This does not alter the drift's necessary `(2n+3)Φ` dependence (`SumZeroDyn.xiRhs_stochDom`, `Hierarchy/SumZeroDyn.lean:3012`). No assertion `Φ≺1` is made.

The first cell is genuinely forward: `firstCellS τ′ N=0` (`Gauss/DetFlucThreshold.lean:454`) and `first_cell_window_nondegenerate` (`Gauss/Eq45FlowGrid.lean:24`) gives `0<firstCellT τ′ N` eventually for `τ′>0`; T354's `positive_first_cell_numeric_witness` (`Gauss/Lemma514HnumBudget.lean:1074`) uses `[0,1/2]` and strictly positive T253 error budget. The initial datum's vanishing is the paper's exact (2.67), not an empty-event or zero-time-interval trick. Taking `ζ_N>0`, `Phi_N(q)>0`, and the actual growing `Dims.exampleGrow` gives a nondegenerate *control/window* witness even though the true initial observable is zero. The `hMψI` argument consumes neither current-cell Lemma 5.14 nor A′; later cells need the previous cell's BoundsCore input and cannot reuse this zero identity.

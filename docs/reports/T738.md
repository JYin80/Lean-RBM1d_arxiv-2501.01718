## Result and proof

For every `W k : ℕ` and actual `π : PermΩ W`, `prefixCell_card_eq_factorial` proves

`(prefixCell W k π).card = (W - min k W).factorial`.

The explicit equivalence `prefixCellEquivFree` maps an arbitrary permutation `τ` of the unrevealed positions `{i : Fin W // k ≤ i.val}` to `π * Equiv.Perm.ofSubtype τ`. The extension fixes each revealed position, so its image agrees with `π` there. Conversely, if `ρ` belongs to the cell, `π⁻¹ * ρ` fixes each revealed position. Mathlib's `Equiv.Perm.mem_range_ofSubtype_iff` places it in the range of the extension map, giving surjectivity. Cancellation and `ofSubtype_injective` give injectivity. `prefixFree_card` counts the complement of the revealed `min k W` positions. `Fintype.card_perm` then yields the factorial formula.

Boundary theorems state `W=0` gives cardinality 1 (`0!`), `k=0` gives `W!`, and `W≤k` gives 1. At `W=2`, the `k=0` cell has cardinality 2 and the `k=1` cell has cardinality 1. These are nondegenerate finite examples; T711 already supplies distinct identity and swap permutations at width 2.

The proof uses the actual `π` as the realizability witness. It makes no assertion for an arbitrary prescribed prefix, and introduces no hypotheses or probabilistic conclusion. This finite combinatorial lemma is only a downstream component relevant to the paper's (2.3)–(2.4) route; it does not assert those formulas.

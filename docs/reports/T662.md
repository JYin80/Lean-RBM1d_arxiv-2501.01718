# T662 — independent audit of T656's conditional arbitrary-charge QV core

## Verdict

**PASS WITH CORRECTIONS.**

The exact conditional Lean card in T656 is type-correct once a profile-preserving
`MomNormDom` is assumed.  Independent scratch proofs compile for:

1. the same-sample conditional `hEE` composition;
2. the exact `eeFun` stochastic-domination profile
   `A_u⁻⁴ η_u⁻¹ flowR⁵`;
3. the post-`MomNormDom` pointwise kernel estimate with the fourth power
   `((1-u)/(1-v))⁴`, moment order lowered from `2P` to `P`, all two-loop charges, and output
   label `Fin.append q.2 q.2`;
4. the separate `v=s_N` zero-length integral branch.

T656's main warning is correct for the **stripped card it actually states**: `t_N<1` alone
does not give the polynomial absolute or relative envelope required by
`Gauss.momNormDom_of_stochDom`, and stochastic domination alone cannot be upgraded to fixed
moments.

Two corrections are required.

* The actual conditional `hEE` composition used in the scratch proof takes
  `hc : Cond272 (band d) E s t`.  If this already-available paper hypothesis is retained in
  the consumer card, it gives a polynomial endpoint floor; the repository already has the
  exact bound
  `Gauss.eventually_rpow_neg_two_le_etaT_window`.  Thus the endpoint issue is real for
  T656's **erased-`hc` card**, but it is not a new analytic theorem gap for the producer bundle
  that still carries `Cond272`.  T656's statement that the only repairs are a new endpoint
  assumption or a new relative-envelope theorem is too strong: retaining `Cond272` is a third,
  and presently cheapest, repair.
* T656's clean-import table is false under the ticket's literal prohibition on Step 3.
  `Hierarchy.LKDecayQuant`, `Gauss.XiLow`, and `Gauss.MomentDuhamelRhs` all reach
  `Hierarchy.Step3` through `Hierarchy.SumZeroDyn`.  The scratch results remain valid
  conditional implications, but they are not an upstream-clean implementation surface.

Accordingly, T656 may be accepted only with those corrections.  No positive QV
implementation ticket is justified from T649/T654 alone.

## Literal audit of `momNormDom_of_stochDom`

`Gauss.momNormDom_of_stochDom` is the composition of
`Gauss.momentDom_of_stochDom` and `momNormDom_of_momentDom`.  Its hypotheses literally include:

```text
measurability of every indexed variable;
integrability of every even moment;
strict positivity of Phi;
an eventual polynomial lower bound N^(-Blow) <= Phi;
a deterministic all-sample envelope |Y| <= Env_N;
an eventual polynomial bound Env_N <= N^Kenv;
StochDom |Y| Phi.
```

The proof uses the polynomial envelope on the exceptional event.  With
`D' = 2p(Kenv+Blow)+1`, it pays

```text
P(exceptional event) * Env_N^(2p).
```

There is no route in this theorem from stochastic domination alone to the missing moments.
This is essential: super-polynomial spikes on super-polynomially small events can satisfy all
fixed polynomial tail tests while having super-polynomial fixed moments.

For two charges, `Gauss.norm_eeFun_herm_le` gives the deterministic bound

```text
‖eeFun‖ <= 2 * L_N * W_N^(-4) * eta_u^(-6).
```

The desired control is

```text
Phi(N,u) = A_u^(-4) * eta_u^(-1) * R^5
         = W_N^(-4) * ell_u^(-4) * eta_u^(-5) * R^5.
```

Hence the crude relative envelope is

```text
Env/Phi <= 2 * L_N * ell_u^4 * eta_u^(-1) * R^(-5).
```

The five powers already present in `flowR^5` cancel the five endpoint powers of `ell` in the
extreme saturated regime, but they do **not** cancel the final `eta_u^(-1)`.

For the window

```text
s_N = 0,
t_N = 1 - exp(-N^2),
```

one has

```text
eta_{t_N} = exp(-N^2) * Im(m_E).
```

Eventually `ell_{t_N}=L_N`, `ell_{s_N}=1`, and `R=L_N`; at `u=t_N` the displayed relative
envelope reduces to `2*eta_{t_N}^(-1)`, which is larger than every fixed power of `N`.
The absolute Hermitian envelope is likewise super-polynomial.  Thus the stripped assumptions
`0<=s_N<=t_N<1` do not discharge either an absolute or a relative polynomial envelope.

This example does not satisfy `Cond272`; that is precisely the correction to T656's
interpretation.  The same-sample `hEE` composition already assumes `Cond272`, and retaining it
gives the existing exact mathematical implication

```lean
Gauss.eventually_rpow_neg_two_le_etaT_window B hE hs0 hst ht1 hc :
  eventually N, forall u : TimeIcc s t N, N^(-2) <= etaT E u
```

equivalently `eta_u⁻¹ <= N²`.  Combined with `L_N<=N` eventually and `W_N>=1`, the Hermitian
bound is eventually at most `2*N^13`, hence at most `N^14` after enlarging the eventual
threshold.  The same `Cond272` flow facts also give the needed polynomial lower bound for
`Phi` (one may take order `N^-4`).  Therefore the moment bridge obstruction disappears if
`hc` is retained.

The existing declaration is not currently an admissible upstream import:
`Gauss.Lemma514XiPoly` has a 124-module closure reaching Step 3, Step 45, and the dirty A-prime
path.  Its short endpoint-floor argument would need a clean extraction or local reproving in
the eventual implementation core.

## Formula (5.42)

Formula (5.42) was checked directly in `paper/250520-YinJun-v2.pdf`, printed page 58.  It is

```text
[((U_{u,t,sigma} tensor U_{u,t,bar sigma}) o (E tensor E)_{u,sigma})_{a,a}]
  / T_{t,D}(|a_1-a_2|)^2
  \prec
  1(|a_1-a_2| <= 6 ell_t^*)
    * eta_u^(-1) * (eta_u/eta_t)^4 * (ell_u/ell_s)^5
  + eta_u^(-1) * (W eta_u ell_u)^(-1/3) * (J^*_{u,D})^3.
```

The following sentence in the paper says that the factor `(eta_u/eta_t)^4` in the far row
has been absorbed by changing the exponent of `W eta_u ell_u` from `-1/2` to `-1/3`.

The T656 scratch factor is consistent with the unnormalised near-source bookkeeping:

```text
A_u^(-4) eta_u^(-1) flowR^5,
kernel expansion (eta_u/eta_v)^4 = ((1-u)/(1-v))^4.
```

Here `A_u=W ell_u eta_u`, so the source has the exact `W^-4`, `ell_u^-4`, and `eta_u^-5`
powers, while `flowR^5=(ell_t/ell_s)^5`.  There is no additional `N` power in (5.42).

The scratch theorem is not the full formula (5.42): it does not prove the
`T_{t,D}^{-2}` spatial normalisation, the near indicator, the far
`(W eta_u ell_u)^(-1/3)(J^*)^3` row, a general `hEE` producer, or a QV integral.  No such
conclusion may be inferred from the conditional pointwise theorem.

## Precise remaining implication and dispatch decision

For T656's exact stripped card, the smallest missing implication is one of

```text
exists K >= 0, eventually N, forall u,sigma,c,omega,
  ‖eeFun(N,u,omega,sigma,c)‖ <= N^K,
```

or the relative form

```text
exists K >= 0, eventually N, forall u,sigma,c,omega,
  ‖eeFun(N,u,omega,sigma,c)‖ <= N^K * Phi(N,u).
```

Neither follows from `t_N<1` plus `StochDom` alone.  The present Hermitian estimate leaves an
uncontrolled `eta_u^-1` in the relative ratio.

For the actual producer bundle, the smallest correction is not a new stochastic theorem:
retain the already-required `hc : Cond272 ...` in the claim card, use its uniform
`N^-2<=eta_u` consequence, and extract/reprove that short deterministic consequence together
with the fixed-time Gaussian leaves below the prohibited imports.

Even after that correction, a positive QV implementation ticket is not honest yet because:

1. the current scratch roots reach `Hierarchy.Step3`;
2. the fixed-time leaves and the existing `Cond272 -> eta` theorem are in downstream facades;
3. no accepted theorem supplies the simultaneous same-sample `hapriori` and full `hEE`
   premises;
4. the conditional pointwise theorem is not the full spatially normalised (5.42), a QV
   integral, `hfamily`, `APrimeSlot'`, T230 A-prime, the six-step chain, or paper closure.

No new ticket number is allocated here.

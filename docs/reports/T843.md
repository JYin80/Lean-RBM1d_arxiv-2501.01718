**Verdict: PASS**, for the finite deterministic next-image increment bound only.

## Statement and proof

I independently inspected `RBM1D/Gauss/PermutationNextImageIncrementBound.lean` after T842 explicitly reported proof and root-import completion. `permutation_nextImage_increment_le` quantifies over every `W`, valid `k : Fin W`, actual `π : PermΩ W`, `f : Fin W → ℝ`, and `C : ℝ`, with `0 ≤ C` and `∀ v, |f v| ≤ C`. Its conclusion is exactly

```lean
|f (π k) - (∑ v ∈ unusedImages W k π, f v) /
  ((W - k.val : ℕ) : ℝ)| ≤ 2 * C.
```

The proof uses T785's exact `unusedImages_card = W-k.val` and T833's strictly positive real denominator. It bounds the sum's absolute value by `C` times that cardinality, divides by the positive denominator, and applies `|a-b| ≤ |a|+|b|`. No probabilistic assertion is an input to the inequality. The `0 ≤ C` binder is unused in the proof, but the uniform bound on the nonempty `Fin W` domain implies it; it creates no vacuity.

## Boundary and satisfiability checks

- `W=0` admits no `k : Fin 0` (`no_next_index_zero`), as required by the universal valid-index statement.
- At the final valid index, `permutation_nextImage_increment_last_eq_zero` proves that the unused set is the singleton `{π k}` by permutation injectivity, so the increment is exactly zero. Its `W=1` specialization also proves zero.
- At `W=2,k=0`, `permutation_nextImage_increment_two_witness` uses `f(0)=0`, `f(1)=1` and identity/transposition samples. The unused average is `1/2`, so the respective increments are `-1/2` and `1/2`. Both samples belong to positive-mass actual-prefix cells; the accepted `uniformPerm_prefixCell_two_one_witness` identifies these cells as distinct singletons of mass `1/2`. Taking `C=1` satisfies both bound hypotheses, giving a nonconstant, nonzero instance. The witness does not turn the pointwise bound into a tail estimate.

## Statement and proof

`uniformPerm_nextImage_centered_condExp` quantifies over every `W : ℕ`, `k : Fin W`, and deterministic `f : Fin W → ℝ`. Under `uniformPerm W`, it proves almost-everywhere equality

```lean
(uniformPerm W)[(fun ρ => f (ρ k) - unusedImageAverage W k f ρ) |
  prefixSigma W k.val] =ᵐ[uniformPerm W] (fun _ => 0)
```

The conditioning algebra is the generated sigma algebra of coordinates strictly before `k`, not the ambient top algebra. The result is almost everywhere and does not assert a pointwise value for Mathlib's conditional-expectation representative.

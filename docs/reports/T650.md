### Formula (2.73)

For every loop length and every charge word, uniformly on the same closed interval,

```text
L_(u,sigma,a) \prec (ell_u/ell_s)^(n-1) * (W ell_u eta_u)^(-n+1),
s <= u <= t.
```

After multiplying by the definition of `xiL` and taking `n = 6`, the current Lean conversion
is exactly

```text
xiL_6 \prec flowR^5,
```

where the replacement `ell_u <= ell_t` makes the control uniform on `[s,t]`.  Since (2.73)
already maximizes over `sigma`, this source covers all two-charge words needed after doubling.

### Formulas (2.75) and (2.76)

Formula (2.75) is the strong local law uniformly for the same `u ∈ [s,t]`.  Formula (2.76)
immediately follows in the same Step-2 paragraph, for the same flow realization, the same
energy and window, and the alternating charge `sigma=(+,-)`.  The paper supplies no license
to choose their samples or windows separately.

There is a frozen Lean/paper shape difference that the conditional card must not hide.  The
paper prints

```text
R_(s,u)^4 A_u^(-2) exp(-sqrt(dist/ell_u)) + W^(-D),
```

whereas `AprioriDecayFlow` uses

```text
R_(s,u)^4 A_u^(-2) * (exp(-sqrt(dist/ell_u)) + W^(-D)).
```

This audit preserves the frozen Lean input and does not call it a literal restatement of
(2.76).

### Formula (5.42)

Formula (5.42) transports the doubled tensor through four kernel coordinates.  Before the
paper's stated absorption, its near row contains the fourth-power expansion
`(eta_u/eta_t)^4` and the Step-1 factor `(ell_u/ell_s)^5`; the source size immediately before
transport is `eta_u^(-1) A_u^(-4)` (the power count in (5.38) and the source estimate (5.36)).
Replacing the terminal time `t` by the running endpoint `v` gives exactly

```text
R_(u,v)^4 A_u^(-4) eta_u^(-1) flowR^5.
```

The sentence after (5.42) absorbs the fourth power only later, by weakening the exponent in a
different term.  It does not justify deleting `R_(u,v)^4` from this pointwise ledger.

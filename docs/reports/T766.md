## Exact proposed theorem signature

Suggested new module: `RBM1D/Gauss/PermutationNextImageConditionalCell.lean`, importing `PermutationPrefixCellFiberBridge`, `PermutationPrefixCellMass`, and `PermutationPrefixCellMeasurable` (with T703 in the bridge's import closure). The following is the proposed **statement**, with no proof supplied:

```lean
open MeasureTheory ProbabilityTheory

namespace RBM.Gauss

theorem uniformPerm_nextImage_given_actualPrefix
    (W : ℕ) (k : Fin W) (π : PermΩ W) (v : Fin W)
    (hv : ∀ i : Fin W, i < k → π i ≠ v) :
    uniformPerm W {ρ | ρ ∈ prefixCell W k.val π ∧ ρ k = v} /
      uniformPerm W {ρ | ρ ∈ prefixCell W k.val π} =
        (1 : ENNReal) / ((W - k.val : ℕ) : ENNReal)

end RBM.Gauss
```

The proposition with exactly these binders and terms was checked in a `/tmp` scratch file against the three actual imports using `lake env lean /tmp/T766Signature.lean` (exit 0). That checks elaboration only, not the theorem. The displayed declaration is a signature, not a compiled theorem or proof.

The left side is `P(ρ(k)=v, ρ∈C_k(π))/P(ρ∈C_k(π))` for `C_k(π)=prefixCell W k.val π`. T736 proves `0 < uniformPerm W {ρ | ρ ∈ C_k(π)}` for every actual `π`; the numerator and denominator are finite `ENNReal` values because the sample space is finite. Also `k.isLt` gives `0 < W-k.val`, so the right side is not `1/0`. The unused-value hypothesis `hv` is necessary for the displayed uniform law: a previously used `v` has zero next-image mass. No arbitrary prescribed prefix `p` is quantified, so an inconsistent, zero-mass prefix cannot satisfy the theorem by vacuity.

An intermediate counting identity, valid even for used `v`, is

\[
\frac{\operatorname{uniformPerm} W\{\rho\in C_k(\pi):\rho(k)=v\}}
     {\operatorname{uniformPerm} W(C_k(\pi))}
=\frac{|\operatorname{prefixCellNextFiber}(W,k,\pi,v)|}{|C_k(\pi)|}.
\]

This is not presently a Lean theorem. T749 supplies the exact subtype equivalence and card transport to T703's `permutationPrefixFiber W k (fun i => π i) v`; T736 supplies the denominator's mass and positivity. `uniformOn_univ`, `Measure.count_apply_finset`, and `Fintype.card_subtype` are the relevant installed counting APIs. T740 also identified `uniformOn_inter`/`uniformOn_apply_finset` as alternatives for the normalized conditional measure.

## Exact remaining finite proof obligations

1. Show the intersection event has cardinal `Fintype.card (prefixCellNextFiber W k π v)` and obtain the preceding ENNReal quotient by cancelling the positive, finite total permutation cardinal. T749 identifies the subtype; it does not itself evaluate its probability.
2. Partition the actual cell by the next value: every member has exactly one `ρ k`. Injectivity of permutations makes fibers for already used values empty. Prove that the unused value set has cardinal `W-k.val` (for example via the bijection `π` and `Fin.card_filter_val_lt`). Neither partition/count identity is supplied by T703 or T749.
3. For `W≥2`, apply T703's equal-cardinality theorem to `p i=π i` and any two distinct unused values. Each unused fiber is positive: `π k` is unused and has witness `π`, then equal cardinalities transfer positivity; for `v=π k` use that witness directly. Treat `W=1` separately, because T703 requires `2≤W` and there is no distinct pair of values. Consequently `|C_k(π)|=(W-k.val)|\operatorname{prefixCellNextFiber}(W,k,π,v)|`, and positive ENNReal cancellation yields `1/(W-k.val)`. T741's independently proved factorial cell-cardinality formula offers another route after identifying a next fiber with a `(k+1)`-cell; that identification is also still needed.
4. For a *full* next-image distribution, add the used-value zero-mass lemma; the proposed signature deliberately covers only unused `v`. For a conditional expectation or regular conditional distribution given `prefixSigma W k`, additionally prove that the T711 cylinder sigma algebra has exactly the actual prefix cells as atoms, or define a finite prefix observation `prefixObs` and prove `prefixSigma W k = (⊤ : MeasurableSpace (Obs W)).comap (prefixObs W k)`. T735 proves `measurableSet_prefixCell` in `prefixSigma`, and T742 proves a finite cell partition, but these facts alone do not identify all sigma-algebra atoms or a `condDistrib` kernel. T740/T705 list `condDistrib_apply_of_ne_zero` and `ae_eq_condExp_of_forall_setIntegral_eq` as possible later APIs; using either requires this additional bridge.

The proposed module can import the three named modules without a cycle: T749 imports T711 and T703, while T736 and T735 import T711. It must not assume the law or a conditional expectation identity as a premise for its own proof. No new analytic hypotheses are introduced; the explicit nondegenerate `W=2,k=0,π=id` example has both `v=0` and `v=1` unused, with two distinct one-point fibers and conditional mass `1/2` each by direct finite counting. T749 proves those fibers positive, and T736 proves that the conditioning cell at `k=0` has mass one. At `W=1`, `k=0` and `v=0` give mass one. At `W=0`, there is no `k:Fin 0`. At `k=W-1`, the one remaining unused image has mass one. `k=W` and `k>W` are excluded by the `Fin W` index; T711's full-information cells for natural-indexed `k≥W` are a terminal case, not another reveal.

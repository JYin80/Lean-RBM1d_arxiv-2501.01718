## Claim and scope

`RBM.APrimeGeneralMovingCrossBudgetP1Slot.eventually_integral_positiveTimeCrossBudget_p1_le_small_slot`
assumes `|E| < 2`, `60 ≤ D`, `0 ≤ s N ≤ t N < 1`, `c > 0`,
`Cond272Reg`, `BoundsCore (Gauss.sample Dims.exampleGrow) E s`, and
`0 < δ ≤ min 1 (c/100)`. With the exact T995 schedule
`deltaWeight = δ/100`, it proves that there are `C > 0` and one eventual
cutoff in `N`, uniform over every active target-mesh index `k` and every
output `a`, for which, with
`v = cutNetPt s (targetMesh D) N k`,

```text
∫_(s N)^v positiveTimeCrossBudget E D (δ/100) s t N k 1 a u du
  ≤ C N^(5δ/32) ratR(E,s,N,v)^(-2).
```

The constant is the T1211 constant plus `15/4`. The integrand is T1207's exact
literal positive-time budget, including its actual norm-good event, strict
transition, `jointRate`, `Step2.sigPM`, canonical smooth weight, Gaussian law,
and moving mesh endpoint. T1211 controls the matching literal norm-good
integral. No all-p extension, `hfamily`, A′ assembly, or paper stopping

## Mathematical preflight

I inspected the authorized `paper/250520-YinJun-v2.pdf`, formulas (5.21),
(5.24), and (5.43)–(5.46). Formula (5.21) is the loop hierarchy; (5.24)
controls the stochastic-integral moment through integrated quadratic
variation. Formula (5.43) defines the stopping time, (5.44) is its stopped
quadratic-variation bound, (5.45) changes the endpoint, and (5.46) takes a
time-net maximum. The present theorem invokes none of those stopped-process
claims: it composes the already accepted smooth-prefix estimates T1207 and
T1211. Thus it does not identify its auxiliary budget with the paper's stopped
martingale estimate.

For the active endpoint, the target-mesh domain gives
`0 ≤ s N ≤ v ≤ t N < 1`, hence
`0 ≤ sqrt(v)-sqrt(s N) ≤ 1`. At `p=1`, T1207's remainder coefficient is
exactly `15/4`, so its integrated remainder is at most `(15/4) N^(-1)`.
Cond272Reg and the dimension/scale bound give `ratR(v)^30 ≤ N`, while
`ratR(v) ≥ 1`; consequently `ratR(v)^2 ≤ N` and
`N^(-1) ≤ ratR(v)^(-2)`. Also `N^(5δ/32) ≥ 1` eventually. Therefore the
remainder is at most `(15/4) N^(5δ/32) ratR(v)^(-2)`. Adding T1211's
norm-good estimate proves the exact displayed claim with the same eventual
quantifier order: first fix `δ`, then choose one eventual cutoff before all
active `k` and outputs `a`.

The re-exported T995 `scheduled_positive_cell_witness` is a nondegenerate
model witness: it supplies an actual positive scheduled cell and a sample in
the common event with positive actual smooth weight. It does not assert
transition residence, positive mass of the common-event/transition
intersection, or nonzero rate. No such assertion is needed for this upper
bound.

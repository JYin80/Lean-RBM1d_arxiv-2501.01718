# T1061 — actual weighted-drift integral at fixed `D = 60`

## Verdict: mathematical preflight PASS; exact local slot proved

Added `RBM1D/Gauss/APrimeGeneralMovingActualDriftSlot60.lean`. The public
theorem `eventually_actual_weighted_g_integral_le_small_slot` proves that for
fixed `p : ℕ` with `1 ≤ p`, fixed `0 < δ ≤ min(1,c/100)`, and the stated
T615 window assumptions, one eventual cutoff in `N` works before every active
target-mesh index `k` and every output coordinate `a`. At
`v = cutNetPt s (targetMesh 60) N k`, the literal T615 actual-smooth weighted
`g` is interval integrable and

```text
∫_[s_N,v] g(E, D=60, actual weight(deltaWeight δ), p, N, k, a; u) du
  ≤ (11/Im(m_E)+3) N^(5δ/32) ratR(E,s,N,v)^(-2).
```

The output retains the T615 endpoint and normalization. `k=0` is included;
T615 handles its degenerate integral directly, while the profile and payment
bounds are also uniform over every active `k`. The fixed `p` is an input
before the eventual quantifier, so this does not assert a cutoff uniform over
all moment orders.

## Mathematical preflight

I checked the actual T615 source signature and the exact paper formulas in the
sole project source, `paper/250520-YinJun-v2.pdf`:

- Paper (5.40) is the propagated quadratic source bound, with the
  `η_u⁻¹(η_u/η_t)²(Wη_u ell_u)^(-1)(J*_{u,D})²` factor.
- Paper (5.41) is the propagated near/far drift bound. Its near row is
  `1_{|a₁-a₂|≤3ell*_t} η_u⁻¹(η_u/η_t)²(ell_u/ell_s)²`; its far row is
  `η_u⁻¹(η_u/η_t)²(Wη_u ell_u)^(-1/3)(J*_{u,D})³`.
- T615 is a more explicit capped deterministic envelope used by the Lean
  chain. It retains its exact cap and leakage terms; its near exponent and
  decomposition are among the already recorded paper distinctions (#136,
  T1023a, T1025a). This theorem proves a local bound for that Lean `g` and
  envelope. It does not identify the result with (5.40)–(5.41) literally.

The producer hypotheses align as follows. T615 requires the energy/window
conditions, `Cond272Reg`, `BoundsCore (sample d) E s`, five positive losses,
fixed `p ≥ 1`, and positive `β`. T995's schedule supplies

```text
deltaWeight = xi = δ/100,   deltaCap = δ/50,
tauG = δ/1600,              zetaSrc = zetaCtr = δ/3200,
```

with all T590/T603 room conditions and
`deltaWeight + xi = deltaCap`. At `β=1`, T615's all-cell inequality is
exactly its actual `g` integral bounded by the T615 profile integral plus
`(v-s_N)N⁻¹`. Its high-probability common-event input is not an extra free
assumption: `highProb_commonEvent` builds the source event from `BoundsCore`,
`Cond272Reg`, and the window hypotheses, deriving the required `Step1.Hyp`
through the Gaussian scale producer. `BoundsCore` remains an explicit
hypothesis in the general theorem.

Here `g` is T615's `MomentDuhamel.momNormW` applied to the actual
`APrimeSmoothWeightActual.weight` and the literal
`APrimeDriftTimeFamily.driftAt` at the target-mesh endpoint and output `a`.

The three estimates have compatible quantifiers and endpoints. T615, T1055,
and T1047 each give an eventual `N` cutoff followed by all active `k` (and, for
T615, all `a`). Intersecting these three eventual sets before introducing
`k,a` gives the requested common cutoff. T1055 contributes
`(11/Im(m_E)+2)Q`; T1047 contributes `Q`, where
`Q=N^(5δ/32)ratR(E,s,N,v)^(-2)`. Their sum is exactly
`(11/Im(m_E)+3)Q`. The T995 equality for `deltaCap` is used to align T615's
profile with T1055's profile; both use the same endpoint and terminal
`ratR(v)^(-2)` factor.

The hypotheses have a nondegenerate witness from
`scheduled_positive_cell_witness`: `E=0`, `D=60`, `s_N=0`, positive window
length and active first cell, `Cond272Reg`, `BoundsCore`, and `Step1.Hyp` all
hold together. For each admissible fixed `δ`, it supplies a sample in the
literal common event with widened weight one and strictly positive actual
smooth weight, at `p=1`. Thus the common-event and `BoundsCore` assumptions
used by the source are jointly satisfiable; the witness is not a claim that
the whole event has an integrated estimate.

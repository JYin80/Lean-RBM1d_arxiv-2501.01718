## Results

* `prefixCell_eq_iff_agree`: for every `W`, `k`, `π`, and `ρ`, their actual-prefix cells are equal iff `π` and `ρ` agree at every coordinate with value below `k`.
* `prefixCell_disjoint_of_disagree`: any coordinate below `k` where they differ proves the two cells are disjoint.
* `prefixCell_partition` and `prefixCell_iUnion_eq_univ`: each permutation lies in its own cell; any two indexed cells are equal or disjoint; and the union of all cells is the full permutation space.
* `prefixCell_two_id_swap_eq_zero` and `prefixCell_two_id_swap_ne_one`: at `W=2`, identity and swap label the same cell at `k=0`, but distinct cells at `k=1`.
* `prefixCell_zero_width_singleton`: at `W=0`, the actual cell at every `k` is the singleton containing the unique empty permutation.

The boundary cases `k=0` (every cell is `Finset.univ`) and `W ≤ k` (every cell is a singleton) use T711's `prefixCell_eq_univ_of_zero` and `prefixCell_eq_singleton_of_width_le`. T711 also supplies `mem_prefixCell` and `prefixCell_card_pos`, so every realized cell is nonempty. The partition is therefore formed by the distinct sets among these actual-prefix cells; repeated permutation labels for the same cell are identified by the equality theorem.

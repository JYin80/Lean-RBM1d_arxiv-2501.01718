The event retains the full `∃ u : U N` spatial union and the exact `W^ρ ζ` threshold. The proof works on one fixed `Band Ω` and one `StochDom` premise; it does not change the probability space or exchange eventual quantifiers.

The theorem makes no assertion about Gaussian event measurability.

The two paper scales remain separate specializations of `ζ`: entry `(Wℓη)⁻¹ᐟ²`, partial trace `(Wℓη)⁻¹`, each multiplied by `W^ρ`. The generic bridge neither combines the two events nor yields a simultaneous-in-energy or time event. This conditional difference from the literal paper (2.3)–(2.4) is recorded as `T844a` in `docs/paper-deltas.md`.

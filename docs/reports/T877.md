# T877 — actual-prefix Fourier component adapters

## Standalone result

**PASS for the finite-permutation component adapters.** Added
`RBM1D/Gauss/PermutationFourierDoobComponents.lean`. The module proves that the
real and imaginary components of T861's complex actual-prefix mean are exactly
the corresponding real `prefixCellMean`s, that both component means are
measurable for `prefixSigma W j`, and that each component's actual increment
has absolute value at most `8/W` under T861's bounds and `W ≥ 2`.

The module also gives pointwise width-zero and width-one component checks, the
zero-increment last-reveal check, and a width-two nonconstant witness. It makes
no conditional-MGF, Azuma-tail, Gaussian local-law, `Q0`, or paper-closure
claim. Its only imported mathematical facts are the accepted T861 actual-prefix
Fourier mean/increment module and T853 finite conditional-expectation module.

## Proof details

`prefixFourierRealMean` and `prefixFourierImagMean` are the corresponding
`prefixCellMean`s applied to the real and imaginary parts of
`permutationFourierSum`. `prefixFourierRealMean_eq_re` and
`prefixFourierImagMean_eq_im` use `Complex.re_sum` / `Complex.im_sum` and the
real-coordinate lemmas for division by a natural number. Thus they identify
the real finite average with `Complex.re` / `Complex.im` of
`prefixFourierMean`, whose actual-cell denominator is positive by the
inherited `prefixCell_card_pos` fact.

The two measurability proofs use constancy of each cell mean on its actual
prefix cell. Each inverse image of a measurable set is written as the union
of the cells whose representative mean lies in that set; every such cell is
measurable for `prefixSigma W j` by `measurableSet_prefixCell`.

For the increment bounds, apply `Complex.abs_re_le_norm` and
`Complex.abs_im_le_norm` to the difference of the adjacent complex means,
then use T861's pointwise complex norm bound. This yields the requested
absolute real and imaginary increment bounds with no new hypothesis beyond
`W ≥ 2`, `‖x i‖ ≤ 2`, and `‖χ i‖ ≤ 1`.

Boundary and witness declarations:

- `prefixFourierComponents_zero_one` checks `W=0` and `W=1`: their permutation
  spaces are singletons, and every actual prefix component mean equals the
  corresponding component of the observable. No `8/W` expression is formed.
- `prefixFourierComponents_last_eq` derives zero real and imaginary increments
  at `k.val + 1 = W` from the accepted T861 final-reveal equality.
- `prefixFourierRealMean_two_nonconstant_witness` specializes T861's concrete
  tables `x i = if i = 0 then 2 else -2` and
  `χ i = if i = 0 then 1 else -1`. For the identity permutation it gives
  `M_Re(0)=0` and `M_Re(1)=2`; hence the first reveal is nonconstant. The
  component bounds are inherited from the concrete table bounds.

The new statements add no assumptions to the model. There is no paper-delta:
the adapter and its finite witness are auxiliary permutation facts, not a
formalization or reformulation of paper (2.3)–(2.4). No `sorry`, `axiom`, or
new external input was added.

## Mathematical preflight and proof

I checked the authorized `paper/250520-YinJun-v2.pdf` directly at (5.21),
(5.24), and (5.43)–(5.46). Those equations concern the stopped loop hierarchy,
stopped martingale/QV estimate, first-crossing stopping time, and time-net
bounds. This ticket proves a counterexample to an internal *smooth-prefix*
transition-exclusion route, not a statement about the paper's stopping time.
I also checked the actual T995, T1051, T1101, and T475 reports and producers,
plus the T1209 FAIL and T1210 independent PASS reports.

For even `N`, set `M=N^258`, `j_H=M/8`, `k_H=j_H+1`, and `k_0=N^10`.
The file proves the divisibility of `M` by 8, the exact node identity
`cutNetPt s mesh N j_H=1/8`, and `k_0<k_H≤cutNetTop`. At that node,
`4√2=2/sqrt(1/8)`, so T1051's scalar lower-bound producer yields
`J_{j_H}≥W_N/64`. The actual stored-node comparison yields
`P_{k_H}≥J_{j_H}`, and the eventual bandwidth bound gives
`P_{k_H}>2Θ_N`, where
`Θ_N=8(exp 1)^2 N^(1/10000)`. At the same sample, T1101 gives
`P_{k_0}/Θ_N<1` because its matrix norm is at most `N`.

The first-crossing proof preserves the local no-jump argument. At every
adjacent pair before `k_H`, T1101's all-pairs norm-event modulus gives
`|J_{j+1}-J_j|≤N^122(1/N^258)^(1/2)=N^-7≤1/2`.
This is an adjacent-node estimate across the whole cell; the early-prefix
bound is used only at `k_0`. The literal smooth nodes satisfy
`J_j≤f_j≤q_N^(1/(2m_N))(J_j+N^-10)`. Since
`m_N=cutNetTop·q_N+1`, the accepted mesh has `m_N≥4q_N`, and Bernoulli's
inequality proves `q_N^(1/(2m_N))≤9/8`. The exact even-power soft maximum
obeys `P_{k+1}≤(3/2) max(P_k,|f_k|)`. At the least crossing `k_*`, these
bounds give `P_{k_*}≤(243/128)Θ_N<2Θ_N`, while its definition gives
`Θ_N<P_{k_*}`. Hence the transition is strict and `N^10<k_*≤cutNetTop`.

## Mathematical preflight

I checked the authorized `paper/250520-YinJun-v2.pdf` formulas (5.21),
(5.24), and (5.40)–(5.43). Formula (5.21) is the loop hierarchy identity,
(5.24) bounds its martingale term using the integrated covariance, (5.40)–
(5.42) are bounds for deterministic drift and covariance rows, and (5.43)
defines the stopping time. The present theorem composes accepted internal
smooth-prefix estimates; it neither formalizes those paper estimates nor
claims the stopped-process conclusion.

  `eventually_actual_weighted_g_integral_le_small_slot_anyD` has the same
  `E,D,c,s,t,δ,N,k,a`, `BoundsCore (Gauss.sample Dims.exampleGrow) E s`,

I intersected these three eventual sets before introducing `k` and `a`.
Interval-integral addition is justified by both integrability results. The
resulting coefficient is
`C = 2 * (|11 / (mE E).im + 3| + Ccross + 1) > 0`; the extra unit absorbs
the possibly signed drift coefficient without imposing a new premise. The
common target factor is nonnegative because the endpoint lies in `[s_N,t_N]`
and `ratR` is positive there.

T995's `scheduled_positive_cell_witness` remains a nondegenerate
satisfiability witness for the shared loss schedule: at `E=0,D=60`, it gives
an eventually active positive cell and a same-sample common-event point with
positive actual smooth weight for each admissible fixed `δ`. As specified,
this witness does not claim transition residence. The upper-bound composition
does not require such residence.

## Proof and scope

The Lean proof applies the T1073 theorem at `p=1`, obtains T1217's positive
cross constant and integral bound, and obtains T1123 integrability at `p=1`
with the exact T995 `deltaWeight`. It then rewrites the endpoints by their
definitions, adds the two integrals, and combines the bounds with the common
nonnegative scale `N^(5δ/32) ratR(v)^(-2)`. `k=0` is retained throughout.

No new hypotheses were introduced. The only witness relied on is the
nondegenerate T995 witness above. This does not prove a fit to
`APrimeOneStep.driftTerm`, all-`p` `N1`, `hfamily`, assembled A′, or the
paper's stopped result.

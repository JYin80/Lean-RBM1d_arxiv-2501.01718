# T985 — centered-event measurability and satisfiability

## Verdict

**PASS.** The exact fixed-`N` event `APrimeGeneralMovingCommonSources.centeredEvent`
is measurable whenever `|E| < 2` and `t N < 1`. The proof checks both charge
values and the full `TimeIcc s t N × ZMod (d.L N)` quantifier. It expresses the
event as an arbitrary intersection of closed norm sublevel sets.

witness at `E=0`, `s=0`, `t=1/2`, for every fixed `ζ>0`.

## Mathematical preflight

I checked the actual definitions and producers:

- `centeredEvent` quantifies over every `sigma : Bool` and every
  `p : TimeIcc s t N × ZMod (d.L N)`. Thus it includes both charges, both time
  endpoints, and every physical block.
- `centeredTrace` is the trace of
  `(Gsig (Hflow ...) (zt E u) sigma - mSigma E sigma • I) * Eblk ...`.
  At fixed `u`, `continuous_Gsig_Hflow` gives continuity in `ω`; matrix
  subtraction and multiplication, `Matrix.trace`, and the complex norm preserve
  continuity.
- For `p.1 : TimeIcc s t N`, its upper bound gives `u ≤ t N < 1`. Together
  with `|E|<2`, `zt_im_ne_zero_of_lt_one` places the spectral parameter away from
  the real axis for either charge. No hypothesis on `s` is required for this
  measurability implication.
- Each fixed `(sigma,p)` constraint is the inverse image of a closed real
  half-line under the continuous map `ω ↦ ‖centeredTrace ...‖`. The right side
  is constant in `ω`. Arbitrary intersections of these closed sets remain
  closed, hence measurable. This does not rely on a finite intersection or on
  `goodSetFlow` measurability and introduces no circular dependency: the new
  module imports the existing source definitions and T971's accepted witness;
  neither imports this module.

The fixed-time and all-time distinctions are preserved. Paper formula (2.4)
is the partial block-trace local-law probability estimate, with a normalized
`W⁻¹` block average. `centeredTrace` uses `Eblk` to encode that normalized
partial trace. The repository event adds both charges, the closed moving-time
index, and its `N^ζ · 2 qExt` threshold. Formula (4.1) is the fixed-time
entrywise event `Ω(t,c)`; it is a different predicate. This theorem proves
measurability only and supplies no local-law probability or high-probability
claim. The only paper source checked was `paper/250520-YinJun-v2.pdf`, formulas
(2.4) and (4.1).

## Satisfiability witness

The wrapper theorem
`eventually_nonempty_centeredEvent_E0_halfTime_nondegenerate` applies the
accepted T971 theorem
`eventually_exampleGrow_joint_goodSetFlow_centeredEvent_pos_nondegenerate`.
For every fixed `ζ>0` and all sufficiently large `N`, it provides one actual
sample in the literal `centeredEvent 0 (fun _ => 0) (fun _ => 1/2) ζ N`, with
`Xmat ≠ 0` and every zero-variance Gaussian coordinate equal to zero. T971
also proves positive actual Gaussian mass for the intersection with
`goodSetFlow`. This is an eventual witness, not a lower bound uniform in `N`.
Thus the hypotheses are jointly satisfiable with a nonzero matrix on a
positive-length time interval.

No new assumptions were added to the measurability theorem. Its assumptions
`|E|<2` and `t N<1` are concretely met by the witness parameters `E=0` and
`t N=1/2`.

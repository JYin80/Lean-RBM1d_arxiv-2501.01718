# T310 — feasibility preflight for the same-family near cross term

## Verdict

**The present producers do not justify a nontrivial `NearCrossBridge` for the actual A′ prefix weight.** There are two independent missing edges, before any final exponent comparison: the selected `prefixSoftW` has no `Gauss.WeightC1` derivative producer, and (5.42) controls the full evolved quadratic variation by a **near plus far plus residual** envelope, whereas `crossAt` contains `qHatNear` alone. The latter comparison has not been established on one event compatible with the weight. Do not open a Lean ticket that merely renames either assertion as an input. The independent continuation is a genuinely differentiable finite-coordinate weight (or a proved weak-derivative Stein formula for the hard maximum), followed by T280c's global (S5) plus bad-event norm split and a full near/far/residual time budget.

## Exact objects and Hölder arithmetic

T292's `NearCrossBridge` has left side `crossPart d N (momentAt …) wD r` and right side

`2p · (∫ w |flowY(coordAt … r)|^(2p) dP)^((2p−1)/(2p)) · crossInt κ (qHatNear E s v) r`.

As a `Prop`, this definition quantifies neither `WeightC1` nor a cutoff identity: `w`, `wD`, and `κ` are free arguments. A blanket theorem for arbitrary arguments would in particular allow `κ=0` and arbitrary scaled `wD`; it cannot be the intended positive target. Its useful specialization must name the actual weight and derivative and prove their regularity and rate bounds.

`momentAt` is the `2p` power of `coordAt`; `qvAt` is the *uncut* `Gauss.quadVar` of that same normalized coordinate. T300 proves its exact fixed-scale identity with the propagated raw rate: `qvAt = driftScale⁻² · Qraw`, where `driftScale=T_{v,D}(a)(η_s/η_v)^4`. Thus no alternative unpropagated QV may be substituted.

T275's `crossPart_le_integral` starts with `(2√r)⁻¹ ∫Σ_q gvar_q |wD_q|‖∂_q momentAt‖`. T265's (S5) for a **smooth power soft weight** gives the samplewise factor

`(2p)^2 · |wscale(w,p,Y)|^(2p−1) · [(15/8)/Θ] · k^(1/(2m)) · K · √qvAt`,

where `m≥1` is the soft-max order, `k` the prefix cardinality, and `K` the normalized gradient rate of its input coordinates at the same matrix. Hölder with exponents `2p/(2p−1)` and `2p` yields `φ^α‖√qvAt‖_{2p}`, `α=(2p−1)/(2p)`. At `p=1`, `α=1/2`; there is no `p=0` route. If one had `K=√u_j K₀` and a sharp rate envelope `qvAt≤Q̂`, the cross budget would have coefficient `Cs₀√(u_j/r)√Q̂/(4p)` with `Cs₀=(2p)^2(15/8)k^(1/(2m))K₀/Θ`. T275's `crossBudget_le_crossInt_model` requires `u_j≤1` and the **numerical fit**

`[p(15/8)k^(1/(2m))K₀/Θ]^2 ≤ κ`.

This is only the last algebraic conversion; it produces neither `K₀` nor `Q̂=qHatNear`. If using the raw fixed-weight derivative without the `√u_j` factor, the corresponding fit is the pointwise inequality

`[p(15/8)k^(1/(2m))K/Θ]^2 qvAt ≤ κ qHatNear`;

the `(2√r)⁻¹` prefactor leaves the integrable `r⁻¹/²` singularity. In the widened weight, `Θ=2e N^(2δ)`, so its reciprocal contributes `N^(−2δ)`, but there is no existing sharp `N,W,η` bound for the required `K` of the *actual* prefix input. The factor `k^(1/(2m))≤e` from `canonicalR` controls cardinality only. It does not bound the derivative of `jSnorm`.

`crossPart_le_of_S5` uses a different `hwD≤√r‖∂Wm(H_r)‖` premise and `hwDoff` outside `Good`. This cannot be inferred for the fixed prefix derivative from T265's smooth-matrix (S5): a prefix point is at time `u_j`, so its natural chain factor is `√u_j`, independent of running `r`. In particular, imposing `√r` on a fixed, nonzero derivative throughout `r↓0` with a fixed bounded matrix gradient would force that derivative to vanish. The project decision in `docs/CODEX-TICKETS.md` §9(4)–(5) explicitly routes around `hwDoff`: use the global (S5) and T280c's `crossPart_le_of_S5_event`, then split the **rate norm** on a measurable auxiliary event. This route preserves the exponent `α`, but adds `√Env · P(Eᶜ)^(1/(2p))` to the sharp `√Qev`; that term also needs a budget.

## The exact near/far ratio

Put `A_u=Wℓ_uη_u` and `R=η_s/η_v`. The deterministic chosen rate is

`qHatNear(u)=η_u⁻¹(η_u/η_v)^4(η_s/η_u)^(5/2) R⁻⁸ = η_u^(1/2)η_v^4/η_s^(11/2)`.

The near part of paper (5.42), after the same `R⁻⁸` normalization and `ℓ_u/ℓ_s≤√(η_s/η_u)`, is bounded by this rate, with its spatial indicator. Its far part, **before residuals and hidden `≺` constants**, has normalized shape `η_u⁻¹ A_u^(−1/3)(J*_{u,D})³ R⁻⁸`. Relative to `qHatNear`, the far/near quotient is exactly

`A_u^(−1/3)(J*_{u,D})³ (η_v/η_u)^4 (η_u/η_s)^(5/2)`.

The term is nonnegative even when the near spatial indicator is zero. T293 proves positive *inverse-scale exponent margins* after the widened `J` cost `N^(3τ)` under the actual separate-scale `Cond272Reg`; it does not prove this pointwise quotient is ≤ a fixed `κ`, nor that the residual of T277 fits it. T300's actual endpoint theorem displays all three square-root pieces and retains hypotheses `hGm`, `hSmax`, `h273`, `h564`, `h42sq` at each `(u,ω)`. No supplied theorem turns those premises into one full-rate `Qev≤C qHatNear` on the calibrated cutoff event. The safer target is an explicit `Qnear+Qfar+Qres` norm/time envelope, not a near-only assertion. Paper (5.40) is a quadratic drift estimate and (5.41) a drift near/far estimate; neither removes the far QV term of (5.42). Paper (2.71) is the theorem's input bound on the `E(L−K)` error, not a cross-term estimate.

## Boundary, support, quantifiers, and dependency

For `E=0,s=0,v=1/2`, `η_0=1`, `η_v=1/2`, and `qHatNear(r)=√(1−r)/16`; hence `crossAt(0,0,1/2,κ,r)=r⁻¹/²√κ(1−r)^(1/4)/4` for `r>0`. It is positive for `κ>0` and integrable on `[0,1/2]`. At `r=0`, Lean's totalized inverse makes `crossAt=0`; the Stein and cross-budget lemmas require `r>0`, and time integration handles this one endpoint by almost-everywhere equality. T292 proves this integrability. At `p=1`, the Hölder exponent is genuinely `1/2`. At prefix index `k=0`, the weight is identically `1`, so `wD=0` and the cross part vanishes; this is an **empty-prefix case**, not a nonzero cross witness. For `k=1,s=0`, its sole input is the initial net point; it likewise cannot certify the first genuinely active prefix derivative. A nontrivial cross proof must cover `k≥2` and the correct `u_j/r` factor on its time cell.

The actual `prefixSoftW` takes `Step2Moment.jSnorm` at `j<k`. `jSnorm` divides `Step2.jS`, and `jS` is `jStar`, a finite supremum of normalized absolute coordinates. The soft maximum **over these hard maxima** is measurable (`measurable_prefixSoftW`) and bounded between zero and one, but neither `APrimeWeight` nor the inspected producers supplies the six `WeightC1` fields or `wD` for it. T265's derivative theorem assumes each soft-max input is `ContDiff ℝ 1`; it cannot be applied to `jSnorm` from its measurability alone. `weightC1_cosW` is a nonconstant Gaussian witness for the *interface*, not this weight. Switching points of a hard maximum within the cutoff transition band are a concrete differentiation concern; no theorem excludes them or supplies a weak derivative.

T286 gives nonemptiness of its (4.2)/`goodSetFlow` event. T280a's `sat_piecewiseW_on_good` witnesses a nonconstant cutoff on an abstract scalar event. These **separate** witnesses do not establish a nonempty common Gaussian event where T277's pointwise premises, the cutoff transition band, and calibrated support all hold. For T275's `crossPart_le_of_S5`, taking `Good` to be the stochastic event would still require `wD=0` outside it; taking `Good=univ` would require a sharp global QV envelope. Neither follows. T280c's alternative needs no such support inclusion, but does need a genuinely global smooth-weight (S5), a measurable high-probability event, a polynomial global envelope, and a complement budget uniform in the required `(δ,p,N,k,u,a)` quantifiers. T294 supplies fixed-`N` integrability only. It does not supply a sharp `N` exponent.

There is a noncircular proof order in principle: smooth weight/derivative and global (S5); T277/T300 propagated full-QV bound on the independent (4.2)/Step-1 event; T280c bad-event split; deterministic near/far/residual integration and fit; then the first-pass moment/slot. Using `Step2.jS_stochDom`, `JSNormDom`, or the desired first-pass `WeightedMoment` to establish the cutoff event would reverse this order and create a dependency cycle. No existing producer establishes all the preceding steps, so this report asserts no `NearCrossBridge`, no (G), and no slot.

## Narrowest next proof ticket

The narrowest useful **positive** target is a model-pinned, global pointwise (S5) for a specified smooth finite-coordinate replacement of the actual prefix cutoff, with a proven `WeightC1` derivative and a comparison that transfers the widened weighted moment to the required prefix weight. It must retain the true `√u_j` factor and the full evolved `qvAt`. Only after that can the independent event-norm estimate target a deterministic `crossInt` built from `qHatNear` **plus** an explicitly integrated far/residual rate (or prove the displayed quotient and residual are absorbed under the actual hypotheses). If preserving the hard-max cutoff is essential, the alternative first ticket is a valid Gaussian Stein identity for its weak derivative, including switching sets and a nonzero transition-band witness. A near-only `NearCrossBridge` is not presently a justified next Lean theorem.

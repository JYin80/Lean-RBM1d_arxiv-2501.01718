## Formal statement and scope

`RBM1D/Flow/OUMarginalGaussian.lean` defines `ouProductMeasure d N` as the product of the actual
band Gaussian law and an independent normalized Hermitian GUE coordinate field. For every
`d : Dims`, every parameter `N : ℕ`, every fixed `t`, and every finite set `I : Finset (Coord d)`,
`fixedTimeMarginal_jointGaussian` proves `HasGaussianLaw` for the vector of interpolated primitive
real coordinates indexed by `I`. This is the full finite-dimensional Gaussian law, rather than
just a second-moment identity. For every `t ≥ 0` and every matrix entry `(i,j)`,
`ouMatrix_entry_covariance` proves

```text
∫ ‖ouMatrix(t)ᵢⱼ‖² = (1-ζ(t)) Sblkᵢⱼ + ζ(t)/M.
```

The proof treats diagonal entries as one real coordinate and off-diagonal entries as the sum of
their real and imaginary coordinate variances. `ouMatrix_isHermitian` and `ouMatrix_start` give
the pointwise Hermitian and time-zero statements. `ouBandMatrix_indep_ouGueMatrix` proves
independence on the explicit product measure.

Here `M = L N · W N = (Gauss.band d).size N`; it is the matrix dimension, distinct from the
sequence parameter `N`. The assumptions `three_le_L` and `W_pos` make `M > 0` for every `N`,
including `N = 0`. The covariance theorem assumes only `0 ≤ t`; the joint Gaussian theorem is
stated for each fixed `t` and finite coordinate set. There is no time-uniform event or exchange of
the fixed-time and eventual-`N` quantifiers.

The witness theorem `exampleGrow_ouBandGue_nonzero_witness` gives, for every `N` including zero,
one index and one sample `(s,s)` in the same product space at which both diagonal entries are `1`.
Thus the band and GUE matrices are simultaneously nonzero in the requested actual `exampleGrow`
model. This is a pointwise witness, not a positive-probability event, and the proof introduces no
probabilistic event assumption.

## Exact claim and scope

For `W ≥ 2`, `T ≥ 1`, any deterministic table
`x : Fin T → Fin W → ℂ` with `‖x t j‖ ≤ 2`, and `r > 0`,
`uniformPerm_permutationFourierGridGoodSet_prob_lower_bound` proves

`1 - 4 * T * (W - 1) * exp(-W * r² / 256) ≤ (uniformPerm W).real goodSet`,

where one uniformly sampled permutation `π : Equiv.Perm (Fin W)` must satisfy
`‖permutationFourierSum W (x t) (permutationFourierCharacter W q) π‖ < r`
for **every** `t : Fin T` and `q : Fin W` with `q ≠ 0`. The sum is exactly
`W⁻¹ ∑_j χ_q(j) x_t(π(j))` (`PermutationFourierSwap.lean:18–20`), and
`uniformPerm W` is `uniformOn Set.univ` on the finite permutation space
(`PermutationPrefixFiltrationCore.lean:13–24`). The theorem makes no
independence assumption on the time rows or modes.

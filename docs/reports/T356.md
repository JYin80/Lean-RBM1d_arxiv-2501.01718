## Proof and exact normalization

1. **Scalar resolvent and loop.** `green_scalar`, `gloop_scalar_two`, and `gloop_scalar_zero_energy` compute the actual inverse and normalized trace. At energy zero,

   \[
   L^{+-}_{u,(a,b)}(xI)
    =\frac{\mathbf1_{a=b}}{W[x^2+(1-u)^2]},\qquad
   K^{+-}_{u,(a,b)}=W^{-1}\Theta_u(a,b).
   \]

   The real scalar `x` in this display denotes the evolved matrix, not its unevolved Gaussian coordinate. `lk_scalarSample` applies the formula with `x = √u` times the coordinate value.

2. **Zero sample.** The existing resolvent estimate gives `‖Θ_u−I‖` entrywise at most `4u` for `0 ≤ u ≤ 1/2`. The scalar inverse-square error is at most `6u`, hence `norm_lk_zero_le` gives raw error at most `10u/W`. The floor `W^(-D)` in the actual tail denominator and `R_u ≥ 1` then give `jSnorm_zero_le_two`: `J_u ≤ 2` when `10uW^(D−1) ≤ 1`. Both prefix times `0,h` satisfy this condition.

3. **Both smoothing factors.** The imported actual-prefix bound retains the product of the inner label factor `L^(1/m)` and outer two-time factor `2^(1/(2m))`, equivalently `(2L²)^(1/(2m))`. With the exact smoothing scale `ε=W^(-D)N^(-10)`, it yields

   \[
   S_2(0)\le (2L^2)^{1/(2m)}(2+N^{-10})\le3e
      \le\Theta_N,\quad \Theta_N=8e^2N^{2\delta}.
   \]

   The actual cutoff plateau is `χ(y)=1` for `y≤1`; therefore `weight_zeroSample_eq_one` proves the literal value 1. This theorem also allows any natural `p` and handles the inactive branch directly.

4. **Second sample.** At time `h`, its evolved matrix is `2I`. The diagonal scalar loop is at most `1/(4W)`. Rather than introducing a new positivity theorem for `Θ`, the formal proof uses `‖Θ_h(a,a)−1‖≤4h≤1/2`, so the diagonal primitive has norm at least `1/(2W)`. Reverse triangle gives `norm_lk_transition_lower`, raw error at least `1/(4W)`.

5. **Time-specific denominator preserved.** `actual_tail_normalization_le_sixteen` proves

   \[
   T_h(0)R_h^4
    =\left([W\ell_h(1-h)]^{-2}+W^{-D}\right)(1-h)^{-4}
    \le16W^{-2}.
   \]

   This is stronger than the requested `48W⁻²` at the smaller time range implied by the finite assumptions. It uses the actual `Step2.tT` and `Step2Moment.ratR`; no endpoint substitution or omitted `R_h⁴` occurs. Combined with the weaker raw lower bound above, it still gives `jSnorm_transition_lower`: `J_h ≥ W/64`.

6. **Literal zero cutoff.** `jSnorm_net_le_prefixSample` proves the exact lower sandwich from any prefix net observable into the nested smooth prefix. Thus `S_2 ≥ J_h ≥ W/64 > 16e²N^(2δ)=2Θ_N`. The actual cutoff is zero on `[2,∞)`, and `2p>0`; `weight_transitionSample_eq_zero` gives weight zero.

This proves nonconstancy without assuming a Good event, A′, a stochastic local law, a gradient bound, or support induction. The construction is deterministic on actual Gaussian coordinates.

## Exponent and endpoint calculation

Let `R_u = ratR(u)`, `R_v = ratR(v)`, and
`C0 = 3 + 9 exp(sqrt 3) (4 exp(1)+2)`. The local block-cap estimate is
`blockCap(u) ≤ C0 N^(tauG+2 deltaCap) R_u^4`. Also,
`(ell_u/ell_s)^2 ≤ R_u` by `Step2MomentStep.ratio_sq_le`. These give the
pointwise power reduction

```text
(ell_u/ell_s) R_u^(-2) blockCap(u)
  ≤ C0 N^(tauG+2 deltaCap) R_u^(5/2).
```

The exact `integral_ratio_power` identity then gives

```text
∫[s_N,v] R_u^(5/2) eta_u^(-1) du
  ≤ R_v^(5/2) / ((5/2) Im(m_E)).
```

Combining this with the retained endpoint factor `R_v^(-2)` leaves
`R_v^(1/2) (flowDelta + W^(-1))`. Cond272Reg's thirtieth-power margin,
`scale(t_N) ≤ N`, and `W ≥ N^(5/8)` prove this factor is at most `2`.
The two remaining coefficient bounds are `xiK ≤ N^theta` and
`cFar(W,ell_u) ≤ N^theta`.

For the T995 schedule and `theta=δ/100`, the exponent is

```text
alpha = zetaCtr + tauG + 2 deltaCap + 2 theta
      = 39 δ/640 < 5 δ/32.
```

theorem `eventually_far_linear_integral_le_small_slot` proves, for each


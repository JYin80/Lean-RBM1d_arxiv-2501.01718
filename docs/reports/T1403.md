## Direct producer and mismatch audit

No exact compiled random-`Lmax` fluctuation-average producer was found by searching the direct Gaussian sources and declarations.

* `RBM1D/Gauss/DetFlucAvgComplete.lean` proves moment-to-`UnifDomIcc` at a deterministic scale `Psi^2`, with a deterministic local law. It does not yield control by the realized `Lmax`.
* `RBM1D/Gauss/FlucIter.lean` supplies the actual conditional-row singleton-pivot and moment iteration for `flucDiag`, once a deterministic `FlucGain`/`FlucGainUpTo'` estimate is available.
* `RBM1D/Gauss/MinorDiffGain.lean` proves all finite minor-difference orders under a deterministic `Psi` and the corresponding pointwise minor-good hypothesis. `RBM1D/Gauss/MinorDiffCond.lean` handles the exceptional-set tower for that deterministic-scale interface. Neither transports a samplewise random `Gamma` through resampled factors.
* `RBM1D/Gauss/CondStableInst.lean` has fixed-time entry-square/minor estimates at `Lmax`, but no random-scale fluctuation average. The deterministic upper comparison `Lmax <= eta_t^(-2) W^(-1)` cannot be made uniform by silently absorbing `eta_t^(-2)` when the endpoint moves with `N`.

The exact missing implementation edge is a random-normalizer version of the fixed-order singleton-pivot product estimate. For each fixed `p` and `epsilon`, it must define the actual embedded minor loop controls `Gamma_ab^S`, use the moment-independent smooth proxy

```text
q_N = 2 ceil(2 log(N+2)) + 8,
Lambda^S = (W^(-q_N) + sum_(a,b) (Gamma_ab^S)^(q_N))^(1/q_N),
Y_i^S = (G_ii^S-m) / Lambda^(S union {i}),
```

and prove the finite-difference bounds for `Lambda^S` and its inverse through the fixed deletion budget. It must then expand the actual `2p`-th moment of `sum_i w_i Q_i Y_i^empty`; every resampled factor in a branch has to retain the same fully deleted `Lambda^R`, so the positive and negative powers combine before the good-event comparison. The required missing lemma is, schematically,

```text
E |sum_i w_i Q_i Y_i^empty|^(2p) <= C_(p,epsilon) N^epsilon
```

uniformly in deterministic `u` and admissible deterministic `w`, with the exceptional contribution paid using the actual polynomial `eta` envelope. Existing `FlucIter` does not accept these row-normalized variables, and existing `MinorDiffGain` does not supply their random finite-difference estimate. Without this lemma, the floor removal from `Lmax + W^(-1)` and conversion to the literal random `Lmax` StochDom output cannot be completed. This is a formalization gap in the inspected Lean route, not a counterexample to (4.12).

## Same-event actual-model witness

`RBM1D/Gauss/RandomLmaxFlucAvg.lean` proves `firstCell_randomLmax_nondegenerate_sameEvent_witness`, using `firstCell_step1_localLaw_witness` with `d = Dims.exampleGrow`, `E=0`, and the actual flow event `flowNetEvent`. It packages one sample from that event, the bounds at every time and entry, and a positive interior time with positive diagonal Gaussian variance:

* the threshold `firstCellDelta = N^(1/16) firstCellPsi` is eventually at most `N^(-1/8)`, giving the polynomial WLL bound;
* `firstCellPsi^2 = 1/(4W)`, and the event's diagonal lower bound gives `W^(-1) <= 4 Lmax`; hence on that same sample `|G_ij-m 1_(i=j)|^2 <= N^(1/8) Lmax` for all entries and times.

The interval starts at zero and is eventually nondegenerate, with its right endpoint at most `1/2`. At the positive interior time `u=t_N/2=(s_N+t_N)/2`, the actual diagonal coordinate has
`E |Hflow_ii|^2 = u S_ii = u/(3W) > 0`, by the compiled Gaussian coordinate moment formula and `Sblk` definition. Thus the witness is not certified only at `u=0`, and the entry inputs are not assembled from independent samples. Its `eta` satisfies `eta_(t_N) >= 1/2`, so the polynomial endpoint-floor hypothesis is met with `K=0`.

The source theorem records the event as nonempty eventually; the existing producer derives that fact from the first-cell local law and the actual Gaussian flow good event. This witness validates satisfiability only. It does not establish the arbitrary-`Dims`, moving-endpoint random-normalizer implication.

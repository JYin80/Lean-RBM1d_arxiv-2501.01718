| Endpoint masses and Jacobian | At `x=±2` and outside `(-2,2)`, the density is zero (`:170–176`), so restricting the density integral to the image loses no contribution. The accepted `semicircleMeasure_singleton` also gives zero endpoint masses. Since `sqrt(4-φ(t)²)=2/sqrt(1+t²)`, direct multiplication gives `ρ_sc(φ(t))φ'(t)=2/[π(1+t²)²]` (`:105–128`). `:178–201` uses the one-dimensional image integral theorem with positive derivative and injectivity, and converts `withDensity` to Lebesgue integration with finite density everywhere. | Passed |
| Algebra for `0<u<1` | Put `b=1-u`, `c=1+u`. Then `0<b<c`, `c²=b²+4u`, and `b+c=2`. The substituted denominator satisfies `u φ(t)²+b²=[b²+c²t²]/(1+t²)`, so the integrand becomes `(2b/π)/[(1+t²)(b²+c²t²)]` (`:225–258`). | Passed |
| Partial fractions and integrability | For `d=c²-b²>0`, `1/[(1+t²)(b²+c²t²)]=d⁻¹[c²/(b²+c²t²)-1/(1+t²)]`. Separately, the original kernel is continuous and bounded by `1/b` under the finite semicircle measure (`:204–221`). | Passed |
| Exact value and nondegeneracy | Multiplying by `2b/π` and using `b+c=2` yields exactly 1 (`:260–263`). The separate `u=0` identity, the `u=1/2` instance, and unequal kernel values at `x=0` and `x=1` are proved at `:265–283`. No new hypothesis risks an empty statement. | Passed |
| Noncircular scope | The new source invokes the explicit density, the Jacobian image theorem, and evaluated ordinary Cauchy integrals. It does not invoke `RBM.msc` or a semicircle-transform theorem. The density module transitively imports `Defs/Semicircle.lean`, but no algebraic `msc` declaration occurs in the new proof. | Passed |

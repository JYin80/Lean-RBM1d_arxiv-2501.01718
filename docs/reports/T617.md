## Power and endpoint ledger

1. `Cond272Reg` is used only through its scale component.  The existing
   scale lemma gives `N^-1 <= etaT E (t N)` eventually.
2. Every stored prefix time and the current endpoint belong to `[s_N,t_N]`.
   Antitonicity of `etaT` therefore gives the same `N^-1` floor at each such
   time, including both closed endpoints.
3. For the raw length-two coordinate observable,
   `etaT^-1 <= N` makes the resolvent derivative constant
   `2(1+etaT^-1)^3 <= 16 N^3`.  With
   `card(Idx N) <= N` and `coordWeight <= 8N^2`, the square root of the
   matrix QV is bounded by `2^15 N^8`.
4. The exact tail floor `W^-D <= tT`, together with `W <= N` and `D >= 0`,
   costs at most `N^D`.  Hence each raw normalized prefix rate is bounded by
   `2^15 N^(D+8)`.
5. The exact pullback has `sqrt(u) <= 1` and `ratR^-4 <= 1`.  The canonical
   smoothing calibration costs only `exp(1)`, which is absorbed by the
   literal threshold `8 exp(1)^2 N^(2 deltaWeight)` because
   `deltaWeight >= 0`.
6. `APrimeQVGlobalPoly.eventually_qvAt_le_poly` at `Keta=1` gives
   `qvAt <= 2^21 N^(2D+16)` for arbitrary charge, output, closed running
   time, and sample.  Taking square roots gives the safe bound
   `2^11 N^(D+8)`.
7. Multiplying the two root bounds gives exactly
   `2^26 N^(2D+16)`.  The only exponent widening is the separate eventual
   payment adapter, where the fixed constant `2^26` is absorbed by one
   additional power of `N`.

The `k=0` branch is included directly in the smooth-prefix theorem; no false
claim that instantaneous `qvAt` vanishes at `k=0` is made.  Both `r=s_N` and
`r=cutNetPt ... k` are retained by the literal `Set.Icc` binder.

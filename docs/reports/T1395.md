## Paper and existing-source preflight

The prescribed source is `paper/250520-YinJun-v2.pdf`. Immediately after (2.27), the paper states that for fixed `t` and bulk energy `E`,

`η ≤ η̃  ⇒  η Im m_t(E+iη) ≤ η̃ Im m_t(E+iη̃)`.

It then takes `η̃ = N^{-1+2τ_U}` and invokes (2.26) to bound the smaller-scale `Im m_t(z_i)` before substituting that bound into the expectation estimate (2.25), obtaining (2.28). The formalization proves precisely the deterministic monotonicity and includes `stieltjes_eta_mul_im_le_of_upper_bound`, which composes it with an upper bound at `η̃`.

The existing consumer definition is `RBM.stieltjes` in `RBM1D/Flow/Universality.lean` around line 1596: `card(n)⁻¹ * trace (green H z)`, with `green H z = (H-zI)⁻¹` from `RBM1D/Delocalization.lean`. The sign agrees with the paper: `Im (λ-(E+iη))⁻¹ = η/((λ-E)²+η²)`. The same consumer source already contains `RBM.imGreen_eq_spectral`. A search of the imported and adjacent flow, Green, definitions, and hierarchy Lean sources found no compiled theorem for this Stieltjes scale monotonicity.

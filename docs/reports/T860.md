# T860 — read-only preflight of semicircle equal-mass quantiles

## Verdict and scope

**Mathematical/API route: viable; no Lean quantile or Q0 theorem proved.** The accepted `RBM1D/Defs/SemicircleDensityMeasure.lean` and `RBM1D/Defs/SemicircleDensitySupportFull.lean` supply a normalized, atomless semicircle measure, strict positive mass for every nonempty open subinterval of `[-2,2]`, reflection invariance, and exact support `[-2,2]`. These suffice for a continuous, strictly increasing CDF on `[-2,2]` and unique equal-mass boundary and midpoint quantiles for every natural `W≥1`. They do **not** give the still-missing analytic Stieltjes integral or its equality to the algebraic `RBM.msc`. No Q0, paper local law, A-prime, `hfamily`, or six-step closure follows from this preflight.

Only this report was written. No Lean file, `RBM1D.lean`, shared status/task/delta file, index, commit, or remote was changed. No Lean probe, build, or axiom print was run; the API route below is based on source inspection, not on a compiled proof.

## CDF route and exact Mathlib API

Set `F := ProbabilityTheory.cdf semicircleMeasure : ℝ → ℝ` (the definition is bundled as a `StieltjesFunction`). Installed `Mathlib/Probability/CDF.lean:69,78,82,90` contains `monotone_cdf`, `ofReal_cdf`, `cdf_eq_real`, and `measure_cdf`. The smallest acyclic next statement is the **CDF increment**

```lean
theorem semicircleMeasure_Ioc_cdf (a b : ℝ) :
    semicircleMeasure (Set.Ioc a b) =
      ENNReal.ofReal (ProbabilityTheory.cdf semicircleMeasure b -
                     ProbabilityTheory.cdf semicircleMeasure a)
```

It follows directly by rewriting `← ProbabilityTheory.measure_cdf semicircleMeasure` and using `StieltjesFunction.measure_Ioc` (`Measure/Stieltjes.lean:528`); it needs no new hypothesis or analytic transform. For `−2≤a<b≤2`, `Ioo a b ⊆ Ioc a b` and `semicircleMeasure_Ioo_pos` make this increment positive. Together with `monotone_cdf`, this proves `StrictMonoOn F (Set.Icc (-2:ℝ) 2)`. In particular `F` is injective on that interval. Do not infer strictness from full support alone without explicitly extracting positive mass of `(a,b)`.

Continuity is a separate, short measure-to-CDF bridge. `StieltjesFunction.measure_singleton` (`Measure/Stieltjes.lean:533`) and `measure_cdf` identify `semicircleMeasure {x}` with `ENNReal.ofReal (F x - Function.leftLim F x)`. The accepted atomlessness, monotonicity (so this difference is nonnegative), and `ofReal` cancellation give `Function.leftLim F x = F x`. The installed `Monotone.continuousWithinAt_Iio_iff_leftLim_eq`, `continuousWithinAt_Iio_iff_Iic`, `StieltjesFunction.right_continuous`, and `continuousAt_iff_continuous_left_right` then give `Continuous F`, hence `ContinuousOn F (Set.Icc (-2:ℝ) 2)`. This left-limit bridge is a needed producer; `ProbabilityTheory.cdf` itself promises only right continuity for arbitrary measures.

The accepted support/normalization/atomlessness results imply `F (-2)=0`: `Iic (-2)` meets `[-2,2]` only at the null singleton `{-2}`. Similarly `F 2=1`, since the complement of `Iic 2` lies outside the supported interval. Use `ofReal_cdf` or `cdf_eq_real` plus finite-measure conversion for these endpoints. Installed `intermediate_value_Icc` (`Topology/Order/IntermediateValue.lean:558`) applied to `ContinuousOn F (Icc (-2) 2)` maps every `t∈[0,1]` to some `x∈[-2,2]` with `F x=t`; strict monotonicity makes this `x` unique. `ContinuousOn.image_Icc_of_monotoneOn` (`ibid.:869`) packages the same surjectivity once the endpoints are known. A choice-based inverse on `Icc 0 1` is enough; no pre-existing real quantile inverse API is required.

Reflection invariance gives `F(-x)=1-F(x)`, but the atomless endpoint correction must be kept: `(-·)⁻¹'(Iic (-x))=Ici x`, whose mass is `1-μ(Iio x)=1-F(x)` only after using `μ{x}=0`. `Measure.measure_preimage_of_map_eq_self` (`Measure/Map.lean:189`) and `semicircleMeasure_map_neg` provide the map step. It follows that `F 0=1/2`.

## Finite quantiles and nondegenerate checks

For `W:ℕ` with `hW:0<W`, define `γ_j` for `0≤j≤W` as the unique inverse image of `(j:ℝ)/W`, and `λ_j` for `0≤j<W` as the unique inverse image of `((j:ℝ)+1/2)/W`. Require `hW` before denominator cancellation or asserting level membership. There are exactly `W+1` boundaries, with `γ_0=-2`, `γ_W=2`; strict monotonicity gives

`γ_j < λ_j < γ_(j+1)` and `γ_j < γ_(j+1)` for every `j<W`.

The increment theorem gives `μ(Ioc γ_j γ_(j+1))=1/W` after `ENNReal.ofReal` arithmetic. These half-open cells are disjoint. Their union is `(-2,2]`; adding the null singleton `{-2}` gives all of `[-2,2]`, and the exterior has zero mass. Thus the first and last cells are genuine positive-mass cells even though `γ_0` and `γ_W` are endpoint density zeros; both endpoint atoms vanish. Each `λ_j` lies inside its own cell, rather than merely its closure.

Reflection and inverse uniqueness yield `λ_(W-1-j)=-λ_j` for each `j<W` (after finite-index arithmetic). At `W=1`, its sole midpoint level is `1/2`, hence `λ_0=0` and the cell mass is one. At `W=2`, levels are `1/4` and `3/4`; strictness around `F0=1/2` yields `λ_0<0<λ_1=-λ_0`, and the two disjoint cells each have mass `1/2`. These are explicit nondegenerate witnesses, not an eventual-in-`W` assertion.

The quantile theorem can be proved in a new `Defs/SemicircleQuantiles.lean` importing only the accepted density/support modules and Mathlib CDF/interval continuity; it should not import `Flow`, `Gauss`, or any Q0 consumer. Its output should expose the CDF equations, order, endpoint identities, cell masses, and reflection, so a later quadrature module need not reprove measure facts.

## Boundary of this ticket and Q0 dependency

The scalar resolvent is `g_u(x)=(sqrt u·x-i(1-u))⁻¹` for `0≤u≤1/2`. Its denominators have norm at least `1-u≥1/2`, including `u=0`, `u=1/2`, and `x=±2`; at `u=0`, `g_0=i` pointwise. The T714/T718 telescoping estimate over **all** `W` cells, including the two endpoint cells, bounds quadrature minus the **actual density integral** by `8√2/W`. To replace that integral by `i` requires the missing direct Stieltjes integral identity with the paper's `(x-z)⁻¹` sign. The repository's algebraic `RBM.msc_mul` and positive-imaginary-root statements cannot substitute for this identity without a proof connecting them to the density integral. Thus a quantile module and an independent analytic transform module are separate upstream leaves before a closed-`u∈[0,1/2]` Q0 theorem. Q0 itself is deterministic and supplies no sample, high-probability event, (2.3)/(2.4), A-prime, `hfamily`, or chain closure.

The public generic theorem `permutationFourierThreshold_exp_eq_rpow` proves,
for `N≥2`, `W>0`, and `log N>0`, the exact identity

```text
exp(-W*(64 sqrt(log N/W))²/256) = (N:ℝ)^(-16:ℝ).
```

Its proof uses `sq_sqrt` for the positive quotient, simplifies the exponent
to `-16 log N`, and applies `rpow_def_of_pos`. The separate generic theorem
`permutationFourierConstraintCount_rpow_bound`, under the natural-number
premise `K≤2N²`, proves

```text
4(K:ℝ)(N:ℝ)^(-16:ℝ) ≤ 8(N:ℝ)^(-14:ℝ) < 1.
```

The strict inequality follows from `N≥2`, hence `N^14≥2^14>8`. No `N=0`
or `N=1` power convention enters the proof.

## Growing-model specialization and nondegeneracy

`permutationFourierExampleGrowThreshold N` is exactly
`64 sqrt(log N/W_N)`. The theorem
`permutationFourierExampleGrow_eventually_exponent_and_premise` proves on a
single eventual set, for the actual `Dims.exampleGrow` and T925 definitions
`T_N=permutationFourierExampleGrowGridCount N` and
`K_N=permutationFourierExampleGrowConstraintCount N`, that

```text
3≤L_N,  2≤W_N,  0<T_N,  0<K_N,  0<r_N,
exp(-W_N*r_N²/256) = N^-16,
4K_N exp(-W_N*r_N²/256) ≤ 8N^-14 < 1,
4(T_N*(W_N-1):ℕ) exp(-W_N*r_N²/256) < 1.
```

The last line has exactly the numerical `hprem` shape of the accepted T914
`exists_permutationFourierSum_norm_lt_of_union_bound`, with the same natural
product cast and strict inequality. The proof gets `K_N≤2N²` and the
nondegenerate `L_N,W_N,T_N,K_N` from T925. Since `L_N≥3`, natural arithmetic
gives `T_N(W_N-1)≤K_N`; multiplying by the positive exponential transfers
the conservative bound to the one-permutation premise. This statement does
not supply T914's bounded table `x`, and its conclusion is only an arithmetic
premise: it constructs neither a permutation nor an `L_N`-tuple of block
permutations.

There are no new axioms or unproved assumptions. The actual growing witness
has eventually `W_N≥2`, `L_N≥3`, and positive `T_N,K_N`, so the theorem is not
made true by an empty mode set, zero grid count, or fixed block count. The
generic hypotheses are simultaneously realized on that same eventual set.

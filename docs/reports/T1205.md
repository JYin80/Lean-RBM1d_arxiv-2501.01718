## Exact theorem and proof

For the general-moving hypotheses of T1201—`|E| < 2`, `60 ≤ D`,
`0 ≤ s_N ≤ t_N < 1`, `c > 0`, `Cond272Reg`, and `deltaWeight ≥ 0`—and each
fixed integer `p ≥ 1`, the theorem
`eventually_intervalIntegrable_normGoodCrossBudget` proves that one eventual
cutoff in `N` works uniformly for every active `k`, every output `a`, and the
whole interval
`[s N, cutNetPt s (targetMesh D) N k]` for the literal
`T1201.normGoodCrossBudget`.

The proof follows T1123's subtype-domination pattern. On each closed cell,
the time × sample function is measurable: it uses the continuous prefix
gradient, the actual `APrimeQVRateTime.measurable_qvAt_on_window` producer,
the strict transition indicator, and the measurable norm-good event, all on
the same `Gauss.Ω Dims.exampleGrow`. Product integration makes the actual
`2p` moment norm measurable in time. The rate retains T1201's exact
`Step2.sigPM` charge, canonical smoothing order, target mesh, moving endpoint,
sample, and strict transition.

For the bound, T617's all-sample estimate gives
`prefixGradient * sqrt(qvAt) ≤ jointEnvelope D N`, and its accepted envelope
lemma gives `jointEnvelope D N ≤ N^(2D+17)` eventually. Multiplication by the
norm-good indicator cannot increase the absolute rate. Since `Gauss.P` is a
probability measure, the `2p` moment norm is at most `N^(2D+17)`. Thus, for
positive `r`, the budget is bounded by a fixed-in-`r` coefficient times
`r^(-1/2)`. The exponent `-1/2 > -1` makes that profile interval-integrable
from `s N ≥ 0`, including when `s N = 0`. At `r=0`, Lean's totalized real
division makes the exact budget zero; the real-power majorant is also zero
there. Endpoint ordering follows from `target_endpoint_mem_window`, and the
argument includes `k=0` and degenerate cells.

The quantifier order is fixed `p`, then eventually `N`, then all active `k`
and outputs. No new assumptions are introduced. The module re-exports
T1185's `nondegenerate_active_k2_witness`, which realizes `E=0`, `D=60`,
`c=1/2`, `s=0`, `t=firstCellT 1`, a positive scheduled `deltaWeight`, and

## Proved statement

The public theorem is
`RBM.centeredVariance_wirtProduct_kernel_bound`. For arbitrary `d : Gauss.Dims`, sequence index
`N`, finite set `s`, Hermitian `H : Matrix (d.Idx N) (d.Idx N) ℂ`, and `z : ι → ℂ` with
`0 < (z i).im` for every `i ∈ s`, it proves

```text
‖∑ a,b centeredVarianceEntry d N a b *
      Gauss.wirtSecond d N (fun K => ((∏ i∈s, (stieltjes K (z i)).im : ℝ) : ℂ)) H a b‖
≤ ∑ i∈s (∏ j∈s.erase i, (stieltjes H (z j)).im) * paperL1Kernel d N H (z i)
  + ∑ i∈s ∑ j∈s.erase i
      (∏ k∈(s.erase i).erase j, (stieltjes H (z k)).im)
        * paperL2Kernel d N H (z i) (z j).
```

All norms remain outside the full signed centered double-sum contractions in the kernel
definitions. The L1 estimate uses the Hermitian resolvent relation to identify the conjugate of
the `(+,+)` contraction with `(-,-)`, then bounds twice the absolute imaginary part by those two
terms of the four-sign kernel. The L2 estimate applies the triangle inequality to the exact
`-1/4` four-sign expression and uses `1/4 ≤ 1`. The remaining factors are nonnegative by the
actual normalized spectral formula and `specWeight_nonneg`; this also covers empty products.

The identity and bound retain the ordered labels `i ∈ s`, `j ∈ s.erase i`; repeated spectral
values are allowed. The theorem covers empty and singleton `s`, and all sequence indices `N`,
including `N = 0`. The source keeps `N` distinct from the physical matrix dimension
`Fintype.card (d.Idx N)` used in the centered covariance and kernels.

## Same-Hermitian nondegeneracy witness

I reuse the witness explicitly checked in the scoped preflight and recorded in the T1436 acceptance:
physical size `M = 4` with `L = 4`, `W = 1`, the single Hermitian matrix
`H = diag(1,0,0,0)`, and every spectral parameter `z = i`. The normalized spectral formula gives
`Im (stieltjes H i) = (1/4)(1/2 + 1 + 1 + 1) = 7/8 > 0`. On this same matrix the single-factor
contraction is `-1/96` and an ordered cross contraction is `1/768`, so both corresponding kernel
families are nonzero (and positive as sums of norms). The corrected total signed contraction is
`-1/64` for two labels and `-35/2048` for three labels. No event or nonzero-integrand premise is
used by the bound, and this deterministic example makes no claim of a Brownian realization.

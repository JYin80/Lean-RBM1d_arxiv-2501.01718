## Mathematical preflight

This agrees with RBM.edgeKer and RBM.edgeKer_eq in RBM1D/Hierarchy/Kernel.lean (definitions/identity at lines 102–117); the source identity uses SᴮΘ, and RBM.Theta_commute_SB in RBM1D/Propagator/Basic.lean (line 128) verifies that this is the same order. In particular, the coefficient is t - s ≥ 0 after rewriting.

SB is the circulant of the nonnegative kernel supported on {0,1,-1} with value 1/3 (RBM1D/Defs/Block.lean, lines 47–53). Theta is the inverse resolvent (RBM1D/Propagator/Basic.lean, line 80), and Theta_eq_tsum there (line 201) supplies its Neumann series for norm less than one. The proof checks all powers of SB have real nonnegative entries by induction; multiplying the kth term by t^k preserves nonnegativity for t ≥ 0. For ‖ξ‖ = 1, the corresponding complex-phase term and real term have equal norm. Summing gives entrywise phase domination for Theta, and convolution by the nonnegative SB gives the same domination for SB * Theta.

The edge identity leaves the diagonal contribution 1[a=b] explicit. When a=b, the triangle inequality bounds |1+v| by 1+|v|; when a≠b, the identity term vanishes. At s=t, the correction is zero and the kernel is the identity, so both cases reduce to the corresponding identity-matrix entry. The endpoint t=0 is included by the Neumann series, whose only nonzero term is the constant term.

## Nondegenerate witness

Take L=3, s=0, t=1/2, ξ=i, and off-diagonal entries a=0, b=1. On ZMod 3, SB = J/3 and (J/3)^2=J/3, so Theta(z)=I + z/(1-z)·J/3. Thus
edgeKer 3 i 0 (1/2) 0 1 = (-1+2i)/15, with norm √5/15 > 0; the unit-charge entry is 1/3 > 0. This is a strict, nonzero instance of the comparison, with all time and phase hypotheses satisfied.

(5.41). Formula (2.72) is the thirtieth-power near-time condition used in the

that factor against both terms in `flowDelta + W⁻¹`, leaving exactly the
terminal `ratR(v)⁻²`.

For T995's schedule,

```text
alpha = zetaCtr + tauG + 2 deltaCap + 2 theta
      = δ/3200 + δ/1600 + 2δ/50 + 2δ/100

```

Since `δ/8 = 80δ/640`, the remaining gap is `41δ/640 > 0`. Thus the fixed
factor `16 capC/5` is eventually absorbed into `N^(δ/8-alpha)`, without
changing the coefficient `(Im m_E)⁻¹` or endpoint normalization. This also
checks the all-cell claim: the proof quantifies after one eventual cutoff over
every `k ≤ cutNetTop`, and its zero-length `k = 0` case is covered by the same
integrability and interval-integral argument.

## Assumptions and nondegeneracy

The public theorem assumes `|E| < 2`, `60 ≤ D`, `0 ≤ s_N ≤ t_N < 1`,
`c > 0`, `Cond272Reg`, and `0 < δ ≤ min(1,c/100)`. These are exactly T1041's
assumptions. The proof introduces no new hypothesis. T995's
`scheduled_positive_cell_witness` is re-exported, preserving its concrete
`E = 0`, `D = 60` positive-length first-cell and same-event positive-weight
witness for an admissible positive `δ`; hence the assumptions have a
nondegenerate joint realization.

## Mathematical audit

- The source uses the actual `Dims.exampleGrow` inhabitant, `s N = 0`, and
  `t N = 1/2`. `qExt_eq_q` removes the clamp on the closed interval.
  `q_eq_inv` then gives `1/(W_N ell_N(0) etaT 0 u)`; `ellHat_zero`
  gives `ell_N(0)=1` and `mE_im` gives `etaT 0 u=1-u`. Thus the
  stated identity `qExt = 1/((W_N:ℝ)*(1-u))` holds for every `N` and
  `u∈[0,1/2]`, including both endpoints.
- `W_N>0` and `0≤u≤1/2` imply `0<1-u≤1`, hence
  `1/W_N≤qExt`. There is no reversal in the reciprocal inequality.
- For each fixed `ζ>0`, natural-cast real power growth yields eventually
  `4√2<N^ζ`. Multiplication by the strictly positive `2/W_N` gives
  `8√2/W_N<N^ζ(2/W_N)≤N^ζ(2qExt)`. The first inequality is strict;
  the second uses nonnegative `N^ζ`. T933 supplies the actual
  permutation Fourier sum's `≤8√2/W_N` error bound. The eventual
  quantifier precedes **all** `u∈[0,1/2]` and **all**
  `π:Equiv.Perm (Fin W_N)`, so the comparison is simultaneous in time
  and permutation, with no time- or permutation-dependent cutoff.
- The width-one witness is genuine: `exampleGrow.W 0=1`, while the
  same actual producer has `qExt(0)=1` and `qExt(1/2)=2`, both positive.
  The time interval and permutation type are nonempty. T933 separately
  proves the width-one half-time Fourier error is exactly `1`; the
  asymptotic theorem makes no claim at `N=0`. `Dims.exampleGrow` also has
  growing width and block count, so the sequence is not a degenerate
  constant-width stand-in.
- The numerical right-hand side matches the source definition of
  `APrimeGeneralMovingCarrierCore.centeredEvent` exactly:
  `N^zetaCtr * (2*qExt E s t N u)`. The theorem's left-hand side is
  a deterministic semicircle quantile Fourier sum, whereas the event's
  left-hand side is a random centered trace. No identification of these
  left-hand sides is present or claimed.

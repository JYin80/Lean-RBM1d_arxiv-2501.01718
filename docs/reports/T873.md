## Paper convention and proof

I extracted PDF page 7 of `paper/250520-YinJun-v2.pdf`, immediately before (2.1). It defines the semicircle Stieltjes transform by `∫ ρ_sc(x)/(x-z) dx`; thus the sign in this theorem is the paper's `(x-z)⁻¹` sign. The measure is the accepted explicit density measure, and the imaginary part is exactly T871's audited scalar Poisson integral. No algebraic `RBM.msc` identity is invoked.

For `b=1-u`, the complex denominator has real part `√u x`, imaginary part `-b`, and squared norm `u x²+b²`. Since `b≥1/2` on the stated interval, its norm is at least `b>0`, and its inverse is continuous and bounded by `b⁻¹`; hence it is integrable against the finite semicircle measure. Its real part is `√u x/(u x²+b²)`. The accepted `semicircleMeasure_map_neg` and oddness show that real integral is zero. Its imaginary part is `b/(u x²+b²)`, whose integral equals one by T871 for `u>0`. `integral_re_add_im` then reconstructs the complex integral as `i`.

The `u=0` branch is a separate constant-integrand proof. The `u=1/2` branch has its own theorem, plus a pointwise witness `g(0)=2i` and a proof that `g(0)≠g(1)`. Thus the latter case is genuinely nonconstant. There are no new hypotheses beyond the displayed interval bounds and no satisfiability issue.

## Statement and proof check

`unusedImages W k π` is precisely `{v : Fin W | ∀ i : Fin W, i < k → π i ≠ v}`. Here `π : PermΩ W` is an actual equivalence of `Fin W`, so the prefix is realizable. The private equivalence `unused_iff_inverse_ge` proves that `v` satisfies this predicate exactly when `k.val ≤ (π.symm v).val`. Its forward direction applies the predicate at the unique preimage of `v`; its reverse direction uses injectivity/inverse identities to rule out an earlier index. `Finset.card_bij` maps each unused `v` to `π.symm v`, with inverse witness `π j` for every index `j ≥ k`. The complementary initial-segment count then gives `(unusedImages W k π).card = W - k.val` for every `W`, `k : Fin W`, and `π`. There is no extra assumption and no empty-premise route to the identity.

The boundary statements match the general theorem. `Fin 0` has no next index; at `W=1,k=0` the count is one; at `W=2,k=0` it is two and at `W=2,k=1` it is one, for every permutation. The explicit identity and swap of `Fin 2` are distinct and have different remaining values after one reveal (`1` and `0` respectively); cardinality one makes those remaining sets singletons. Thus this witness is nonconstant. At the final valid index `k.val+1=W`, the count is one, and `k.isLt` proves the denominator `W-k.val` is positive. The type `k : Fin W` excludes `k=W` and larger values.

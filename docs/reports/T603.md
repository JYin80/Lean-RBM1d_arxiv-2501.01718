## Boundary cases

- `k >= 1` is retained from T590.  The theorem is pointwise on a positive
  cell and includes both closed endpoints `u = s_N` and `u = v_k`.
- `k = 0` is intentionally absent.  Its zero-length contribution belongs only
  to the later time-integrated theorem.
- `p >= 1` is required.  The `p = 0` branch is intentionally absent because
  T599 reads support information from a positive power of the cutoff.
- The last active prefix `k = cutNetTop` is retained.

## Satisfiability witness and its scope

`buffered_absorption_hypotheses_witness` reuses T590's literal
positive-duration hypothesis witness.  If its cap exponent is `deltaCap`, it
sets

```text
deltaWeight = deltaCap / 2,
xi          = deltaCap / 2.
```

Hence `xi > 0` and `deltaWeight + xi = deltaCap > 0`, and every cap-room
inequality transfers by equality.  The witness explicitly includes the fixed
`E=0`, `D=60`, `p=1` inequalities, all regularity and `BoundsCore`
assumptions, and eventual strict positive duration `s_N < t_N`.

Its scope is deliberately limited to the assumptions of the bridge.  It does
**not** claim that the literal common event intersects positive actual-smooth
support on a positive cell: no source theorem proves that intersection is
inhabited.  In particular, T599's `k=0` support witness is not misrepresented
as a positive-cell witness.

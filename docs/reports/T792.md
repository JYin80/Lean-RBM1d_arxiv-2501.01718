## Exact declarations and proof

`measurableAtom_prefixSigma_eq_prefixCell` proves for every `W k : ℕ` and `π : PermΩ W`:

```lean
@measurableAtom (PermΩ W) (prefixSigma W k) π =
  {ρ | ρ ∈ prefixCell W k π}
```

The explicit measurable-space argument ensures that the atom belongs to `prefixSigma W k`, not the ambient full measurable space. T732's measurability of the actual cell and T711's `π ∈ prefixCell W k π` give atom ⊆ cell. For the reverse inclusion, a permutation in the cell agrees with `π` on each revealed coordinate, hence has equivalent membership in every generating coordinate cylinder. Mathlib's `MeasurableSpace.forall_generateFrom_mem_iff_mem_iff` extends this equivalence to every prefix-measurable set. Unfolding `measurableAtom` then puts that permutation in every measurable set containing `π`. No atom identity is assumed in the argument.

The accompanying declarations verify the boundaries: at `k=0` the atom is `Set.univ` for every `W`; for `W=0` it is `Set.univ` at every `k`; for `W≤k` it is `{π}`. At `W=2,k=0`, identity and swap are distinct elements of the same nonsingleton atom and their atoms are equal. At `W=2,k=1`, their atoms differ. There are no new hypotheses; the width-two witness rules out vacuous or ambient-top interpretations.

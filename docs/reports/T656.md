# T656 — corrected-net scratch preflight for the conditional arbitrary-charge QV core

## Primary verdict

**Incomplete, with the smallest missing implication being the profile-preserving
`StochDom -> MomNormDom` bridge under the exact stated window assumptions.**

The provisional T649 and T654 cores remove the two previously identified import barriers, and
the corrected public names are exactly

```text
RBM.meshK
RBM.meshK_pos
RBM.MomentDuhamelCut.netFinset
RBM.MomentDuhamelCut.netFinset_subset_Icc
```

The T650 report's unqualified `RBM.netFinset` and `RBM.netFinset_subset_Icc` names were wrong.

The source stochastic-domination chain now typechecks cleanly, on one sample and one window,
with the exact source profile

```text
A_u^(-4) * eta_u^(-1) * flowR^5.
```

After a `MomNormDom` with that time-dependent profile is supplied, the rest of the desired
positive-length pointwise theorem also compiles: Lyapunov lowers `2P` to `P`, and
`momNorm_Uker_apply_le` produces exactly `R_{u,v}^4`, without an inner `L_N^4` cardinality
loss.  The `v=s_N` branch compiles separately as `intervalIntegral.integral_same` on the same
fixed parameters.

What does **not** follow from the exact T656 card is the intervening `MomNormDom`.  The current
reverse bridge `Gauss.momNormDom_of_stochDom` needs a deterministic polynomial envelope.  The
card assumes only `t_N<1`; it supplies no eventual polynomial lower bound on `eta_{t_N}` and no
equivalent polynomial relative envelope.  The fixed-time resolvent envelope therefore need not
be polynomial in `N`.  This is a genuine analytic obstruction, not a namespace or tactic issue.

There is also a current module-placement obstruction: the needed fixed-time Gaussian
measurability/integrability/envelope leaves are still housed in
`Gauss.MomentDuhamelHypGauss`, whose import closure reaches the forbidden Step-3/Step-45/Step-6
families.  They can be re-proved or extracted cleanly, but doing so does not remove the
endpoint-scale obstruction.

Consequently the exact conditional pointwise QV theorem is **not dispatchable after T649 and
T654 alone**.  No implementation ticket is selected in this report.  The result remains a
conditional implication; no simultaneous nondegenerate same-sample producer for all of its
premises exists.

This report makes no `hEE` production, general QV closure, `hfamily`, `APrimeSlot'`, T230
A-prime, six-step, or paper-closure claim.

  ‖Uker (d.L N) (SumZeroDyn.xi2 E q.1)
    (u : Complex) (v : Complex)

Only `v` is a net point.  The integration variable `u` is continuous on `[s_N,v]` and has no

## Exact remaining obstruction

`Gauss.momNormDom_of_stochDom` requires, in addition to stochastic domination:

```text
measurability;
integrability of every even moment;
positivity and an eventual polynomial lower bound for the control;
a deterministic envelope Env_N;
an eventual polynomial bound Env_N <= N^Kenv.
```

For the desired control

```text
Phi(N,u) = A_u^(-4) eta_u^(-1) flowR^5,
```

positivity is immediate.  Its polynomial lower bound is not the problem: on the current band
regime, `A_u<=N`, `eta_u<=1`, and `flowR>=1` give an eventual lower bound of order `N^-4`.

Measurability and fixed-time integrability are also mathematically available from Gaussian
continuity plus the Hermitian resolvent envelope.  The problem is uniform polynomial growth.
At two charges, `Gauss.norm_eeFun_herm_le` gives, with a window lower imaginary part `eta`,

```text
‖eeFun‖ <= 2 * W_N * L_N * eta^(-6) * W_N^(-5)
         = 2 * L_N * W_N^(-4) * eta^(-6).
```

Uniformly over `u<=t_N`, the available choice is proportional to `eta_{t_N}`.  The exact card
allows, for example,

```text
s_N = 0,
t_N = 1 - exp(-N^2),
```

which satisfies `0<=s_N<=t_N<1` but has `eta_{t_N}` smaller than every fixed negative power
of `N`.  The displayed envelope is then super-polynomial.  No fixed `Kenv` exists.

This is not repaired by the fact that `Phi` itself grows near the endpoint.  The deterministic
envelope-to-profile ratio supplied by the current resolvent estimate still contains an
uncontrolled inverse endpoint scale (at `u=t_N`, it is comparable to an inverse power of
`eta_{t_N}`).  More abstractly, stochastic domination alone never implies fixed moments:
super-polynomial spikes on super-polynomially small events satisfy every fixed polynomial tail
test while their fixed moments may diverge super-polynomially.  A polynomial absolute or
relative envelope is indispensable.

Therefore one of the following would be needed before implementation:

1. add an explicit eventual polynomial endpoint-scale hypothesis, with its exponent fixed
   before `eventually N`; or
2. prove a new polynomial relative envelope for `eeFun/Phi` from the existing exact
   hypotheses.

The repository currently has neither result in a clean upstream module.  Adding (1) would
change the exact claim card and require the project's assumption/delta and nondegenerate
satisfiability review.  This ticket is read-only and does not make that change.

## Implementation-ticket decision and stop conditions

**No implementation ticket is selected.**  The exact target is not dispatchable merely after
independent acceptance of T649 and corrected T654, because the profile-preserving moment bridge
still lacks a polynomial envelope under the stated hypotheses.  Extracting fixed-time leaves
would be useful but would not make the exact theorem provable, so presenting that extraction as
the next QV implementation would overstate the dependency state.

A future implementation ticket may be opened only after one of these stop conditions is
cleared:

1. an accepted theorem supplies a clean polynomial relative envelope from the exact card; or
2. the card is explicitly amended with an endpoint-scale condition, accompanied by a
   nondegenerate same-sample satisfiability witness and any required paper-delta entry.

If cleared, the future writable surface should be one new upstream Gaussian pointwise-QV core
plus its report; its imports should be limited to accepted clean equivalents of

```text
Hierarchy.Step2PPCore
Defs.TimeNetCore
Hierarchy.LKDecayQuant
Gauss.XiLow
Gauss.MomentDuhamelRhs
the extracted fixed-time Gaussian leaves.
```

It must remain forbidden to import `MomentDuhamelHypGauss`, `Step3FlowSharp`, Step 45, any
Step-6 module, FirstCell, any A-prime module, `Step345Producer`, or `Thm221NoEL`.  Required
acceptance would include the new module build, affected consumer builds, a root build, the
exact public axiom audit, arbitrary constant-charge checks, and the separate equal-endpoint
branch.

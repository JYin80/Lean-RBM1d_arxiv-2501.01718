## Paper and accepted producers

I read `paper/250520-YinJun-v2.pdf`, PDF page 7, immediately before (2.1). Its definition is `m_sc(z)=∫_ℝ ρ_sc(x)/(x-z) dx`, with `ρ_sc(x)=√((4-x²)_+)/(2π)`. Thus the target uses the paper's `(x-z)⁻¹` sign. `SemicircleDensityMeasure.lean` defines this density via `Real.sqrt (4-x²)/(2π)` and its `withDensity` measure, proves total mass one, support in `[-2,2]`, and a nontrivial interior mass.

`Semicircle.lean` defines `msc` by choosing one of two algebraic square-root expressions, selecting a root with positive imaginary part. Its public facts `msc_mul z : msc z * (msc z + z) = -1` and `msc_im_pos` on `Im z>0` do **not** themselves identify it with a density integral. `SemicircleStieltjesBridge.lean` does identify the explicit integral with `msc` on the **narrow** flow line `q(u)=i(1-u)/√u`, `0<u≤1/2`, by scaling the independently computed `SemicircleFlowResolvent` density integral. This narrow accepted line is already enough for analytic continuation. `SemicircleStieltjesImagAxis.lean`/T898 is in flight, so I did not use its statements as accepted input, even though its source is present locally.

## Bounded implementation route

Let `U : Set ℂ := {z | 0 < z.im}`, `S(z) := ∫ x, ((x:ℂ)-z)⁻¹ ∂semicircleMeasure`, and `P(z):=S(z)*(S(z)+z)+1`.

1. **Integral and holomorphicity.** Mathlib's `MeasureTheory.resolventTransform semicircleMeasure z` is exactly `S z` after unfolding `resolventTransform`/`resolvent` and rewriting `Ring.inverse_eq_inv`. I typechecked that equality. `MeasureTheory.integrable_resolvent` supplies integrability on `U`; `MeasureTheory.analyticOn_resolventTransform` supplies `AnalyticOn ℂ` on the complement of the image of the measure support. The image lies on the real axis, so `U` is a subset. With `DifferentiableOn.analyticOnNhd` and openness of `U`, this gives `AnalyticOnNhd ℂ S U`. I typechecked the subset, integrability, and analytic restriction. The standard analytic addition/multiplication lemmas then give `AnalyticOnNhd ℂ P U`; this also typechecked.
2. **Zeros with an interior accumulation point.** For every `u∈(0,1/2)`, accepted `semicircleMeasure_stieltjes_flow` gives `S(q(u))=msc(q(u))`; `msc_mul` then gives `P(q(u))=0`. I typechecked this exact polynomial-zero step. The map `q` is continuous at `1/2`, and `1/2 ∈ closure (Ioo 0 (1/2))`, so `q(1/2) ∈ closure (q '' Ioo 0 (1/2))`; both facts typechecked using `fun_prop`, `closure_Ioo`, and `mem_closure_image`. Also `q(u) ≠ q(1/2)` for `u<1/2`: applying `msc_flow_value` to an assumed equality forces `√u=√(1/2)`, hence `u=1/2`. This nonisolation proof typechecked. Consequently `q(1/2)` belongs to the closure of `{z | P z=0} \ {q(1/2)}`. Its imaginary part is positive (`q(1/2)=i/√2`), so it lies **inside** `U`, not at its boundary.
3. **Identity theorem.** `U` is preconnected by `convex_halfSpace_im_gt 0`; this typechecked. Apply the installed `AnalyticOnNhd.eqOn_of_preconnected_of_mem_closure` to `P` and the zero function at `q(1/2)` to obtain `P(z)=0` for every `z∈U`, hence `S(z)*(S(z)+z)=-1`. The identity theorem's exact signature and the analytic/preconnected/closure seams were probed, but the final application was not assembled into a repository theorem in this preflight.
4. **Select the correct root.** For every `z∈U`, `Im ((x-z)⁻¹)=z.im / Complex.normSq ((x:ℂ)-z)>0` for all real `x`. The generic resolvent integrability, `integral_im`, `integral_pos_iff_support_of_nonneg`, and `semicircleMeasure_univ=1` imply `0<(S z).im`; this complete positivity calculation typechecked in scratch. Finally accepted `eq_msc_of_mul_im_pos` applied to `P(z)=0` and this positivity yields `S z=msc z`. No analytic regularity of the arbitrary square-root choice `mscDisc` is needed, and the integral's quadratic equation is derived from the **explicit density integral on an accumulating set**, not assumed circularly.

The exact remaining Lean work is to package these scratch steps in one new `Defs` module, write the closure-set inclusion and identity-theorem application, convert the resulting `EqOn` statement into the quadratic equation, and invoke `eq_msc_of_mul_im_pos`. The target requires no new mathematical hypothesis. A direct all-`z` algebraic integral evaluation would be more work: the accepted flow evaluation is only for zero real spectral part, whereas analytic continuation of the polynomial identity already transfers it to all of `U`.

## Boundaries, normalization, and import graph

The normalization is fixed by the accepted density computation on `q((0,1/2])`. At `u=1/2`, `q(u)=i/√2`, `S(q(u))=i/√2`, and `SemicircleFlowResolvent_half_nonconstant` certifies a genuinely nonconstant kernel; the density measure has mass one and positive mass in `(-1,1)`. This rules out a vacuous zero-measure or wrong-sign branch. The positive imaginary part of `S` excludes the second quadratic root throughout `U`.

The theorem is only for `Im z>0`. The real boundary `Im z=0` is excluded: the kernel can be singular on the measure support, and the cited resolvent-transform holomorphicity theorem is stated off the support. The flow parameter `u=0` is also excluded from `q(u)` because of division by `√u`; the accepted closed-flow `u=0` integral is a separate scaled-kernel endpoint and is not the finite-`z` Stieltjes statement.

The proposed acyclic import path is `Semicircle → SemicircleDensityMeasure → SemicirclePoissonIntegral → SemicircleFlowResolvent → SemicircleStieltjesBridge → new all-z module`, with direct Mathlib imports `MeasureTheory.Measure.ResolventTransform`, `Analysis.Analytic.IsolatedZeros`, and `Analysis.Complex.Convex`. There is no need to import T898 or any Gaussian, local-law, A-prime, quadrature, or Q0 module. This identity alone does not establish equal-mass quadrature, Q0, or any paper closure.

## 2. Exact actual-model modulus, including conditional integration

Write `X=Xmat_N(ω)`, `G_u=(√u X-z_u)⁻¹`, `η_u=Im z_u`, and

\[
 Z_k(u,\omega)=(G_u(k,k)-m)-E_k(G_u(k,k)-m).
\]

`FlowHolder.norm_green_flow_sub_le` proves

\[
 \|G_u-G_v\|\le(\eta_u\eta_v)^{-1}
   \bigl(|\sqrt u-\sqrt v|\,\|X\|+|u-v|\bigr).
\]

One cannot condition this inequality while holding the same `‖X(ω)‖` fixed. The row inside `E_k` is resampled. The exact needed correction is already compiled:

\[
 \int\|X(\operatorname{rowSplit}_k(\omega,\omega'))\|\,dP(\omega')
 \le\|X(\omega)\|+2\|X(\omega)\|^2+5/2.
\]

This is `CondExpMod.integral_norm_Xmat_rowSplit_le`. It follows from the finite row Frobenius bound and the exact Gaussian second moments; `integrable_rowFrobSq` and `integral_rowFrobSq_le` provide the integrability and expectation bound. `norm_condExpDiag_flow_sub_le` then proves, pointwise in the outside sample and simultaneously for all rows,

\[
 \|E_k(G_u(k,k)-m)-E_k(G_v(k,k)-m)\|
 \le(\eta_u\eta_v)^{-1}
 \left[|\sqrt u-\sqrt v|
  (\|X\|+2\|X\|^2+5/2)+|u-v|\right].
\]

Its proof uses the actual row-integrability lemmas before subtracting the two integrals. There is no exchange of a random good-event indicator with conditional expectation.

Thus, for `0≤u,v<1`, the two estimates and `|√u−√v|≤√|u−v|`, `|u−v|≤√|u−v|` give

\[
 |Z_k(u)-Z_k(v)|\le\eta_{t_N}^{-2}
 (2\|X\|+2\|X\|^2+9/2)|u-v|^{1/2}.
\tag{1}
\]

The coefficient `9/2` retains the two spectral-parameter difference terms. It is uniform over `k`.

For both actual families, the weights are time independent, nonnegative, and sum to one:

- row weights `Sblk i k`, with support cardinality `3W`;
- block weights `blkCoef b k`, with support cardinality `W`.

Consequently (1) holds unchanged for the complex weighted average `flucAvg`, and hence for its norm. There is no factor `W`, `L`, `N`, or support size in this modulus. The support sizes matter separately in the fixed-moment proof and label count.

## 3. A conservative exponent ledger

Assume `0≤s_N≤t_N<1`, eventually `η(t_N)≥N^(-K)` for fixed `K≥0`, and let

\[
 \Xi_N=\{\|Xmat_N\|\le N\}.
\]

This is measurable and high probability by `stochDom_norm_Xmat_gauss`. On this event, eventually the coefficient in (1) is at most `N^H`, where `H=2K+3`: for `N≥2`, `2N+2N²+9/2≤7N²`, and absorb `7` by one additional power. The same event works for every row, block, and time; no conditional-row union is necessary.

If the deterministic control `Φ_N(u)=Ψ_N(u)²` satisfies `Φ_N(u)≥N^(-B)`, choose the conservative mesh exponent

\[
 A=2(H+B+1),\qquad\Delta_N\le N^{-A}.
\]

Then interpolation costs at most `N^H Δ_N^(1/2)≤N^(-B-1)≤N⁻¹Φ_N(u)`. The existing net engine has at most `N^(A+1)` points eventually. Actual row labels number `WL≤N` and block labels number `L≤N` eventually. Paying both families together costs at most `N^(A+3)` including a spare factor for two families. Request pointwise failure power `M=D+A+4`, and norm-event failure power `D+1`; the union and norm complement together cost at most `N^(-D)` eventually.

For the usual general deterministic scale `Φ≥W⁻¹≥N⁻¹`, take `B=1`, so `A=4K+10`. For the literal first-cell scale `firstCellPsi²=1/(4W)`, use `B=2` eventually. With the first-cell conservative `K=1`, take `H=5` and **A=16**. Its net and both label families cost at most `N^19`; requesting fixed-time failure `D+20` and norm failure `D+1` suffices. These powers are deliberately nonoptimal.

For completeness, before the Markov consumer the fixed-`2p` estimate has the form

\[
 E|F_{N,u,a}|^{2p}\le C_pN^{8p\theta}\Phi_N(u)^{2p}.
\]

For final tolerance `ε>0`, use node threshold `N^(ε/4)Φ`, choose `0<θ<min(a/2,1,ε/64)`, and take fixed `p` with `p ε/4 > D+A+4`. Markov gives a tail exponent better than `p ε/4` after absorbing the fixed `C_p`, because `ε/2−8θ>3ε/8`. Choose the input exceptional-event powers after this `p` and the polynomial envelopes in the finite-word argument. Only then take eventual `N`; the support constraints `2p≤W` are eventual. One may choose `θ` before `p`, as in T345, since its upper bound here is independent of `p`. The logical order is window/mesh and requested failure power, a small tolerance, a fixed moment order, input exceptional powers, then size. It is never all `p` at one fixed finite size.

## 4. Time-varying Ψ and weights

An arbitrary positive time-dependent `Ψ` is insufficient for the interpolation step. A polynomial lower bound alone gives no comparison between `Ψ(v)` at the nearest net point and `Ψ(u)` at the target time. The exact existing net engine therefore retains `hslow` explicitly. The constant first-cell control satisfies it immediately.

For T331's actual deterministic choice,

\[
 \Phi_N(u)=\frac{\ell_u/\ell_s}{W\ell_u\eta_u}+W^{-1}
          =\frac1{W\ell_s\eta_u}+W^{-1},
\tag{2}
\]

there is a particularly simple comparison: the moving `ell_u` cancels exactly. No derivative through the saturation of `ell_u` is needed. Since `|η_u−η_v|≤|u−v|` and `η_v≥N^(-K)`,

\[
 \Phi_N(v)\le(1+N^K|u-v|)\Phi_N(u).
\]

At mesh `N^(-A)` with `A>K`, this is at most `N^α Φ_N(u)` eventually for every fixed `α>0`. Equation (2) also gives `Φ≥W⁻¹`. The T345 applicability conditions still require the **uniform** upper bound `sqrt Φ≤N^(-a)` and the local-law family at that scale; the time-net argument does not produce these inputs. Fixed multiplicative constants can be enlarged into `Ψ` and absorbed by tolerance, with the same quantitative slack.

Only `Sblk` and `blkCoef` are averaged here. No time-dependent propagator weight is silently added. Other weights would need their own total-variation modulus and envelope.

## 5. Singleton-to-uniform quantifiers and measurability

Here is a valid general-window route using T345 without assuming that its eventual thresholds are uniform over sequences. Suppose every deterministic selector `u_N∈[s_N,t_N]` satisfies its entry-law assumptions with `Ψ_N=Ψ_N(u_N)` and the same fixed `a,K`, with the scale/window bounds uniform in time. This follows, for example, from an ordinary fixed-time entry-law family uniform in time at that scale.

If the resulting row `UnifDomIcc` failed, there would be fixed tolerance and failure power and infinitely many sizes admitting a bad time and row label. Choose one such time at each bad size, and choose `s_N` elsewhere. T345 applied to this single selector gives an eventual bound uniform over its row labels, contradicting those infinitely many bad sizes. The block argument is identical. This is a diagonal selection proof of a uniform eventual threshold; merely writing “for every sequence” and exchanging quantifiers without the contradiction argument would be inadequate. All-size conditions are met by selectors from an all-size admissible nonempty window; finite bad-size parameter choices must be repaired explicitly if a proposed producer assumes window admissibility only eventually.

The common event can be defined as the finite intersection of `Ξ_N` with row/block bounds at the chosen deterministic mesh points. `FlucAvg.measurable_flucAvg` proves node measurability. Finite intersections are measurable. The pointwise modulus then makes this event imply the bounds at all real times. Thus no measurability of a raw uncountable union is needed to construct the event. Alternatively, continuity in time from (1) reduces the supremum to a countable dense set.

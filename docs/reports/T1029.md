# T1029 — deterministic general-moving cross-profile budget

## Result

**PASS for the requested deterministic profile estimate.** The module
`RBM1D/Gauss/APrimeGeneralMovingCrossProfileSlot.lean` proves
`eventually_integral_crossProfile_le_small_slot`, uniformly for every active
target-mesh index `k` and every coordinate argument `a`:

```text
∫_[s_N,v_k] (2√u)⁻¹ · transitionPrefixGradientRate(k)
  · absorbedRootProfile(u,v_k,a)
≤ C_E · N^(5δ/32) · ratR(E,s_N,v_k)^(-2),
```

where `C_E = transitionRootConst * absorbedRootConst / Im(m_E)` is positive
and independent of `N`, `k`, and `a`. The theorem also covers `k = 0`; its
interval has equal endpoints and the transition-prefix rate takes its
inactive value zero.

## Numerical preflight and proof route

For the T995 schedule,
`ζSrc = δ/3200`, `ε = δ/100`, `δWeight = δ/100`,
`δCap = δ/50`, and `τG = δ/1600`. The transition-prefix pointwise estimate
has exponent `(ζSrc + ε)/2 = 33δ/6400`. The absorbed root-profile estimate
has exponent `ζSrc/2 + 3ε/2 = 97δ/6400`. Their product has exponent
`130δ/6400 = 13δ/640`, strictly less than `5δ/32 = 1000δ/6400` for every
`δ > 0`.

The root transport cancellation uses
`ratR(E,s_N,u) = etaT(E,s_N)/etaT(E,u)`. It gives one factor
`etaT(E,s_N)^(-1/2)` from each pointwise bound. The literal time kernel
contributes `(2√u)⁻¹`; its integral is controlled by the proved estimate
`etaT(E,s_N)⁻¹ ∫_[s_N,v_k] (√u)⁻¹ ≤ 2/Im(m_E)`. The factor `1/2` in the
kernel cancels this `2`, leaving exactly the displayed fixed `E` constant.

The profile estimate preserves its endpoint `ratR(E,s_N,v_k)^(-2)`. The
transition cap uses `deltaWeight`; the absorbed profile cap uses `deltaCap`.
The near residual `2W⁻¹`, the buffered `Jbar` caps, both transport factors,
the spatial indicator, the `W⁻ᴰ` leakage, and the absorbed far rows remain in
the pointwise profile definitions and bounds. The eventual power estimates
come from the T995 schedule and the `Cond272Reg` margin
`N^(c/10) ratR(u)^27 ≤ scale(u)`.

The assumptions have a nondegenerate instance: the imported T995 theorem
`scheduled_positive_cell_witness` supplies `E = 0`, `D = 60`, a positive
`Cond272Reg` window, and admissible positive `δ` values. No claim that a
sample belongs to a transition/common-event intersection is made here. This
also does not prove an actual integrated cross term, `N1`, or the A′ assembly.

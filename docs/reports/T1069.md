# T1069 — exact actual-weighted drift integrability for the moving `hGi` slot

## Verdict: mathematical preflight PASS; bounded integrability theorem proved

For each fixed `p ≥ 1`, the literal `hGi` input of
`APrimeDuhamelModel.momFlowDeriv_le` is integrable on the full Gaussian
probability space under the general-moving hypotheses. The proof uses the
actual smooth weight and normalized drift already defined by T615. It is
eventual in `N`, uniform over every active target-mesh cell including `k=0`,
every two-loop output `a`, and every `r` in the closed interval from `s N` to
the cell endpoint.

## Exact consumer and mathematical checks

The consumer at `APrimeDuhamelModel.momFlowDeriv_le` requires exactly

```text
Integrable (fun ω => w ω * |G ω|^(2*p)) (Gauss.P d).
```

The new theorem instantiates `w` with
`APrimeGeneralMovingSmoothDriftNormBudget.weight E D s t deltaWeight p N k`
and `G` with the same file's
`drift E D s t N k a r`. Unfolding these definitions gives the actual
`APrimeSmoothWeightActual.weight` with canonical smoothing order `m`, and the
literal `APrimeDriftTimeFamily.driftAt` at charge `Step2.sigPM`, start `s N`,
target endpoint `cutNetPt s (targetMesh D) N k`, and running time `r`. Thus
the proved integrand is the consumer's `hGi`, without changing its weight,
normalization, endpoint, or exponent.

The regularity and envelope checks are:

- T615's public `measurable_weight` proves measurability of that exact smooth
  weight for every active cell. Its public `weight_nonneg` and `weight_le_one`
  prove `0 ≤ w ≤ 1` pointwise.
- The normalized drift is measurable in `ω`: T615's private proof obtains
  continuity from `Gauss.continuous_uker_driftF_omega` and divides by the
  positive `driftScale`.
- T611's public `eventually_abs_driftAt_le_rpow` supplies the all-sample bound
  `|G| ≤ N^(D+8)` simultaneously for all active `k`, `r ∈ [s N,v]`, `a`, and
  `ω`. It explicitly includes `k=0` and has no event restriction.
- Since `0 ≤ w ≤ 1`, the absolute value of the integrand is bounded by
  `(N^(D+8))^(2*p)`. Measurability and `Integrable.of_bound` give the required
  full-space integrability. The bound is finite-size after the eventual
  cutoff; it introduces no additional probabilistic assumption.

This independently checks the structure of T615's private
`integrable_weighted_drift_pow` argument and its dependence on T611's public
global polynomial envelope. The closed interval is used directly, including
its endpoints; at `k=0` it reduces to the singleton starting time and no
pointwise drift vanishing is assumed.

## Scale and absorption check

For `mesh_N = N^(4D+18)`, `v_k−s_N = k/mesh_N`. On the growing prefix,

```text
v_k − s_N ≤ N^D / N^(4D+18) = N^(-3D-18),
√v_k − √s_N ≤ N^(-3D/2-9).
```

The literal T615 drift has the all-sample envelope `N^(D+8)`, hence

```text
∫_[s_N,v_k] g ≤ N^(D+8) (v_k−s_N) ≤ N^(-2D-10).
```

Integrating T1245 and T1247 with T1207's full positive-time reduction, and
using `etaT(E,s_N)^(-1) ≤ N`, gives the exact cross estimate

```text
∫_[s_N,v_k] positiveTimeCrossBudget
  ≤ (15/2) K N^(13δ/640−3D/2−8) ratR(v_k)^(-2)
    + (15/2) N^(-3D/2−10).
```

The two `N^(-1)` payments are both included: the pointwise T1247 remainder
and T1207's integrated norm-bad payment. No event occupancy assumption is
used. Since `ratR(v_k) ≥ 1`, doubling the sum of the drift and cross bounds
leaves at most

```text
2 N^(-2D-10) + 15 K N^(13δ/640−3D/2−8) + 15 N^(-3D/2−10).
```

For `D ≥ 60` and `δ ≤ 1`, each exponent is at most `−1`. With
`C = 17 + 15K`, the displayed quantity is at most `C/N`. For fixed
`m = Im(mE E) > 0`, the eventual cutoff can enforce `2mC ≤ N`, giving the
consumer's left side at most `1/(2m)`.

For the concrete A′ drift slots, `x=N^(δ/8) ≥ 1`,
`Ξ=slotXi' x=x^(1/4) ≥ 1`, and `q=R²/2`. The near term from
`driftTerm_ge_near`, after division by `R⁴`, is exactly
`x^(5/4)/(2m) ≥ 1/(2m)`. The remaining slot terms are nonnegative. This
proves the required absorption with the actual `R` (and in particular the
proved `R≥1` bound); it introduces no large-`R` assumption.

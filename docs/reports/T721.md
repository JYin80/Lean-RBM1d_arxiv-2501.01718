# T721 — first-image partition of finite permutations

## Files and scope

Added `RBM1D/Gauss/PermutationFiberFirstPartition.lean` and this report. The
module imports only the accepted `PermutationFiberCount` layer, hence reuses
T694's fiber and T703's finite-type instance. No existing source, root import,
task row, or status row was changed.

## Results

For every `W ≥ 1`, arbitrary `p : Fin W → Fin W`, and permutation `π`,
`permutationFirstFiber_mem_iff` identifies membership in the fiber at `k=0`
with `π 0 = v`. Consequently `permutationFirstFiber_existsUnique` identifies
the unique index as `π 0`, and `permutationFirstFiber_disjoint` proves that
two such fibers cannot contain the same permutation unless their indices
coincide. The prescribed prefix is empty, so no consistency assumption on
`p` enters these statements.

`permutationFirstFiberEquiv` gives the explicit bijection

```lean
(Σ v : Fin W, permutationPrefixFiber W ⟨0, hW⟩ p v) ≃
  Equiv.Perm (Fin W)
```

and `permutationFirstFiber_card_sum` proves the exact cardinal identity

```lean
(∑ v : Fin W, Fintype.card (permutationPrefixFiber W ⟨0, hW⟩ p v)) =
  Fintype.card (Equiv.Perm (Fin W)).
```

## Nondegenerate witnesses and boundary

`permutationFirstFiber_one_card` proves the sole `W=1` fiber has cardinality
one for *every* `p : Fin 1 → Fin 1`. `permutationFirstFiber_two_card_pos`
states that, for `W=2` and `p=id`, the fibers at images zero and one both
have positive cardinality; T694 supplies their identity and transposition
inhabitants and T703 supplies their positivity. `W=0` is excluded by
`0 < W`, since it has no first position `k : Fin 0`.

Paper (2.3)–(2.4) are downstream probabilistic local laws. This module proves
only finite counting. It contains no conditional law, concentration/P2,
N/time power, first-cell or shared-event claim.

## Conditional satisfiability

The numerical, regularity, fixed-model, and positive-duration assumptions are
jointly nondegenerate by T603's public
`buffered_absorption_hypotheses_witness`, which in turn uses T590's explicit
positive-cell witness and splits `deltaCap` as

```text
deltaWeight = deltaCap/2,  xi = deltaCap/2.
```

The added implication is conditional on the literal same-sample membership

```text

```

No current upstream theorem proves that `commonEvent ∩ transition` is
inhabited on a positive cell.  This module therefore does not assert such an
inhabitance statement or misrepresent the theorem as unconditionally
nonvacuous.  Establishing same-sample favorable prefix-gradient control and
the later cross interfaces remains separate work.

`numHalfShort_relaxed_witness` use a positive-length first grid cell and
positive error entries; they witness the relaxed **arithmetic shape**, not a
full model producer. T289's `eventually_Fpath_fastDecay_nonempty` gives an
inhabited decay event under its existing `FlowInputs` and `Cond272`; T296's
`eventually_Fpath_fastDecay_forward_nonempty` combines this with a strict
forward window. The growing-band first cell supplies that window and scale
condition. T290's polynomial `xiSum` estimate is samplewise on the actual
flow under `Cond272`. These checks exclude a collapsed-window reading of
the accepted intermediate statements. They do not establish joint
satisfiability or model production of T296's two remaining `≺ 1` inputs,
and they cannot certify either T278 scaled producer family.

`Hierarchy.Decay → Hierarchy.DriftBound → Gauss.Lemma514XiPoly`;
`Gauss.Lemma514NonAlt → Gauss.Lemma514QAssembly` (which also imports
`Lemma514XiPoly`); `Lemma514XiPoly → Lemma514FDecayEvent`;
`Lemma514FDecayEvent + Lemma514QAssembly → Lemma514FBridge`.
The first implication in this shorthand means source dependency, not
that Decay itself imports DriftBound.

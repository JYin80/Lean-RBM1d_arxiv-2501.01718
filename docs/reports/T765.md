## Result

`uniformPerm_prefixCell_full_mass` proves, for every `W k : ℕ`, `W ≤ k`, and `π : PermΩ W`,

```lean
uniformPerm W {ρ | ρ ∈ prefixCell W k π} =
  (1 : ENNReal) / (Nat.factorial W : ENNReal)
```

The proof reduces the cell to `{π}` using T711's `prefixCell_eq_singleton_of_width_le`, applies T736's exact cardinality-to-mass formula, and rewrites `Fintype.card_perm` and `Fintype.card_fin`. The positivity check `Nat.factorial_pos W` covers every width, including `W = 0`, so the denominator is never zero.

`uniformPerm_prefixCell_full_mass_small_widths` records mass one for `W=0` at every `k`, mass one for `W=1` when `1≤k`, and mass one half for `W=2` when `2≤k`. `uniformPerm_prefixCell_full_mass_two_witness` combines T736's time-zero mass-one result and its identity/swap distinct half-cell witness at `k=1`, with the two fully revealed `k=2` half-mass conclusions.

There are no added hypotheses beyond the explicit full-reveal condition `W ≤ k`; no conditional law, first-cell/shared event, P2, or paper-local-law conclusion is asserted. These finite counting statements make no change to paper statements, so no paper delta is needed.

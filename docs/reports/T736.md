## Results

The module imports only the accepted T711 core. For every `W k : ℕ` and `π : PermΩ W`, `uniformPerm_prefixCell_event` proves the exact ENNReal identity

```lean
uniformPerm W {ρ | ρ ∈ prefixCell W k π} =
  (prefixCell W k π).card / Fintype.card (PermΩ W)
```

It unfolds `uniformPerm`, applies Mathlib's `ProbabilityTheory.uniformOn_univ`, and evaluates the counting measure of the coerced finite cell. `uniformPerm_prefixCell_event_pos` combines that formula with T711's realized-cell cardinality positivity and finiteness/nonzeroness of the permutation-space cardinality.

The edge cases are recorded directly: at `k = 0`, every actual-prefix cell has mass one; whenever `W ≤ k`, the cell is `{π}` and has positive mass; when `W = 0`, the unique permutation's cell has mass one for every `k`. For `W = 2`, `k = 1`, `uniformPerm_prefixCell_two_one_witness` computes identity and swap cells as distinct singletons, each with mass `1/2`.

The only hypotheses in the main formula and positivity result are the explicit natural-number parameters and permutation `π`; there are no added assumptions. The witness results above verify the width-zero and two-point cases. No paper delta is needed: these declarations are finite counting facts about T711's measure and cells. They make no claim to prove either local law (2.3) or (2.4) of the paper.

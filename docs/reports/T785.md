## Declarations and proof

`unusedImages W k π` is exactly the finite set of `v : Fin W` satisfying `∀ i : Fin W, i < k → π i ≠ v`; `mem_unusedImages_iff` exposes that predicate. `unusedImages_card` proves, for every `W`, `k : Fin W`, and actual permutation `π : PermΩ W`, that

```lean
(unusedImages W k π).card = W - k.val
```

The proof sends each unused value `v` to its unique preimage `π.symm v`. It proves that `v` is unused exactly when that preimage has index at least `k`, then uses `Finset.card_bij` and the Mathlib count of the complementary initial segment of `Fin W`. No arbitrary prescribed prefix is used; the permutation itself supplies a consistent realized prefix.

`unusedImages_card_pos` derives `0 < W-k.val` from `k.isLt`. `unusedImages_card_last` gives cardinality one at the last valid position `k.val+1=W`. The type `Fin W` excludes `k=W` and larger indices. `no_next_index_zero` proves there is no `k : Fin 0`.

The boundary declarations give count one at `W=1,k=0`, count two at `W=2,k=0`, and count one at `W=2,k=1`, for every permutation of those widths. `unusedImages_two_distinct_samples` proves the identity and swap on `Fin 2` are distinct, that their remaining images after one reveal are respectively `1` and `0`, and that both realize the stated counts at `k=0` and `k=1`. Thus the width-two case is genuinely nonconstant.

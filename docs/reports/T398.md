# T398 — all-running-time first-cell near-`eG` mathematical gate

## Verdict

The actual raw-three law, raw-four/raw-six laws, and both-charge averaged one-loop estimate **can be put on one measurable high-probability event for every real running time in the first cell**. They do not yet close the all-time `N^ν` near-`eGpm` profile of (5.35): the first missing real producer is an **unconditional all-running-time bound for the actual `jG(u,ω)`** strong enough to absorb the far-internal residual in T357/T364. The available `jG≤N` theorem is only at `firstTime=N⁻²⁴⁸`; T388's running cap is conditional on a positive active prefix weight. Neither is a bound on every time and every sample of the common event. This is a mathematical gate, not a Lean elaboration issue, and the conditional event must not be advertised as an all-time closure.

## Endpoints, geometry, and losses

`firstCellS τ' N=0` exactly and `firstCellT τ' N≤1/2` for every `N`; the latter is eventually `1/2`, **not** `firstTime`. Thus throughout the closed first cell, `η_u∈[1/2,1]`, `1≤ell_u≤√2`, `A_u≥W/2`, and `A_u≤W≤N` eventually. Also `ell_0=1`, `r_u≥1`, `η_u⁻¹≤2≤N`, `L≤N`, and `N≤W²`; the fixed `D=60` log threshold eventually holds. The source bound has no singularity at either endpoint. At `u=0`, `Hflow_zero` and `DetAvgIBPTimeNet.trace_at_zero` give an exactly zero centered true-charge trace; T372 gives the false charge as well. Hence `EGDef.norm_eGpm_le` with zero trace gives `eGpm=0` there. Raw laws and T384's source event also include `u=0` by their `TimeIcc` quantifier.

For actual output labels `(a₁,a₂)`, `EGDef.norm_eGpm_le` writes the three-loop as `[false,true,true]` at `[a₂,b,a₁]`. Instantiate the abstract T364 near split with `(a₂,a₁)`. No far-output assertion follows from this branch. The latter deterministic bound supplies


Without a cap on `J_u`, T364's unabsorbed `eG_near_le_scaled` yields, on `G_N` and at every running time, the principal near term

```text
2 N^(ζ₁+ζ₃) η_u⁻¹ r_u³ cNear(W,ell_u) T_{u,60}(d(a₂,a₁))
```

plus the exact nonnegative residual

```text

  [η_u⁻¹ J_u T_{u,60}(ellStarStar_u-ellStar_u)].
```

Both are multiplied by the near-output indicator. This accounts for the `L` sum, both occurrences of `η_u⁻¹` before comparison with `nearRate`, and the `W⁻⁶⁰` far-internal tail. The `cNear=(2(log W)^3+2/ell_u) exp((log W)^(3/4))` factor is uniformly subpolynomial since `ell_u≥1` and `W≤N`; T393's numerical argument extends uniformly from `sourceTime` by replacing its pointwise `ell_u≥1` with this first-cell fact. With `ζ₁=ζ₃=min(ν/8,1/2)`, the principal coefficient and fixed factor `2` fit into `N^ν` with strict slack. They are not the obstruction.

T357's `residual_ratio_eq` exposes the unsuppressed linear factor `L J_u/(ell_u η_u r_u²)` after dividing by `nearRate=η_u⁻¹r_u³T_{u,60}(d)`. Its `residual_ratio_le_W` obtains `4W^(11+2ζ₁-60)` **using `J_u≤N`** (and `L≤N`, `A_u≤N`, `η_u⁻¹≤N`, `N≤W²`). If `J_u` is left free, the same power count carries the extra multiplier `J_u/N`. The `W⁻⁶⁰` tail alone cannot cancel an unspecified `J_u`; choosing a larger floor exponent would also enlarge the definition of `J_u`. The missing input is precisely a real all-time estimate on this actual `J_u` (the simple sufficient form is `J_u≤N`), independent of the desired near drift. T389/T393 provide it only at `N⁻²⁴⁸`; `APrimeSupportRunning.eventually_jG_le_eighth_on_support` provides it only on positive active support.

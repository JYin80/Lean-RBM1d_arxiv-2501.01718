uniformly over every positive active `k`, every `σ : Fin 2 → Bool`, every

```text
ω ∈ commonEvent N ∩ transition N k
  ⟹ qvAt d E D N σ a (s N) v_k r ω
      ≤ (absorbedRootProfile E s zetaSrc N r v_k D
```

side would also suffice. Its proof must account for the `σ` dependence of
`coordAt` and `qvAt`; the existing one-loop norm identity is insufficient.

bound, use the empty `k=0` transition, and check the two consumer fields on

claim nonemptiness of `commonEvent ∩ transition` without a separate witness.

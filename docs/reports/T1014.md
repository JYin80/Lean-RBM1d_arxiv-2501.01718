## Exact target and independent mathematical check

The target is an eventual bound, uniformly for every active `k`, on

```text
∫_[s_N,v_k] xiK(L_N,W_N,Im m_E) R_u^(-2) R_v^(-2)
    exp(1) jSCap(u)^2 [36 eta_u^(-1) A_u^(-1) + W_N L_N W_N^(-D)] du,
```

where `v_k = cutNetPt s (targetMesh D) N k`, `R_u = eta_s/eta_u`,
`A_u = W_N ell_u eta_u`, and the *actual Step2* cap is
`jSCap(u) = (4 exp(1)+2) N^(2 deltaCap) R_u^4`.
The desired right side has a constant depending on fixed `E`, times
`N^(5δ/32) R_v^(-2)`, under `|E|<2`, `D≥60`, `Cond272Reg`, and the T995
schedule (`deltaCap=δ/50`, `0<δ≤min(1,c/100)`).

I compared `normalizedQuadratic` with `quadCap` and `profile` in
`APrimeGeneralMovingSmoothDriftNormBudget.lean`: it preserves `xiK`, both
`R^(-2)` factors, the exact `jSCap`, and **both** positive bracket terms.
Squaring the cap and combining with `R_u^(-2)` yields `R_u^6` and the
loss `N^(4δ/50)`. For the inverse-scale term, the accepted
`Cond272.pow_thirty_le` gives `R_u^30≤A_u` on the whole interval, hence
`R_u^6/A_u≤R_u^(-24)`. Since `eta_u=(1-u) Im m_E`, the exact integral is
`(1-R_v^(-24))/(24 Im m_E)≤1/(24 Im m_E)`. The factor `R_v^(-2)` stays
outside. For leakage, `R_u≤R_v`, `R_v^30≤A_v≤N`, `W_N L_N≤N`, and
`W_N≥N^(5/8)` eventually give, for `D≥60`,
`W_N L_N W_N^(-D) R_u^6 ≤ N^(1+1/5-75/2)=N^(-363/10)`;
the interval length is at most one. These are valid fixed-`E` preflight
estimates, subject to the usual eventual hypotheses just listed.
The `xiK` allowance `N^(δ/100)` and cap loss total `9δ/100`, leaving
the strict exponent gap `5δ/32-9δ/100=53δ/800>0`. There is no hidden
replacement of `jSCap` by the different `blockCap`.

At `k=0`, `cutNetPt_zero` makes `v_0=s_N`, so the proposed interval
integral is zero. The T995 `scheduled_positive_cell_witness` is a genuine
same-event witness at `E=0,D=60,p=1,k=1`, with positive actual smooth
weight; T1013 re-exports it without adding an assumption. It supports
satisfiability of the cap event, not the missing integrated theorem.

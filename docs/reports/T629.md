## Claim card: mathematics that must remain unchanged

The eventual regularity theorem remains exactly the T622 target.  Fix
`p : ℕ` with `1 ≤ p` **before** the eventual `N` quantifier.  Then, eventually
in `N`, quantify over every active target-mesh prefix `k`, every
`sigma : Fin 2 → Bool`, every output `a : LoopArg (d.L N) 2`, every
`r ∈ Icc (s N) v_k`, and the identical Gaussian sample in both fields.  The
conclusion consists of measurability and integrability of

```text
Y = cutoff * ‖coordAt ... (Hflow ... omega)‖,
Z = jointRate = 1_transition * prefixGradient * sqrt(qvAt),
Y^(2p), Z^(2p), and Y^(2p-1) Z.
```

Both closed time endpoints remain included.  At `k=0`, the exact conclusion is
`jointRate = 0`; the instantaneous `qvAt` is not asserted to vanish.  The mixed
field uses only

```text
x^(2p-1) * y ≤ x^(2p) + y^(2p),  x,y ≥ 0,  p ≥ 1.
```

There is no event-inhabitance assumption, no assertion that
`commonEvent ∩ transition` is inhabited, and no cross budget.  The
`2D+16` exponent belongs only to the event-free polynomial envelope; the
separate `2D+17` complement-payment adapter does not enter the five fields.

# T1087 — selected-high-q Gaussian model preflight

## Verdict: preflight FAIL to establish the exact producer

This is a proof-obligation failure, not a counterexample to the desired bound. I created no Lean module and did not alter T999, T995, the root imports, or shared project files.

## Normalized claim

For the admissible moving-window data and `mesh = APrimeGeneralMovingMesh.targetMesh D` (`D ≥ 60`), the missing premise of T999 is the following: for each fixed `0 < δ ≤ δ₀` and each `p ≥ 1`, choose `q ≥ p` and `C > 0` before choosing the eventual threshold in `N`, such that eventually, uniformly for every active `k ≤ cutNetTop`,

```text
∫ widenedW (canonicalR) 1 jSnorm s t mesh δ q N k ω
    * |cutTrunc (N^(2δ)) (jSnorm at cutNetPt k)|^(2q) d(Gauss.band d).P
  ≤ C * N^(δq/2).
```

The integrand, quantifier order, and exponent above are exactly T999's `hHigh` (`APrimeGeneralMovingLowMomentTransfer.lean:281–292`). In particular, `q` may depend on `δ,p`, but not on the eventual `N`; `C` may depend on the selected data; and the bound includes `k = 0`.

## Decisive checks

1. **The widened weight does not cap the endpoint.** `prefixSoftW` is the soft maximum over `Finset.range k`, hence over `j < k` (`APrimeWeight.lean:26–29`). On the active branch `widenedW` is this prefix cutoff at threshold `2e·N^(2δ)`, raised to `2q` (`:101–106`). At `k = 0` the prefix is empty and the weight equals one. Thus neither the definition nor positivity of the weight bounds the endpoint `jSnorm` at `k`; in particular, the zeroth index needs its own endpoint estimate.

2. **Available deterministic bounds miss the requested rate.** T999 proves integrability using the truncation cap and `0 ≤ widenedW ≤ 1` (`APrimeGeneralMovingLowMomentTransfer.lean:323–339`), but no rate. Those facts give at most

   ```text
   ∫ widenedW * |cutTrunc (N^(2δ)) jSnorm_k|^(2q)
     ≤ 2^(2q) * N^(4δq),
   ```

   since `jSnorm ≥ 0` and `|cutTrunc θ x| ≤ 2θ`. The target exponent is `δq/2`; the gap is `7δq/2 > 0`. The support propagation result only applies for `k ≥ 1`, on the good norm event, and gives an `O(N^(2δ))` running cap (`APrimeGeneralMovingPrefixSupport.lean:26–44`), still at the wrong scale and with no result at `k = 0`.

3. **The high-order coordinate input is absent.** `APrimeInit.integral_cutTrunc_le_family_high` can turn suitable weighted coordinate moments into the target family exponent, provided `32 ≤ 3δq` (`APrimeInit.lean:396–419`). Selecting such a `q` after fixing `δ,p` is compatible with T999's quantifiers. But the needed coordinate moment bound is itself a new model estimate: for the same order-`q` widened prefix weight, it must bound each endpoint coordinate moment at scale `N^(5δ/32)` uniformly over active net indices. The reviewed general-moving producers provide initial-slot moments (`APrimeGeneralMovingInitialHinit.lean:180–206`), time-integrated evolved-QV profiles (`APrimeGeneralMovingQVNormBudget.lean:479–508`), integrability, or deterministic endpoint-to-coordinate reductions; none supplies those weighted endpoint coordinate moments for all `k`. T999/T1000 expressly leave the selected-high-q Gaussian input open.

4. **T995 does not witness the moment bound.** Its loss schedule is chosen after `δ`, and its `scheduled_positive_cell_witness` supplies a genuine `E=0`, `D=60` positive-length window and a same-event `k=1` resident with positive actual smooth weight (`APrimeGeneralMovingSlotLossSchedule.lean:27–114`). This checks nondegeneracy of the model context, but gives no integrated high-order estimate. It cannot fill `hHigh` or handle every active `k`.

5. **The cited paper formulas have a different scope.** I checked the rendered sole source, `paper/250520-YinJun-v2.pdf`, printed pp. 54 and 58. Formula (5.21) is the stopped integrated loop-hierarchy identity, including its stochastic-integral term. Formula (5.44) bounds the time-integrated quadratic variation of that stopped martingale term. The surrounding argument then uses (5.24), the stopping time (5.43), and the later net/stopping steps. Neither (5.21) nor (5.44) states the T999 static Gaussian expectation with an order-dependent widened prefix weight and a separately truncated endpoint. No derivation of that exact expectation from these formulas is supplied by the paper.

## Exact remaining implication

For a selected fixed `q ≥ p`, prove a Gaussian-model estimate that improves the available `N^(4δq)` truncation-envelope bound to `C N^(δq/2)`, with the literal order-`q` prefix weight, the literal `cutTrunc (N^(2δ))` endpoint, and uniformity over all active target-mesh indices including `k=0`. The prefix-support estimate and the T995 same-resident witness do not provide this implication. I found no valid proof route from the inspected T999/T995 producers or the paper formulas, so I stop here rather than replacing it by a weaker exponent, a `q=p` premise, or a conditional/event-empty substitute.

## Result

`Step2.eLL L W (Step2.lk (sample d) 0 N r ω) ![a₁,a₂]`

and proves its pointwise bound directly from `Step2.norm_eLL_le`:

`‖quadGlue_r(a₁,a₂)‖ ≤ exp(1) J_r²
  (36 η_r⁻¹ A_r⁻¹ + W L W⁻⁶⁰) T_{r,60}(d(a₂,a₁))`,

where `J_r` is literally `Step2.jS (sample d) 0 60 N r ω` and
`A_r = W ell_r eta_r`.  The change from `d(a₁,a₂)` in `norm_eLL_le` to
`d(a₂,a₁)` uses only symmetry of cyclic distance.  No bound is imposed on
`J_r`, and the `W⁻⁶⁰` floor is unchanged.

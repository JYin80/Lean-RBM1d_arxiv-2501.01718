Its own `hEnvI` and all-order `hMψI` therefore hold directly. The missing input is a **single common** `ψ` covering the initial, drift, commutator and dot envelopes at all times. The first smaller formal target is the actual `Fpath` time-zero identity (T383); interior moments, size and the joint event remain open. ## Verdict and scope

**No complete Gaussian-model joint witness is presently proved.** T354 proves the deterministic, positive-error weighted inequalities conditional on uniform model budgets. The `'''` Q and short producers still take the actual event, envelope, and moment rows as hypotheses. In particular, the first growth-grid cell has no proved model-level `hMψI` for a concrete common envelope `ψ`; neither the arbitrary-`Φ` drift row nor the `E ⊗ E` row may be replaced by a bound with control `1`. This is an unfilled producer, not a counterexample to the paper's (5.92) or (7.16).

This is a read-only audit at fixed `m=n+2`, using (5.92) and (7.16) of `paper/250520-YinJun-v2.pdf`, the actual declaration signatures, and T354's compiled report. Only this report was written. No Lean, shared/root file, or Git state was changed.

Write `A_u=B.scale E N u`, `ρ_u=(etaT E u)⁻¹`, `I=∫_s^v ρ_u du`, and `c=√Λ+Φ`. Both weighted producers require, for **every** `ε>0` and `p≥1`, a positive constant independent of `N,u,q`, then an eventual bound uniform over every loop label `q` and every `u∈[s_N,v_N]`. Their `ψ`/`ψE` are initially *parameters*, not identified Gaussian observables. One must choose them so that all pointwise envelope rows and all moment rows hold simultaneously. `Lemma514Premises` (`Lemma514Moment.lean:388`) is also a four-field hypothesis; it does not supply its own fields or assert `Φ≺1`.

## Window, quantifier order, and circularity

**First cell.** T354's `positive_first_cell_numeric_witness` (`Lemma514HnumBudget.lean:1074`) uses the genuine interval `[0,1/2]`, a positive inverse-time integral, and actual positive `errBudget`; it is a deterministic satisfiability witness at fixed size. It is **not** a growing-Gaussian model witness. The `δ′≥1` example cannot pay `A_v^{2m}δ′` as `A_v→∞`. `F_stochDom` and the Q reconstruction `pHalf514_of_wardP` route (documented at `Lemma514QAssembly.lean:61–71`) require `0<s_N`; these declarations cannot be applied at `s_N=0`. `B.scale_pos'` (`Flow/Hypotheses.lean:207`) is available with `0≤s_N`, so the strict-start condition in the drift lemma appears repairable, but no such new declaration is asserted here. The first missing *actual-model* leaf in the weighted RHS is a concrete `ψ` and proof of `hEnvI` plus the all-`p`, all-`q` `hMψI` at `s=0`, using the first-cell BoundsCore input and a proved polynomial all-sample envelope.

**Later cells.** For adjacent `s_k=1-W^{-kτ′}`, `v_k=s_{k+1}`, T354 proves `(1-s_k)/(1-v_k)=W^{τ′}` and `I_k≤N^{η/8}` uniformly in `k` (`gridS_adjacent_ratio_eq`, `eventually_gridS_integral_le_rpow`). At fixed finite `k≤k₀`, `(1-v_k)⁻¹≤N^{(k₀+1)τ′}` if `W≤N`; hence a uniform polynomial size exponent is plausible. The initial BoundsCore bound at `s_{k+1}` must come from the previously closed cell. Using the **same cell's** desired Lemma 5.14 output, or `A′`, to prove its `ψ` moments would be circular. The existing `psiF_stochDom_etaT_inv_of_drift_inputs''` avoids the old top-length additive use, but its `xiRhs≺1` input is not the general `Φ` premise. No induction over all cells with all `'''` inputs is presently compiled.

**Order of choices.** Fix `m`, a finite last cell `k₀`, grid step `τ′`, and target tolerance/decay. Pick the kernel exponent `θ>0` sufficiently small for both lengths `m,2m`; then pick the event and raw-error exponents `D` (and the exponential-tail cutoff) after the resulting fixed polynomial costs and the moment order `p`; then pass to large `N`. T354's `q_decay_exponents_exist`, `short_decay_exponent_exists`, `eventually_q_coefficient_le_rpow`, and normalized-error theorems implement the deterministic part of this order. A single fixed `D` cannot serve arbitrary `m,p` and decay demands. For a given `ε,p,D`, only finitely many cell/loop event families need intersection; `C` and the eventual threshold may depend on `ε,p,m,k₀`, while the resulting bounds remain uniform in `u,q,k`. A single event asserted for *all* `p` is neither provided nor required by the stated moment quantifiers.

The paper's (5.92) concludes with `√Λ+Φ` and no extra `η_s/η_v` factor. Its (7.16) has a `W^{C_mτ}` kernel loss and `W^{-D+C_m}` error. T354 absorbs those fixed losses after `θ` and `D` are selected; it does not make the random moment and event inputs true. In particular, the factor `(2n+3)Φ` is retained in the drift envelope, with its fixed coefficient absorbed into `C_m`; treating it as `1` would change the theorem.

## Routing decision

**Reroute to the first missing model producer; do not open an assembly ticket yet.** The next mathematical obligation is to identify one actual Gaussian `ψ` at the first cell and prove both the projected initial envelope `hEnvI` and the full weighted `hMψI` quantifier (`∀ε>0 ∀p≥1 ∃C>0 ∀ᶠ N ∀q`) from `Bounds_zero`/`BoundsCore.LmK` plus explicit measurability, integrability, and an all-sample polynomial bound. This must preserve the eventual `Φ` budget for the subsequent drift extension. A narrowly disjoint primed `F_stochDom` with `0≤s_N` may then repair its separate first-cell signature, but alone it does not furnish the required moments or common event. Until the initial producer is proved, naming a Lean ticket that claims the whole model-level `hnum` interface would merely repackage missing hypotheses.

## Dependencies and limitations

The proof depends on the actual T579 common event/HighProb producer, the
actual T584 positive-support profile, `APrimeQVRateTime`,
`APrimeQVGlobalPoly`, the neutral weighted event-split helper, and deterministic
integration.  The dependency on T584 is literal; T584 was present during this
work, but scheduler acceptance was still pending as specified by the ticket.

This module does **not** use or claim stopping/BDG, a martingale terminal-time
net, `hfamily`, `WeightedMoment`, `APrimeSlot'`, the six-step chain, or paper
closure.

The unresolved support mismatch from T587 remains unchanged: the compiled
comparison is

```text
widenedW <= APrimeSmoothWeightActual.weight,
```

not the reverse.  Therefore positive smooth differentiable weight does not
imply positive widened support.  This QV budget alone cannot feed the current
smooth-weight Duhamel generator on its whole support; a support bridge or a
different differentiable localization is still required.

For fixed `p ≥ 1`, `δ`, `E`, `D`, `c`, `s`, and `t`, T1207 gives, eventually
in `N` and uniformly over active target-mesh cells `k` and outputs `a`,

```text
∫_(s N)^v positiveTimeCrossBudget
  ≤ ∫_(s N)^v normGoodCrossBudget
      + (15 p / 4) N⁻¹ (√v − √(s N)),
```

with `v = cutNetPt s (targetMesh D) N k`. Its coefficient is the exact
integral of T1207's `(15p/8) N⁻¹ r⁻¹/²` payment. T1215 bounds the matching
literal norm-good integral by `Cgood N^(5δ/32) ratR(v)^(-2)` for the same
fixed `p`, schedule, mesh, all active cells, and outputs. T1215 chooses
`Cgood` before its eventual cutoff; the new theorem sets
`C = Cgood + 15p/4`, so the constant is selected before `∀ᶠ N` and can
depend on the fixed parameters and `p`, but not on `N`, `k`, or `a`.

The endpoint is in `[s N,t N]` and `t N < 1`, so
`0 ≤ √v − √(s N) ≤ 1`. Cond272Reg gives `ratR(v)^30 ≤ scale(E,N,v)`;
the band scale estimate gives `scale(E,N,v) ≤ N`. Also `ratR(v) ≥ 1`.
Thus `ratR(v)^2 ≤ ratR(v)^30 ≤ N`, hence `N⁻¹ ≤ ratR(v)^(-2)`.
Since `N ≥ 1` and `δ > 0`, `N^(5δ/32) ≥ 1`. Therefore the full payment
is at most `(15p/4) N^(5δ/32) ratR(v)^(-2)`, which adds to the T1215
bound with the stated endpoint factor intact.

The case `k=0` is included by T1215's exact zero-rate/zero-integral case.
The interval begins at `s N`, which may equal zero; T1207's payment is
integrated as an inverse-square-root envelope and is integrable there. The
proof also covers degenerate cells through the accepted interval-integral
identities.

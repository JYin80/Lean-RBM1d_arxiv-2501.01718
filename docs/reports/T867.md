## Result and proof

`semicircleMeasure_cdf_continuous` proves

```lean
Continuous (ProbabilityTheory.cdf semicircleMeasure)
```

for every real input, with no support restriction. For each `x`, the accepted
`semicircleMeasure_singleton x = 0` is transported through
`ProbabilityTheory.measure_cdf` and `StieltjesFunction.measure_singleton` to
show that the left limit of the CDF equals its value at `x`: the atom formula
gives `ENNReal.ofReal (cdf x - leftLim cdf x) = 0`, while monotonicity gives
`leftLim cdf x ≤ cdf x`. The monotone-function left-continuity criterion then
provides continuity from the left; the Stieltjes function's right-continuity
provides continuity from the right. The two-sided criterion gives continuity
at `x`. This argument applies uniformly at `x = -2`, `0`, and `2` as well as
outside the support.

The result is nonconstant in a concrete sense: the accepted theorem
`semicircleMeasure_nontrivial` gives
`0 < semicircleMeasure (Ioo (-1 : ℝ) 1)`. Thus the probability measure has
positive mass on an explicit interval, rather than the continuity claim
describing a vacuous or zero measure.

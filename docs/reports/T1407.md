# T1407 — finite-Hermitian Stieltjes product and collision split

**Result: PASS for the deterministic first bridge only.** This report proves the finite-Hermitian spectral expansion of a product of Stieltjes imaginary parts and partitions its eigenvalue tuples into the injective tuples appearing in `corrPairing` and the noninjective collision tuples. It does not prove a collision estimate, smoothing, GreenComparison, or Theorem 2.6.

The new theorem assumes only a finite index type, a Hermitian matrix, and positive broadenings. It is a pointwise deterministic identity and does not assert a probability law or event. This witness is not a Gaussian or OU-flow producer and makes no such claim.

## Declarations and proof content

`RBM1D/Flow/GreenComparisonSpectralProduct.lean` provides:

- `RBM.stieltjes_im_product_eq_normalized_spectral_sum`: for `k` factors, the product is `(card n)^(-k)` times the sum over all functions `Fin k → n` of the product of the corresponding factors `η_i / ((λ_(f i)-E_i)^2 + η_i^2)`. This includes repeated eigenvalue indices.
- `RBM.injectiveSpectralProductSum`: the explicit sum over injective tuples, matching the tuple index of `corrPairing`.
- `RBM.collisionSpectralProductSum`: the explicit sum over noninjective tuples.
- `RBM.spectralProductSum_eq_injective_add_collision`: the exact disjoint partition of the full finite sum.
- `RBM.stieltjes_im_product_eq_injective_add_collision`: the normalized product identity with both contributions displayed separately.
- `RBM.spectralProduct_injective_and_collision_positive_example`: positivity of both sums on the same zero `2 × 2` Hermitian matrix, with `k=2`, `E_i=0`, and `η_i=1`.

The product formula follows by applying the accepted one-factor identity to each coordinate, distributing the finite product over finite sums, and identifying the resulting sum with the sum over functions. The split is a finite-set filter partition by injectivity. No comparison bound is assumed.

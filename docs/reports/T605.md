# T605 — Post-T600 initial-flow seam audit

## Primary verdict

**Verdict: incomplete, with one smallest missing implication.**

T600 is correct as written and has the exact weight, endpoint, charge,
normalization, quantifier order, and initial-slot powers needed by a future
general-moving Minkowski consumer.  However, its observable
`APrimeAssembly.initialEvolvedNormAt` is **not definitionally equal** to that
consumer's left-endpoint observable

```text
APrimeDuhamelModel.flowY d N
  (APrimeDriftTimeFamily.coordAt d E D N Step2.sigPM a (s N) v) (s N).
```

The mismatch is only the already-proved semantic reduction of
`Gauss.ukerObsT` along `Gauss.Hflow`; after rewriting once with
`APrimeDriftTimeFamily.flowY_coordAt`, all remaining terms are definitionally
identical.  Thus no new estimate or power absorption is missing.  The single
smallest dispatchable addition is the function equality in §7 below.

This audit does **not** supply a drift, cross, or QV budget, a Minkowski
assembly, a coordinate-family estimate, `hfamily`, `APrimeSlot'`, A-prime, the
six-step chain, or paper closure.

## 1. Normalized claim and exact consumer seam

Fix the concrete Gaussian model `d = Gauss.Dims.exampleGrow`, bulk energy
`|E| < 2`, a moving window `s N ≤ t N < 1`, `D ≥ 60`, `δ > 0`, and a fixed
moment order `p ≥ 1`.  For

```text
v_{N,k} = cutNetPt s (APrimeGeneralMovingMesh.targetMesh D) N k,
```

with `k ≤ cutNetTop ...`, T600 proves, under the T488 regularity and initial
`BoundsCore` hypotheses,

```text
∀ᶠ N, ∀ k, ∀ hk : k ≤ cutNetTop ..., ∀ a,
  momNormW P actualSmoothWeight_{N,k,p} p
    (initialEvolvedNormAt sample E D s N v_{N,k} a)
  ≤ initTerm (N^(δ/8)) R_{v_{N,k}} (slotXi' (N^(δ/8))) / R_{v_{N,k}}^4,
```

where

```text
R_v = etaT E (s N) / etaT E v.
```

The future weighted Minkowski input has the same left time `s N`, endpoint
`v`, weight, moment order, and coordinate `a`, but writes the random function
as

```text
flowY d N (coordAt d E D N Step2.sigPM a (s N) v) (s N).
```

T600 therefore becomes the exact `hinit` premise after a single rewrite by
the connector in §7.

## 3. Exact object identification

The relevant definitions are:

```text
APrimeAssembly.initialEvolvedNormAt ... v a ω
 = ‖Uker ... (s N) v
       (Step2.lk (sample d) E N (s N) ω) a‖
   / (Step2.tT ... D v dist * (etaT E (s N) / etaT E v)^4).

 = Step2.tT ... D v dist * (etaT E (s N) / etaT E v)^4.
```

`flowY_coordAt`, specialized to `r = s N`, gives

```text
flowY d N (coordAt d E D N σ a (s N) v) (s N) ω
 = ‖Uker ... (s N) v
       (SumZeroDyn.lkT (sample d) E N (s N) ω σ) a‖
   / driftScale d E D N a (s N) v.
```

At `σ = Step2.sigPM`:

- `Step2.lk X E N r ω` is definitionally
  `SumZeroDyn.lkT X E N r ω Step2.sigPM`;
- both sides use the charge
  `xiOf (mSigma E) Step2.sigPM` in `Uker`; no charge rewrite or appeal to
  `Step2.sigPM_xi` is needed;
- `driftScale` is definitionally the exact denominator of
  `initialEvolvedNormAt`;
- the subtype coercion `v : TimeIcc s t N` is the same real endpoint on both
  sides.

These definitional checks compiled by `rfl`.  The whole equality did
not: a bare `rfl` failed because `flowY` still contains `coordAt`'s

uses `Gauss.ukerObsT_flow`; the remaining goal then closes by `rfl`.

## 5. Weight, quantifier, power, and integrability audit

### Weight

T600 uses the literal weight

```text
APrimeSmoothWeightActual.weight d E D δ s t (targetMesh D)
  2 p N k (canonicalM d s t (targetMesh D) N).
```

It applies T488 once with constant weight `1`, before choosing `N,k,a`, and
then uses the global pointwise facts `weight_nonneg` and `weight_le_one`.
The comparison does not use positivity of the weight, transition support,
the widened weight, a good event, or a union bound.  This is exactly what the
initial term permits: the future Minkowski computation fixes the same weight
for the whole interval `[s N,v]`.

### Quantifiers

The literal order is

```text
E,D,c,s,t,δ,p and all assumptions;
∀ᶠ N;
∀ k;
∀ hk : k ≤ cutNetTop ... N;
∀ a.
```

Hence no eventual threshold depends on the endpoint selector, coordinate, or
active-index proof.  The required `p ≥ 1` is fixed before `∀ᶠ N`.

### Powers

With `x=N^(δ/8)` and `slotXi' x=x^(1/4)`, the right side is

```text
initTerm x R (slotXi' x) / R^4
 = x * R^2 * x^(1/4) / R^4
 = N^(5δ/32) * R^(-2).
```

This is the compiled identity `APrimeAssembly.initial_slot_scale_eq`.  The
connector changes no expression, so there is:

- no additional `N` or net-cardinality factor;
- no additional bandwidth `W` power;
- no change of `D` in `Step2.tT`;
- no change from `R_v^4` in the coordinate normalization or from the final
  `R_v^(-2)` initial-slot cost.

### Integrability

T600 uses
`APrimeAssembly.integrable_initialEvolvedNormAt_pow_gauss` at the exact
endpoint `v_{N,k}` to justify integral monotonicity from the actual weight to
weight one.  The connector is an equality of random functions, so rewriting
preserves that integrability and the weighted moment exactly.  It supplies no
time-integrability premise for the drift, cross, or QV rates.

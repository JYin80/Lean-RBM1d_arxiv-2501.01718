## Conditional first-failure inequality and quantifiers

Let `u_k=s_N+k/mesh_N`, `K_N=⌊(t_N-s_N)mesh_N⌋₊`, and `J_k=Step2Moment.jSnorm (Gauss.sample d) E D s N u_k`. Let `F_k={J_j≤N^δ for all j<k}` be the **hard narrow prefix**. At a successful prefix the calibrated smooth cutoff is one, provided the same-sample hard-to-smooth plateau comparison just described is established. The desired actual one-step output is an **unconditional** estimate, uniform in `k≤K_N` and in every two-loop label `a`,

`(E |c_k Y_{k,a}|^(2p))^(1/(2p)) ≤ C_p N^ν`,

where `Y_{k,a}` is the propagated endpoint coordinate normalized by `T_{u_k,D}(a)R_{u_k}^4`, and `0<ν<δ`. This is not the existing conditional `hfamily` of `aprimeSlot_of_widened_family` (`Gauss/APrimeAssembly.lean:1190`). The endpoint identity `U_(u_k,u_k)=I` is essential: it identifies the evolved coordinate with the actual `(L-K)` coordinate in `J_k`. From `jStar=1+max_a ‖(L-K)_a‖/T_a` (`Hierarchy/Step2.lean:113`) and `jSnorm=jStar/R_u^4`, with `R_u≥1`, `J_k>N^δ` and `N^δ≥2` force one `|Y_{k,a}|>N^δ/2`.

Consequently, on `F_k` where `c_k=1`, Markov plus a **finite label union** gives

`P(F_k ∩ {J_k>N^δ}) ≤ M_N (2 C_p)^(2p) N^[-2p(δ-ν)]`.

The `k=0` start needs the actual BoundsCore tail `P(J_0>N^δ)`, not an empty-prefix argument. Iterating first failures additively yields

`P(F_(K_N+1)^c) ≤ P(J_0>N^δ) + (K_N+1)M_N(2C_p)^(2p)N^[-2p(δ-ν)]`.

If `mesh_N=N^B` (or is bounded by `N^B`) and `t_N-s_N≤1`, then `K_N+1≤N^B+1` and `M_N≤N²` eventually. Thus one may take any fixed `Cnet>B+2` so `(K_N+1)M_N≤N^Cnet` eventually. For prescribed `Dprob>0`, fix `δ>0`, choose a sufficiently fine **fixed** `B` for the modulus and its first-cell support check, then choose `ν<δ` from the deterministic rate budget and one **fixed integer** `p≥1` satisfying

`2p(δ-ν)>Dprob+Cnet+3`.

Then choose the source-event failure power after `p` and the polynomial complement exponents; only then take eventual `N`. The constants and `N₀` may depend on these fixed parameters, but the event and moment bounds must be uniform in all `k,a` and in the running time on that window. If the initial BoundsCore tail is `≤N^-(Dprob+1)` eventually, the union is `O(N^-Dprob)`; intersecting with `Good_N` gives `P(Good_N∩F_(K_N+1))>0` and in fact high probability. This is an **additive** induction; dividing by `P(F_k)` or conditioning on earlier success would create an unjustified amplification.

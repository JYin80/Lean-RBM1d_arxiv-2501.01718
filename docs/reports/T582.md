## Paper boundary: why one cell is not the grid

Theorem 2.21 fixes an arbitrary energy `E`, an arbitrary left endpoint `s`, and assumes
(2.68)--(2.71) at `s`.  For every later `t` satisfying

```text
(W * ell_t * eta_t)^(-1) <= ((1-t)/(1-s))^30,                 (2.72)
```

it transfers the bounds to `t`.  The page-24 iteration then fixes a terminal time and uses

```text
1 - s_k = W^(-k*tauPrime),
s_n0 = t,
k = 0, ..., n0-1.
```

Thus the induction consumes every adjacent window `(s_k,s_{k+1})`.  Its `k=0` window is
needed, but is not representative of the later moving windows.  The statements used inside
the step are also all-time statements on the closed current interval:

```text
(2.73)  loop a-priori bounds throughout s <= u <= t,
(2.74)  weak local law,
(2.75)  improved local law,
(2.76)  decay, for every D > 0,
(2.77)  sharp loop bounds,
(2.78)  sharp L-K bounds,
(2.79)  sharp spatial decay,
(2.80)  terminal energy derivative bound.
```

Consequently, an existential first cell at `E=0,D=60` cannot be substituted for the
arbitrary cell and arbitrary decay exponent occurring in the iteration.

## First-cell and endpoint boundary

- T576/T581 genuinely include their first-cell `k=0` branch and have a same-sample active
  `k=2` witness.  That fact stays local to their fixed package.
- T579's prefix-support theorem, and hence the proposed general QV theorem, must assume
  `1 <= k`.  The support induction has no `k=0` premise to reuse.
- At positive `k`, `u` ranges over the closed interval `[s_N,v_k]`.  Both `u=s_N` and
  `u=v_k` are included, and `v_k` lies in `[s_N,t_N]`; the T579 source event therefore
  supplies both endpoints.
- At `k=0`, `v_0=s_N`.  A pointwise QV rate need not vanish at this time.  The safe route is
  to discharge the zero-length cell after time integration, not to assert a nonexistent
  pointwise support cap.
- The first grid cell can be used to prove satisfiability of universal theorem hypotheses,
  but never to replace their arbitrary `E,D,s,t` binders.

## Common event and nondegenerate satisfiability

Every pathwise source, block cap, and QV estimate in the next chain must use one literal

```text
omega in APrimeGeneralMovingCommonSources.commonEvent
  E D s t zetaSrc zetaCtr tauG N.
```

Selecting separate nonempty residents from the raw-source, centered, local-law, and block
events would not prove the needed conjunction.  Measurability is supplied by
`measurableSet_commonEvent`; high probability is supplied by `highProb_commonEvent` if T579
passes audit.

The hypotheses of the proposed positive-cell QV theorem are nondegenerate.  T579's
`positive_length_common_support_witness` uses `E=0,D=60,k=1` on an eventually strict first
cell and one resident with `widenedW=1`.  For example,

```text
zetaSrc = zetaCtr = tauG = delta = 1/100,
p = 1
```

satisfies `tauG+2*delta+2/15<1`.  This witness certifies joint satisfiability only.  It does
not specialize the universal theorem being proposed.

T576/T581 separately retain an even more explicit same-sample first-cell witness at active
`k=2`, positive endpoint, weight one, and membership in `firstCellGood`.  Again, this proves
that their restricted package is not empty; it provides no universal quantifier.

## Explicit rejection and non-claims

It is invalid to promote either

```text
exists tauPrime > 0, APrimeSlot' (sample exampleGrow) 0 0
  (firstCellT tauPrime) 60
```

or its `APrimeSlot` conversion to

```text
forall E,s,t,c,D>=60, APrimeSlot' (sample exampleGrow) E s t D.
```

That promotion would reverse quantifiers, replace arbitrary parameters by one witness,
discard the `D`-dependent mesh budget, and erase the moving `ratR` and endpoint powers.

This audit therefore does **not** claim any of the following is closed:

- general-moving `WeightedMoment` or `hfamily`;
- universal `APrimeHypOn`, `APrimeSlot'`, or `APrimeSlot`;
- A-prime iteration;
- the other five universal merged inputs;
- the six-step chain;
- Theorem 2.21;
- formulas (2.68)--(2.80) as a complete package;
- the paper.

The safe next sequence is: independently audit T579; implement the exact positive-cell QV
profile; prove its deterministic exponent simplifications; independently assemble the
general-moving drift; then form the cross, integrated/stopped, coordinate-moment, and
`hfamily` layers.  Only after all six universal merged inputs exist should the grid iteration
be revisited.

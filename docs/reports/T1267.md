active target-mesh index `k` with `(k : ℝ) ≤ N^D`, the theorem gives one
eventual cutoff before `k` and the output `a`, and bounds the literal T1089

The power count checks for real `D`. The target mesh is eventually
`N^(4D+18)`, and `cutNetPt` gives
`v−s_N=k/targetMesh(D,N)`. Thus the added index condition gives
`v−s_N≤N^(D−4D−18)=N^(−3D−18)` and
`sqrt(v)−sqrt(s_N)≤N^(−3D/2−9)`. `Cond272Reg`, `W L≤N`, and `ell_t≤L` give
`etaT(E,s_N)^(-1)≤N`. T1245's exact profile bound and T1247's p=1 norm-good
reduction then integrate against `u^(-1/2)`, whose interval integral is
`2(sqrt(v)−sqrt(s_N))`. The norm-good integral contributes the main coefficient
`15/2` and an error `(15/4)N^(-3D/2−10)`. T1207 adds the other
`(15/4)N^(-3D/2−10)`, giving the requested total coefficient `15/2`.

The proof includes `k=0`: the interval is degenerate, and both literal
budgets vanish at `u=0` under Lean's totalized division. T1123 and T1205
provide the full and norm-good interval integrability needed at that endpoint.
The one eventual cutoff is formed before introducing `k` or `a`.

# T763 — Antitone mass of actual-prefix cells

## Files and scope

Added `RBM1D/Gauss/PermutationPrefixCellMassMono.lean`. It imports the accepted
T736 exact-mass producer and T747 cell-inclusion producer. No root import was
added. The result concerns finite uniform-permutation mass only; paper formulas
(2.3)–(2.4) are the Green-function local laws and are not claimed here.

## Results

* `uniformPerm_prefixCell_mass_antitone`: for all `W k l : ℕ`, `k ≤ l`, and
  `π : PermΩ W`,
  `uniformPerm W {ρ | ρ ∈ prefixCell W l π} ≤
   uniformPerm W {ρ | ρ ∈ prefixCell W k π}`.
  This follows by measure monotonicity from T747's actual-cell inclusion.
* `uniformPerm_prefixCell_mass_antitone_zero`: at `k = 0`, every actual cell
  has mass one.
* `uniformPerm_prefixCell_mass_antitone_zero_width`: when `W = 0`, every
  actual cell, for every `k`, has mass one.
* `uniformPerm_prefixCell_mass_antitone_two_strict_witness`: for the width-two
  identity, mass is one at reveal time zero and one half at reveal time one;
  the latter is strictly smaller. The one-half calculation reuses T736's
  identity/swap witness. No universal strictness is asserted.

There are no added hypotheses, so no satisfiability witness is needed. No
conditional-law, first-cell/shared-event, `P2`, or paper-chain claim is made.

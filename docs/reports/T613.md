The deterministic definitions are exactly:

- `transitionBlockCap = 1 + N^tauG * (9 * exp(sqrt 3) *
  (((16 * exp(1)^2 + 1) * N^(2*delta)) * ratR^4) + 2)`;
- `transitionRawCoordRate = nearSourceRate + 2 * W⁻¹ +
  absorbedFarRate(..., transitionBlockCap)`, where the absorbed far term is
  the T590 rate with coefficient `1200`;
- `storedRootRate = ratR^(-4) * sqrt(transitionRawCoordRate) /
  threshold`, retaining the literal
  `threshold delta N = 8 * exp(1)^2 * N^(2*delta)`;
- `transitionPrefixGradientRate = exp(1) * sup_{j < k}
  sqrt(u_j) * storedRootRate(u_j)` for `k > 0`, and `0` for `k = 0`.

The raw-row theorem is eventual in `N` and quantifies over every active
`1 <= k <= cutNetTop`, the identical sample

```lean
omega ∈ commonEvent ... N ∩ transition ... N k (canonicalM ... N)
```

every `j < k` (including `j = 0`), and every two-loop coordinate `a`.  Its
left-hand side is the same-time QV of `coordFun` at
`(sqrt u_j : Complex) • Xmat omega`, normalized by the exact factors
`ratR(u_j)^(-4)`, `tT(u_j,a)^(-1)`, and `threshold(delta,N)^(-1)`.
The fixed charge `Step2.sigPM` is the one encoded by `coordFun`.

The prefix theorem uses the same event, sample, target mesh, and canonical
truncation.  It applies
`APrimeSmoothWeightActual.sqrt_quadVar_prefixMatrix_le_exp_max` and the
canonical-`M` calibration to the raw rows.  Closed endpoint membership is
handled by `cutNetPt_mem_Icc`, so the last active cell is included.  In
particular, the `j = 0` row at `u_0 = s_N` is not discarded through a
`sqrt(0)` argument.

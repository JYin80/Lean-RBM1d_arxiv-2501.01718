## Exact statement and relation to the existing theorem

The new public theorem is:

```lean
alternatingVertexSum_eq_iteratedUnitIntegral_of_contDiffOn
  {r : ℕ} (f : (Fin r → ℝ) → ℝ) (U : Set (Fin r → ℝ))
  (hU : IsOpen U) (hcube : unitCoordinateCube r ⊆ U)
  (hf : ContDiffOn ℝ r f U) :
  alternatingVertexSum r (fun v => f (fun i => if v i then 1 else 0)) =
    iteratedUnitIntegral r (cubeMixedDerivative f)
```

This uses the existing definitions and convention: the recursive alternating sum is upper face
minus lower face, and `cubeMixedDerivative` applies the order-`r` iterated derivative to each
coordinate vector once in `Fin` order. T1438's compiled theorem
`alternatingVertexSum_eq_iteratedUnitIntegral` proves the same identity assuming global
`ContDiff ℝ r f`; its source remains unaccepted and is imported here for its definitions and
compiled proof pieces only. The new theorem supplies the missing local-domain successor.

The exact T1424 §3.3 consumer is the deterministic repeated-FTC step for `g = f ∘ A` on the
interpolating cube. Its regularity is available on an open neighborhood of the cube from the
positive floor, not on all of the ambient function space. This theorem matches that local scope.

## Local-domain proof and witness

At each induction step, the two face domains are preimages of `U` under the affine face maps.
They are open and contain the lower-dimensional unit cube, so the local hypothesis passes to
both faces and the induction hypothesis applies. For each tail point in the cube, the full
first-coordinate segment and both endpoints lie in `U`. Openness turns `ContDiffOn` into the
pointwise `ContDiffAt` facts used by the face-slice chain rule and the one-dimensional FTC. The
within derivatives on the open preimage domains are converted to ambient iterated derivatives at
the points in question. The FTC step retains both endpoint faces, the upper-minus-lower sign,
and the distinct shifted coordinate directions. Dimension zero is handled by the empty-coordinate
case.

For linearity of the nested integrals, the proof applies the Mathlib Tietze theorem to extend only
the scalar continuous integrands from the closed cube. A separate cube-congruence lemma proves
that the nested integrals observe only values on the cube. No differentiability of `f` is extended,
and no smooth extension is assumed.

The public witness theorem `cubeFTC_coordinateProduct_witness_local` gives, for every `r`,
alternating vertex sum `1`, mixed derivative identically `1`, and nested integral `1` for
`g(t) = ∏ i, t i`. It includes `r = 0` and is reused from T1438's compiled witness.

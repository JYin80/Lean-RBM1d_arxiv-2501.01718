The literal T279 interface in `Gauss/Eq45FlowGrid.lean:560–615` says, for fixed `τ'>0` and `hll : LocalLawUnifIcc d 0 s t ψ`, there must exist functions `Bp : ℕ→ℕ→ℝ`, `Kp : ℕ→ℝ` satisfying


The order is `∀p` **then** eventual `N`, and all `u` in the **same** cell. In `FlucIter.lean:2329`, `FlucGainUpTo'` quantifies over all finite index types `ι`, maps `k`, and lists of word operators, with every word length at most `M=2p` **and** `card ι≤n=2p`. Its product-moment bound has `Bp^(card ι) ρ^(Σ numQ)`. The cardinality budget is essential: the unbudgeted `FlucGainUpTo` at `Bp≍ψ` is known to be unsatisfiable. At `p=0` the cardinality restriction permits only the empty index type; the nontrivial test begins at `p=1` and positive `u`. Neither an all-`p`-at-one-`N` claim nor a mere bound at the right endpoint is equivalent to `hg`.

`Step345Producer.lean:181–183` defines `Eq45FlowInputs` as one common auxiliary field `x` together with `IBPFlow`, `FlucRowFlow`, and `FlucBlkFlow`. Their definitions in `Gauss/Eq45Flow.lean:153–180` place the entire `TimeIcc` and row/block index set inside each `StochDom` failure event. `Step345Producer.eq45Flow_of_eq45FlowInputs` transfers the conjunction to `StepGlue.Eq45Flow`, which itself is a uniform `(+,-)` two-loop-to-one-loop transfer for every nonnegative control `Φ`; it does not assert the two-loop law on its own.

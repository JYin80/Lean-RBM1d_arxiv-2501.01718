## Exact remaining implication

For every fixed `p>=1` and every `epsilon>0`, one still has to prove, eventually in `N` and
uniformly over every positive net point `v`,

```text
sum_{q : LoopData(L_N,2)}
  (A_v^2 * twoCoordDuhamelRhs(d,E,s,N,p,v,q))^(2p)
    <= C * N^(epsilon*p) * A_s^p.
```

This must be derived from earlier Step-1/Step-2 inputs by estimating, uniformly in all four
charges and all `L_N^2` label pairs:

1. the transported initial term;
2. `2 ∫_{s_N}^v ‖U_{u,v} driftF_u‖_{2p} du`;
3. `[2(2p-1) ∫_{s_N}^v ‖U^{xi2}_{u,v} eeFun_u‖_p du]^(1/2)`.

The remaining assembly then consists of:

- using `xiLK_two_init_of_boundsCore` and the reverse moment bridge at `v=s_N`;
- combining that initial branch with the positive-grid family theorem above;
- restoring the literal order
  `delta0; delta; epsilon; p; C; eventually N; every net point`.

The finite maximum and all four charges are **not** remaining logical gaps: they are already
present in the delivered family theorem. What remains is the quantitative absorption of its
explicit `4*L_N^2` summands. No claim is made here that this absorption, the BoundsCore
initial branch, a common-event formulation, `CutHypEvOnSlot`, or (2.77) has been proved.

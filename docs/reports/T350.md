The local actual-model ingredient for the near branch of (5.35) is proved. For every Gaussian sample, bulk energy `|E|<2`, time `u<1`, positive `ell_u`, and `log W≥4`, if the output pair is near (`d(a₂,a₁)≤ell*_u`) and the internal label is far (`d(a₂,b)>ell**_u`), then the actual three-loop obeys

## Claim and arithmetic checked

The relevant expression is the second summand at
`APrimeGeneralMovingDriftAtProfile.lean:36–50`, multiplied by T615's
`xiK * ratR(u)^(-2) * ratR(v)^(-2)`. Write
`A_u=W ell_u eta_u`, `r_u=ell_u/ell_s`, and
`Jbar=APrimeGeneralMovingDriftSource.blockCap`. The summand is

```text
4 N^zetaCtr [L Jbar/(ell_u eta_u r_u^2)]
  A_u^2 exp((log W)^(3/4)) tailT(W,ell_u,eta_u,D,gap)
  [eta_u^-1 r_u^3].
```

For positive `ell_u`, `eta_u`, and `r_u`, its two eta inverses cancel the
two eta factors in `A_u^2`; it becomes
`4 N^zetaCtr L Jbar W^2 ell_u r_u exp((log W)^(3/4)) tailT(gap)`.
The transport factors remain exactly `xiK ratR(u)^(-2) ratR(v)^(-2)`.

From (5.27),
`tailT(gap)=A_u^-2 exp(-sqrt(gap/ell_u))+W^-D`.
The actual gap is `ellStarStar-ellStar`, which is at least
`F=ellStarStar-4 ellStar` because `ellStar>=0`. Put `x=log W`.
For `x>=4`, `F/ell_u=x^3-4x^(3/2)>=x^3/2`; for
`x>=(4D)^2` and `D>=0`, `D^2 x^2<=x^3/16<=F/ell_u`.
Thus `exp(-sqrt(F/ell_u))<=W^-D`.
With `A_u>=1`, antitonicity of `tailT` yields
`tailT(gap)<=2W^-D`. This proves the fixed positive-`D` case needed
here. It does not invoke the historical `D=0` false strengthening.

The all-window crude bounds used in the disposable candidate give
`xiK,N^zetaCtr,L,Jbar,ell_u,r_u,exp((log W)^(3/4))<=N`,
`W<=N`, `ratR(u)>=1`, and `ratR(v)>0`. The exact count is nine powers
of `N`, including the `W^2` factor. Hence the normalized residual is
`<=8 N^9 W^-D ratR(v)^(-2)`. The accepted dimension inequality
`N<=W^2` and `D>=60` give `W^-D<=N^-30`, hence
`<=8N^-21 ratR(v)^(-2)`. On every active prefix,
`0<=v-s_N<1`; the integral obeys the same bound, also when `k=0`
and `v=s_N`. Eventually `8N^-21<=N^(δ/8)` for every fixed `δ>0`,
and `δ/8<5δ/32`.

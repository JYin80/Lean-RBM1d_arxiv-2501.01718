## Dependency and proof audit

* T711 defines `PermΩ W = Equiv.Perm (Fin W)` and `prefixCell W k π` by
  agreement with `π` at every `i : Fin W` with `i.val < k`. Its
  `mem_prefixCell` supplies a member for every cell, including `W = 0`.
* `prefixCellEquivFree` is an actual equivalence between that cell and
  permutations of `{i : Fin W // k ≤ i.val}`. For a free permutation `τ`,
  `π * Equiv.Perm.ofSubtype τ` agrees with `π` below `k`, since `ofSubtype`
  fixes positions outside the free subtype. Injectivity follows from left
  cancellation and Mathlib's `ofSubtype_injective`.
* Conversely, for `ρ` in the cell, `g = π⁻¹ * ρ` fixes all positions below
  `k`. Thus its support lies in the free subtype. Mathlib's
  `mem_range_ofSubtype_iff` identifies exactly these permutations with the
  range of `ofSubtype`, giving surjectivity. This range lemma is a general
  permutation theorem in Mathlib and does not depend on T738's cardinality
  result. The multiplication order is correct: the `ofSubtype` factor acts on
  the input before `π`.
* `prefixFree_card` counts the free subtype as `W - min k W`; the standard
  cardinality of the permutation group of an `n`-element finite type is
  `n.factorial`. The proof contains no assumption that an arbitrary prescribed
  prefix is realizable; `π` itself realizes the prefix.

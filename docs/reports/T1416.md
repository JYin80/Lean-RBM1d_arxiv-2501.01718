## Corrected interface and proved implication

`SharedMinorProductData.rowfree` now requires `Y i S` to be independent only of the rows already deleted in `S`; it may depend on its own row `k i`. Requiring independence of `S ∪ {k i}` would make `qRow (k i) (Y i S)` identically zero. The interface separately supplies a global envelope for `Y` and for its own-row-centered version `qRow (k i) (Y i S)`.

The finite-difference input is stated for `minorDiff (qList L) (Y i)`, not for `minorDiff (qList L) (qRow (k i) (Y i))`. This matches the `Yr`-type numerator bound needed from an actual producer. The proof uses `applyOps_eq_applyOps_minorDiff` on `Y`, commutes the own-row projection through the word, and bounds the conditional row projection by splitting on the good-event tower. The sharp profile is used away from `Bad (length L)`; the row section of that set is charged on `Bad (length L + 1)`. Thus the abstract proof does not assume a sharp pointwise bound for `Delta(Q_i Y_i^S)` on the original good event.

For fixed `n ≥ 1`, `N ≥ 1`, tuple labels `k`, `R = image(k)`, and words `L i`, the hypotheses require:

- each word has distinct row labels, length at most `n - 1`, and labels in `R \ {k i}`;
- one positive shared scale `lambda` is free of every row in `R`, has global lower bound `W⁻¹`, and is at most `1` on the common good event;
- each `Y i S` is bounded and measurable, free of the rows in `S`, and has the supplied global raw envelope `rawEnvelope`;
- on the common good event, each word's finite difference of `Y` is bounded by `diffConstant * lambda^((numQ(L i)-1)/2)`;
- the deterministic main loss satisfies `2^((n+1)n) * diffConstant^n ≤ C * N^epsilon`.

Writing `q = sum_i numQ(L i)`, the exported `integral_sharedMinorProduct_le` proves

```text
E product_i |applyOps(L_i, qRow(k_i, Y_i^empty))|
  <= C N^epsilon W^((n - min(q,n))/2)
     + n * (2n * 2^(2n) * rawEnvelope * delta)
         * max(1, 2^(n+1) * diffConstant * sqrt(W)
                    + 2n * 2^(2n) * rawEnvelope * delta)^n
     + (2^(2n) * rawEnvelope)^n * P(Bad n),
```

where `delta` is the tower's row-section error. In particular, the bandwidth exponent saturates at zero when the total number of `Q` letters reaches `n`; it does not grow with total word length. The exceptional-event contribution is explicit, but this theorem does not estimate `P(Bad n)` by a negative power of `N` or absorb it into `C N^epsilon`.

The proof reuses the compiled finite-minor identity `applyOps_eq_applyOps_minorDiff`, row-projection/word commutation, `norm_minorDiff_le`, `norm_applyOps_le`, and `norm_condRow_le_split`. The new profile version `norm_applyOps_le_badFamily_profile` carries a row-free random envelope through each word. The global product envelope and the integral split retain the full `P(Bad n)` remainder.

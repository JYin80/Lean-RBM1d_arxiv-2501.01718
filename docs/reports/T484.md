# T484 — general moving prefix-support cap

## Result

Added `RBM1D/Gauss/APrimeGeneralMovingPrefixSupport.lean`.

`eventually_running_cap` proves that, under the exact T483 general moving
window assumptions, every active prefix with `1 ≤ k ≤ cutNetTop` satisfies

```text
0 < widenedW ... δ p N k ω
  -> ∀ u ∈ [s_N, cutNetPt_N(k)],
       J_N(u,ω) ≤ (4 exp(1) + 2) N^(2δ)
```

uniformly for `ω ∈ good_N`, for every fixed `δ > 0` and `p ≥ 1` before the
eventual size threshold.

The proof opens the active branch of `widenedW`.  Positivity of its positive
power implies that the underlying `prefixSoftW` is nonzero.  T483 supplies the
same-sample modulus and mesh-fineness hypotheses to
`APrimeWeight.le_of_prefixSoftW_ne_zero_of_modulus`, with

```text
θ = 2 exp(1) N^(2δ),   Θ = 1.
```

This gives the exact bound `4 exp(1) N^(2δ) + 1`.  Since `N ≥ 1` and
`δ > 0`, the final `1` is absorbed into the stated
`(4 exp(1) + 2) N^(2δ)` cap.  The theorem deliberately excludes `k = 0`,
whose prefix is empty.

## Nondegenerate witness

`cond272Reg_grid_step_domain`, so its `τ'`, `c`, endpoints, and
`Cond272Reg` proof come from the same construction.  Eventually the window is
`[0,1/2]`; the target mesh at `D = 60` has an active `k = 1` prefix.  The zero
Gaussian sample belongs to the same concrete event `good_N`.  At its sole
prefix point, `J_N(0,0) = 1`, hence the canonical old weight is one and its
comparison with `widenedW` shows

```text

```

for every `p ≥ 1`.  Thus positive support, the norm event, active positive
index, admissibility, and positive window length are simultaneous.

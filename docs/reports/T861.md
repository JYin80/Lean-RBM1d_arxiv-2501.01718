## Proof content

`prefixFourierMean` is defined as the complex sum of `permutationFourierSum` over `prefixCell`, divided by that cell's cardinality. The main theorem assumes `2 ≤ W`, `‖x i‖ ≤ 2`, and `‖χ i‖ ≤ 1`, and proves

\[
\|M_{k+1}(\pi)-M_k(\pi)\|\le 8/W.
\]

The proof establishes a sum partition of the actual cell by its next-image fibers. Each unused-image fiber is positive, all such fibers have equal cardinality, and their card sum is the actual prefix-cell cardinality. The next cell is identified with the actual fiber at `π k`. The accepted T704 comparison theorem bounds the difference between any two normalized fiber means by `8/W`; the current prefix mean is their equal-weight finite average. The triangle inequality gives the result. `prefixCell_card_pos` supplies positive actual-cell denominators.

`prefixFourierMean_last_eq` shows the final reveal changes no mean. For `W=2`, `prefixFourierMean_two_nonconstant_witness` uses `x=(2,-2)`, `χ=(1,-1)`, and the identity permutation, and proves `M₀=0`, `M₁=2`.

The sum identity is proved locally for the arbitrary complex Fourier observable rather than inferred from the existing cardinality identity.

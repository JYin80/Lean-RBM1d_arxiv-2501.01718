## Exact rates and normalization

`APrimeDriftTimeFamily.qvAt` (`Gauss/APrimeDriftTimeFamily.lean:134`)
is the uncut `quadVar` of `coordAt`. Its `qvAt_eq_evolved` identifies it
definitionally with `APrimeDuhamelModel.qvRateEvolved`; the older
`APrimeModel.qvRate` (`Gauss/APrimeModel.lean:124`) is the unevolved
`quadVar(lkFun)` and cannot fill (G). `APrimeFullQV.qvAt_full_absorbed`
(`Gauss/APrimeFullQV.lean:743`) is genuinely about the evolved `qvAt`.

Its bound has the exact order

`qvAt(u,a,ω) ≤ [driftScale(a,s,v)^(-1) ·
 rootProfile(u,v,ellSource,J_u(ω),Smax,W^-1,a)]²`,

with `driftScale=T_(v,D)(a)R⁴`, `R=η_s/η_v`. The reciprocal is applied
**once to the square root, then squared**; multiplying by it a second time
would be false. In `rootProfile` (`:535`) the main kernel factors are
`(η_u/η_v)² ξK T_v`, the repaired near term is
`sqrt(diagNearRate+2W^-1)` times a `6ell*_v` indicator, and the far term
is `sqrt(diagFarRate)`. The latter retains both `J² A_u sqrt(Smax)` and
`J³ A_u^-1`, plus the spatial `WL W^-D J³` leakage. The near kernel has
an additional `256 exp(3)(η_u/η_v)²W^-D T_v` leakage. T334 absorbs
`nearEpsilon≤W^-1` *before* this endpoint division; it has not erased
either leakage term or the `cNear2,cFar2,ξK` logarithmic losses.
The paper's printed (5.42) has the near term and a coarser cubic far
term `η_u^-1 A_u^-1/3(J*_{u,D})³`, after absorbing its expansion factor.
The A′ audit deliberately retains the sharper, unsimplified `J²` and
`J³` pieces in T334/T344; it is not a literal copy of the printed
far-field display.

Writing `x=η_s/η_u`, cancellation of the one `T_v` and the endpoint
`R⁴` gives a main square-root factor `R^-2 x^-2 ξK`. After squaring,
the desired polynomial near/J²/J³ profile of T331 §4 is

`C N^ν η_u^-1 R^-4 [x^-3/2
 + N^(4δ+2τ) A^-1/2 x^(19/4)
 + N^(6δ+3τ) A^-1 x^8]`.

That expression is *not* a direct rewrite of the compiled `rootProfile`:
one must bound `ellSource`, `Smax`, `J_u`, the two log coefficients, `ξK`,
and both leakages with their actual multipliers. The length-six source
level in `sourceEvent_of_step1` costs an explicit factor two. The
`ellSource_N=(2N^ζ)^(-1/5)` first-cell choice is below one and therefore
adds a polynomial loss; it cannot be silently reset to one. T334's
`ExponentRows.coefficient_bound` and `leak_paid_by_dims` prove related
**early diagonal** algebra, but no theorem in T334 rewrites the evolved
`rootProfile` into the displayed `qvEnvelope` on a common event.

## Weighted norm, bad event, and time

`APrimeOneStep.qv_bound_of_envelope` (`Gauss/APrimeOneStep.lean:340`)
requires `hp:1≤p`, `hgint`, `hgbd:∀u∈Icc s v,g u≤gbd u`, and the
integral threshold. T344 proves the exact integral for its deterministic
`qvEnvelope` and the time implication *if* this `hgbd` and its numerical
threshold are supplied. This is a weighted `L^p(P_d)` norm of the **uncut**
evolved rate, not a pointwise value and not the norm of an event indicator.
`APrimeDuhamelModel.rateNormW_qvRateEvolvedOn_le` (`:1069`) bounds only
`1_Good·qvRateEvolved`; applying it to the uncut generator would discard
the complement.

The correct existing consumer is
`APrimeBadSplit.evolved_qv_norm_le_of_event`
(`Gauss/APrimeBadSplit.lean:108`): for each running `u`, a measurable
`Good_N`, event bound `Qev_N(u)`, global all-sample bound `Env_N`, and
`P_d(Good_N^c)≤pr_N` give

`g_N(u)≤Qev_N(u)+Env_N·pr_N^(1/p)`.

No indicator is inserted into the weight or `qvAt`; the weight remains
differentiable. To preserve a target `N^ν qvEnvelope`, fix the integer
`p≥1` and desired tolerance `ν>0` first, prove a window-uniform
`Env_N≤C_0N^K` with fixed `K`, then demand a single window event with
`pr_N≤N^-M` for a failure exponent `M>p(K+ν+K_low)`.
Here `K_low` is any certified polynomial lower-envelope exponent for
the positive near term; on the concrete `E=s=0,v=1/2` cell it can be
taken as zero, while on a general cell it depends on `R^30≤A≤N` and
the lower/upper `η` bounds. The complement then costs at most
`C_0N^(K-M/p)`, uniformly in `u`. Choosing a probability exponent
*before* fixing `p` would fail for large `p`. `HighProb` allows the
requested fixed `M` only after the actual common-event producer exists.

`APrimeQVRateTime.intervalIntegrable_rateNormW_qvAt`
(`Gauss/APrimeQVRateTime.lean:219`) already proves `hgint` for every fixed
measurable weight `0≤w≤1` and `p≥1`; its `intervalIntegrable_...prefixSoftW`
is a specific old prefix weight. It derives from joint measurability and
`exists_bdd_qvAt` (`:168`), whose constant comes from a compactness bound
on the `Uker` row (`:131`). That proves finite-
`N` integrability but no `Env_N≤C_0N^K`. `qvEnvelope` is integrable by
T344. An actual new smooth weight can use the general T294 theorem once
its measurability and `0≤w≤1` are instantiated; it need not be replaced
by the old weight.

## Regime and nonvacuity

T344 proves the two strict scale rows
`(4δ+2τ+ν)/c+(19/4)/30<1/2` and
`(6δ+3τ+ν)/c+8/30<1` and interpolates the separate premises
`N^c≤A`, `R^30≤A`; multiplying those two premises is invalid. On the
intended actual model `A_u=W ell_u η_u≤WL≤N`; if `A_u≥1`, it also gives
`η_u≥N^-1` (T330 §14). For the growing first-cell family of T330 §6,
`E=s=0`, `v=1/2`, `W≈N^(3/4)`, `L≈N^(1/4)`, `c=1/2`, one has `R=2`,
`A_v≈N^(3/4)/sqrt(2)`, and eventually `N^c≤A_v`, `R^30≤A_v`,
`η_u≥1/2` on the cell. This is a nondegenerate **numerical** window.
T334's `first_cell_gaussian` proves positive length, positive endpoint
scale and `jG≥1` for an actual Gaussian sample. T344's
`first_cell_nonvacuous` proves its deterministic envelope is positive.
Neither theorem proves `qvAt(u,a,ω)>0` for an actual nonzero Gaussian
coordinate while all `SourceEvent`/support hypotheses hold on that same
sample. That anti-vacuity check remains open; a positive envelope is
not evidence that the random QV is positive.

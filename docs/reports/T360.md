# T360 — polynomial all-sample envelope for actual evolved QV: mathematical preflight

## Verdict and quantifiers

**Yes.** Fix a bulk energy `|E|<2`, real `D≥0`, and real `Kη≥0`. For any `Gauss.Dims d`, eventually in `N` along `d.dim`, let `0≤s≤u≤v<1`, `η_v=etaT E v≥N^(-Kη)`, and let `a : LoopArg (d.L N) 2` and `σ : Fin 2 → Bool` be arbitrary. Then, for **every** Gaussian sample `ω : Gauss.Ω d`, the actual uncut evolved rate satisfies

```text
0 ≤ APrimeDriftTimeFamily.qvAt d E D N σ a s v u ω
  ≤ 2^21 N^(2D+4+12Kη).                                      (T360.1)
```

All constants are independent of `N,u,a,σ,ω`. If a literal integer polynomial exponent is desired, replace the displayed exponent by `K=⌈2D+4+12Kη⌉` for `N≥1`. The additional target-regime conditions `A_v≤N` and `R^30≤A_v`, with `R=η_s/η_v`, are compatible but not needed for this upper bound. There is no Good, support, A′, or sample-size assumption. This is a preflight calculation, **not yet a compiled Lean theorem**.

## Exact factor ledger

Write `M=card(d.Idx N)=W_N L_N` and `η=(1-v) Im m(E)=η_v`. The eventual dimension condition gives `M≤N` and, since `L_N≥3`, `W_N≤N`.

1. `norm_xiOf_mSigma` gives `|ξ_i|=1` for both edges and any charge `σ`. For `s≤u≤v`, `0≤v<1`, `Hierarchy/Kernel.lean:sum_norm_edgeKer_row_le` gives each edge-row sum at most
   `1+(v-u)/(1-v)=(1-u)/(1-v)=η_u/η_v≤R`. The product-row identity for two independent loop coordinates therefore gives
   `Gauss.ukerRow (xiOf (mSigma E) σ) v a u ≤ (η_u/η_v)^2 ≤ R²`.
   This is valid for a complex unit phase; positivity of `ξ_i` is unnecessary.
2. `driftScale=T_(v,D)(a) R⁴`. The tail floor is `T_(v,D)(a)≥W_N^(-D)`. Thus its **one** reciprocal before squaring satisfies
   `driftScale⁻¹ · ukerRow ≤ W_N^D R^(-2) ≤ N^D`, using `R≥1` and `D≥0`. Applying the reciprocal again here would double-count the normalization.
3. `MomentDuhamelBddT.lean:bddC2C_ukerObsT`, as used by `APrimeQVRateTime.lean:exists_bdd_fderiv_coordAt`, has two-loop first-derivative coefficient exactly
   `M · 2 · [2(1+η_v⁻¹)^3]^2 = 8M(1+η_v⁻¹)^6`.
   Since `η_v⁻¹≤N^Kη` and `N≥1`, it is at most `512 N^(1+6Kη)`. Consequently, uniformly in `u∈[s,v]` and every matrix `M'`, the operator norm of the matrix derivative of the actual normalized `coordAt` is at most
   `C₁=512 N^(D+1+6Kη)`.
4. The exact number of `usedCoord` directions is `M²`: one real coordinate per diagonal pair and two per unordered off-diagonal pair. For a simple Lean bound, `usedCoord⊆Idx×Idx×Bool` gives `card usedCoord≤2M²≤2N²`. Each variance is `S_ij` or `S_ij/2`, hence at most `1` by nonnegativity and `sum_Sblk_row=1`. Each `Bmat` is a sum of at most two matrix units with unit coefficients, so its L² operator norm is at most `2`. Therefore the coordinate weight in `quadVar` is bounded by `2N²·1·2²=8N²`.
5. `qvAt` is the sum of those nonnegative variance weights times squared coordinate derivatives. `Gauss.norm_coordD1_le` gives each derivative at most `C₁‖Bmat‖`. Hence
   `qvAt≤C₁²·8N²=512²·8 N^(2D+4+12Kη)=2^21 N^(2D+4+12Kη)`.

This deliberately uses a coarse coordinate count. The row-sum of `S` could lower the `N²` coordinate weight to order `N`; no such sharpening is needed for (T360.1).

## Primitive bounds: removing the compactness constant

`APrimeQVRateTime.lean:exists_bdd_fderiv_coordAt` obtains `cK` from `Gauss.exists_bdd_Kval_Kprim` and passes its `Kval` bound into `bddC2C_ukerObsT`. In that lemma the `Kval` constant appears in the **zeroth** derivative coefficient only. The first-derivative coefficient displayed above has no `cK`, and `Kprim` does not occur in this matrix derivative. Thus the compactness-only constant is not part of the QV exponent.

It can nevertheless be replaced quantitatively if the Lean producer needs an explicit `hKb`. For a two-loop index, `Kgen_two` and `kTwo` express `Kval` as `W⁻¹m_1m_2 Θ_{u m_1m_2}`. Both `|m_i|=1`; the resolvent estimate `norm_Theta_le` yields

```text
|Kval(E,N,u,I)| ≤ [W_N(1-u)]⁻¹ ≤ η_u⁻¹ ≤ N^Kη.
```

The middle inequality uses `W_N≥1` and `Im m(E)≤1`. Equivalently, `Band.norm_Kval_le` gives a bulk-energy constant independent of `N` for length two. The pinned `Kprim` is `primRhs`; `norm_primRhs_le` at loop length two gives `|Kprim|≤4W_N L_N B_k²`, where every cut-and-glued loop has length two and `B_k=N^Kη` suffices. Hence `|Kprim|≤4N^(1+2Kη)` eventually. Neither bound grows faster than polynomially, and neither enlarges (T360.1).

## Positive-length first-cell probe with actual positive QV

Use the growing dimensions `Gauss.Dims.exampleGrow` of `Gauss/DimsExample.lean`, `E=0`, `s=0`, `v=u=1/2`, `D=60`, `σ=Step2.sigPM`, and a diagonal endpoint label `a=(x,x)`. Then `η_v=1/2≥N⁻¹` for `N≥2`, `R=2`, and `A_v=W_N/√2`. Since `W_N≈N^(3/4)` and `W_N L_N≤N` eventually, both `A_v≤N` and `R^30=2^30≤A_v` hold eventually. The window has length `1/2>0`.

At the explicit sample `ω=APrimeSmoothTransition.scalarSample d 1`, `Hflow(v,ω)=√v I`. Since `U_(v,v)=id`, the diagonal observable along the scalar matrix path `M=tI` is

```text
coordAt(v,tI) = [1/{W_N(t²+(1-v)²)} − Kval(v,a)] / driftScale(a,0,v).
```

The derivative in `t` at `t=√v>0` is `−2√v / {W_N(v+(1-v)²)² driftScale}`, which is nonzero. The identity direction is the sum of diagonal `Bmat(i,i,true)` directions. Thus at least one diagonal coordinate derivative is nonzero; every diagonal variance is strictly positive by `Sblk_diag_pos` (`S_ii=1/(3W_N)`). The defining nonnegative sum therefore gives **actual** `qvAt(v,ω)>0`. This probe is pointwise; its singleton need not have positive Gaussian probability. It rules out a vacuous zero-QV envelope.

Fix `|E|<2`, `D≥60`, `0≤s_N≤t_N<1`, `c>0`, `Cond272Reg`, `BoundsCore`, and
`0<δ≤min(1,c/100)`, with T995's losses. Set
`x=N^(δ/8)`, `v=cutNetPt s (targetMesh D) N 1`, and
`R=ratR E s N v`. For an active first cell, the endpoint formula and the
eventual target mesh give

```text
v - s_N = 1/targetMesh(D,N) = N^(-(4D+18)),
√v - √s_N ≤ N^(-(2D+9)).
```

T1249 bounds the literal T615 smooth weighted drift at `p=1` by
`∫_[s_N,v] g ≤ N^(-3D-10)`, retaining the actual smooth weight and arbitrary
output `a`.

For the cross term, T1247's pointwise norm-good estimate is
`normGoodCrossBudget ≤ (15/4) crossProfile + (15/8)N^(-1)/√u`.
T1245 bounds that exact T1029 profile by
`K(E)N^(13δ/640)etaT(E,s_N)^(-1)R^(-2)/√u`, where `K(E)>0` is its fixed
cross-profile constant. Integrating on the first cell uses
`∫u^(-1/2)=2(√v−√s_N)`. T1207 contributes the full norm-bad payment
`(15/4)N^(-1)(√v−√s_N)`. Since `etaT(E,s_N)^(-1)≤N` eventually,
`R≥1`, and `0<δ≤1`, these give

```text
∫_[s_N,v] positiveTimeCrossBudget
  ≤ (15/2)K(E)N^(13δ/640−2D−8)R^(-2)
    + (15/2)N^(-2D−10).
```

Consequently, twice the sum of the drift and full cross integrals is at most

```text
2N^(-3D−10) + 15K(E)N^(13δ/640−2D−8)R^(-2)
  + 15N^(-2D−10).
```

Each term tends to zero for `D≥60`. For the exact T230A′ slot consumer,
`APrimeInit.slotXi' x=x^(1/4)` and `slotQ R=R²/2` make its nonnegative
near-field contribution, after dividing by `R⁴`, equal to
`x^(5/4)/(2 Im(mE))`. Since `x≥1` eventually and `Im(mE)>0`, the consumer is
at least `1/(2 Im(mE))`. The fixed-parameter eventual cutoff absorbs the
displayed first-cell upper bound into this exact lower bound. This argument
does not use a large-`R` premise or assume a strict-transition resident.

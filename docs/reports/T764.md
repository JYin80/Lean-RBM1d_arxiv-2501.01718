## Exact claim and dependencies

For every `W k l : ℕ`, `hkl : k ≤ l`, and actual reference permutation `π : PermΩ W`, the theorem states

```lean
uniformPerm W {ρ | ρ ∈ prefixCell W l π} ≤
  uniformPerm W {ρ | ρ ∈ prefixCell W k π}
```

Here `PermΩ W = Equiv.Perm (Fin W)`, `uniformPerm W` is `uniformOn Set.univ`, and `prefixCell W k π` contains precisely the permutations agreeing with `π` at positions `i.val < k` (T711). T747 proves inclusion of the later actual cell in the earlier one, and the T763 proof applies `measure_mono` to that inclusion. The two direct imports are T736 `PermutationPrefixCellMass` and T747 `PermutationPrefixCellMono`; each imports only the T711 core. This import graph is acyclic. The reference `π` itself lies in every cell, so the universal claim is not made true by empty actual cells.

The `k ≤ l` premise is satisfiable, and the explicit width-two case witnesses genuine decrease in a nontrivial sample space. No additional analytic or probabilistic hypothesis is introduced.

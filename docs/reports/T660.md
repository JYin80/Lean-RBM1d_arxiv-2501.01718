## 6. Corrected topology obligation for `blockEvent`

T655 incorrectly grouped `jG` with continuous finite maxima.  The term

```text
if ellStar W ellu / 2 <= zdist ... then gsqBlk / tailT else 0
```

has a time-dependent hard cutoff.  It is not jointly continuous across cutoff crossings.

Two honest repairs are available, neither of which is proved by this ticket.

### Upper-semicontinuity route

For a fixed block pair, the active time set is closed because `ellStar W (ell u)/2` is
continuous.  The active value is nonnegative and, while `u<1`, continuous in time and the
finite effective coordinates; `tailT` is strictly positive because of its `W^(-D)` floor.
Extending that nonnegative value by zero outside the closed active set is jointly upper
semicontinuous.  A finite `sup'` and addition of one preserve upper semicontinuity.  Since the
right side of `blockEvent` is continuous, the left-minus-right gap is upper semicontinuous.
One then needs the compact-parameter tube lemma: a uniform negative gap on the centre over
the closed time interval gives one effective-coordinate neighbourhood on which the gap stays
negative for every time.

Every step above, especially the joint statement in both time and coordinates, must be
compiled; pointwise upper semicontinuity is insufficient.

### Fixed-far continuous-majorant route

T643 gives more structure at its proposed centre: `gsqBlk(x,y)` is zero unless the block
distance is at most two, while uniformly over the target closed window
`ellStar W ell_u / 2 > 2` eventually.  Under that deterministic separation, every pair in the
literal hard-cutoff supremum belongs to the fixed finite set `2 < zdist(x-y)`.  Therefore
`jG` is bounded by the fixed-far finite supremum of `gsqBlk/tailT`.  This majorant has no
time-dependent predicate and is jointly continuous; at the T643 centre it equals one.

This route still requires compiled versions of the uniform separation, the centre's fixed-far
zero property, the joint continuity of the quotient, and the resulting compact tube.  An
unrestricted all-pair majorant is not automatically useful, because near-pair numerators need
not vanish.

All other literal carrier rows can use ordinary continuity once their stated uniform strict
margins are compiled.  Thus the mathematical gap after the definition extraction remains:
the coupled-block centre/realization, one simultaneous uniform margin package, this corrected
block-row semicontinuity or majorant argument, and the relative-interior cylinder theorem.

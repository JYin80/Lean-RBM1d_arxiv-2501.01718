## Exact process and arithmetic

Fix `W ≥ 2`, bounded tables `x χ : Fin W → ℂ` with `∀ i, ‖x i‖ ≤ 2` and `∀ i, ‖χ i‖ ≤ 1`, and choose `M j` to be either `prefixFourierRealMean W j x χ` or `prefixFourierImagMean W j x χ`. Let `g π` be the matching real or imaginary component of `permutationFourierSum W x χ π`. Use the natural-number process

```lean
Y 0       := fun _ => (0 : ℝ)
Y (i + 1) := fun π => M (i + 1) π - M i π
cY 0       := (0 : NNReal)
cY (i + 1) := (⟨64 / (W : ℝ) ^ 2, by positivity⟩ : NNReal)
```

The successor formula is defined for every natural `i`; after `i ≥ W` it is zero because both full-prefix cells are singletons. The parameter may remain positive there: the theorem is used only at `n=W+1`, so no later term is counted. The process is `StronglyAdapted (prefixFiltration W) Y` for **all** natural times: T877 makes `M (i+1)` measurable at `prefixSigma W (i+1)`, and makes `M i` measurable there by `prefixSigma_mono W` (or `prefixFiltration W |>.mono`). The zero time is strongly measurable. This is a pointwise adaptation proof, not an a.e. replacement of a conditional expectation.

For `i < (W+1)-1`, natural arithmetic gives `i<W`, hence `k : Fin W := ⟨i, hi⟩`. The exact T881 public conditional-MGF statement (once audited) becomes the Azuma premise for `Y (i+1)` at `prefixFiltration W i = prefixSigma W i`. `Y 0=0` and `cY 0=0` discharge the initial premise with `ProbabilityTheory.HasSubgaussianMGF.fun_zero`. Thus the theorem consumes precisely the `W` reveals `i=0,…,W-1`, including the first reveal conditioned on `prefixSigma W 0`; it never asks T881 for `i=W`.

For every sample `π`, `Finset.sum_range_succ'` and `Finset.sum_range_sub` give the **pointwise** identity


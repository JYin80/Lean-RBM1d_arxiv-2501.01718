## Statement and proof audit

The universally quantified `prefixCellNextFiberEquiv W k π v` has the required two sides:

* left: permutations `ρ` in the actual `prefixCell W k.val π` with `ρ k = v`;
* right: `permutationPrefixFiber W k (fun i => π i) v`.

T711 defines membership in the actual cell by `∀ i : Fin W, i.val < k.val → ρ i = π i`. T694 defines membership in the fiber by `∀ i : Fin W, i < k → ρ i = π i`, together with `ρ k = v`. The private `prefixCell_mem_iff_prefix` translates the equivalent `Fin` comparisons. Both maps retain the same underlying permutation, and their inverse laws are definitional. `prefixCellNextFiber_card_eq` therefore gives literal finite-cardinality equality for **every** `W`, `k : Fin W`, actual `π`, and `v`. Neither statement assumes an arbitrary possibly inconsistent prefix function, introduces a denominator, or divides by a zero cardinality.

At `W=0`, `Fin W` has no `k`, so there is no next-image query. At `W=1`, the explicit theorem gives card one for the unique `k`, `π`, and `v`; uniqueness of permutations and values on `Fin 1` makes this the sole case. At `W=2`, `prefixCellNextFiber_two_witness` places the identity in the `v=0` subfiber and the transposition in the `v=1` subfiber at `k=0`. It proves the permutations distinct and both subfiber cardinalities positive. This rules out a merely empty or inconsistent-prefix interpretation of the main equivalence.

The paper's (2.3)–(2.4) are high-probability Green-function local laws. This finite deterministic bridge is only a possible downstream exposure input. It states no `N` or time scale, first-cell bound, common event, conditional law, P2 estimate, or closure of those local laws.

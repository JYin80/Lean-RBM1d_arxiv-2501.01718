# T1119 — full selected high-order expectation at the first positive cell

## Verdict: mathematical preflight PASS; exact `k=1` theorem proved

`RBM.APrimeGeneralMovingHighQK1Full.eventually_selected_high_k1_full` proves
the complete Gaussian expectation at the literal first positive target-mesh
point. For each `0 < δ ≤ δ₀` and `p ≥ 1`, it selects `q ≥ p` with
`32 ≤ 3δq`, then a positive `C`, before the eventual-`N` conclusion. Whenever
`k=1` is active, the estimate is

```text
∫ widenedW(order q, k=1) ·
  |cutTrunc(N^(2δ), jSnorm(endpoint k=1))|^(2q) dP
≤ C N^(δq/2).
```

The integrand retains the actual order-`q` widened weight, the actual
`cutTrunc`, `jSnorm`, `cutNetPt ... 1`, `Dims.exampleGrow`, and `Gauss.P`.
The assumptions are the same general-moving `BoundsCore` and `Cond272Reg`
context used by the accepted good-event producer.

## Producer composition and integrability

The order choice is essential. Set
`pHigh = max(p, ⌈32/(3δ)⌉₊)`. Apply the accepted T1103 good-event theorem
with `pHigh`; its selected order is at least `pHigh`. At that very same
returned `q`, apply T1105's fixed-order bad-event estimate. Thus no estimate
with two different selected orders is combined. The arithmetic gives
`q ≥ p` and `32 ≤ 3δq`.

For all sufficiently large `N`, the fixed endpoint is below one and the
Gaussian measure is a probability measure. `Step2Bootstrap.integrable_weight_mul`
then proves integrability of the full nonnegative integrand from measurability
of `jSnorm`, measurability and the `[0,1]` bounds of the actual `widenedW`, and
the deterministic truncation envelope. `integral_add_compl` splits this same
integrand over the measurable `Gauss.P` event `good N` and its complement.
The good contribution is at most `Cgood N^(δq/2)` by T1103; the bad
contribution is at most `2^(2q) N^(δq/2)` by T1105, whose fixed probability
decay order was selected after this same `δ,q`. Their sum gives the stated
positive constant `Cgood + 2^(2q)` and unchanged exponent.

`nondegenerate_positive_cell_witness` reexports T995's concrete
`Dims.exampleGrow`, `E=0`, `D=60` same-resident witness, with positive moving
window and an eventually active `k=1` cell in the scheduled context. The
witness establishes joint satisfiability; it does not replace the sample in
the universal expectation proof.

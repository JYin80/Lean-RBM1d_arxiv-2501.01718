## Results

`semicircleMeasure_Ioc_cdf` proves, for all real `a,b`,

```lean
semicircleMeasure (Ioc a b) =
  ENNReal.ofReal (ProbabilityTheory.cdf semicircleMeasure b -
    ProbabilityTheory.cdf semicircleMeasure a)
```

It rewrites the probability measure as the Stieltjes measure associated to
its CDF (`ProbabilityTheory.measure_cdf`) and applies
`StieltjesFunction.measure_Ioc`.

`semicircleMeasure_cdf_strictMonoOn` proves strict increase on `[-2,2]`.
For `a < b` in that interval, the open interval `(a,b)` is contained in
`(a,b]`; `semicircleMeasure_Ioo_pos` therefore gives positive half-open mass.
Monotonicity of the CDF explicitly gives
`0 ≤ cdf b - cdf a`. The proof then cancels `ENNReal.ofReal` with
`ENNReal.toReal_ofReal` and uses positivity of the real mass to conclude
`cdf a < cdf b`.

The module also proves concrete nondegenerate checks:

- `semicircleMeasure_Ioc_neg_one_one_pos`: `(-1,1]` has positive mass.
- `semicircleMeasure_Ioc_leftEndpoint_pos`: `(-2,b]` has positive mass for
  every `-2 < b ≤ 2`.
- `semicircleMeasure_Ioc_rightEndpoint_pos`: `(a,2]` has positive mass for
  every `-2 ≤ a < 2`.

Thus the hypotheses have explicit witnesses: `a=-1,b=1` for the interior
case, and `b=0` / `a=0` for the respective endpoint forms.

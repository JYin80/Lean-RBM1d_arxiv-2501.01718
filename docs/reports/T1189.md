For fixed `δ>0`, `q≥1`, and `C₀>0`, assume the literal T999 full order-`q`
`k=0` integral is eventually at most `C₀ N^(δq/2)`. Then one `C>0` and one
eventual threshold in `N` work uniformly for every active index satisfying

```text
N^4 < k,    (k : ℝ) ≤ N^(4+δ/2),    k ≤ cutNetTop.
```

The conclusion is the full order-`q` T999 integral with the same `sample d`,
Gaussian law `B.P`, `widenedW`, canonical `R`, target mesh, `cutTrunc
(N^(2δ))`, `jSnorm`, endpoint, and rate `N^(δq/2)`. It introduces no order
selection and changes no observable. The returned constant is the sum of the
T1177 good-event constant and `2^(2q)`, so it is positive and independent of
`N,k`.

# T722 — Positive support transfer for permutation prefix fibers

**Files:** `RBM1D/Gauss/PermutationFiberSupportTransfer.lean` (new), this report.

**Acceptance:** `lake build RBM1D.Gauss.PermutationFiberSupportTransfer` exit 0 and full `lake build RBM1D` exit 0. The three public declarations and the private emptiness helper were checked with `#print axioms`; they use only `propext`, `Classical.choice`, and `Quot.sound`.

## Result

Imported the accepted T703 `PermutationFiberCount` module. The theorem
`permutationPrefixFiber_card_pos_iff` states, for every `W ≥ 2`, `k`, prescribed
prefix `p`, distinct unused candidates `a,b`, that the `a`-fiber has positive
cardinality iff the `b`-fiber does. Its proof rewrites by T703's exact equality
of fiber cardinalities; no division or conditional law is involved.

The theorem `permutationPrefixFiber_two_support_transfer_witness` retains T703's
explicit `W=2`, `k=0`, identity-prefix witness, where both candidate fibers have
positive cardinality.

The theorem `permutationPrefixFiber_repeatedPrefix_empty_witness` records the
required `W=3`, `k=2`, `p 0 = p 1 = 0`, `a=1`, `b=2` example. Both candidates
are distinct and unused by the prescribed prefix, while both fibers have
cardinality zero: no permutation can map two different inputs to the same
value. Thus unusedness alone does not imply support; the equivalence remains
non-vacuous by its separate `W=2` witness.

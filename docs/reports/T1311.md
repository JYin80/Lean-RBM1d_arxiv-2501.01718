## Proof and interface preflight

The frozen one-step theorem already takes `hB : BoundsCore X E s` and converts its
`APrimeSlot'` producer into `JSNormDom` using `jsNormDom_of_aprimeSlot' ... hB`. The new one-step
theorem instantiates that frozen producer at the existing `hB`. In the merged theorem,
`Thm221NoEL.step` supplies its `hB` to the new producer before applying the one-step assembler.
No new estimate or changed consumer is needed. This also shows why the original universal
`Hy` was unnecessarily stronger than the consumer requires.

The paper preflight is consistent with this being only an assembler. Formula (5.21) is the
hierarchy's Duhamel decomposition, including drift and martingale contributions; (5.24) bounds
the stopped martingale moment by its quadratic variation. Formulas (5.40)–(5.42) provide
normalized drift and quadratic-variation estimates, and (5.43) introduces the stopping time.
Those are mathematical inputs behind a future producer. The Lean field `APrimeSlot'` instead
packages an event-restricted `APrimeHypOn` for `jSnorm` and a `HighProb` event. These assembler
theorems do not formalize (5.21), (5.24), (5.40)–(5.43), identify the event interface with the
paper's stopped process, or claim paper closure. No paper-delta entry is needed: the new
declarations are conditional wiring and do not assert a paper theorem.

## Satisfiability and remaining arrow

No new analytic hypothesis is asserted as an unconditional fact. The `Hy` premise is explicitly
conditional, and this report does not provide a witness for that Gaussian producer. The
nondegenerate premise `BoundsCore` itself is satisfiable in the Gaussian model: the existing
`RBM.APrimeGeneralMovingCommonSources.positive_length_common_support_witness` supplies the same
sample model with `E = 0`, `s ≡ 0`, a positive-length first cell, `Cond272Reg`, `BoundsCore`, and
`Step1.Hyp`. That witness supplies no `APrimeSlot'`; it therefore must not be read as evidence
that `Hy` has been produced.

The remaining arrow is the actual Gaussian producer
`∀ D ≥ 60, BoundsCore X E s → APrimeSlot' X E s t D` on the intended windows, with its required
uniformity and event semantics. This ticket only makes the consumer accept that exact dependency.

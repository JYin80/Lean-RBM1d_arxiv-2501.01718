# T1291 — all-active-cell p=1 N1 drift consumer

## Verdict

**Mathematical preflight PASS; the requested all-active-cell Lean consumer is
proved.** For fixed `E,D,c,δ,s,t` satisfying the stated bulk, regularity, and
loss hypotheses, one eventual cutoff works for every active target-mesh cell
`k` (including `k=0`) and every output `a`. The conclusion is the literal

## Claim and derivation

The public theorem is
`RBM.APrimeGeneralMovingAllCellP1N1Consumer.eventually_all_cell_p1_N1_consumer`.
It assumes `|E|<2`, `D≥60`, `c>0`, `0<δ≤min(1,c/100)`, the moving-window
conditions, `Cond272Reg`, and `BoundsCore`. With

```text
x = N^(δ/8),  v = cutNetPt s (targetMesh D) N k,
R = ratR E s N v,
```

it proves, eventually in `N`, uniformly for all `k≤cutNetTop ... N` and all
`a : LoopArg (d.L N) 2`,

```text
2 ∫_[s_N,v] (T615.g(E,D,deltaWeight δ,p=1,N,k,a,u)
              + positiveTimeCrossBudget(E,D,deltaWeight δ,s,t,N,k,p=1,a,u)) du
  ≤ driftTerm(Im(mE E),x,R,slotXi' x,slotA x R,slotEps x R,
              slotQ R,slotBeta x R,slotGamma x R,slotJv x R) / R^4.
```

The proof invokes the accepted T1265 theorem directly. Its coefficient is
`C(E)=2*((12/Im(mE E)+2)+(15/4)*(|crossProfileBudgetConst E|+2))>0` and its
right side is `C(E)·N^(δ/8)·R^(-2)`. The eventual set intersects that producer
with `2 Im(mE E) C(E) ≤ N^(δ/32)` and `N≥1`; these bounds are fixed before
introducing `k` and `a`.

The concrete slot definitions give `slotXi' x=x^(1/4)` and
`slotQ R=R²/2`. Also `R≥1`. Applying `driftTerm_ge_near` and dividing by `R⁴`,
the exact near term is

```text
slotXi' x · x · Im(mE E)^(-1) · R² · slotQ R / R⁴
  = x^(5/4)/(2 Im(mE E)),
```

with no remaining `R^(-2)`. The `R^(-2)` factor in the T1265 upper bound is
nonnegative and at most one; the Lean proof compares the upper bound first to
`x^(5/4)/(2 Im(mE E))·R^(-2)` and then to the exact near term. Since
`slotXi' x=N^(δ/32)`, the positive exponent gap absorbs the fixed `E`-
dependent coefficient. All other drift-slot terms are nonnegative.

The original dispatch's description of the near term as *exactly* carrying an
extra `R^(-2)` was not the exact slot algebra. The definitions instead cancel
all `R` powers; `R≥1` makes the stated `R^(-2)` version a valid weaker lower
bound. The target consumer itself is unchanged and follows.

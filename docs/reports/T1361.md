# T1361 — arbitrary-Dims QV root and actual-weight integral preflight

## Verdict: FAIL at the current source/API boundary

This is a scope/API failure, not a mathematical counterexample. I found no
reason the estimates should fail for an arbitrary admissible `Gauss.Dims`,
but the exact Lean producers consumed by the requested pair are all specialized
to `Dims.exampleGrow`. The arbitrary-Dims target cannot be proved in the
assigned single new module by applying the available declarations. Making the
direct chain generic would require duplicating or refactoring a substantial
source stack, beginning with T579/T584/T590/T591/T603 and continuing through
their fixed-dimension prerequisites. The assignment forbids edits to those
files and does not authorize that source-chain duplication. I therefore
stopped before writing a Lean proof; no Lean source file was created and no
Lean build was run.

## Existing theorem and exact-consumer check

Repository search found the requested theorem names only in
`APrimeFreeLossQVRoot.lean`. That module binds `d` to
`Dims.exampleGrow` at line 23. Its pointwise theorem at line 417 and its
integral theorem at line 781 are therefore exampleGrow statements, not
arbitrary-Dims counterparts.

The requested integral consumer is exact: it uses actual-weight loss
`lambda`, source loss `h = lambda / 1000`, and absorbed cap `2 * lambda`;
calls T993's exact-profile integral, the complete absorbed root theorem, and
the exact-profile-to-absorbed comparison; then integrates uniformly over all
active cells, including `k = 0`, retaining the endpoint `R_v^(-4)` factor.
No equal-strength arbitrary-Dims theorem with that actual smooth weight,
source schedule, and endpoint was found. Nearby arbitrary-Dims files cover
individual near/far/quadratic rows, while `APrimeGeneralMovingActualDriftSlotAnyD`
still binds its model dimension to `exampleGrow` (`AnyD` there concerns the
mesh exponent `D`). These do not provide the requested QV root or actual-weight
QV integral consumer.

## First fixed-dimension declarations in the dependency chain

1. `APrimeGeneralMovingActualSmoothQVNormBudget.lean:24` sets its private
   `d := Dims.exampleGrow`. Its `weight`, `qv`, `g`, and `Qabs` definitions
   consequently have types over `Omega d`, `P d`, and `LoopArg (d.L N)`.
   The exact-profile integral theorem at line 518 has the same specialization.
   The final wrapper cannot pass `hreg : Cond272Reg (band d) ...` or
   `hB : BoundsCore (sample d) ...` for a variable dimension to this theorem.
2. That exact-profile integral calls the T603 actual-support pointwise
   producer, whose private dimension is also fixed at
   `APrimeGeneralMovingSmoothQVProfile.lean:24`. T603 calls the T590 absorbed
   common-support producer, fixed at
   `APrimeGeneralMovingQVAbsorption.lean:22`, and T584's profile, fixed at
   `APrimeGeneralMovingQVProfile.lean:23`.
3. The same-event input is T579's
   `APrimeGeneralMovingCommonSources.highProb_commonEvent`; its module fixes
   `d := Dims.exampleGrow` at line 29. T591's norm/integral producer also
   fixes `d` at `APrimeGeneralMovingQVNormBudget.lean:25`.

These are actual declaration types, not merely proof-local choices. A wrapper
that states `forall d : Dims` while invoking them would still prove only the
exampleGrow case; it cannot be transported to general `d` without a dimension
equality that is absent from, and not implied by, the theorem hypotheses.
The directly relevant source modules alone comprise 2,991 lines (T579,
T584, T590, T591, T603, and T993), before their fixed-dimension imports are
generalized. That is the source-chain duplication/refactor excluded by this
ticket's ownership boundary.

## Existing accepted proof inventory and source scope

- T590's accepted report records the exact deterministic absorption: the
  near `2/W` residual, full absorbed far coefficient, spatial indicator,
  propagated endpoint leakage, and endpoint normalization are retained.
- T993's accepted report records the literal actual smooth weight, exact
  QV-profile integral and complement payment, with all active cells including
  `k = 0` handled after integration. The accepted construction is an
  actual-smooth-prefix replacement for the paper's stopped martingale
  argument, not a formalization of the stopping-time statement itself.
- The existing free-loss wrapper composes those accepted results at the
  corrected same-loss schedule. Its root and integral conclusions inherit
  the `exampleGrow` specialization.

I visually checked page 58 of `paper/250520-YinJun-v2.pdf`: (5.42) is the
two-loop propagated QV bound with the near spatial indicator, full
`(eta_s/eta_u)^4 (ell_u/ell_s)^5` factor, and far `(... )^(-1/3) (J^*)^3`
term; (5.43) defines the stopping time by `J^* >= (eta_s/eta_t)^4`; (5.44)
integrates the stopped QV and retains the endpoint ratio and spatial indicator.
The paper's (2.2) bandwidth condition does supply eventual `N <= W^2` for
arbitrary `d`; this preflight did not find a mathematical obstruction in that
schedule step. The blocker is the specialized Lean source API above.

The existing reports describe exampleGrow positive-cell witnesses, including
a positive actual smooth weight in T993. I did not construct the additionally
requested joint witness proving nonzero actual source/observable and positive
event probability, because that witness would not repair the missing
arbitrary-Dims producer chain and the proof was stopped at preflight.

# T859 — actual finite Fourier Doob increment: read-only preflight

## Verdict

**PASS as a mathematical/API preflight, not a Lean proof or an acceptance of T853.** For every `W ≥ 2`, `k : Fin W`, actual permutation `π`, and bounded complex tables `x, χ : Fin W → ℂ` with `‖x i‖ ≤ 2` and `‖χ i‖ ≤ 1`, the finite prefix-cell averages of `F(ρ) = permutationFourierSum W x χ ρ` satisfy, pointwise,

\[
 \left\|M_{k+1}(\pi)-M_k(\pi)\right\|\leq 8/W,
 \qquad M_j(\pi)=\frac{1}{|C_j(\pi)|}\sum_{\rho\in C_j(\pi)}F(\rho),
 \quad C_j(\pi)=\{\rho: \rho(i)=\pi(i)\text{ for }i<j\}.
\]

Both denominators are positive because `π` belongs to its actual cells (`mem_prefixCell`, `prefixCell_card_pos`). This theorem requires no zero-character-sum hypothesis. It is a deterministic increment bound for the actual Fourier observable, not T842's different centered increment of a fixed function of `π k`.

## Finite proof with positive fibers

Fix the actual prefix of `π` at `k`, and put `U = unusedImages W k π`. For `v ∈ U` set

\[
 A_v=\{\rho\in C_k(\pi):\rho(k)=v\},\quad
 n_v=|A_v|>0,\quad m_v=n_v^{-1}\sum_{\rho\in A_v}F(\rho).
\]

The `A_v` form a **disjoint partition** of `C_k(π)`: every completion has exactly one next image, and that image is unused by injectivity. The actual next image `π(k)` belongs to `U`; its fiber contains `π`. Every other `v ∈ U` has a positive fiber: `prefixCellNextFiber_card_pos_of_unused` transports the positive actual fiber's cardinality to `v`. The bridge `prefixCellNextFiberEquiv` identifies this actual-cell fiber with `permutationPrefixFiber W k (fun i => π i) v`, so T704's accepted `norm_permutationFourierSum_prefixFiber_mean_sub_le` applies. In particular, for all `v,w ∈ U`, `‖m_v-m_w‖ ≤ 8/W` (the `v=w` case is zero). `prefixCellNextFiber_card_eq_of_unused` says all these positive `n_v` are equal; `unusedImages_card` gives `|U|=W-k.val>0`.

More generally, the partition gives the **cardinality-weighted convex identity**

\[
 M_k(\pi)=\sum_{v\in U}\frac{n_v}{|C_k(\pi)|}m_v,
 \qquad \sum_{v\in U}\frac{n_v}{|C_k(\pi)|}=1.
\]

Here the weights are nonnegative and well-defined; by equal cards each is also `1/(W-k.val)`. The successor cell is exactly `C_{k+1}(π)=A_{π(k)}`, including when `k.val+1=W`, so `M_{k+1}(π)=m_{π(k)}`. Subtract the displayed convex identity and use the triangle inequality:

\[
 \|M_{k+1}(\pi)-M_k(\pi)\|
 \leq\sum_{v\in U}\frac{n_v}{|C_k(\pi)|}
   \|m_{\pi(k)}-m_v\|
 \leq (8/W)\sum_{v\in U}\frac{n_v}{|C_k(\pi)|}=8/W.
\]

Thus the range lemma alone does **not** already state the Doob bound: the missing deterministic producer is the arbitrary-`ℂ` **sum** partition and successor-cell identification. The current `prefixCell_card_eq_sum_unused_nextFibers` supplies the cardinality partition, and its proof has a private sigma equivalence, but there is no public arbitrary-Fourier-sum version in the inspected sources. The future worker can expose/prove that sum identity and the successor-cell equality in one narrow module, then use T704. No conditional-expectation theorem is needed for this pointwise estimate.

## Boundaries and nondegeneracy

* `k=0`: `prefixCell_eq_univ_of_zero` makes `M_0` the global permutation mean, with a positive denominator. A nonzero Fourier character can later make that mean zero, but mean-zero is not used here.
* `k.val+1=W`: `unusedImages_card_last` gives `|U|=1`, so the sole fiber is the whole `C_k(π)` and `M_{k+1}(π)=M_k(π)` exactly. The final reveal has zero increment. This also covers `W=1, k=0` if the conclusion is separately stated without the `W≥2` range-lemma premise; the unique permutation makes every prefix mean equal to `F(π)`.
* `W=0`: `Fin 0` has no next-reveal index (`no_next_index_zero`); its unique permutation still makes each actual prefix cell a singleton. No `8/W` assertion is formed.
* `W=2`: take `x=(2,-2)`, `χ=(1,-1)`, identity `e`, and transposition `s`. Both empty-prefix next-image fibers are positive (`permutationPrefixFiber_meanRange_two_witness` and `prefixCellNextFiber_two_witness`). Directly, `F(e)=2`, `F(s)=-2`, `M_0(e)=M_0(s)=0`, `M_1(e)=2`, `M_1(s)=-2`, and `M_2=F`. Hence the first increments are `+2,-2` and the last increment is zero. This is a nonconstant, nonvacuous instance of the proposed bound (`8/W=4`). The `8/W` **fiber-range** constant is attained here, while the Doob increment need not attain it.
* An arbitrary prescribed prefix can be inconsistent: at `W=3, k=2`, `p(0)=p(1)=0` makes the prescribed fibers empty even when their proposed next images are unused in the syntactic sense. The theorem must use an **actual** prefix cell, or explicitly require positive fiber cardinality before division. It cannot interpret Lean's empty-fiber `0/0` as a conditional mean.

The coordinate consequences are pointwise `|Re(M_{k+1}-M_k)| ≤ 8/W` and `|Im(M_{k+1}-M_k)| ≤ 8/W`, by `Complex.abs_re_le_norm` and `Complex.abs_im_le_norm`. Because real and imaginary parts commute with these finite sums and positive real-cardinality divisions, these are exactly the corresponding bounds for the real and imaginary prefix-cell means.

## Statements and witnesses

`abs_re_permutationFourierSum_swap_le` and `abs_im_permutationFourierSum_swap_le` bound the absolute real and imaginary components of the same normalized complex swap difference by `8 / W` for every `W ≥ 2`, permutation, pair of positions, and tables satisfying the pointwise bounds `‖x i‖ ≤ 2` and `‖χ i‖ ≤ 1`. Each follows from the corresponding complex component inequality `|z.re| ≤ ‖z‖` or `|z.im| ≤ ‖z‖`, then T676's complex theorem; the normalization remains exactly `W⁻¹`.

The two sharp `W=2` witnesses are explicit and nonconstant:

- Real component: `x=(2,-2)`, `χ=(1,-1)`, identity permutation, and swap positions `0,1` give absolute component `4 = 8/2`.
- Imaginary component: `x=(2I,-2I)`, `χ=(1,-1)`, with the same permutation and positions give absolute component `4 = 8/2`.

Both witnesses include proofs of the pointwise bounds and `x 0 ≠ x 1`, `χ 0 ≠ χ 1`. Thus the hypotheses are jointly satisfiable and nondegenerate. No assumptions beyond those in the underlying T676 bound are introduced.

Their finite intersection has complement at most `4N^-(Dprob+2)≤N^-Dprob` eventually, hence is nonempty by `HighProb.nonempty` (`Defs/StochDom.lean:342`). The norm/entry events and the Step-1 loop events all concern the **same** `ω∈Gauss.Ω Dims.exampleGrow`; no transition sample or separate nonempty event is used. The Step-1 `StochDom` already has running time and `LoopData` inside its index, so no further `N^248` or `L^6` union factor is charged there. Intersecting their closed sublevel sets over all running times remains closed, hence measurable. This avoids relying on measurability of an uncountable intersection of arbitrary indicated-loop events; its high probability follows by the same-sample inclusion from the Step-1 indicated event and the entry event. A Lean theorem packaging that closed common event is still to be written.

This positive-time witness is nondegenerate: `Dims.exampleGrow` has growing `W,L` and a genuinely random `X`; `h_N>0` is a real running time inside the genuine first paper cell.

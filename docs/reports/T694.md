## Result

For `W ≥ 2`, `k : Fin W`, a prescribed prefix `p`, and distinct `a,b` unused among `p i` for `i < k`, `permutationPrefixFiberSwap` is an equivalence between completions with `π k = a` and those with `π k = b`. Its underlying map is exactly `π.trans (Equiv.swap a b)`. The prefix lemma proves that every revealed value is fixed; the involution lemma proves that the same map is its inverse. Neither fiber is assumed nonempty. A separate concrete witness at `W=2`, `k=0`, `a=0`, `b=1` exhibits the identity in the first fiber and the transposition in the second.

The optional Fourier corollary identifies the codomain swap with a domain swap at `π.symm a` and `π.symm b`, then applies T676's bound `8/W` under `‖x i‖ ≤ 2` and `‖χ i‖ ≤ 1`.

## Assumptions and boundary

All hypotheses are finite deterministic data. The unused-value hypotheses are satisfiable in the displayed `W=2` example, and the two completion fibers are genuinely occupied. There are no new probabilistic, Gaussian, matrix, time, carrier, or paper-level assumptions. This file does not prove conditional expectation bounds, an Azuma tail, P2, a first-cell event, or any instance of (2.3)–(2.4).

## Mathematical and signature audit

The adapter keeps arbitrary `n : ℕ`, including `n = 0`. It splits
`LoopArg L (n+n)` with `Fin.castAdd n` and `Fin.natAdd n`, and the two simp laws
recover both halves of `Fin.append`. `eeArg` passes the *same* `σ : Fin n → Bool`
to `Gauss.toIdx` on each half and then to the original `Gauss.eeTens d N z M`.
Thus the underlying `d.L N`, `d.W N`, matrix, and spectral parameter are unchanged.
The doubled `(2n+2)` glued-loop indexing of Definition 5.4, (5.22)–(5.23), is
upstream in `eeTens`; the adapter itself has two length-`n` label arguments.
No time or window parameter, first-cell restriction, event, or new assumption appears.
The separate conditions (2.72) and estimate (5.42) are not claimed here.

`#check @` confirmed all six public signatures after re-export, along with the
unchanged `norm_eeArg_le` and direct downstream consumer
`RBM.MomentDuhamel.eeFun`. In particular `eeArg_append` still states, for every
`n : ℕ`, that the doubled `Fin.append a a'` is sent to
`eeTens d N z M (toIdx σ a) (toIdx σ a')`.
The probe also elaborated all three append laws at `n = 0`.

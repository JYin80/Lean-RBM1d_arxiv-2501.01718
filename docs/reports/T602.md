# T602 — post-T596 analytic preflight

## Verdict

T596 is a correct algebraic reduction, but the current pre-Step-3 library does **not** supply
the three uniform all-charge coordinate estimates needed to absorb its right-hand side.  In
particular, the available general-moving `APrime` initial, drift, and quadratic-variation (QV)
packages all specialize to `Step2.sigPM`; they cannot be applied to the `LoopData (L_N) 2`
family in T596 and cannot control the independent `(+,+)` charge.

The first honest dispatchable analytic theorem is therefore the **transported-initial row**
stated in §6 below.  It is all-charge, includes `(+,+)`, is valid at the boundary `v=s_N`, and
uses only `BoundsCore.LmK 2` plus the generic moment/kernel infrastructure.  It does not pretend
to close T596.  The decisive unresolved implication is the raw drift row: at length two it
contains the self-quadratic term

\[
  A_u^{-3}\eta_u^{-1}\,\Xi^{(L-K)}_{u,2}{}^2,
\]

so a bound for it from the present raw Duhamel RHS would be circular.  The next genuinely new
analytic construction after the initial row must retain the cutoff/stopping information in the
drift identity (or prove an equivalent localized bound); it cannot invoke (2.77).

## 2. Exact T596 reduction

Write

\[
 A_x=W_N\ell_x\eta_x,\qquad R_{u,v}=\frac{1-u}{1-v}.
\]

For `q : LoopData (L_N) 2`, `twoCoordDuhamelRhs d E s N p v q` is exactly

\[
\begin{aligned}
 I_p(q)&=\|U_{s_N,v}^{\xi(q_1)}(L-K)_{s_N,q}\|_{2p},\\
 D_p(q)&=2\int_{s_N}^{v}
       \|U_{u,v}^{\xi(q_1)}F_{u,q}\|_{2p}\,du,\\
 Q_p(q)&=\left(c_{\rm MD}'(p,0)\int_{s_N}^{v}
       \|U_{u,v}^{\xi_2(q_1)}(E\otimes E)_{u,(q,q)}\|_{p}\,du\right)^{1/2},
\end{aligned}
\]

and is `I_p(q)+D_p(q)+Q_p(q)`.  For `1 <= p` and `s_N < v`, T596 proves

\[
 \int |\operatorname{cutTrunc}(N^{2\delta}A_{s_N}^{1/2},
       \Xi^{(L-K)}_{v,2})|^{2p}
 \le \sum_q\{A_v^2(I_p(q)+D_p(q)+Q_p(q))\}^{2p}.
\]

The family cardinality is not an asymptotic estimate but the exact identity

\[
  \#\operatorname{LoopData}(L_N,2)=4L_N^2.
\]

For `p=0`, `xiLK_two_cut_moment_zero` gives the exact integral `1`.  T596 has a nonempty
positive-mesh witness with `q=(ppCharge,a)`, so the independent `(+,+)` sector and the condition
`s_N<v` are genuine rather than vacuous.

## 4. Exact scaling ledger

### Initial row

For every two-charge word, `norm_xiOf_mSigma` gives coordinate modulus one.  The generic kernel
Minkowski bound therefore has the exact two-coordinate cost

\[
 \|U_{s_N,v}^{\xi(q_1)}A\|\le R_{s_N,v}^{2}\max_b\|A_b\|.
\]

`BoundsCore.LmK 2` supplies the source scale `A_{s_N}^{-2}`.  Hence the transported normalized
row has the exact ledger

\[
 A_v^2 I_P(q)
 \ \lesssim\ N^\tau A_v^2 R_{s_N,v}^2 A_{s_N}^{-2}
 =N^\tau\left(R_{s_N,v}\frac{A_v}{A_{s_N}}\right)^2
 =N^\tau\left(\frac{\ell_v}{\ell_{s_N}}\right)^2.
\]

Thus the `W_N` and `eta` factors cancel exactly in the last expression.  `Cond272` gives
`R_{s_N,v}^{30} <= A_v`, while scale monotonicity gives `A_v <= A_{s_N}`.  Eventually
`1 <= A_{s_N}`, so this row weakens to

\[
 A_v^2 I_P(q)\lesssim N^\tau A_{s_N}^{1/15}
 \le N^\tau A_{s_N}^{1/2}.
\]

This computation is unchanged at `v=s_N`: then `R=1`, `A_v=A_{s_N}`, and the exact normalized
cost is one.

### Drift row and the obstruction

For `n=0`, the literal definition is

\[
 \operatorname{xiRhs}(2)=\Xi^{(L-K)}_1
   +A_u^{-1}\Xi^{(L-K)}_2{}^2+\Xi^{(L)}_3.
\]

After the arbitrary-charge two-coordinate kernel estimate, the main raw drift profile is

\[
 2A_v^2\int_{s_N}^{v}R_{u,v}^{2}A_u^{-2}\eta_u^{-1}
 \left(\Xi^{(L-K)}_{u,1}+A_u^{-1}\Xi^{(L-K)}_{u,2}{}^2
       +\Xi^{(L)}_{u,3}\right)du,
\]

apart from the stated spatial-leakage term in `norm_driftF_le`.  The middle summand is exactly
`2 A_v^2 integral R^2 A_u^-3 eta_u^-1 XiLK_2^2`.  T596 obtained its RHS only after replacing
the cutoff by the raw variable (`cutTrunc_le_self`), so its drift term retains no cutoff-support
information with which to linearize that square.  Assuming the desired all-charge estimate here
would be the forbidden Step-3 input in another form.

### QV row

The compiled arbitrary-charge endpoint kernel power is exactly

\[
  R_{u,v}^{4}.
\]

At `n=0`, the deterministic source delivered by `stochDom_norm_eeFun_det` has scale
`A_u^-4 eta_u^-1 Psi_E(N)`, where `Psi_E` must dominate the Step-1 quantity `xiL_6`.
Consequently the unabsorbed QV ledger is

\[
 A_v^2\left(c_{\rm MD}'(P,0)\int_{s_N}^{v}
 R_{u,v}^{4}A_u^{-4}\eta_u^{-1}\Psi_E(N)\,du\right)^{1/2}.
\]

This records the exact `A`, `eta`, time-ratio, and moment-order powers.  Turning the Step-1
`xiL_6` estimate into the required deterministic `Psi_E`, doing the moment bridge, and
integrating this moving profile uniformly over the mesh remain unassembled.  No current
all-charge theorem states the needed final `N^(epsilon/4) A_s^(1/2)` bound.

## 5. The `4 L_N^2` cost and correct moment order

A same-order coordinate estimate cannot simply hide `4L_N^2` in `N^(epsilon p)` for arbitrary
small `epsilon` (already `p=1` shows the problem).  The correct device is to prove the coordinate
rows at a higher order `P`, apply T596 at `P`, and then use Lyapunov to descend to the requested
order `p`.

Choose, after `epsilon>0` and `p>=1`, an integer

\[
 P\ge p,\qquad P\ge 4/\epsilon.
\]

If every normalized coordinate row at order `P` is at most
`K N^(epsilon/4) A_s^(1/2)`, then the family estimate and
`(x+y+z)^(2P) <= (3 max{x,y,z})^(2P)` give

\[
 4L_N^2(3K)^{2P}N^{\epsilon P/2}A_s^P.
\]

After the `p/P` Lyapunov power, the exact family cost is

\[
 (4L_N^2)^{p/P},
\]

not `4L_N^2`.  From `LKDecayQuant.eventually_L_le`, eventually `L_N<=N`, and hence

\[
 (4L_N^2)^{p/P}\le 4^{p/P}N^{2p/P}
 \le 4N^{\epsilon p/2}.
\]

The remaining `N^(epsilon p/2)` comes from the three row estimates, yielding the requested
`N^(epsilon p) A_s^p`.  One may take the final constant `C=4(3K)^(2p)` (or a harmless larger
positive constant).  This explains why a future absorption theorem must quantify

`delta0`, then `delta`, then `epsilon`, then the target `p`, choose `P` internally, then choose
`C`, followed by `eventually N` and **all** mesh points.  For `p=0`, it must use T596's exact
zero-moment theorem instead of the high-moment argument.

## 7. Boundary, event/sample, and satisfiability requirements

* **Boundary `v=s_N`:** T596's positive-net theorem must not be called.  The proposed initial
  theorem includes the boundary directly.  In a later full absorption theorem, handle this
  branch from `BoundsCore.LmK 2` and the initial moment bridge; use T596 only for `s_N<v`.
* **Positive grid:** for `s_N<v`, use the existing positive-mesh/domain witness and the T596
  theorem.  The mesh contains both kinds of points, so neither branch may be dropped.
* **`p=0`:** use `xiLK_two_cut_moment_zero`, choose (for example) `C=1`, and use eventual
  positivity of the right side.  Do not send `p=0` through BDG or Lyapunov.
* **Event/sample bookkeeping:** T596 is an unconditional integral under the single measure
  `(band d).P`.  Any future high-probability source must be converted to an unconditional
  `momNorm` by splitting its complement and using the deterministic polynomial envelope;
  the high-probability exponent is chosen after the high moment order `P`.  No event parameter
  may survive in the row hypothesis.  A PM event cannot be reused for `(+,+)`.
* **`BoundsCore`:** the same `sample d`, energy `E`, and left endpoint `s` must be threaded from
  `hB.LmK` into the initial row.  No change of sample or silent re-anchoring is allowed.
* **Nondegeneracy:** take `d=Dims.exampleGrow`, `E=0`, and derive directly from
  `cond272Reg_grid_step_domain` the first positive grid step under terminal cap `1/2`.  Its left
  endpoint is `s_N=0`, it has `Cond272Reg` for some `c>0`, and it has `s_N<t_N` eventually;
  `BoundsCore_zero` supplies the same sample at that left endpoint.  Taking a positive mesh
  point and `q=(ppCharge,a)` witnesses the window, mesh, hypotheses, and independent charge
  index.  However, because `s_N=0`, the transported initial value itself is the zero probe;
  this witness must not be advertised as a nonzero coordinate.  A genuinely nonzero
  positive-left-endpoint witness would require a separate noncircular `BoundsCore`
  construction not presently identified.  The proof should call the grid-domain theorem
  itself, not a first-cell moment producer.

# T313 — read-only preflight of the actual `Qb` in the A′ first-cell slot

## Verdict

`APrimeSlotArith.fitLhs` has no produced Gaussian drift envelope at present. The
available T268 bounds concern the cross-term integral and its near QV input;
they do not bound the independent `tc * Qb` term. There is a plausible
noncircular first-cell route through the model's actual drift, but its required
uniform, spatially normalized, weighted moment estimate is not a current
theorem. Consequently T287's positive `fitLhs ≺ 1` assertion is not ready for
dispatch. This is an interface gap, not a counterexample to Gaussian drift
control.

## What `Qb` must bound

`APrimeSlotArith.fitLhs` (`Gauss/APrimeSlotArith.lean:303`) is exactly

```text
2 [ tc Qb + 4 N^(εs−2δ) (η_tc/η_0)^2 sqrt(2 Qm/(Im m)) ].
```

`drift_bound_first_cell_slot` (:470–481), via
`APrimeModel.drift_bound_first_cell` (:333–356), uses
`∀u∈[0,tc], Adr(u)≤Qb` and therefore replaces `∫ Adr` by `tc Qb`.
Here `Adr(u)` is the weighted `2p`-moment norm of the sample drift of the
**same fixed endpoint coordinate**, as specified in `APrimeOneStep.lean:239`.
The concrete choice is

```text
Adr_N,p(u) = momNormW(P,w,p,driftAt(d,E,D,N,σ,a,0,v,u)),
driftAt = ‖Uker_{u,v}(DriftDef.driftF_u)_a‖ / [T_{v,D}(a)(η_0/η_v)^4].
```

The latter definition is `APrimeDriftTimeFamily.lean:65–89`; it is not a free
drift field. Its `continuousOn_momNormW_driftAt` and
`intervalIntegrable_momNormW_driftAt` (:186–237) imply a *finite* constant
envelope on each fixed compact window, but provide no bound uniform in `N`
or `p`. The natural `Qb_N,p := sup_{0≤u≤v} Adr_N,p(u)` therefore satisfies
`hAbd` at finite size, with no established `Qb_N,p ≺ 1`. The `≺ 1` notation
allows `N^ε` for every ε>0; it does not demand convergence to zero.

The real two-loop drift decomposition is available without a
`SumZeroDyn.Hierarchy`: `EGDef.F_eq_eGpm_add_quadGlue`
(`Hierarchy/EGDef.lean:694`) identifies the `(+,-)` drift as the (5.51)
error plus the (5.49) quadratic gluing term, assuming an actual
`MomentDuhamel.Hyp`. `EGDef.eGpm_le_reduced` (:267) gives a spatially
decaying conditional model estimate for the error. It still requires the
listed (2.73), (5.54), (5.31), (4.2), and row-sum inputs for the *same*
flow matrix. `Step2.norm_Uker_flow` (`Hierarchy/Step2.lean:712`) transports
a `T_u` profile to `T_v` with `(η_u/η_v)^2 ξ_K`, conditional on an input
profile. `DriftBound.norm_driftF_le'` (`Hierarchy/DriftBound.lean:461`)
is another real, pointwise raw-drift producer, but its sup-norm bound
contains `A_u^(-2) η_u^(-1)` plus a length/error term; by itself it does
not preserve the pair-dependent `T_v(a)` needed after division by
`driftScale`. Neither path currently concludes the weighted norm bound
for `driftAt` on one nonempty Good/soft-support event. The normalization
and the event cannot be omitted.

## Exponent and time audit

Write `m = Im m(E)>0`, `η_u=m(1-u)`, `ℓ_u=min((1-u)^(-1/2),L)`,
`A_u=W ℓ_u η_u`, and `R=η_s/η_v=(1-s)/(1-v)`.

* **First cell `s=0`, `0<v≤1/2`.** `tc=v≤1/2`, `1≤R≤2`,
  `1/2≤η_u/m≤1`, `1≤ℓ_u≤√2`, and in fact
  `mW/√2≤A_u≤mW`. Hence the time multiplier and both endpoint
  ratios cost only constants, independent of `N`. If a same-family
  `Qb_N,p ≺ 1` is proved, then `v Qb_N,p ≺ 1`. If also `Qm_N,p ≺ 1`
  and `εs<2δ`, the second term of `fitLhs` is `≺ N^(εs−2δ)` and
  is absorbed. T268's `s=0` estimate uses
  `∫_0^v u^(-1/2)du=2√v≤√2`; no `log(v/0)` is invoked.
  `slotDrift` has the lower bound `N^(δ/4)/(4m)` at
  `x=N^(δ/8)` (`APrimeSlotArith.lean:333–357`), so `fitLhs≺1`
  is a sufficient asymptotic target, stronger than the bare numerical
  slot inequality.
* **General cell `0<s<v<1`.** The literal `fitLhs` theorem is a
  first-cell theorem and has no generic-cell instance. In a translated
  constant-envelope argument, the time length would be
  `Δ=v-s=(η_s−η_v)/m`, hence `Δ/η_v=(R−1)/m`.
  The near part of the paper's *unnormalized* evolved drift estimate
  (5.41) has rate
  `η_u^(-1)(η_u/η_v)^2(ℓ_u/ℓ_s)^2`. Since
  `(ℓ_u/ℓ_s)^2≤η_s/η_u`, its pointwise supremum is at most
  `η_s/η_v²`; multiplying by `Δ` gives
  `R(R−1)/m = O(R²)`. The actual `driftAt` divides this by
  `R^4`, so its corresponding `Δ Qb` near contribution is bounded
  by `(R−1)/(m R³)=O(R^(-2))`. On the paper's grid
  `R=W^τ′`, the raw cost is `W^(2τ′)` and the normalized cost
  is `W^(-2τ′)`, before stochastic `N^ε` losses. The bandwidth
  bounds put these at `N` exponents between
  `(1+2c)τ′` and `2τ′` in absolute value.

  The repository's sharper reduced (5.35) uses
  `(ℓ_u/ℓ_s)^3` instead (`Lemma57.lean:85`,
  `Step2Near47.lean:107`). Its *pointwise supremum* is at most
  `η_s^(-1) R^(5/2)`, so the constant-envelope route gives
  `Δ Qb ≤ m^(-1) R^(-3/2)(1−R^(-1))` after the same `R^4`
  normalization, up to its common stochastic factor.
  `Step2Near47.integral_driftNearInt_le` integrates the reduced
  near profile more sharply, at raw `O(R²)`; that improvement
  does not itself produce the constant `Qb` demanded by `fitLhs`.
  For comparison, a rate containing only `η_u^(-1)` would cost
  `(R−1)/m` by a supremum and `(log R)/m` by integration,
  before normalization. Those are different integrands from (5.41).
* **Scale condition.** `Cond272Reg` (`Flow/Thm221Bare.lean:126`)
  supplies `R^30≤A_v` and, separately, `N^c≤A_v`; it does not
  supply their product. Its valid interpolation spends an inverse
  power of `A_v` only when `e/c+b/30≤a` for a row
  `N^e R^b/A_v^a`. The raw near drift row `R²` has `a=0`;
  its smallness comes from the **actual** `R^(-4)` coordinate
  normalization, not from `Cond272Reg`. The far drift
  pieces in `EGDef.eGpm_le_reduced` do carry inverse powers
  `A_u^(-1/2)` and `A_u^(-1)` and must be checked rowwise
  **after** their spatial-tail and event assumptions are supplied;
  the separate-scale condition alone is no replacement for those
  model estimates. On a grid point `v=1-W^(-(k+1)τ′)`, one has
  `η_v=mW^(-(k+1)τ′)` and
  `A_v=mW min(W^((k+1)τ′/2),L)W^(-(k+1)τ′)`,
  which displays both unsaturated and saturated regimes without
  assuming a nonexistent product margin.

## Why the present integral results cannot supply `Qb`

`APrimeTimeInt.integral_early_le_budget`,
`integral_inv_sqrt_mul_sqrt_kappa_le`, and the `qHatNear` variant
control `∫u^(-1/2)√(κ Q_u)du` from their QV/rate inputs; none has
`Adr` or `Qb` in its conclusion. T294 proves finite-size time
integrability of the actual evolved QV, and T300 transports a
conditional pointwise endpoint QV estimate. They do not estimate the
weighted drift supremum or a quantitative QV integral on a common
event. Paper (2.71) is an input expectation bound; (2.73) is the
Step 1 loop estimate. The drift estimate needed here is in
(5.40)–(5.41), not (2.71) alone.

`APrimeExponents.not_fitLhs_detDom_for_arbitrary_Qb`
(`Gauss/APrimeExponents.lean:364`) compiles the interface
counterexample `tc=1/4`, `Qm=0`, `Qb=N²`, giving `fitLhs=N²/2`.
Even adding continuity and a small time integral would not repair the
supremum step: on `[0,1/4]`, the nonnegative triangular rate
`Adr_N(u)=N² max(1−N⁴|u−1/8|,0)` has integral `N^(-2)`
for all large `N`, while its required constant envelope is at least
`N²`. This is a nonzero deterministic interface example, not a
claim that the Gaussian model realizes such spikes.

## Narrowest next theorem and non-vacuity gate

For T287, first restrict to the actual `sigPM` two-loop, fixed
`0<v≤1/2`, `driftAt` of T292, and the *same* weighted coordinate
and event as the A′ moment step. Prove, for each fixed `p` and every
ε>0, a constant `C_{p,ε}` and eventual `N` such that

```text
sup_{0≤u≤v} momNormW(P,w_N,p,driftAt_N(u)) ≤ C_{p,ε} N^ε.
```

Build this from the actual `F=eGpm+quadGlue` identity, the
conditional reduced (5.35)/(5.34) spatial profiles, and the
`Uker` tail transport; then pass the pathwise good-event estimate to
the weighted moment **with its polynomial bad-event control**. This
would provide `Qb` and `tc Qb≺1` on the first cell. Alternatively,
the weaker but directly useful target is the same-family integrated
`2∫_0^v Adr ≤ slotDrift−(cross budget)`, bypassing the
supremum-based `fitLhs` producer; it needs a new first-cell wiring
theorem and a genuine quantitative drift integral. Neither target
can be discharged by T268 alone or by choosing `Qb` arbitrarily.

The existing `APrimeDriftTimeFamily.first_cell_witness` gives a
strictly positive interval, positive normalization, and positive
cross rate, but not a simultaneous realization of the Step 1 loop,
tail, QV, and Good/soft-support hypotheses. Any positive producer
must exhibit or inherit a nonempty common event and avoid using the
pending A′ conclusion itself. No such claim is made here.

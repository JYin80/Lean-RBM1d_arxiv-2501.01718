`p_{σ,α}(z) = (λ_α - z_σ)⁻¹`, where `z_true = z` and `z_false = star z`. For every finite

```text
∑ₓ (Gsig(Hm,z,σ₁)²)ₓₓ S°ₓᵧ Gsig(Hm,z,σ₂)ᵧᵧ
  = N⁻¹ ∑_{α,γ} p_{σ₁,α}(z)² p_{σ₂,γ}(z) M_{y,α} |ψ_γ(y)|²,
```

with `M_{y,α} = blockM Hm a₀ α = N ∑ₓ |ψ_α(x)|² S°ₓᵧ` by `blockM_eq`. The associated norm bound

```text
  ≤ N⁻¹ (∑α |p_{σ₁,α}(z)|² |M_{y,α}|)
```

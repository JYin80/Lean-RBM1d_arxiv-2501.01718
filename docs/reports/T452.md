## 6. Boundary and quantifier audit

* **`k=0`.**  `prefixMatrix` is constant zero, so `prefixGradient=0`; also
  `v=u_0=0` and every time integral is empty.  T333's positive-`k` theorem
  is not invoked.
* **`k=1`.**  The only stored index is `j=0`.  Its pullback factor is
  `sqrt(u_0)=0`, so `prefixGradient=0` exactly.

  multiplies it by zero.  No division by `u_0` occurs.
* **`k=2`.**  The range is `j=0,1`; `u_1>0` is the first possible nonzero
  gradient contribution.  T447's positive plateau carries `j=0` and `j=1`
  for every label on the same T434 resident, with weight one.
* **Event nonemptiness.**  The T434/T447 positive `k=2` plateau gives an
  eventual resident of the exact sharp common event.  T361's transition
  subset need not itself be inhabited: if it is empty, the cross term is
  identically zero.  On every transition sample, positivity of the cutoff
  supplies the T438/T447 support hypothesis.
* **Order.**  Choose the actual first-cell `tau'` and `c` first, then
  `delta=c/1000`, the fixed moment `P`, `nu<=delta/100`, and the source
  losses.  Choose the complement exponent `D` after `P`.  The eventual
  `N` threshold is then uniform in `omega`, active `k`, stored `j`, running
  `r`, and output label `a`.

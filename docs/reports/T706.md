# T706 — positive-fiber Fourier mean range

## Files and scope

## Result

The theorem `norm_permutationFourierSum_prefixFiber_mean_sub_le` proves that
for every `W ≥ 2`, `k`, prefix map `p`, distinct unused candidates `a,b`,
tables `x,χ` satisfying `‖x i‖ ≤ 2` and `‖χ i‖ ≤ 1`, and a positive
`a`-fiber cardinality, the norm of the difference between the two normalized
finite sums of `permutationFourierSum` over the `a` and `b` fibers is at most
`8/(W:ℝ)`. The `a`-fiber cardinality is required positive before division.

The proof first applies `permutationPrefixFiber_card_eq` to identify the
denominators, then uses `permutationPrefixFiber_sum_codomainSwap` to express
the numerator difference as a sum of pointwise codomain-swap differences.
The latter are bounded by T694's
`norm_permutationFourierSum_codomainSwap_le`; the triangle inequality and
positive cardinality give the claimed bound. Thus the `b` fiber is also
positive by the equivalence, and no `0/0` convention is involved.

`permutationPrefixFiber_meanRange_two_witness` supplies explicit admissible
tables `x=(2,-2)` and `χ=(1,-1)` at `W=2`, `k=0`, identity prefix, and
`a=0,b=1`, together with positive cardinalities of both fibers. This is a
nondegenerate support witness. The normalized means' equality case is not
separately proved; T676's pointwise swap estimate is sharp for these tables,
but establishing equality after averaging the full fibers was outside the
central bound proof.

This is a comparison between finite fiber means only. It proves neither a
conditional expectation construction nor a centered Doob increment, Azuma
bound, P2, or formulas (2.3)–(2.4). Empty or inconsistent prefixes remain
outside the normalized statement because of the explicit positivity premise;
`W=1` has no distinct pair of candidates.

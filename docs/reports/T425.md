## Orientation and cyclic edge reduction

`xBlock_swap` proves pointwise from `Gauss.Xentry_swap` that

```text
xBlock N ω y x = (xBlock N ω x y)ᴴ.
```

The entry proof uses `Matrix.conjTranspose_apply` literally: the `(p,q)` entry on the left is
`Xentry (y,p) (x,q)`, while the right side is the conjugate of `Xentry (x,q) (y,p)`.
`norm_xBlock_swap` then applies `Matrix.l2_opNorm_conjTranspose`, so opposite orientations have the
same operator norm.  No independence between them is claimed; they are the same norm event.

`adjacent_pair_eq_forward_or_reverse` combines the public T414 cyclic-distance classification with
`x ≠ y` to prove, for every `L ≥ 3`,

```text
zdist L (x-y) ≤ 1  ⇒  y = x+1 or x = y+1.
```

The specialization `adjacent_pair_three` compiles this at the smallest cycle `L=3`.  Thus the
three edges of the triangle cover all six ordered distinct adjacent pairs through the adjoint
identity.

The file defines `forwardEdgeBad N x` for the edge `(x,x+1)` and its indexed union
`forwardEdgeBadUnion N`.  `card_forward_edges` proves that the index set has exactly `L_N` elements.
The main set inclusion

```text
(APrimeFirstCellJGAllTime.adjacentBlockEvent N)ᶜ
  ⊆ forwardEdgeBadUnion N
```

is `compl_adjacentBlockEvent_subset_forwardEdgeBadUnion`.  In the reverse case it rewrites the bad
norm with `norm_xBlock_swap`; hence no factor two and no second orientation event is introduced.
Both individual forward events and their finite indexed union are proved measurable.

## Exponent margin and eventual absorption

`fifteen_lt_twentyfour_sub_four_log_nine` proves

```text
15 < 24 - 4 log 9.
```

The proof establishes `log 9 < 9/4` via `Real.log_lt_iff_lt_exp` and the first six nonnegative
terms of the exponential series at `9/4`; their rational sum is strictly greater than `9`.

For every `D>0`, `eventually_four_mul_L_exp_neg_fifteen_W_le_rpow_neg` proves

```text
∀ᶠ N,
  4 * L_N * exp(-15 W_N) ≤ N^(-D).
```

The Lean proof uses only the actual `exampleGrow` data:

- `Dims.bandwidth_grow` gives `N^(5/8) ≤ W_N` eventually;
- `d.dim.1` and `W_N ≥ 1` give `L_N ≤ N`;
- monotonicity of `exp` reduces the left side to
  `4 N exp(-15 N^(5/8))`;
- `SumZeroDyn.eventually_exp_small 4 (D+1) 15` absorbs this into `N^(-D)`.

This theorem is deterministic asymptotic arithmetic.  It does not turn a per-edge tail into a
`HighProb` statement; the later assembly must provide the T424 probability input.

## Nondegeneracy

`exists_nonzero_mem_good` reuses T414's actual `diagonalWitness`.  For every `N`, it produces one
sample in T407's full deterministic `good N` event whose matrix `Xmat` is nonzero.  Thus the event
whose adjacent component is being prepared here has a simultaneous nonzero inhabitant; no
nonemptiness inference from separate witnesses is used.

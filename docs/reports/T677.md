- `RBM.Gauss.closedCutoff_Ici_zero_witness` gives the explicit closed set `C = Set.Ici (0 : ℝ)` and constant function `f = 1`. It proves the hypotheses, upper semicontinuity of the resulting step, and `¬ ContinuousAt step 0`.

## Proof and boundary orientation

The proof uses Mathlib's exact `UpperSemicontinuous` spelling and `upperSemicontinuous_iff_isOpen_preimage`. For a strict sublevel threshold `r ≤ 0`, the preimage is empty because both branches are nonnegative. For `0 < r`, the preimage is `f ⁻¹' Set.Iio r ∪ Cᶜ`, hence open. The `if` selects `f` when `x ∈ C`, including equality on the cutoff boundary. Thus the step witness jumps upward from `0` to `1` at zero; its strict sublevel sets have the orientation needed for upper semicontinuity.

For discontinuity, continuity at zero would make the preimage of `Set.Ioi (1/2)` a neighborhood of zero. That preimage is `Set.Ici 0`, whose interior is `Set.Ioi 0` and does not contain zero. This also establishes nonvacuity with a genuine discontinuity rather than a zero or empty cutoff.

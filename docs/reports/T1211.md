## Claim and exact scope

`eventually_integral_normGoodCrossBudget_le_small_slot` assumes T1207's exact
context: `|E| < 2`, `60 ≤ D`, `0 ≤ s N ≤ t N < 1`, `c > 0`, `Cond272Reg`,
`BoundsCore (Gauss.sample d) E s`, and `0 < δ ≤ min 1 (c/100)`. It sets
`deltaWeight = T995.deltaWeight δ = δ/100` and proves that one `C > 0` and one
eventual cutoff work uniformly for every active `k` and output `a`, with
`v = cutNetPt s (targetMesh D) N k`:

```text
∫_(s N)^v normGoodCrossBudget E D (deltaWeight δ) s t N k 1 a u du
  ≤ C N^(5δ/32) (ratR E s N v)^(-2).
```

The proof chooses
`C = (15/4) (|crossProfileBudgetConst E| + 1)`. The integrand remains the
exact T1201 definition: Gaussian law `Gauss.P Dims.exampleGrow`, the T1197
norm-good indicator, the strict-transition-indicated T1089 `jointRate`,
`Step2.sigPM`, `canonicalM`, the target mesh and moving endpoint, and the
`crossMomentCoeff 1 / (4 √u)` coefficient applied to the `2`-moment norm.
No high-q family or domination of the actual budget by the favorable profile
is assumed.

## Mathematical preflight and proof route

I checked the authorized PDF's formulas (5.21), (5.24), and (5.43)–(5.46),
and the exact T995, T1035, T1147, T1201, T1205, and T1207 interfaces. These
paper formulas concern the stopped hierarchy, its moment/QV control, and
stopping-time estimates. This result is an internal smooth-prefix A′ auxiliary
bound; it neither formalizes nor alters the paper's stopped argument.

For each positive `u`, put `Z` equal to the literal norm-good restricted
joint rate and let `H` be the product of T613's transition-prefix rate and
T606's absorbed-root profile. On the common source event, `|Z| ≤ H`: off the
norm-good event `Z=0`; on the good event but outside the strict transition
`Z=0`; on their intersection with the common source event, T613 bounds the
prefix factor and T606 bounds the QV factor at that same sample. This uses
only the pointwise implications on `commonEvent ∩ transition`; it assumes no
resident or positive probability for that intersection. T617 supplies a
global polynomial envelope for `Z`. T1035's event-pay estimate and the
`q=2` affine moment split with comparison variable zero therefore give
`momNorm Z ≤ H + N^(-1)`.

At `p=1`, `crossMomentCoeff 1 = 15/2`. Multiplication by `1/(4√u)` turns the
`H` term exactly into `(15/4) crossProfile`; the residual is
`(15/8) N^(-1) u^(-1/2)`. T1147 bounds the integral of `crossProfile` by its
budget constant times `N^(5δ/32) ratR^(-2)`. The inverse-square-root integral
is at most `2`. Finally, T995's `ratR^30 ≤ N`, together with `ratR ≥ 1` and
`N^(5δ/32) ≥ 1`, pays `N^(-1) ≤ N^(5δ/32) ratR^(-2)`. The two contributions
give the stated `C`.

The zero-time point is handled by the literal totalized budget definition,
and T1205 supplies interval integrability of that exact budget, including
degenerate cells. T995's `scheduled_positive_cell_witness` is re-exported as
`nondegenerate_positive_cell_witness`; it certifies a genuinely positive
scheduled cell, an active index, and a common-event sample with positive
smooth weight. It does not assert strict-transition membership, positive mass
of `commonEvent ∩ transition`, or a nonzero norm-good budget. T1212 identified
and independently checked this correction.

T1095's empty common-event/transition intersection at one first-cell index
does not obstruct this conditional bound. T1210's far-index norm-good
transition example also does not supply a common-event resident. The proof
does not use an all-active transition exclusion; it bounds the literal
restricted rate pointwise on the common event and controls the complement by
the polynomial envelope.

## Verdict: mathematical preflight PASS; requested bound proved

The theorem `RBM.APrimeGeneralMovingCrossBudgetPoly.eventually_positive_time_cross_budget_le_polynomial`
proves, under exactly the T1089 hypotheses and for each fixed `p ≥ 1`,
eventually in `N` and uniformly over every active target-mesh prefix `k`,
output `a`, and `r ∈ [s_N, cutNetPt(k)]` with `r > 0`,


## Mathematical preflight

I checked only `paper/250520-YinJun-v2.pdf`. Formula (5.21) is the stopped
two-loop hierarchy identity with its terminal stochastic-integral term;
(5.24) is the BDG moment estimate for that term in terms of its integrated
quadratic variation. T1107 proves neither paper estimate: it bounds the
already-defined T1089 positive-time budget pointwise. It does not bound its
time integral or establish the smallness needed for N1.

The producer chain matches the literal observable:

1. `eventually_prefixGradient_mul_sqrt_qvAt_le_poly` bounds the product of the
   actual prefix gradient and `sqrt(qvAt)` for every sample, charge, output,
   active `k`, and closed-cell time. Its factors are `2^15 N^(D+8)` and
   `2^11 N^(D+8)`, giving `jointEnvelope D N = 2^26 N^(2D+16)`.
2. `eventually_jointEnvelope_le_rpow` uses `N ≥ 2^26` to pay the constant as
   one extra power, yielding `jointEnvelope D N ≤ N^(2D+17)`.
3. `APrimeGeneralMovingJointMeasurable` supplies measurability for the exact
   canonical prefix and joint rate on every closed cell, including `k = 0`.
   `APrimeGeneralMovingJointRateIntegrable` combines that measurability with
   the all-sample bound to supply integrability of the actual `2p`-power.
4. Since `Gauss.P d` is a probability measure, the pointwise absolute bound
   implies `momNorm (Gauss.P d) (2*p) jointRate ≤ jointEnvelope`. Multiplying
   by the nonnegative factor `crossMomentCoeff p / (4*p*sqrt r)` gives the
   claimed budget bound. The existing positive-budget lemma supplies the
   lower bound.

The empty-prefix producer proves the strict transition set is empty at
`k = 0`, and the corresponding joint rate is identically zero. At `p = 1`,
the moment order is `2` and `crossMomentCoeff_one` gives `15/2`, so the budget
coefficient is `15/(8√r)`.

The included `t995_positive_cell_witness` is the accepted T995 witness: it
gives a satisfiable `E=0`, `D=60` context, a positive-duration active cell,
and one same-sample common-event member with positive actual smooth weight at
`p=1`. It does not assert that this sample lies in the strict transition
event.

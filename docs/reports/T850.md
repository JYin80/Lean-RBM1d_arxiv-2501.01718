## Boundaries and nondegeneracy

- `W=0`: `Fin 0` has no reveal, yet `PermΩ 0` has its unique permutation. Use `Y 0=0`, `n=1`; no conditional MGF index is requested. The sum is zero.
- `W=1`: the only reveal is the last one. T840 and T842 both prove its centered increment is pointwise zero. The general positive-`C` bound remains true but is not sharp.
- Any `W≥1`, last valid `k=W-1`: `unusedImages` is the singleton `{π k}`, so `d_k=0` pointwise. Retaining `4C²` at this index is a safe conservative parameter; a later refinement can set it to zero.
- `W=2`, `f(0)=0`, `f(1)=1`, `C=1`: T840 proves the identity and transposition atoms both have positive probability and give `d_0=-1/2,+1/2`; `d_1=0` at both atoms. Thus `Y_0=0`, `Y_1=±1/2`, `Y_2=0`, and the event in (T850-target) at `ε=1/2` has probability `1/2`. The proposed route is genuinely random despite conditional mean zero and a deterministic final reveal.

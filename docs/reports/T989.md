## Numerical obstruction in the currently stated loss interface

The loss parameters of T591/T615 are independent of the small-slot `delta`.  This independence prevents a theorem that fits their **exact profiles** for every allowed positive choice.  A nondegenerate admissible regime is the `E=0`, `D=60`, `s_N=0`, eventually `t_N=1/2` witness in `APrimeGeneralMovingInitialWitnessCore.positive_length_hinit_witness'`, which supplies `Cond272Reg` and `BoundsCore` for `Dims.exampleGrow`. T591's room condition is satisfied: `1/100+2/100+2/15<1`.

At the last target-mesh point `v=cutNetPt s (targetMesh 60) N (cutNetTop s t (targetMesh 60) N)`, one has eventually `1/4 <= v <= 1/2`: the mesh is `N^(4D+18)` and the floor leaves a gap smaller than one mesh step.  Take a diagonal output coordinate `a` (distance zero), so the near indicator in `rootProfile` equals one.  For `u in [0,1/4]`, `R_u=1/(1-u)` and `R_v=1/(1-v)` are bounded above and below by constants.  The near coefficient in T591 is, exactly through `sourceEll_ratio_fifth`,

```text
nearSourceRate(u)
  = 4 N^zetaSrc eta_u^(-1) cNear2(W,ell_u) (ell_u/ell_s)^5.
```

Here `eta_u=1-u`, `ell_s=1`, `ell_u>=1`, `cNear2(W,ell_u)>=1` eventually, and `xiK>=1`.  The other terms in the whole root sum are nonnegative, including the `2W^(-1)` near residual.  Endpoint normalization therefore gives a fixed `C>0`, independent of `N`, with `Qexact(u)>=C N^zetaSrc` on this positive-length interval.  Hence the **profile** side of N2 has lower order at least `N^(zetaSrc/2)=N^(1/200)` after integration and square root.  The entire N2 small-slot right side at bounded `R_v` is `O(N^(5 delta/32))`; for `delta<=1/1000`, `5 delta/32<=1/6400<1/200`.  No fixed multiplicative constant closes this gap.  This is an obstruction to fitting T591's deterministic profile, not a counterexample to a sharper estimate of the actual QV.

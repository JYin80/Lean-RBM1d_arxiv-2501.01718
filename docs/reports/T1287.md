## Preflight and proof

I checked the exact current T1207, T1277, T1245 and T995 sources against their
accepted reports. Their module hashes matched those reports. I also compiled
`#check` probes for the precise declarations used. The proof combines:

1. T1207's integral reduction for the literal full positive-time budget,
   which yields the norm-good integral plus its `15p/4 · N⁻¹` endpoint-gap
   payment.
2. T1277's all-fixed-order pointwise norm-good bound, whose remaining term is
   `(15p/8) N⁻¹ / √u`.
3. T1245's pointwise bound for the literal T1029 cross profile, with exponent
   `13δ/640`, coefficient `crossProfilePointwiseConst`,
   `etaT(E,s_N)^(-1)`, and the exact terminal `ratR(v)^(-2)`.
4. The accepted norm-good interval-integrability producer and inverse-square-
   root envelope integrability/evaluation producer.

The T1245 profile envelope integrates with
`∫_[s_N,v] u^(-1/2) du = 2(√v−√s_N)`. Since
`etaT(E,s_N) = (1−s_N) Im m(E)` and
`√v−√s_N ≤ 1−s_N` for `0 ≤ s_N ≤ v < 1`, its integrated scale factor is at
most `1/Im m(E)`. Thus the profile coefficient is exactly
`C(E,p) N^(13δ/640) ratR(v)^(-2)`. The strict gap
`13δ/640 < δ/8` absorbs this into the stated `δ/8` term.

Integrating T1277's pointwise remainder gives the first displayed payment;
T1207 contributes the second. They are intentionally left as two summands.
At `u = 0` the literal norm-good budget is zero under Lean's totalized
definition, and the inverse-square-root envelope has formal value zero. The
closed-interval integral therefore includes `s_N = 0`; no positive-time
restriction is added to the integral. The pointwise producers and reduction
include `k = 0`. The fixed `p` parameter precedes all eventual quantifiers,
and the resulting cutoff is independent of `k` and `a`.

The module re-exports T995's `scheduled_positive_cell_witness`. It supplies a
nondegenerate `E = 0`, `D = 60` window and the same sample in the literal
common event with positive actual smooth weight. No new assumptions were
introduced. This witness makes no claim of strict-transition membership or
positive cross-budget mass.

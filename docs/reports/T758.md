## Statement and boundary review

T711 defines `prefixCell W k π` as the permutations `ρ` satisfying `ρ i = π i` for every `i : Fin W` with `i.val < k`. Its `prefixCell_eq_singleton_of_width_le` proves `prefixCell W k π = {π}` whenever `W ≤ k`, using extensionality of permutations. T756 rewrites both `prefixCell W k π` and `prefixCell W W π` with that theorem, then derives card `1` from the same singleton. The quantifiers are exactly all `W k : ℕ`, `W ≤ k`, and actual `π : PermΩ W`; no potentially inconsistent prescribed prefix is introduced.

The bundled boundary theorem proves singleton and card `1` at `(W,k)=(0,0)` and `(1,1)`, and for `W=2` at every `k≥2`. T711's identity/swap witness supplies two distinct points in the `W=2,k=0` cell, so stabilization is not merely a statement about a one-point sample space. The general theorem also covers every `k≥0` when `W=0` and every `k≥1` when `W=1`. There is no new assumption requiring a separate satisfiability witness.

This is finite support stabilization only. The paper's local laws (2.3)–(2.4) are probability bounds for Green-function quantities and remain downstream. The source contains no conditional law, `N` or time exponent, first-cell/shared event, P2 estimate, or six-step closure.

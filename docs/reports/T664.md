## 8. Topology of `blockEvent`

### Why joint continuity is false

For each fixed block pair the relevant term has the form

```text
if ellStar W (ell u) / 2 ≤ dist then activeValue(u,x) else 0,
```

where `x` denotes the finite effective-coordinate vector.  At a cutoff crossing the value can
jump from zero to a positive number.  Thus one may not place `jG` among ordinary jointly
continuous finite maxima.

### Joint upper-semicontinuity route

T660's replacement route is mathematically valid under the stated window hypotheses:

1. The active set is closed in `(u,x)` because the cutoff depends continuously on `u` and is
   independent of `x`.
2. The active value is nonnegative and jointly continuous: Green entries are continuous while
   `u<1`; `gmBlk` and `gsqBlk` are finite maxima/products; and `tailT` is continuous and
   strictly positive because `W>0` and the `W^(-D)` floor is positive.
3. Extending a nonnegative continuous function by zero off a closed active set is jointly
   upper semicontinuous.  Nonnegativity is essential at the boundary.
4. A finite maximum and addition of one preserve upper semicontinuity, so `jG` is jointly
   upper semicontinuous.
5. `Step2.jS` and the remaining right side of `blockEvent` are jointly continuous.  Therefore
   `jG - RHS` is upper semicontinuous.
6. On the compact closed time interval, a strictly negative centre gap has a negative maximum.
   The open strict sublevel set and the compact tube lemma then give one effective-coordinate
   neighbourhood on which the block inequality holds for every time.

Pointwise-in-time upper semicontinuity is insufficient; the compiled statement must be joint
in time and the effective coordinates.

### Fixed-far continuous-majorant route

This alternative is also valid, but only after compiling the T643 quantitative separation:

```text
2 < ellStar (W N) (ell_u) / 2
```

uniformly for every `u` in the target closed window.  Then every pair active in the literal
cutoff belongs to the fixed finite class `2 < zdist(x-y)`.  The fixed-far maximum of
`gsqBlk/tailT` is jointly continuous and majorizes the literal `jG`.  T643's centre has
`gsqBlk=0` on that fixed-far class, because its Green matrix is supported within matched
block pairs and the intervening `SB` neighbour increases the possible distance only to two.
Hence the majorant equals one at the centre, and the same compact-tube argument applies.

Without the explicit support-separation margin, the literal active set may contain distance-two
pairs and this majorant argument is not justified.  An unrestricted all-pair majorant is not a
replacement because near-pair numerators need not vanish at the centre.

### Exact missing compiled implications

Neither route is currently a resident or cylinder theorem.  The missing compiled package is:

- realization of T643's coupled-block centre by one sample, including the zero-variance
  coordinate condition;
- one simultaneous uniform strict-margin package for norm, the `3/4/6` raw rows,
  `goodSetFlow`, both centered charges, `blockEvent`, and the strict transition;
- joint continuity of the active quotient and of the block-event right side;
- either joint upper semicontinuity of the literal hard-cutoff `jG`, or the uniform
  `ellStar/2>2` separation plus fixed-far zero-support majorant;
- the compact tube lemma in time × effective coordinates;
- the final relative-interior inclusion and subsequent positive-cylinder/local-`measCore`
  transfer.

The definition-only extraction must not attempt to prove any of these implications.

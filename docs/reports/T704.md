# T704 — preflight for nonempty conditional prefix-fiber means

## Verdict and mathematical statement

**PASS as a read-only preflight.** The next finite lemma is true. Put

\[
 F(\pi)=\operatorname{permutationFourierSum}(W,x,\chi,\pi),\qquad
 A_v=\{\pi:\pi(i)=p(i)\ (i<k),\ \pi(k)=v\},\qquad
 n_v=|A_v|.
\]

Quantify **every** `W : ℕ` with `2 ≤ W`, `k : Fin W`, `p : Fin W → Fin W`,
distinct `a b : Fin W` absent from `p i` for all `i < k`, and tables
`x χ : Fin W → ℂ` satisfying `∀ i, ‖x i‖ ≤ 2` and `∀ i, ‖χ i‖ ≤ 1`.
Require **before forming a conditional mean** `0 < n_a`, equivalently
`Nonempty (permutationPrefixFiber W k p a)`. T694/T703 then give
`n_b=n_a>0`. Define `m_v = (∑ π ∈ A_v, F(π))/n_v`. The strongest convenient
single estimate is

\[
                 \|m_a-m_b\|\leq \frac{8}{W}.                 \tag{C}
\]

Consequently, for the *uniform finite counting means* of the coordinates,

\[
 \left|\frac{\sum_{\pi\in A_a}\operatorname{Re}F(\pi)}{n_a}
       -\frac{\sum_{\rho\in A_b}\operatorname{Re}F(\rho)}{n_b}\right|
 \leq\frac8W,
 \qquad
 \left|\frac{\sum_{\pi\in A_a}\operatorname{Im}F(\pi)}{n_a}
       -\frac{\sum_{\rho\in A_b}\operatorname{Im}F(\rho)}{n_b}\right|
 \leq\frac8W.                                                \tag{R/I}
\]

No zero-character-sum assumption is needed for (C) or (R/I).

## Exact finite proof and denominator order

Let `S π = permutationCodomainSwap W a b π = π.trans (Equiv.swap a b)`.
T694's `permutationPrefixFiberSwap` maps `A_a` bijectively to `A_b` and
fixes the prescribed prefix. T703's `permutationPrefixFiber_card_eq` first
identifies `n_b=n_a`; the explicit positive-cardinality hypothesis then
ensures that the common real or complex denominator is nonzero. Only **after**
this identification, T703's `permutationPrefixFiber_sum_codomainSwap` gives

\[
 m_a-m_b
 =\frac{\sum_{\pi\in A_a}\bigl(F(\pi)-F(S\pi)\bigr)}{n_a}.
\]

T694's `norm_permutationFourierSum_codomainSwap_le`, inherited from T676's
domain-swap lemma, bounds each summand by `8/W`. The triangle inequality
therefore bounds the numerator by `n_a(8/W)`, and positive division yields
(C). Taking real and imaginary parts gives (R/I), since projection commutes
with a finite sum and division by the positive real cardinality, and
`|z.re|, |z.im| ≤ ‖z‖`.

The denominator here is the **fiber cardinality**, not the cardinality of
all permutations. A formally defined `0/0=0` would mask an empty
conditional event and is not a valid conditional mean. An inconsistent
example is `W=3`, `k=2`, `p 0=p 1=0`, `a=1`, `b=2`: both candidates are
distinct and unused, but both fibers are empty because a permutation cannot
repeat the first image. Thus distinctness and unusedness alone do not imply
support. Conversely, positive `A_a` plus T694's equivalence gives positive
`A_b`; a second nonemptiness premise is redundant.

At `W=2`, `k=0`, `p i=i`, `a=0`, `b=1`, the identity and transposition
inhabit the two fibers, each of cardinality one. Taking `x=(2,-2)` and
`χ=(1,-1)` gives respective real means `2,-2`, so their difference is
`4=8/2`: the constant is attained. Taking `χ=(I,-I)` makes the imaginary
coordinate sharp as well. At `W=1`, there is a `k : Fin 1` but no distinct
`a,b`; at `W=0`, no `k : Fin 0` exists. At a supported prefix with only one
unrevealed value (`k=W-1`), there are no two distinct unused candidates, so
the comparison premise cannot arise.

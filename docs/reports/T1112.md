## Paper and analytic scope

I checked only `paper/250520-YinJun-v2.pdf`: (5.21) is the stopped,
integrated loop hierarchy with a stochastic integral; (5.24) is the stopped
martingale moment bound by integrated quadratic variation. T1111 proves
neither. The model's derivative identity `hasDerivAt_momFlow` assumes `0<u`,
and its integrability proof uses the open interval. Thus the formal value
`crossPart ... 0 = 0` must **not** be read as an actual derivative formula or
as a limit of the singular positive-time cross term at zero. The result
supplies only the boundary `hcross` input to the pointwise generator
inequality. Positive-time control, integrated N1, moving `hfamily`, and A′
assembly require their separate arguments.

## Exponent and scale calculation

Write `r_u = ell_u/ell_s`, `R_u = eta_s/eta_u`, and
`A_u = W ell_u eta_u`. The literal summand, including the T615 normalization,
is

```text
xiK * R_u^-2 * R_v^-2
  * eta_u^-1 * (4 N^zetaCtr r_u)
  * (Jbar * sqrt(Jbar)) * (168 A_u^-1).
```

The literal block cap satisfies
`Jbar <= C0 N^(tauG + 2 deltaCap) R_u^4`, where
`C0 = 3 + 9 exp(sqrt 3) (4 exp(1) + 2)`. Also
`r_u^2 <= R_u` by `Step2MomentStep.ratio_sq_le`, and `ell_s <= ell_u`.
Consequently `Jbar sqrt(Jbar)` costs at most
`C0^(3/2) N^(3(tauG + 2 deltaCap)/2) R_u^6`.

After applying `r_u <= sqrt(R_u)`, rewriting
`eta_u^-2 = R_u eta_s^-1 eta_u^-1`, and integrating with the exact
`integral_ratio_power` substitution at power `11/2`, the integral is bounded by

```text
R_v^-2 (R_v^(11/2) - 1) / ((11/2) m W ell_s eta_s),
  where m = Im m_E.
```

The printed (2.72) gives `R_v^30 <= A_v`; flow scale monotonicity gives
`A_v <= A_s`. Since `R_v >= 1`, the factor
`R_v^-2 (R_v^(11/2)-1)/A_s` is at most one. This uses the genuine direction
of scale separation and retains the terminal `R_v^-2` throughout; `2/(11m)`
is a fixed constant absorbed in the strict power gap.

The losses are selected after the small-slot `delta` as in T995:
`deltaCap = delta/50`, `tauG = delta/1600`, and `zetaCtr = delta/3200`.
Using `eventually_xiK_le` with exponent `delta/100`, the total `N` exponent is

```text

  = 57 delta/800  < 5 delta/32.
```

The strict gap is `17 delta/200`; fixed constants are absorbable by
`eventually_const_mul_rpow_le`. The target `5 delta/32` is the same strict
small-slot power used by `APrimeInit.coordinate_integral_of_small_slots`.

## Size and quantifier ledger

Write `r=ℓ_u/ℓ_s`, `A=W ℓ_u η_u`, `F=ℓ_{**}-ℓ_*`, and `J=jG` at the first cell. With independently chosen positive losses `ζ₁,ζ₃`, a suitable simultaneous event gives

* `c ≤ C₁ N^ζ₁ r/A` for both charges and every block label. T335 with `Ψ'=W^(-1/2)` gives `N^ζ₁ W⁻¹`; since `E=s=0`, `ℓ_s=1`, and `η_u≤1`, one has `W⁻¹≤r/A`. Thus `C₁=1` is mathematically available once the charge bridge and event are proved.
* `L3(b) ≤ C₃ N^ζ₃ r²/A²` for every `b` from the raw `n=3` Step-1 law, with `C₃=1` before harmless interface constants.
* `L3(b) ≤ η_u⁻¹ J T_u(F)` for far internal `b` when the actual output labels are near, by T350. This is pointwise and has no stochastic loss.

The factor `2 W c` in `EGDef.norm_eGpm_le` is therefore at most `2 C₁ N^ζ₁ r/ (ℓ_u η_u)`. T364's near principal coefficient keeps **both** losses: `2 C₁ C₃ N^(ζ₁+ζ₃) η_u⁻¹ r³ cNear(W,ℓ_u) T_u(d)`. Its near residual carries `ζ₁` through `J` and is absorbed by T357 into `W⁻¹ η_u⁻¹ r³ T_u(d)` under its stated caps. Neither `N^ζ₁` nor `N^ζ₃` may be hidden in an unqualified constant. For a later integrated exponent margin `ν`, fix `ζ₁,ζ₃>0` with `ζ₁+ζ₃<ν` (and leave room for any later finite losses); choosing each below `ν/4` is one safe budget.

The order is: choose the requested error exponent and `ζ₁,ζ₃`; obtain one `τ'>0` from T358; set `u_N=min(h_N,firstCellT τ' N)` for the all-size T335 interface; obtain high-probability raw `n=3`, averaged one-loop, norm, raw `n=4,6`, and Good events at that fixed `τ'`; intersect those **events**; then take sufficiently large `N` so the intersection is nonempty, `0<u_N<1`, `u_N=h_N=firstTime N`, T366's deterministic cap and T357's eventual numeric hypotheses hold. Finally choose **one** `ω` in the intersection. `HighProb.nonempty` supplies this sample; separate existential samples do not. Uniformity over `b` and loop labels must be part of each event before intersection. The `N^ζ` bounds arise at an eventual threshold depending on the requested polynomial failure exponent, as specified by `StochDom`/`HighProb`; there is no all-`N` assertion.

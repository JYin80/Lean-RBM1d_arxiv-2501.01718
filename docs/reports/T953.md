## Statements and mathematics

For `1 ≤ W`, `π : Equiv.Perm (Fin W)`, and actual midpoint semicircle
quantiles `λ_j = semicircleLambda W hW j.val j.isLt`, define

```text
F(j,a) = (sqrt W)⁻¹ permutationFourierCharacter W a j,
A_π = Fᴴ diag(j ↦ (λ_(πj) : ℂ)) F.
```

`permutationFourierBlock_hermitian` proves `A_π.IsHermitian` for every
permutation. The exact factorization says

```text
(sqrt u : ℂ) • A_π - (i (1-u) : ℂ) • I
  = Fᴴ diag(j ↦ (sqrt u · λ_(πj) - i(1-u))) F.
```

For `0 ≤ u ≤ 1/2`, the imaginary part of every diagonal denominator is
`-(1-u) ≤ -1/2`, so it is nonzero, including at `u=0`. T946 gives both
`FᴴF=I` and `FFᴴ=I`; these and pointwise diagonal invertibility yield the
inverse of the conjugated diagonal. The resulting theorem
`permutationFourierBlock_resolvent_entry` proves, for all `a,b : Fin W`,

```text
[((sqrt u : ℂ) • A_π - i(1-u) • I)⁻¹](a,b)
  = permutationFourierSum W
      (j ↦ semicircleFlowKernel u λ_j)
      (permutationFourierCharacter W (b-a)) π.
```

The coefficient is precisely `W⁻¹ ∑_j χ_(b-a)(j) g_u(λ_(πj))`. The
diagonal theorem specializes it to the constant-one zero mode;
`permutationFourierBlock_resolvent_offdiag_mode_ne_zero` verifies that
`a ≠ b` gives `(b-a).val ≠ 0`. The positive-phase orientation is therefore
`b-a`.

`permutationFourierBlock_resolvent_two_offdiag_nonzero` instantiates the
actual quantiles at `W=2`, `π=id`, `u=1/2` and proves the `(0,1)` resolvent
entry is nonzero. The proof uses the accepted strict inequality `λ₀<λ₁`,
positivity of `sqrt (1/2)`, and injectivity of inversion. This is a
nondegenerate witness for all the numeric hypotheses; `W=1` is also covered
by the diagonal identity.

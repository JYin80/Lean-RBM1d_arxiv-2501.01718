## Verdict

The actual Gaussian slot is **not produced**.  Its event/modulus/mesh branches have
noncircular sources (plus short finite-supremum and measurability lemmas), but there is no


## 2. What the paper permits

In Theorem 2.21, Step 2 proves (2.76) for `σ = (+,-)` on `s ≤ u ≤ t`.  Step 3 then proves
(2.77), the all-charge loop bound, on the same interval and for every loop length.  The paper
states that the steps are sequential and that each uses the preceding steps.

Consequently a proof of the slot may use the actual Step-1/Step-2 estimates, including
(2.76), but it may not use (2.77), the desired `flow_hTheta` conclusion, or any later
hierarchy result.  This distinction is essential at length two:

* cyclicity reduces `(-,+)` to `(+,-)`;
* conjugation reduces `(-,-)` to `(+,+)`;
* `(+,+)` remains independent of the `(2.76)` charge.

These reductions are exactly `lkErr_two_flip` and `lkErr_two_const` in
`RBM1D/Hierarchy/ChargeReduce.lean:182,191`.  The same file explicitly records
`AprioriDecayPP` as the remaining constant-charge gap.  Thus (2.76), an A′ slot, or a
`jSnorm` moment theorem cannot be relabelled as a producer for `xiLK ... 2`.

## 4. A concrete event modulus and compatible mesh

There is a clean noncircular route with fixed numerical exponents.

1. `Cond272Reg` contains `N^c ≤ scale(t_N)`.  The endpoint-floor theorem
   `APrimeGeneralMovingWindowFloor.eventually_endpoint_floor` gives
   `N⁻¹ ≤ 1-t_N`.  Since `E` is fixed in the bulk, this gives eventually
   `etaT E (t N)⁻¹ ≤ N²` after absorbing the fixed factor `(mE E).im⁻¹`.
2. On `Good N`, eventually `‖Xmat d N ω‖ + 1 ≤ N²`.
3. At length two, the sharper base theorem `Gauss.norm_Kval_two_le_rpow` uses the same
   endpoint floor and gives the deterministic `K` envelope with exponent `1`.  (The general
   `Gauss.hKb_flow` is also noncircular, but pays an unnecessary length-dependent exponent.)
4. Weaken all three inputs to exponent `2` and apply `Gauss.hHol_flow`
   (`Gauss/Lemma514Holder.lean:721`) with `m=2`, `c=2`.  Its exponent is
   `2(3·2+4)+1 = 21`, and `γ=1/2`.
5. A finite-supremum inequality transfers the coordinate estimate
   `|a_q(v)-a_q(w)| ≤ N^21 |v-w|^(1/2)` to
   `|sup_q a_q(v)-sup_q a_q(w)|` and hence to `J`.

Thus a suitable choice is

```lean
Kmod := 21
γ     := 1 / 2
mesh  := RBM.meshK 21 (1 / 2)
Ccard := 21 / (1 / 2) + 1   -- 43
```

`mesh_fine_at_meshK` gives a left side at most `1`, while
`eventually_one_le_flowAs_rpow_half` gives `1 ≤ Θ`; `card_le_at_meshK` gives the matching
cardinality bound on every `0 ≤ s_N ≤ t_N < 1` window.  Therefore mesh fineness and
cardinality are jointly satisfiable; they are not the blocker.  The more conservative
`APrimeGeneralMovingMesh.polynomialMesh` is also usable, but its existing packaged theorems
target `jSnorm` and `Θ=1`, so `meshK` is the more literal slot-3 choice.

The finite-supremum transfer is real implementation work, not an adapter: it proves the
modulus of the actual `xiLK`.  It is nevertheless a bounded deterministic lemma, while the
missing moment estimate below is the first unresolved probabilistic argument.

## 5. Why the moment branch is genuinely missing

The `moment` field is unconditional in `ω`; only the modulus is restricted to `Good`.  It
must hold for every small `δ`, every `ε>0`, and every `p : ℕ`, uniformly over every net point.
The cutoff level is exactly `N^(2δ) Θ`, with `Θ = flowAs^(1/2)`.

The repository contains three nearby but unusable routes.

1. **Desired conclusion / circular reverse bridge.**  Once
   `flowXiLK ... 2 ≺ Θ` is known, `Gauss.momentDom_of_stochDom_of_nonneg` together with a
   polynomial envelope can recover even moments.  But `flow_hTheta_of_cutHypEvOn` derives
   precisely that stochastic domination from this `CutHypEvOnSlot`.  Using it here is a
   cycle.
2. **Step 2 / A′.**  `APrimeSlot`, its `hfamily`/`APrimeSlot'` variants, and the first-cell or
   general-moving weighted-moment modules control `Step2Moment.jSnorm`, hence (2.76)'s
   `(+,-)` charge.  They do not control `(+,+)` and therefore do not dominate `xiLK`.
3. **A priori polynomial envelope.**  `Lemma514XiPoly.xiLK_le_poly_of_K` bounds `xiLK`
   polynomially pointwise.  Applying only that bound, or only the cutoff bound, costs a fixed
   positive power of `N` per moment.  It cannot yield `N^(ε p)` for **every** `ε>0`.

`Hierarchy/Step2MomentStep.lean:652` identifies the same missing mathematical operation in
the equivalent prefix-bootstrap formulation: the fixed-net-time one-step improvement `hev`
must be supplied by the truncated moment Duhamel.  The cutoff must linearize the
self-quadratic `(+,+)` term on its support.  No file proves that estimate for the actual
Gaussian `xiLK` on a general moving window.

This also explains why neither (2.76) nor a finite number of stochastic-domination passes is
enough.  `Step2PP.xiLK_two_apriori` retains an extra factor `A_u`, and
`Step2PP.no_finite_pass` shows that the self-map with the term `θ² A_t⁻¹` is not a contraction
above `A_t`.  The cutoff/moment argument is essential, not merely one possible packaging.

The general-moving A′ modules extend that same `jSnorm` construction to arbitrary windows and

`APrimeSlot'` or `hfamily` assumption would merely rename the outstanding analytic input and
is not a producer for this ticket.

## 7. Boundary cases and anti-vacuity

* **`p=0`.**  This row is elementary: the integrand is the zeroth power, so probability
  normalization gives the bound with `C=1`.  It should be split before any positive-moment
  argument.
* **Net index `k=0`.**  Here `ws=s_N`.  `xiLK_two_init_of_boundsCore` gives
  `xiLK(s_N) ≺ 1`; with measurability and the deterministic polynomial envelope, the reverse
  moment bridge supplies the positive moments.  This is an endpoint lemma, not the moving
  Duhamel step.
* **Closed endpoints.**  `hHol_flow` is stated on `Set.Icc`, so both `s_N` and `t_N` are
  covered.  `netFinset_subset_Icc` supplies the same closed-window fact for all net points.
* **Positive window.**  `positive_length_grid_window_witness` in
  `APrimeGeneralMovingWindowFloor.lean:70` supplies an admissible window with
  `s_N<t_N` eventually.
* **Nonempty event.**  `Good N` contains the zero Gaussian sample for every `N`
  (`APrimeSlotFields.zero_mem_normX_le`), and is HighProb.  Thus the modulus is tested on a
  genuinely nonempty event even on a positive-length window.

These witnesses show that the event, modulus, and mesh/cardinality conditions are jointly
nondegenerate.  They do **not** witness the complete `xiLK` moment package: no nonzero
positive-grid-point cutoff moment theorem is currently present.  A zero-functional probe or
an abstract `satCutHypEvOn` record must not be used to claim satisfiability of the actual
Gaussian slot.

Required proof discipline for that ticket:

* split `p=0` and `ws=s_N` explicitly;
* for positive net indices, prove the actual Gaussian truncated Duhamel estimate for the
  `(+,+)` coordinate and then take the finite maximum over all length-two charges;
* use earlier actual Step-1/Step-2 estimates only; do not assume `flow_hTheta`, (2.77),
  `hfamily`, `APrimeSlot'`, or the theorem being proved;
* keep the statement on arbitrary `s,t : ℕ→ℝ`, including `s_N=0` and both closed endpoints;
* provide a positive-window, nonempty-event application after the theorem is compiled.

After this theorem, the remaining slot assembly is bounded work: prove the finite-supremum
modulus and measurability lemmas described above, fill the deterministic fields with
`Kmod=21`, `γ=1/2`, `meshK 21 (1/2)`, `Ccard=43`, and use the actual `Good` event.  No

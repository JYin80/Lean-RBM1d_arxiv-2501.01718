# T1157 — fixed-order early norm-good transfer

## Verdict: mathematical preflight PASS; exact conditional theorem proved

Added `RBM.APrimeGeneralMovingHighQEarlyGoodFixedOrder.eventually_early_good_of_exact_k0`.
For a fixed `δ > 0`, fixed integer `q ≥ 1`, and `C₀ > 0`, assume the exact
full order-`q` T999 integral at `k = 0` is eventually at most
`C₀ N^(δq/2)`. The theorem returns one `C₁ > 0` and one eventual threshold,
uniformly for every active `2 ≤ k ≤ N^4`, bounding the literal order-`q`
T999 integral restricted to the norm-good event by `C₁ N^(δq/2)`. The order
does not change in the transfer.

## Mathematical preflight

I checked the actual T1117 proof and source, T1097's exact k0 producer, the
T475 moving-window modulus, the target mesh, and T1117's T995-derived k2
witness. Their domains agree: one `Dims.exampleGrow` Gaussian sample and law,
the same `E`, `D`, moving endpoints `s,t`, target mesh, order, `δ`, canonical
`widenedW`, cutoff `cutTrunc (N^(2δ))`, and `jSnorm` at the mesh endpoint.
The restriction is the literal event
`{ω | ‖Xmat exampleGrow N ω‖ ≤ N}`.

For active `k ≤ N^4`, the endpoint displacement is exactly `k / mesh N`.
T475 gives the same-sample bound
`N^(2D+7) |v-w|^(1/2)` on that norm-good event. The accepted target-mesh
estimate is `N^(2D+7) mesh(N)^(-1/2) ≤ N^(-2)`. Thus the endpoint change is
at most
`N^(2D+7) sqrt(k / mesh N) ≤ N^(2D+7) N^2 mesh(N)^(-1/2) ≤ 1`.
This is T1117's actual `N^-2 sqrt(k)` route, not a fixed-terminal-time
substitution.

The global `19/4` Lipschitz bound for `cutTrunc` then gives a cutoff change at
most `19/4`. Since the actual widened weight is in `[0,1]` and both truncated
arguments are nonnegative, pointwise on the same good event,
`widenedW(k) * cutTrunc(jSnorm(k))^(2q)` is at most
`2^(2q) * (cutTrunc(jSnorm(s_N))^(2q) + (19/4)^(2q))`.
At `k=0`, `cutNetPt = s_N` and the actual empty-prefix `widenedW` is exactly
one. Therefore the first term integrates to the assumed full k0 integral.
For fixed `δ,q`, `N^(δq/2) ≥ 1` eventually absorbs the additive constant.
The proof establishes measurability and integrability for the literal
truncated integrands before applying integral monotonicity. Its constant is
`C₁ = 2^(2q) (C₀ + (19/4)^(2q))`.

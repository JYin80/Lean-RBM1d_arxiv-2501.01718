# T315 — joint first-cell producer preflight for `xiRhs₂`

## Verdict and exact target

**Incomplete as a joint model-level producer.** The proposed four-piece conclusion
`XiLK₁≺1`, `XiLK₂≺1`, `XiL₃≺1`, and growing `A_u` has no present same-window producer: the full-charge `StepGlue.AprioriDecayAll` needed for `XiLK₂≺1` is an unproved input, and the first-cell `LocalLawUnifIcc` needed for `Eq45Flow` is conditional on Step 2. There is, however, a strictly weaker noncircular route to the actual target `xiRhs₂≺1`: it needs only `XiLK₂≺A_s^{1/2}`, because `XiLK₂` occurs **quadratically divided by `A_u`**. That weaker bound is exactly the output of `flow_hTheta_of_cutHypEv`, conditional on its still-open `CutHypEv` input. No `Lemma514 2` is needed by this proposed route.

By `SumZeroDyn.xiRhs` (the sum version of paper (5.77)), on every `u∈TimeIcc s t N`,

```text
xiRhs X E 2 N u ω = XiLK₁(N,u,ω) + XiLK₂(N,u,ω)^2 / A_u + XiL₃(N,u,ω),
A_u = W_N ell_u eta_u.
```

The quantifier in each `StochDom` is `∀τ>0 ∀D>0 ∀ᶠ N`, with failure measured after an **existential over every time and label** in its index family. Thus the three bounds, once independently proved for the *same* `P`, `E`, `s`, and `t`, may be put on one high-probability event by choosing smaller tolerance and larger tail exponents and using `HighProb.inter`. `HighProb.nonempty` then gives a common sample eventually for the Gaussian probability measure. Separate nonempty witnesses cannot be intersected.

## Corrected noncircular calculation

Assume the two still-open **same-window** inputs `LocalLawUnifIcc d 0 s t firstCellPsi` and `CutHypEv` for `J=XiLK₂`, `Θ=A_s^{1/2}`, are produced independently of `Lemma514 2`. Then the available Step 1 theorem gives `AprioriFlow` and `XiL₃≺1`; the first input gives `Eq45Flow` and hence `XiLK₁≺1`; the second gives `XiLK₂≺W_N^{1/2}`. Since `A_u≥W_N/√2`, stochastic-domination multiplication and a deterministic comparison give

```text
XiLK₂² / A_u ≺ W_N / A_u ≤ √2 ≺ 1,
xiRhs₂ ≺ 1 + 1 + 1 ≺ 1.
```

All three `StochDom` outputs use `Gauss.P d` and the exact same `TimeIcc s t N`. At each requested `(τ,D)`, choose the three bounds at a fraction of `τ` and tail exponent above `D`; their high-probability events intersect by `HighProb.inter`. This proves a common event **conditionally on those two inputs**. The strict window and growing scale are unconditional model witnesses, but do not witness the still-open event premises. This argument does not establish `XiLK₂≺1`, and makes no arbitrary-length `xiRhs_m` claim.

## Next source and fallback

The narrowest missing source for this corrected first-cell route is a concrete `CutHypEv` (or direct `StochDom XiLK₂ A_s^{1/2}`) on the same Gaussian first cell, together with the `LocalLawUnifIcc` instance feeding `Eq45Flow`. A positive ticket should first establish the second input via a Step 2 moment/event estimate that does **not** appeal to `Lemma514 2`; the existing `flow_hTheta_of_cutHypEv` then handles the conversion. In parallel, instantiate `LocalLawUnifIcc` from a real Step 2 `LocalLawFlow` producer and check its deterministic control at `firstCellPsi`. If the latter is not available, an independent first-cell proof of `Eq45Flow` from its three actual `IBPFlow`/fluctuation-averaging fields is a separate fallback. Either route must retain the same `(N,u,ω)` probability quantifiers; a fixed-time or individual-sample estimate does not suffice.

  eventual-in-`N` statement. The two `∀ᶠ N` conclusions are intersected once;

  `initTerm(..., R_v, ...)/R_v^4`; the N2 conclusion keeps
  `tailTerm(..., R_v, ...)/R_v^4`. Here

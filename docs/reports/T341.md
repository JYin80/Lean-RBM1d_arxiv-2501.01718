## Scale check and nondegenerate first cell

`Cond272 B E s t` (`Flow/Hypotheses.lean:296`) says eventually
`A_t^(-1)≤((1-t_N)/(1-s_N))^30`, hence `R_N^30≤A_t`.
For `s_N≤u≤t_N<1`, `1≤r_u≤R_N` and flow-scale monotonicity gives
`A_t≤A_u`. Consequently, pointwise on a common eventual set,

```text
r_u^4 / A_u ≤ R_N^4 / R_N^30 = R_N^(-26) ≤ 1,
r_u^4 A_u^(-2) ≤ A_u^(-1).
```

The fixed factor 2 in item 2 and fixed `C` in item 3 cost no stochastic
exponent. `Cond272Reg` (`Flow/Thm221Bare.lean:126`) adds only the **separate**
eventual inequality `N^c≤A_t`; it does **not** imply
`r_u^4/A_u≤N^(-c)R_N^(-26)`. That stronger product would require
`Cond272'`, namely `N^c R_N^30≤A_t`. No product gain is needed here.

There is a positive-duration actual Gaussian parameter regime for the
**already-produced** leaves. In `Gauss/FirstCellStep1LocalLaw.lean:245–279`, take
`Dims.exampleGrow`, `E=0`, `kappa=1`, endpoint `t_N=1/2`, and paper-grid
exponent `tau=1/2`; for `N≥4`, `N^(-1/2)≤1/2`.
`cond272Reg_grid_step_domain` (`Flow/Thm221NoEL.lean:993`) then chooses
`tau'>0,c>0` and supplies `Cond272Reg` for the first grid cell `k=0`.
Its times are `firstCellS tau' = gridT W tau' (1/2) 0 = 0` and
`firstCellT tau' = gridT W tau' (1/2) 1`, with `0≤s≤t<1`.
`first_cell_joint_grid_scales` (`Gauss/Eq45FlowGrid.lean:295`) gives
`s_N<t_N` eventually and `r_t>1` eventually, so the window does not
collapse. The T326 proof uses **this same grid witness** to obtain
`LocalLawUnifIcc` from Step 1; `first_cell_step5_of_localLaw'` gives
`Eq45Flow`. The exported T326 existential
`firstCell_step1_localLaw_witness` does not itself retain `Cond272Reg` in
its statement; a simultaneous packaged Lean witness would have to reuse
the proof's chosen `tau'`, rather than intersect two unrelated existentials.
None of these facts supplies actual `jS_D ≺ r_u^4`.

## Why the `xiRhs ≺ 1` target is wrong here

Paper Lemma 5.14 (5.92), PDF printed p. 67, retains the lower-loop
control `Φ` in addition to `Λ^(1/2)`. The four fields of
`Gauss.Lemma514Premises` (`Gauss/Lemma514Moment.lean:388`) put the lower
`flowXiLK` terms, their scaled products, and `flowXiL_(n+3)` under an
arbitrary deterministic `Φ`. The definition `SumZeroDyn.xiRhs`
(`Hierarchy/SumZeroDyn.lean:1566`) sums exactly those terms for `m=n+2`.
`SumZeroDyn.xiRhs_stochDom` (`:3012`) concludes precisely
`xiRhs_(n+2) ≺ (2n+3)Φ`, not `≺1`. `Φ≥0` is not required to be
eventually bounded by 1. Thus the arbitrary-premise interface does not
justify `xiRhs ≺1`; replacing its control by 1 would add a substantive
assumption. Using Step 4's later all-length `XiLK≺1` to prove the base
Lemma 5.14 is circular (T321). The main projected-drift row must carry
`Φ` through the weighted-time producer; T290/T296 only eliminate its
additive top-length cycle.

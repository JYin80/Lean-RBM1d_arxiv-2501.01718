# T885 — density Stieltjes integral versus algebraic `msc`: mathematical/API preflight

## Verdict

**Feasible, with a compiled first seam; no repository theorem is produced by this ticket.** The shortest immediate bridge identifies the ordinary density Stieltjes integral with `RBM.msc` at the finite spectral parameters

`z_u = i (1-u)/sqrt(u)`, `0 < u ≤ 1/2`.

This uses T873's accepted complex density integral and a separate algebraic uniqueness proof for `msc`. It is noncircular: the density integral is evaluated without `msc`, while `msc` is identified by its quadratic equation and positive imaginary part. The broader statement for **every** `y>0` is also mathematically available from T871's scalar identity, but the current public T873 theorem covers only `u≤1/2`. A general-`y` Lean theorem still needs a general complex reconstruction (or a direct odd-real-part/Poisson calculation) and the elementary `y↔u` parameter algebra. No Q0 or paper closure follows from this report.

## Paper, definitions, and sign

I read the actual `paper/250520-YinJun-v2.pdf`, PDF page 7 immediately before (2.1). It gives

`ρ_sc(x) = sqrt((4-x²)_+)/(2π)` and `m_sc(z) = ∫_ℝ ρ_sc(x)/(x-z) dx`.

`RBM.semicircleDensity` in `RBM1D/Defs/SemicircleDensityMeasure.lean` uses `Real.sqrt (4-x²)/(2π)`; `Real.sqrt` vanishes on nonpositive real arguments, so this is the same density. `semicircleMeasure` is its `withDensity` measure, with total mass one. The paper uses `(x-z)⁻¹`: at `z=iy`, `y>0`, the kernel's imaginary part is `y/(x²+y²)>0`. Reversing the denominator would reverse the answer's sign.

`RBM.msc` in `RBM1D/Defs/Semicircle.lean` chooses between two square-root formulas. Its public facts are `msc_mul z : msc z * (msc z + z) = -1` for all `z`, and `msc_im_pos` when `0<z.im`. Neither fact alone says that a density integral equals `msc`. Indeed, the integral must first be computed from the explicit measure; T871/T873 supply that computation on the required flow.

## Exact shortest theorem chain

Let `a=√u`, `b=1-u`, and `z_u=i b/a`. For `0<u≤1/2`, `a>0`, `b>0`, and `Im z_u=b/a>0`.

1. **Upper-half-plane root uniqueness.** If `m(m+z)=-1`, `Im m>0`, and `Im z>0`, then `m=msc z`. Subtracting `msc_mul z` gives `(m-msc z)(m+msc z+z)=0`. The second factor cannot vanish: all three imaginary parts are positive. This proof uses the *actual* chosen-root hypotheses, not an unproved branch identity. I typechecked this lemma in `/tmp/T885Probe.lean` against the installed module.
2. **Algebraic flow value.** `a²=u` and `a(a+b/a)=1`, so `(ia)(ia+z_u)=-1`; `Im(ia)=a>0`. Uniqueness yields `msc z_u=ia`. The exact specialization with `0<u≤1/2` also typechecked in the scratch probe.
3. **Kernel scaling.** Pointwise,
   `(x-z_u)⁻¹ = a (a x-i b)⁻¹`.
   The denominator identity is `a(x-z_u)=a x-i b`; the inverse identity needs `a≠0`. This pointwise equality typechecked in the scratch probe. Mathlib's installed `MeasureTheory.integral_const_mul` pulls the constant `a` out of the Bochner integral.
4. **Analytic producer.** T873's accepted `semicircleFlowResolvent_integral u hu0 hu1` says `∫(a x-i b)⁻¹ d semicircleMeasure=i`. Hence `∫(x-z_u)⁻¹ d semicircleMeasure=ia=msc z_u`. The complete specialized integral equality also typechecked as an anonymous scratch `example` with exit 0.

A suitable first public statement in a future `Defs/SemicircleStieltjesBridge.lean` is:

```lean
theorem semicircleMeasure_stieltjes_flow (u : ℝ)
    (hu : 0 < u) (hu1 : u ≤ 1 / 2) :
    (∫ x : ℝ,
      (((x : ℂ) - Complex.I * (((1-u)/Real.sqrt u : ℝ) : ℂ))⁻¹)
        ∂semicircleMeasure) =
      msc (Complex.I * (((1-u)/Real.sqrt u : ℝ) : ℂ))
```

The natural dependency is `SemicircleDensityMeasure → SemicirclePoissonIntegral (T871) → SemicircleFlowResolvent (T873) → proposed bridge`; `SemicircleDensityMeasure` already imports the algebraic `Semicircle`, so this direction has no import cycle. The existing definitions and frozen signatures need no change.

## Endpoints, integrability, and the all-`y` boundary

For every `y>0` and real `x`, `‖(x:ℂ)-i y‖≥y`; the inverse kernel is continuous and bounded by `1/y`, hence integrable against the finite semicircle measure. T873 already proves the corresponding flow-kernel integrability when `u≤1/2`. Thus the proposed statements require no artificial `Integrable` hypothesis.

At `u=1/2`, `z_u=i/√2`, `msc z_u=i/√2`, and T873's nonconstant `u=1/2` density integral is `i`; the scaling relation has nonzero factor `a=1/√2`. At `u=0`, T873 separately proves `∫(-i)⁻¹ d semicircleMeasure=i`, but `z_u` is **undefined** because `√u=0`. In particular, identifying this endpoint with `msc(zt 0 0)=msc(i)` would be false: the flow kernel is a scaled resolvent, and `msc(i)=i(√5-1)/2`, not `i`. The correct closed-flow formulation is the already proved integral `=i` at `u=0`, together with `a⁻¹ msc(z_u)=i` for `0<u≤1/2`; it does not assert a finite-`z` `msc` identity at `u=0`.

The map `u↦(1-u)/√u` takes `(0,1/2]` onto `[1/√2,∞)`, not all `(0,∞)`. Consequently the compiled specialized bridge cannot be advertised as an all-`y>0` theorem. For general `y>0`, put

`r(y)=(√(y²+4)-y)/2`, `u=r(y)²`.

Then `0<r<1`, `r²+y r=1`, `0<u<1`, and `y=(1-u)/√u`. T871's `semicircleMeasure_flow_poisson` already applies throughout this full `u` interval. Reconstruct the complex integral for `0<u<1` by reflection (`semicircleMeasure_map_neg`) for its odd real part and T871 for its imaginary part, exactly as T873 does for `u≤1/2`; alternatively apply T871 directly after factoring `r²(x²+y²)`. The existing `kern_integral` and odd-part helper in T873 are `private` and have the narrower `u≤1/2` signature, so a new public/general reconstruction must be written. The resulting value is `i r(y) = i(√(y²+4)-y)/2`; it satisfies `m(m+iy)=-1` and has positive imaginary part, so root uniqueness identifies it with `msc(iy)`. This is a precise remaining Lean producer, not an accepted theorem.

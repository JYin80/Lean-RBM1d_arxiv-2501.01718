## Claim and scope

Under T1201's hypotheses (`|E| < 2`, `60 ≤ D`, `0 ≤ s_N ≤ t_N < 1`,
`c > 0`, `Cond272Reg`, and `deltaWeight ≥ 0`), fix an integer `p ≥ 1`. The
theorem `eventually_integral_positiveTimeCrossBudget_le_normGood_add_error`
gives one eventual cutoff in `N`, uniform over every active target-mesh cell
`k ≤ cutNetTop ... N` and every output `a`, such that, for
`v = cutNetPt s (targetMesh D) N k`,

```text
∫_(s N)^v positiveTimeCrossBudget
  ≤ ∫_(s N)^v normGoodCrossBudget
      + (15*p/4) * N^(-1) * (√v - √(s N)).
```

The good integral is the exact T1201 norm-good restriction. The theorem
introduces no assumptions and proves no small bound for that integral, no full
`N1`, no all-index `hfamily`, and no A′ closure.

the full and norm-good budget definitions both have zero denominator and hence
evaluate to zero under Lean's totalized real division; the payment does too.

`target_endpoint_mem_window` gives `s N ≤ v` and `s N ≥ 0`, the payment is
interval-integrable. T1123 and T1205 supply integrability of the left side and
good term. Interval-integral monotonicity, additivity, and the exact
inverse-square-root integral then give the claim, with
`2 * (15*p/8) = 15*p/4`. This also covers `s N = 0` and `v=s N`.

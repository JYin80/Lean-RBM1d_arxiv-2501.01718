    M_N = targetMesh 60 N = N^(4·60+18) = N^258.

    u_j = j/M_N,

The literal prefix comparison gives

      ((k · card(LoopArg))^(1/(2m))) · (2 + N^-10).

    prefixSample / threshold ≤ 3e/(8e²) = 3/(8e) < 1.

APrimeCrossJointSplit.transition; its upper inequality < 2 is not needed.

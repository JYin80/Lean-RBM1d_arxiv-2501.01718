## Statement and proof

In `RBM.Gauss`, with no additional hypotheses, the module proves:

```lean
theorem measurableSet_unusedImage_prefixSigma
    (W : ℕ) (k : Fin W) (v : Fin W) :
    MeasurableSet[prefixSigma W k.val]
      {π : PermΩ W | v ∈ unusedImages W k π}
```

By `mem_unusedImages_iff`, the event is exactly the intersection, over
`i.val < k.val`, of the complements of the coordinate cylinders
`{π | π i = v}`. Each cylinder is one of the generators of
`prefixSigma W k.val`. Measurability follows from generator membership,
closure under complements, and closure under the indexed intersection. The
proof uses the actual `generateFrom (prefixCylinders W k)` definition and does
not use ambient-top measurability or a conditional law.

The module also proves the requested boundary and nondegeneracy checks:

- `W=0` has no `k : Fin 0`.
- At `W=1, k=0` every value is unused; likewise at `W=2, k=0`.
- For every final valid reveal (`k.val + 1 = W`), the unused set has cardinality
  one.
- At `W=2, k=1, v=0`, `Equiv.swap 0 1` belongs to the event and the identity
  does not. This is an explicit nonempty proper event separating two realized
  permutations.

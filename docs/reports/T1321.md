and nonempty for every `N`; the source proves nonemptiness using `ω = 0`. This witnesses the
deterministic model/window/event data only. **It does not witness or prove `hfamily`.**

displayed moving-window family estimate is supplied. The paper formulas motivate the interface;
the Lean declaration is a conditional assembly interface, not a formalization of those formulas.

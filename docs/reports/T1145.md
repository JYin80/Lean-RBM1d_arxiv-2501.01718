## Mathematical preflight and transfer

I checked the accepted T475 modulus and the literal T1097 and T1117 source
signatures. On `good N`, T475 gives

```text
|jSnorm(v,ω) - jSnorm(w,ω)| ≤ N^(2D+7) |v-w|^(1/2).
```

The target mesh is eventually `N^(4D+18)`, so the modulus coefficient times
the inverse square-root mesh is at most `N^-2`. For an active `k` in this
ticket's extended range, `cutNetPt(k)` lies in `[s_N,t_N]`, and
`√k ≤ N^(2+δ/4)`. Thus the same-sample endpoint change from `s_N` is at most
`N^(δ/4)`. T1117 proves the good-event estimate only through `k ≤ N^4`, so it
does not cover the range proved here; the Lean proof extends the T1097
empty-prefix estimate directly using T475.

The cutoff `cutTrunc` is globally `19/4`-Lipschitz, and the literal
order-`q` `widenedW` is measurable and lies in `[0,1]`. Pointwise on `good N`,
the endpoint transfer therefore bounds the integrand by

```text
2^(2q) (|cutTrunc(jSnorm(s_N))|^(2q)
        + (19/4)^(2q) N^(δq/2)).
```

The first term integrates to the T1097 `k=0` expectation, bounded by
`C₀ N^(δq/2)`. The second already has the target exponent. The proof takes
`C = 2^(2q)(C₀ + (19/4)^(2q))`, independent of `N`, and restricts the result
to the good event. No union bound or mesh-cardinality loss occurs.

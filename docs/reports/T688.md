## Statement and proof check

- The existing definition is exactly `permutationFourierSum W x χ π = (∑ i : Fin W, χ i * x (π i)) / (W : ℂ)`. The new theorems quantify over every `W` with `2 ≤ W`, every complex table `x, χ : Fin W → ℂ`, and assume only `∑ i, χ i = 0`. No norm bound on `x` or character property of `χ` enters the mean theorem.
- `permutation_image_sum_eq_at` reindexes the full permutation set by the bijection given by right multiplication by the domain swap. Thus the sums of `x (π i)` at any two positions agree. Summing over all `W` positions and then interchanging finite sums yields `W · ∑π x (π i) = card(Perm(Fin W)) · ∑j x j`. The proof cancels `W` using `2 ≤ W`; it does not invert `card(Perm(Fin W))` to establish this identity. The counting formula therefore has the correct multiplicity `card(Perm(Fin W))/W`.
- Finite-sum interchange and `∑χ=0` give the unnormalized permutation sum zero while retaining the definition's division by `W`. Dividing zero by the permutation count gives the uniform mean zero. The permutation type is inhabited (identity), and the proof explicitly checks its cardinality is nonzero. No false cardinality inversion or implicit zero-denominator step was found.
- The `W=2` witness takes `x=(2,-2)` and `χ=(1,-1)`. Both are nonconstant, `∑χ=0`, and the Fourier value at the identity is `2 ≠ 0`; its permutation mean is zero by cancellation. The hypotheses are therefore nonvacuous.
- Only `Mathlib` is imported by the swap module; the mean module imports the swap module, which never imports the mean module. There is no circular project import. The root currently imports the swap module but not the mean module.

Paper (2.3) is the Green-function entrywise local law, and (2.4) is the partial trace local law with its own `W⁻¹` block normalization. Neither formula asserts this finite permutation identity. The audited declarations contain no `N`, time, first-cell endpoint, sample, or shared-event assertion. They establish neither Fourier-character orthogonality nor a permutation tail or conditional exposure estimate.

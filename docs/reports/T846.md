# T846 — weighted conditional expectation of two successive permutation images

## Result and scope

`RBM1D/Gauss/PermutationTwoNextImagesWeightedCondExp.lean` proves
`uniformPerm_twoNextImages_weighted_condExp`. For every width, adjacent valid
indices `k,j : Fin W` with `j.val = k.val + 1`, and deterministic
`f : Fin W → Fin W → ℝ`, the conditional expectation of
`f (ρ k) (ρ j)` given the generated `prefixSigma W k.val` equals almost
everywhere the sum of `f v w` over ordered distinct `v,w` in the actual
`unusedImages W k π`, divided by the real product
`(W-k.val)(W-k.val-1)`.

The proof expands the observable over all ordered image pairs, applies
conditional expectation to the finite sum and scalar multiples, and uses
T837's pair-indicator a.e. law term by term. The finite sums are then
restricted to the two unused-image sets. It does not multiply dependent
one-step conditional expectations or assume the weighted target formula.

## Boundaries and nondegeneracy

The module records positive real denominator factors for every valid adjacent
pair, no index at width zero, and no adjacent pair at width one. At width two,
the explicit weight `f(v,w) = 1` only at `(1,0)` has values zero and one on the
two ordered outcomes; its conditional mean is `1/2`. The identity and
transposition singleton events both have positive uniform-permutation mass.
The last eligible pair (`k=W-2`, `j=W-1`) has denominator two.

There are no new hypotheses requiring a separate satisfiability witness. The
width-two example provides a nonconstant concrete instance.

# T848 — finite next-image conditional variance

## Result

`RBM1D/Gauss/PermutationNextImageVariance.lean` proves
`uniformPerm_nextImage_conditionalVariance`. For every `W`, `k : Fin W`, and
`f : Fin W → ℝ`, it gives the almost-everywhere identity under `uniformPerm W`

```lean
(uniformPerm W)[(fun ρ =>
  (f (ρ k) - unusedImageAverage W k f ρ) ^ 2) | prefixSigma W k.val]
  =ᵐ[uniformPerm W]
(fun π => unusedImageAverage W k (fun v => (f v) ^ 2) π -
  (unusedImageAverage W k f π) ^ 2)
```

The proof applies T833's weighted conditional law to `f` and `f²`, then
expands the centered square. T840 supplies the prefix-measurable unused-image
average and its conditional-mean result. The cross term is pulled out with
Mathlib's `condExp_mul_of_stronglyMeasurable_left`; the square of the average
is prefix-measurable. All products and powers are integrable on the finite
permutation space (`Integrable.of_finite`). The proof does not assume a
conditional variance identity.

## Boundaries and nondegeneracy

- `W = 0` has no `k : Fin W`.
- `uniformPerm_nextImage_conditionalVariance_one` and
  `uniformPerm_nextImage_conditionalVariance_last` prove zero variance when
  there is only one available image, including the last reveal.
- `uniformPerm_nextImage_conditionalVariance_two_witness` uses `W = 2`, `k = 0`,
  `f(0)=0`, and `f(1)=1`. Both the identity and transposition have positive
  mass, and the conditional variance is `1/4` on both outcomes.

There are no new assumptions requiring a separate satisfiability witness.

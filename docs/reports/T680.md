# T680 — independent audit of T676's finite Fourier swap

## Claim and verdict

**Verdict: proved as written, as a standalone deterministic P1 lemma.** In
`RBM1D/Gauss/PermutationFourierSwap.lean`, for `W : ℕ`, complex tables
`x, χ : Fin W → ℂ`, `π : Equiv.Perm (Fin W)`, and positions `j,k`,

\[
 F(π)=W^{-1}\sum_{i:\operatorname{Fin}W}χ_i x_{π(i)},\qquad
 F((\operatorname{swap}j k).\operatorname{trans}π)-F(π)
 =W^{-1}(χ_j-χ_k)(x_{π(k)}-x_{π(j)}).
\]

The identity has no `W ≥ 2` hypothesis and includes `j = k`. With `W ≥ 2`,
`∀ i, ‖x i‖ ≤ 2`, and `∀ i, ‖χ i‖ ≤ 1`, the stated norm is at most `8/W`.
The explicit `W=2` witness attains equality with both tables nonconstant.

Paper (2.3)–(2.4), checked in `paper/250520-YinJun-v2.pdf` (PDF page 8),
are later local Green-function entry and partial-trace laws. They do not
state this permutation lemma or follow from it alone. This module has no
sample, expectation, event, `N`, time, first-cell, carrier, or Fourier-mode
claim.

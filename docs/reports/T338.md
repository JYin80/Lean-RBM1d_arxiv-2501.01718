## Quantifier order and conversion

The `LocalLawUnifIcc` and `HighProb` interfaces place stochastic tolerance and tail exponent before `∀ᶠ N`. For a requested domination tolerance `τ>0`, choose `θ>0` with `θ<min(a/4,τ/16)`, then, for a requested tail exponent `D>0`, choose a **fixed** integer `p≥1` sufficiently large. The T337 gain is `∀ p, ∀ᶠ N`; it does not require a single `N₀` uniform in `p`. The exceptional-event estimate likewise chooses its high-probability exponent after fixed `p` and before `N`.

Eventually the floor and cap are inactive, so `δθ²=(N+4)^(2θ) Ψ²`. For `N≥4`, the simple bound `N+4≤N²` gives `δθ²≤N^(4θ) Ψ²`. The moment theorem gives a constant `C_p` such that

`E|flucAvg|^(2p) ≤ C_p (ρ B_p)^(2p) ≤ C'_p N^(8pθ) Ψ^(4p)`.

Markov at `N^τ Ψ²` then gives `P(|flucAvg|>N^τ Ψ²)≤C'_p N^(-2p(τ-4θ))`. Choose `p` so `2p(τ-4θ)>D`; the fixed constant is absorbed by the strict exponent margin. A sharper `N+4≤2N` comparison yields loss `2θ` instead of `4θ`, but is unnecessary. Thus the exponent closes without any bound on `W Ψ²`.

The generic `stochDom_of_momentDom` (`Gauss/Domination.lean:109`) requires its control to be strictly positive for **every** `N`. Raw `Ψ²` need not meet this on the finite prefix. Use `ψ_N²` (globally positive) for that bridge, then transfer to `Ψ_N²` after the eventual equality `ψ_N=Ψ_N`, or use the direct Markov proof of `UnifDomIcc`. This is a second necessary interface detail, not a mathematical obstruction.

At `p=0`, the budgeted moment theorem is a valid zero-moment statement (the natural subtraction `2p-1` truncates to zero); the conversion chooses `p≥1`. At `u=0`, `flucAvg_at_zero` (`Gauss/DetFlucAvg.lean:57`) gives exact vanishing, and all spectral-envelope inputs remain valid for `|E|<2`; the fixed-time argument does not exclude the endpoint.

For a nondegenerate actual-model instance of the eventual assumptions, take T326's positive first cell, `E=0`, `u_N=firstCellT τ' N`, and `Ψ_N=2·firstCellPsi N=W_N^{-1/2}` (T336 uses this same enlarged scale). The first-cell local law transfers by monotonicity and restriction to the terminal time. `firstCellT τ' N` is eventually strictly positive and at most `1/2`, so `η_{u_N}≥1/2`. The growing-bandwidth estimate implies `Ψ_N≤N^{-a}` eventually for, for example, `a=1/8`. Thus the intended eventual premises are jointly satisfiable on a genuinely random time cell; the finite-prefix counterexample above concerns only the erroneous all-`N` weight comparison.

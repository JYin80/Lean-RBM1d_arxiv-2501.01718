# T342 — fixed-time deterministic fluctuation averaging: source and live-model audit

## Verdict and boundary

**Yes, as a fixed-time mathematical implication from the actual entry local law; no, as a theorem currently present at the general `fixedTimeFAStatement` signature.** The finite-minor and resampling argument of [40], Appendix B, survives the replacement of its printed spectral floor `η>M⁻¹` by the repository's eventual `η_{u_N}≥N⁻ᴷ`. The replacement changes polynomial constants on exceptional sets, not the two powers of the deterministic entry scale. The actual Lean chain through T332 and T337 already proves the finite-word gain for each fixed `2p`; the remaining work is the **eventual** row/block moment-to-`UnifDomIcc` wrapper at the original control `Ψ²`. This is not a new stochastic fluctuation-averaging hypothesis. Nothing here yields a time supremum over a nontrivial interval from a singleton fixed-time assertion.

The source checked is Erdős–Knowles–Yau–Yin, *The Local Semicircle Law for a General Class of Random Matrices*, Electronic Journal of Probability **18** (2013), no. 59, [author PDF dated 24 May 2013](https://www.unige.ch/~knowles/SC.pdf), Appendix B, especially Lemmas B.1–B.2 and (B.6)–(B.18). The [published record](https://research-explorer.ista.ac.at/record/2837) identifies DOI 10.1214/EJP.v18-2473. The repository paper `paper/250520-YinJun-v2.pdf` invokes [40] (4.11) in its own (4.12); its (4.5) uses the random scale `max L_{u,(+,-)}`. T332's target instead has deterministic `Ψ²`, assumes the fixed-time entry local law, and asks only for the concrete `Sblk` row and block weights. It is an adapted auxiliary, not a verbatim instance of [40] or the full random-scale paper (4.5).

`B_p=(8 minorDiffC(2p)+4)δ_N`, `ρ=4δ_N`, `ρ B_p=(32 minorDiffC(2p)+16)δ_N²`.

The direct consumer `integral_norm_flucAvg_pow_le_iter_budget` (`FlucIter.lean:2480`) then applies to each actual row/block weight, uniformly in the index, after intersecting the eventual gain, support-cardinality, and `c≤ρ²` events. Therefore the existing `unifDomIcc_flucAvg_iter_budget` (`MinorDiffCond.lean:1060`), which requests `c≤ρ²` for every `N`, cannot be applied unchanged. This is the exact missing Lean interface: either prime that consumer with an eventual `hcρ` or apply `integral_norm_flucAvg_pow_le_iter_budget` inside the eventual filter and prove `UnifDomIcc` by direct Markov. No new finite-minor identity or stochastic FA assumption is needed.

Eventually the floor is inactive because `W≤N` and `W⁻¹/²≤Ψ`, and `δ_N≤(N+4)^θ Ψ_N≤N^{2θ}Ψ_N` for `N≥4`. Thus the direct moment bound has the shape

`E|flucAvg|^{2p} ≤ C_p (ρ B_p)^{2p} ≤ C'_p N^{8pθ} Ψ_N^{4p}`.

At threshold `N^τΨ_N²`, Markov gives `C'_p N^{-2p(τ-4θ)}`. Since `θ<τ/16`, choose a fixed `p≥1` with `2p(τ-4θ)>D` and absorb `C'_p` in the strict exponent margin. The target `UnifDomIcc` quantifies one row or block index at a time, so no index union cost is needed. The `p=0` moment is harmless but is not used for Markov. Keep the globally positive `detFlucControl` for any generic moment bridge, since raw `Ψ_N` may vanish at finitely many `N`; eventual equality transfers back to `Ψ_N²`. All time quantifiers in this argument are the singleton `v∈[u_N,u_N]`.

The actual growing-bandwidth `Dims.exampleGrow` has a Step 1 entry local law on a **positive-length** first cell: `firstCell_localLawUnifIcc_of_step1` (`FirstCellStep1LocalLaw.lean:240`) supplies some `τ'>0` and `first_cell_window_nondegenerate` gives `firstCellS τ' N<firstCellT τ' N` eventually. In particular evaluate at the terminal `u_N=firstCellT τ' N>0`: `H_{u_N}=√u_N X` is genuinely random, `u_N≤1/2` gives `η_{u_N}≥1/2≥N⁻¹` eventually, and `Ψ_N=2 firstCellPsi_N=W_N⁻¹/²≤N⁻¹/⁸` eventually. Restrict the entry law to this endpoint and enlarge its control from `firstCellPsi` to `Ψ`. These are simultaneous hypotheses of the general fixed-time statement with `E=0`, `a=1/8`, `K=1`; they do not use A′ or the desired fixed-time conclusion. T337's current threshold witness instead uses `u=0`, where `flucAvg_at_zero` vanishes identically, so it is a valid premise witness but not the meaningful positive-time test.

# T1123 — time integrability of the actual positive-time cross budget

## Verdict: mathematical preflight PASS; requested integrability proved

For the fixed admissible parameters of T1089 and each fixed integer `p ≥ 1`,
the theorem
`RBM.APrimeGeneralMovingCrossBudgetTimeIntegrable.eventually_intervalIntegrable_positive_time_cross_budget`
proves, eventually in `N`, that the actual T1089 budget is interval-integrable
on `[s_N, cutNetPt ... N k]`, simultaneously for every active target-mesh
prefix `k` and output `a`. This includes `k=0`, `p=1`, and the endpoint
`r=0`. The theorem has the same deterministic hypotheses and no additional
event or rate assumption.

## Mathematical preflight

I checked only `paper/250520-YinJun-v2.pdf`. Formula (5.21) is the loop
hierarchy identity with its stochastic-integral term. Formula (5.24) is the
BDG moment bound for that martingale term in terms of the time integral of the
transported `E⊗E` covariance. T1123 proves the local integrability needed for
the actual smooth cross budget; it does not prove (5.21), (5.24), a small
`N1` estimate, or a later A-prime conclusion.

The preflight checks passed:

1. `APrimeDriftTimeFamily.qvAt_eq_evolved` identifies the exact rate with
   `qvRateEvolved (coordAt ...)` along the actual `Hflow`. The time-measurability
   producer is `APrimeQVRateTime.measurable_qvAt_on_window`: it uses the same
   normalized `coordAt`, its parameterized matrix derivative, and the actual
   flow map. That map is continuous in time; the inspected
   `Gauss.continuous_Hflow_time` also includes the possible endpoint `u=0`.
2. `APrimeGeneralMovingJointMeasurable.measurableSet_transition` and
   `continuous_prefixGradient`, together with joint measurability of this
   `qvAt`, make the literal
   `transition.indicator (prefixGradient * sqrt(qvAt))` measurable on
   time × sample. Integrating its actual `2p`-power over `Gauss.P` gives a
   measurable `momNorm` in time. The generic
   `continuousOn_momNormW_of_envelope` pattern was checked, but this proof
   needs only the available joint measurability; it does not infer
   measurability from a pointwise envelope.
3. T617's all-sample envelope is the producer used by T1107/T1108. The
   accepted T1107 bound for the same T1089 budget is
   `Bc(r) ≤ crossMomentCoeff(p)/(4p√r) · N^(2D+17)` for positive `r`.
   Rewriting the right side as a constant times `r^(-1/2)` gives an integrable
   majorant, since Mathlib's
   `intervalIntegral.intervalIntegrable_rpow'` applies at exponent `-1/2 > -1`.
   At `r=0`, the original budget expression is zero by Lean's field convention
   for division by zero, and the real-power majorant is also zero.
4. The accepted `t995_positive_cell_witness` is re-exported. It supplies a
   satisfiable `E=0`, `D=60` context with an eventually positive-length active
   cell and positive actual smooth weight at `p=1`; the integrability proof
   itself also covers the empty prefix and degenerate interval.

## Implementation

The new module proves joint measurability of the exact cross `jointRate`, then
measurability of its actual `L^(2p)` norm through product integration. It
combines that fact with the T1107 pointwise cap and compares against
`K · r^(-1/2)`, treating `r=0` directly. The only asymptotic quantifier is the
eventual-in-`N` one already present in T1107; `p` is fixed before `N`.

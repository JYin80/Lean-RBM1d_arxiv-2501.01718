## An exact adaptive seam and its first unmet moment

Retain `x=condExpDiag`, the `goodSetFlow` threshold `δ`, and an independent polynomially fine `μ`. The transport theorems `ibpFlow_of_unifDom'`, `flucRowFlow_of_unifDom'`, and `flucBlkFlow_of_unifDom'` (`Eq45FlowInputs.lean:1615–1693`) already accept the *literal* fixed-time `UnifDomIcc` bounds with random control `Lmax`. Their common net theorem (`:1586`) uses the existing Hölder estimates, `flowNetEvent = goodSetFlow ∩ {‖Xmat‖≤N}`, the polynomial lower control `N^{-2}≤Lmax` there, and `Lmax_flow_slow_of_net'` (`:1499`). It does not need `Lmax≍W^{-1}`. This is a usable downstream seam **conditional** on a later-cell `HighProb goodSetFlow` and on the three fixed-time adaptive estimates below; it is not their proof.

For a prospective first leaf, write `L_{N,u}(ω)=Lmax(Hflow d N u ω,zt E u)` and `Q_{N,u}=max(L_{N,u},N^{-2})`. Let `R^{IBP}_{N,u,i}` be the literal norm in `IBPFlow` with `x=condExpDiag`; define `R^{row}` and `R^{blk}` by the other two literal norms. A sufficiently strong, noncircular **moment target** for each of these three observables is, schematically,

```
∀ q≥1, ∀ ε>0, ∃ C(q,ε)>0, ∀ᶠ N,
  ∀ u∈[s_N,t_N], ∀ v in the appropriate finite row/block index,
    E[1_{goodSetFlow_N} (R_{N,u,v}/Q_{N,u})^(2q)] ≤ C(q,ε) N^(q ε).
```

The order `q,ε` **before** eventual `N` is essential; `q=0` is not used. Once a later-cell `HighProb goodSetFlow` is proved, the `N^{-2}≤L_{N,u}` lower bound makes `Q=L` on that common event. Markov with `ε<2τ` and `q` chosen after requested `τ,D` yields the exact fixed-time `UnifDomIcc` failure bound `P{N^τ L<R}≤N^{-D}` uniformly in `u` and the finite index. The net then gives each `TimeIcc` `StochDom`. The expectation is over the actual Gaussian `P d` and the same `ω` appears in `R`, `Q`, and the event; a product of separate high-probability statements is insufficient. `q` may be taken large enough for the polynomial index/net counts. The displayed moment is a **candidate obligation**, not a known estimate; it must be derived from Gaussian row integration/minor replacement and the pre-(4.5) Green estimates, not postulated as a new input for the final chain.

The first concrete failure when trying to derive its IBP member from existing code is `unifDomIcc_condRow_prod_green_diag` (`CondStableFlow.lean:636`): the row integral of `(G_{ii}-m)(G_{kk}-m)` is controlled by **deterministic** `Ψ_N²`, since `E_i[Ψ_N²]=Ψ_N²`. An adaptive version needs to control the row integral against the **same sample's** `L_{N,u}`, for example a moving-time `E_i[L_{N,u}] ≺ L_{N,u}` or a direct normalized conditional-product moment with the off-row minor in the denominator. `CondDom.stochDom_condRow_of_envelope` explicitly requires this conditional stability, because an exceptional event survives row integration. The existing `CondStableInst.condStable_Lmax` (`:464`) is only for fixed real `t<1`; its row-free proxy `W^{-1}` uses `Lmax≤η_t^{-2}W^{-1}` (`:270,440`). At the later right endpoint this upper comparison costs `η_{u_2}^{-2}=W^{4τ'}`, so it is not an `N^{o(1)}` moving-time comparison. The existing lower `W^{-1}/4≤Lmax` does not control `E_i[Lmax]` from above. This is the **first missing random-control moment/comparison** in the IBP route. A row-free minor `Lmax` proxy would be a plausible proof device, but its two-sided same-sample comparison has not been established here; treating it as a field without a witness would only rename the gap.

The row and block branches have an additional independent gap. `FlucGainUpTo'` (`FlucIter.lean:2329`) and `integral_norm_flucAvg_pow_le_iter_budget` (`:2480`) bound unconditional `2q` moments by `(ρ B)^{2q}` with deterministic `ρ,B`; `MinorDiffCond.unifDomIcc_flucAvg_iter_budget` (`:1060`) specializes this to `ρ B≈Ψ_N²`. These estimates contain no correlation between the fluctuation average and a *small realized* `L_{N,u}`. A new weighted/normalized moment for both coefficient families, or a proved same-sample lower bound `L_{N,u}≳_{≺}Ψ_N²`, is required. The latter would itself be a substantial later-cell two-loop lower estimate and cannot be inferred from the deterministic Ward bounds or from (5.8). In particular, simply allowing `Bp` to depend on `u` does not supply the missing random denominator.

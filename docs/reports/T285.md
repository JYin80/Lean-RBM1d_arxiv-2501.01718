# T285 — time integrability for the Gaussian A′ moment route

## Result

The third prefix premise of `MomentDuhamel.weightedMinkowski_of_deriv_le` is now a theorem for the Gaussian flow. `intervalIntegrable_momNormW_mul_flowY` uses `APrimeDuhamelModel.continuousOn_momFlow` to make the weighted moment norm continuous on `[s,v]`. Multiplying the first premise, `IntervalIntegrable (A+B) volume s v`, by this continuous function and restricting to any `[s,u]` gives

`IntervalIntegrable (fun r => momNormW (Gauss.P d) w p (flowY d N Ψ₁ r) * (A r+B r)) volume s u`.

The general lemma `intervalIntegrable_momNormW_mul_of_AB` isolates this implication. It uses genuine time continuity, not the value of an interval integral. `positive_prefix_witness` supplies a jointly satisfiable nontrivial instance on `[0,1]`: the nonconstant Gaussian weight `APrimeDuhamelModel.cosW d e` (nonconstancy is `cosW_ne`), observable `Y≡1`, and positive drift rate `A≡1`.

## Exact remaining producers

The other two premises are **not yet supplied** for the actual A′ coordinate family:

1. `IntervalIntegrable (fun r => A r+B r) volume s v`, where the planned `A` is `momNormW (Gauss.P d) w p (G r)` and `B` is the cross budget.
2. `IntervalIntegrable g volume s v`, where the planned `g` is `APrimeModel.rateNormW (Gauss.P d) w p (Q r)` for the evolved QV rate.

`momFlowDeriv_le` accepts `G` and `Q` independently at each fixed time. Its five sample-space integrability premises and three measurability premises are likewise pointwise in time; neither the theorem nor the current `APrimeAssembly.lean` fixes time families `G r`, `Q r` with a time-continuity or time-measurability theorem. `TestFunT₁` controls the test function `Ψ`, and `WeightC1` controls the fixed weight, but neither imposes regularity on these separate auxiliary rates. `APrimeDuhamelModel.qvRateEvolvedOn` names a possible `Q` family; the inspected A′ modules have no `IntervalIntegrable` theorem for its weighted `rateNormW`. `APrimeTimeInt.intervalIntegrable_qHatNear` covers a deterministic *envelope*, not equality or interval integrability of that weighted rate. The same distinction applies to the continuous drift expectations in `FlowContInt` and the stochastic drift norm in (G).

The minimum additional model producer can therefore be either:

- time continuity of the two displayed scalar rates on the closed finite window; or
- time measurability of each rate together with a genuine integrable finite-size envelope (a constant envelope for bounded terms, and an integrable `u^{-1/2}` envelope for a first-cell cross term).

These are facts to prove for the chosen concrete `G`, `Q`, and `B`; they are **not** new hypotheses added to a conditional `hfamily`. A numerical N1/N2 integral budget cannot replace them, as the T280e counterexample already records.

The new compiled Gaussian-specific certificates `no_time_integrability_from_pointwise_gaussian_moments` and `no_time_integrability_from_pointwise_gaussian_qv` use the actual `Gauss.P d`: a time family constant in the sample variable has a finite Gaussian moment at every fixed time, while its weighted drift or QV norm is `|u⁻¹|`, nonintegrable on `[0,1]`. These refute precisely the pointwise-moment-to-time-integrability inference. They do **not** assert that the eventual concrete evolved coordinate rates have this singularity.

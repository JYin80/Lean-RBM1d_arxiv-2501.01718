## Exact claims and dependency check

For `N=L W`, `3 <= L`, `W>0`, one Hermitian matrix on `ZMod L × Fin W`, and `y=(a0,beta)`, `sum_norm_blockM_le` establishes

\[
\sum_\alpha |M_{y,\alpha}|\le 2N.
\]

`blockM_eq` identifies the coefficient with `N sum_x |u_alpha(x)|^2 (Svar_xy-1/N)`. `Svar_eq_ofReal` identifies the *actual* real block profile `Sblk`; `Sblk_nonneg`, `sum_Sblk_col`, and `sum_sq_norm_eigenvectorBasis` give, in that order, the variation bound, column mass one, and sitewise eigenbasis completeness. Thus the proof's `N sum_x (Sblk_xy+1/N)=2N` has the right normalization and does not assume a scalar surrogate profile. The `L>=3` hypothesis excludes the three-neighbor collision; `NeZero W` excludes `N=0`.

With `kappa>0`, `|E|<=2-kappa`, `J=[-2+kappa/2,2-kappa/2]`, `|Re z-E|<=C0/N<=kappa/4`, and `Im z>0`, every `lambda_alpha` outside `J` satisfies `|lambda_alpha-z|>=kappa/4`. The source correctly derives

\[
\sum_{\lambda_\alpha\notin J}|\lambda_\alpha-z|^{-2}|M_{y,\alpha}|
\le 2N(\kappa/4)^{-2}.
\]

The same distance proof is valid on both sides of `J`, including endpoints of the closed interval. `blockM_bulk_restricted_max_le` correctly proves `|M_{y,alpha}|<=2D` only when `lambda_alpha in J` and all masses of that eigenvector obey `|u_alpha(x)|^2<=D/N`. It assumes `D>=0`; it does not assert an unrestricted maximum. These clauses match the deterministic repair to the manuscript's (2.10), (2.29)–(2.33), with the stated conditional bulk mass input.

## Missing sign bridge

The manuscript's (2.29) permits `G1,G2` to be either `G` or `G*`. T1397 expresses the corresponding alpha factor as `spectralGsigPole hH z sigma1 alpha`; at `sigma1=false`, its definition is `spectralPole hH (star z) alpha`. T1459's `spectral_pole_sq_le_outside_bulk` and `spectral_alpha_outside_bulk_tail_le` conclude only for `spectralPole hH z alpha` under `0<z.im`. Since `(star z).im=-z.im<0`, the tail theorem cannot be applied to `star z`. No theorem in the audited module identifies the two pole norms or quantifies over `sigma : Bool`.

The smallest repair is a compiled norm equality

\[
|\lambda_\alpha-\overline z|^{-1}=|\lambda_\alpha-z|^{-1}
\]

for real Hermitian eigenvalues, followed by a Bool-sign outside-bulk tail theorem whose conclusion uses `spectralGsigPole hH z sigma alpha`. Equivalently, prove the tail directly for a parameter with nonzero imaginary part and the same real-part separation, then instantiate both signs. Rebuild and rerun public axiom checks after that repair. The existing source and report should not be accepted as a complete both-sign T1459 deliverable.

This is a same-matrix support witness, not a probability or OU-event statement.

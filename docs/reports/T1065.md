# T1065 — positive Gaussian mass of the target-mesh strict transition

## Verdict: mathematical preflight PASS; theorem proved

The exact T1051 scalar-ray witness is compatible with the support of the
finite-coordinate Gaussian law. For every fixed `τ,c,δ` with
`τ,c,δ > 0` and `δ ≤ min(1,c/100)`, the new theorem proves eventually
`
0 < (Gauss.P Dims.exampleGrow)
  (APrimeCrossJointSplit.transition Dims.exampleGrow 0 60
    (deltaWeight δ) (fun _ => 0) (targetMesh 60) N 2
    (canonicalM Dims.exampleGrow (fun _ => 0) (firstCellT τ)
      (targetMesh 60) N)).
`
All inputs retain T1051's target mesh, T995 loss, canonical smoothing order,
first-cell window, and active `k=2`.

## Mathematical preflight

The endpoint argument audited in T1051 puts a sample
`ω₀ = scalarSample Dims.exampleGrow x` in the **literal** strict transition,
with `1 < prefixSample/threshold < 2`, at the same `N` and `k=2`. Its scale
is `targetMesh 60 N = N^258` eventually, its threshold uses
`deltaWeight δ = δ/100`, and the first-cell endpoint is eventually `1/2`.
The transition statement contains no common-event requirement.

The finite-coordinate and continuity checks are as follows.

1. `prefixSample` is `prefixMatrix` evaluated at `Xmat d N ω`. By
   `Gauss.Xmat_eq_sum`, `Xmat d N` reads only
   `K = (usedCoord d N).image (crd d N)`, a finite set of coordinates at this
   size. `prefixSample_finDep` proves the resulting exact congruence: agreement
   on `K` gives equal prefix samples.
2. For an active prefix whose stored times are below `1`,
   `APrimeSmoothWeightActual.contDiff_prefixMatrix` makes the matrix observable
   continuously differentiable. Composing with `Gauss.continuous_Xmat` makes
   `prefixSample/threshold` continuous on `Ω d`; the literal transition,
   defined by two strict inequalities, is open.
3. The open neighborhood of `ω₀` first gives a finite product neighborhood.
   Finite dependence then discards restrictions outside `K`: any sample in the
   resulting cylinder on `K` has the same transition membership as a sample
   patched to `ω₀` outside `K`. The effective-coordinate set is open and
   contains the restriction of `ω₀`.
4. A zero-variance coordinate cannot be diagonal: every diagonal coordinate
   has variance `1/(3W_N) > 0`. At a non-diagonal coordinate,
   `scalarSample` is zero. Thus `ω₀` vanishes on all zero-variance
   coordinates, including those in `K`. `supportCylinder` fixes those
   coordinates at zero and restricts only the effective coordinates to the
   nonempty open set. `FiniteSupportCore.support_cylinder_pos` gives this
   cylinder strictly positive `Gauss.P` mass, and its containment in the
   literal transition proves the claim.

The parameters are nondegenerate: T1051's explicit witness
`τ=1`, `c=1`, `δ=1/200` satisfies all assumptions and retains a positive
length window and active prefix.

## Paper scope

The inspected formulas in `paper/250520-YinJun-v2.pdf` are used only to keep
the attribution precise. Formula (5.21) is the stopped loop-hierarchy
identity, (5.24) is the stopped martingale moment estimate, and (5.43)
defines the stopping time by the first threshold crossing of `J*`. T1065
proves none of these statements. It is an internal positive-measure result
for T1051's smooth-prefix transition; it does not assert positive mass of an
intersection with `commonEvent`, prove N1/A′, or close the paper's stopping
time argument.

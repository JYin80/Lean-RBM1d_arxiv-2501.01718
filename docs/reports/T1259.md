## Statement and quantifiers

Assume `|E| < 2`, `60 ≤ D`, `0 ≤ s_N ≤ t_N < 1`, `c > 0`,
`Cond272Reg`, `BoundsCore`, and `0 < δ ≤ min 1 (c/100)`. Eventually in
`N`, whenever the first cell is active (`1 ≤ cutNetTop ... N`), for every
`a : LoopArg ... 2`, the literal integral of
`positiveTimeCrossBudget E D (deltaWeight δ) s t N 1 1 a` from `s_N` to
the moving endpoint `v = cutNetPt s (targetMesh D) N 1` satisfies

```text
∫[s_N,v] positiveTimeCrossBudget
  ≤ (15/2) K N^(13δ/640 − 2D − 8) ratR(E,s_N,N,v)^(-2)
    + (15/2) N^(-2D − 10),
```

where `K = crossProfilePointwiseConst`. The endpoint is not replaced by a
fixed time, and the estimate is uniform over all outputs `a` beyond the
same eventual cutoff.

## Mathematical preflight

The accepted inputs are T1207's integrated full-to-norm-good reduction,
T1247's pointwise norm-good bound, and T1245's pointwise cross-profile
bound. For every positive `u` in the cell, T1247 gives

```text
normGoodCrossBudget(u)
  ≤ (15/4) crossProfile(u) + (15/8) N^(-1) u^(-1/2),
```

and T1245 bounds the profile by
`K N^(13δ/640) etaT(E,s_N)^(-1) ratR(E,s_N,N,v)^(-2) / sqrt(u)`.
The exact inverse-square-root integral
`∫[s_N,v] u^(-1/2) du = 2(√v−√s_N)` therefore gives the norm-good
integral coefficient `(15/2) K` and its remainder `(15/4) N^(-1)` times
the endpoint gap. T1207 contributes a further `(15/4) N^(-1)` times that
same gap. Their sum is exactly `(15/2) N^(-1)` times the gap.

For the first cell, the mesh is eventually `N^(4D+18)` and its width is
`N^(-(4D+18))`. Thus
`√v−√s_N ≤ √(v−s_N) = N^(-(2D+9))`. The scale hypothesis and monotonicity
of `etaT` give `etaT(E,s_N)^(-1) ≤ N`. Consequently,

```text
etaT(E,s_N)^(-1) (√v−√s_N) ≤ N^(-2D−8),
N^(-1) (√v−√s_N)             ≤ N^(-2D−10).
```

Multiplying by the nonnegative profile factors yields the stated powers.
The proof keeps `ratR(v)^(-2)` in the final result. It uses `u=0` handling
for the norm-good integrand and the inverse-square-root envelope's interval
integrability on `[s_N,v]`, including when `s_N=0`; no positivity of the
left endpoint is assumed.

The imported T995 nondegenerate witness is re-exported as
`nondegenerate_positive_cell_witness`. It supplies a concrete schedule
instance with an eventually active first positive cell and a same-event
sample with positive actual smooth weight. As recorded by T1247, it does
not assert transition membership, a resident in the transition event, or
positive cross-budget mass; it witnesses schedule nonvacuity only.

## Cardinality proof

The construction takes a maximal `1/4`-separated subset of the unit sphere.
Compactness supplies finiteness and maximality supplies the `1/4` cover.

For the exact cardinal bound, put disjoint open balls of radius `1/8` around
the separated points.  Every center has norm one, so their union lies in the
ball of radius `9/8`.  Translation invariance and the finite-dimensional Haar
ball formula give

```text
|V| * (1/8)^d ≤ (9/8)^d,
```

and hence `|V| ≤ 9^d`.  The real dimension identity
`finrank ℝ (EuclideanSpace ℂ (Fin W)) = 2 * W` gives the requested exponent.

## Operator-norm proof

For unit vectors `x,y`, choose net points `v,w` within `1/4`.  Expanding

```text
⟪x, M y⟫ = ⟪v, M w⟫ + ⟪x-v, M y⟫ + ⟪v, M (y-w)⟫
```

bounds the two error terms by `‖M‖/4` each.  Taking
`x = normalize (M y)` and then the operator norm yields

```text
‖M‖ ≤ blockBilinearMax M V + ‖M‖/2,
```

which rearranges to the factor-two estimate.

## Endpoint checks

- `W = 1`: `exists_quarterSphereNet_one` gives a nonempty net and the exact
  bound `V.card ≤ 9^2`.
- `M = 0`: `blockBilinearMax_zero` proves that the finite maximum is exactly
  zero; the operator inequality therefore reduces to `0 ≤ 0`.
- No probability event, Gaussian estimate, or `jG` declaration is used.

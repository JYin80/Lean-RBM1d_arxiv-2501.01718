# T570 — independent preflight for the next general moving-window producer

## Verdict

The general moving-window `hfamily` is **not** ready to dispatch.  The accepted
first-cell chain T537–T569 cannot be renamed or transported to it.  The earliest
missing positive producer is an upstream event theorem that the proof of T495
already contains internally but does not export:

```text
HighProb (P d)
  (Gauss.goodSetFlow d E s t (Gauss.flowDelta d E t)).
```

This result is ready as a small, noncircular Lean ticket.  It should be proved
from the current-window `BoundsCore`, `Cond272Reg`, and `Step1.Hyp` by exporting
the `Step1.weakLaw_highProb` event used inside
`APrimeGeneralMovingSingletonLocalLaw.llMax_sq_stochDom`, then applying
`Gauss.goodEv_subset_goodSet_flow`.  A closed wrapper may derive `Step1.Hyp`
from the same `BoundsCore` and `Cond272Reg` by
`Gauss.step1Hyp_gauss_of_scale''`.

This is the first missing actual producer because the next block-resolved
Green estimate, `APrimeJG.highProb_jG_le_of_entryBoundFlow`, already takes
exactly this `HighProb goodSetFlow` as its only open event input.  The floored
entry estimate is an existing theorem.  No coordinate moment, family moment,
`hfamily`, or `APrimeSlot'` follows from the event theorem alone.

## Paper check

The paper's statements are genuinely moving-window statements.

* (2.76) is uniform in `s <= u <= t` and keeps the time-ratio factor
  `(eta_s/eta_u)^4`, the spatial tail at time `u`, and the `W^(-D)` remainder.
* (5.43) defines a stopping time by the first crossing of
  `J^*_{u,D} >= (eta_s/eta_t)^4` and then sets `tau = T ∧ t`.
* (5.44) bounds the stopped quadratic variation by a near contribution with
  `(eta_s/eta_t)^4` plus one.
* (5.45) first proves the martingale estimate at an arbitrary deterministic
  `t'` in the interval, with `(eta_s/eta_t')^2`, and compares it to the terminal
  scale.
* (5.46) extends the bound to an `N^(-C)` time net and takes the maximum over
  its index `k`.
* (5.47) inserts the preceding bounds and the initial estimates into the loop
  equation to obtain `J^*_{tau,D} \prec (eta_s/eta_t)^2`, which makes
  `P(T <= t)` negligible.

Thus the paper does not authorize replacing arbitrary `s,t,E,D` by the first
grid cell, bounding every time ratio by two, or keeping only one positive net
index.

## Exact reason the proposed producer is ready

T495's `llMax_sq_stochDom`
(`APrimeGeneralMovingSingletonLocalLaw.lean:44–112`) has the all-time subtype
in the stochastic index, not a deterministic selector.  In its proof,
lines 90–111 construct

```text
hweak : HighProb (P d)
  (fun N => {omega | forall u : TimeIcc s t N,
    llMax ... u omega < A_u^(-1/4)})
```

and then, using `eventually_scale_facts`, strengthen it to one event on which
every `u` belongs to `Step1.goodEv`, whose threshold is `A_u^(-1/6)`.
`Gauss.goodEv_subset_goodSet_flow`
(`Lemma41FlowGauss.lean:164–174`) compares this threshold with the single
terminal threshold

```text
flowDelta_N = A_(t_N)^(-1/6)
```

by antitonicity of the flow scale.  Therefore the same event is a subset of
`goodSetFlow d E s t (flowDelta d E t) N` eventually.  No time net, selector
exchange, new bandwidth power, or use of Step 2 is needed.

This route is noncircular.  `Gauss.step1Hyp_gauss_of_scale''`


## Quantifiers, exponents, event, and boundary audit

### Quantifiers

The proposed event theorem fixes `E,c,s,t`, their domain hypotheses,
`Cond272Reg`, `BoundsCore`, and optionally `Step1.Hyp` before `HighProb`.  Its
event quantifies over every `u : TimeIcc s t N` inside one literal set.  It has
no `delta`, moment order, high order, net index, or output coordinate; adding
such parameters would only weaken and obscure the producer.

For the eventual general coordinate theorem, the required order remains

```text
fixed E,D,c,s,t and current-window inputs;
forall delta, 0 < delta -> delta <= delta0;
forall p;
let P := highOrder delta p;
eventually N;
forall active k;
forall output a.
```

The family root is taken only after the coordinate bound and only once.  No
theorem currently establishes the general coordinate line at that order.

### Powers and moving-time factors

The accepted general sources retain the necessary moving scales:

* support gives `jSnorm_u <= C N^(2 delta)`;
* since `jSnorm_u = jS_u / R_u^4`, it gives only
  `jS_u <= C N^(2 delta) R_u^4`;
* the existing `jG` event at loss `tau` would therefore give a support-local
  scale of order `1 + N^tau(C N^(2 delta) R_u^4 + 2)`, i.e. order
  `N^(tau+2 delta) R_u^4`, not a first-cell constant;
* the initial term is `N^(5 delta/32) R_v^(-2)`;
* T491's source scales keep all powers of `r_u = ell_u/ell_s` and
  `A_u = W ell_u eta_u`;
* T523 keeps `2 qExt`, where on the window
  `q_u = (ell_u/ell_s) A_u^(-1)`, and adds no hidden `W` or time factor;
* T495 proves `q_u <= N^(-59c/60)` and hence
  `sqrt(q_u+W^(-1)) <= N^(-a)` only for fixed
  `0 < a < 59c/120`.

These are compatible as upstream estimates, but there is no compiled general
ledger showing that the future drift, cross, and QV integrals fit below
`N^(delta/5) R_v^(-2)`.  In particular, the first-cell use of `R_v <= 2`
cannot be repeated.

### Common literal event

T491 supplies a named measurable `sourceGood`; T523 supplies stochastic
domination and therefore events after a loss is selected; T484's pathwise
support theorem uses the norm event and positivity of `widenedW`; and
`APrimeJG.highProb_jG_le_of_entryBoundFlow` needs `goodSetFlow`.  These are
compatible under the same `E,c,s,t,BoundsCore,Step1.Hyp` tuple and HighProb
events can be intersected.  They are not yet packaged as one named literal
event.

More seriously, T484's positive-support witness only identifies the zero
sample in the norm event.  It does not prove that this sample lies in a future
intersection with `sourceGood`, centered-trace events, and the `jG` event.
High probability of the intersection proves the event is nonempty, but does
not by itself prove that `widenedW > 0` on one of its members.  A future
support-local common-event ticket must either prove a plateau implication on
the whole common event or exhibit one member carrying both the event and
positive support.  Without that, a support-conditional theorem can be empty.

The proposed `goodSetFlow` ticket avoids this defect: it has no support
premise, and its positive-window event is eventually nonempty by
`HighProb.nonempty`.

### `k=0`, first cell, and endpoints

The new event includes `u=s_N`, so it covers the time associated with the
empty prefix.  T488 and T491 also include that boundary, and T489 gives the
exact `k=0` endpoint identity.  T484 correctly excludes `k=0`; any future
coordinate and family producer must keep a separate zero-prefix branch.

## Why T537–T569 cannot be renamed as a general theorem

The mismatch is definitional and mathematical:

1. Their model parameters are fixed to `E=0`, `D=60`, `s_N=0`, and
   `t_N=firstCellT tauPrime N`; the general consumer fixes arbitrary admissible
   `E,D,s,t`.
2. Their mesh is the literal `APrimeSmoothTransition.transitionMesh`; T483 and
   the general consumer use `APrimeGeneralMovingMesh.targetMesh D` (or an
   explicitly supplied general mesh).
3. Their weights and endpoints unfold through first-cell namespaces and a
   first-cell `sharpCommonEvent`.  They are not definitionally the general
   `widenedW (canonicalR s t mesh) ...` data.
4. The numerical closure repeatedly uses `0 <= v <= 1/2` and
   `1 <= R_v <= 2`.  On a general moving window, `R_v` remains a moving
   polynomial factor and may not be replaced by two.
5. The closed first-cell package has
   `exists tauPrime > 0, forall delta, forall p, eventually N, ...`; this does
   not instantiate an already fixed arbitrary pair `s,t`.
6. The positive resident is the particular first-cell index `k=2`.  It neither
   supplies every general active index nor a positive-support member of a
   general common event.
7. T568 stops at a target-family norm and T569 at an endpoint norm.  Neither
   has the general cutoff integral with the literal general mesh, and neither
   has the universal `delta -> p -> eventually N -> all k` `hfamily` type.

Renaming any of these statements would change their hypotheses and proof
content.  It would also hide the missing moving-ratio ledger.

# T587 — first honest integration/net step after the moving QV profile

## Scope and verdict

This is a read-only audit of the paper route (5.43)--(5.47) and the repository's
moment route after T584.  No Lean source, scheduling document, import, commit, or
push was changed.  T584 landed while this audit was in progress; the conclusions
below use its actual compiled declaration
`APrimeGeneralMovingQVProfile.eventually_qv_profile_on_common_support`, not only
the earlier T580 proposal.

**Verdict.**  The first honest next theorem is not a stopped-martingale or
terminal-time-net statement.  It is a net-uniform, widened-weight integral budget
for the actual uncut QV rate, with the literal T584 profile on the common event and
a separately paid common-event complement.  The theorem is stated below as the
single recommended dispatch.

That theorem is only the QV component of the moment route.  It does not produce
the missing coordinate moment family `hfamily`.  In particular, the current
smooth-weight Duhamel route has a genuine support-interface gap: T584 is localized
by positive `APrimeWeight.widenedW`, whereas differentiation uses
`APrimeSmoothWeightActual.weight`.  The compiled comparison is

```text
widenedW <= APrimeSmoothWeightActual.weight,
```

not the reverse.  Thus positive smooth weight does not imply positive widened
weight, and T584 cannot by itself bound the QV term on the whole support of the
smooth differentiable weight.  This is the immediate blocker after the proposed
QV integral theorem if the existing smooth-weight Duhamel implementation is used.

## Paper route: what (5.43)--(5.47) actually needs

After the propagated QV estimate (5.42), the paper defines the stopping time

```text
T = min { u : J*_(u,D) >= (eta_s/eta_t)^4 },
tau = T wedge t.                                               (5.43)
```

It integrates the stopped QV in (5.44), applies BDG at each deterministic terminal
time in (5.45), and then takes the maximum over

```text
t(k) = s + (t-s) k N^(-C)                                    (5.46)
```

for a sufficiently fine terminal-time net.  Choosing the first net point after
`tau`, and using near-identity propagation and the tail comparison, yields the
strict stopped bootstrap improvement (5.47).  Therefore a faithful Lean version
would need all of the following, on the actual stochastic integral: a filtration
and adapted process, stopping-time measurability, the stopped QV identity, a BDG
moment/tail estimate at each deterministic terminal time, a polynomial-cardinality
union bound, and a time-modulus estimate that transfers the net point back to
`tau`.

Those producers are not present.  `Hierarchy/Step2.lean`'s `Step2.Hyp.mart`
assumes the stopped martingale domination as a field; it does not construct it.
The object `Hierarchy/Step2FarMart.lean`'s `Step2FarMart.farMart` is the
algebraic Duhamel defect

```text
(L-K)_v - initial - drift,
```

not a stochastic integral and not a martingale.  Bounding it from the desired far
estimate would be circular for a proof of (5.45)/(5.47).  Its collapsed-endpoint
identity is useful only as an example of zero after integration.

Consequently the paper stopping route is not the next dispatchable route in this
repository.  Adding an abstract `mart` assumption would merely restate the missing
theorem, and adding a new stochastic-analysis axiom is forbidden by the project
rules.

## Three different nets must not be identified

1. **T523 source net.**  `APrimeGeneralMovingNetNumerics.meshSpacing N` is exactly
   `N^(-16)`.  It transfers the centered one-loop source law from fixed times to
   all `TimeIcc` times, with Holder exponent `1/2`, modulus exponent `6`, and the
   block-cardinality payment.  By T579 this result is already inside the common
   source event.  It is not a net for the output martingale in (5.46).
2. **Paper martingale net.**  Formula (5.46) uses spacing `(t-s)N^(-C)` for stopped
   terminal-time martingale estimates.  Its exponent is chosen only to be fine
   enough for that continuity/union-bound argument.  T523 does not discharge it.
3. **A-prime moment/continuity net.**  The current general-moving target has density
   `APrimeGeneralMovingMesh.targetMesh D N`, eventually
   `N^(4D+18)`, hence spacing `N^(-(4D+18))`.  It indexes prefix weights and the
   later `WeightedMoment`/continuous-induction consumer.  At `D=60` its exponent
   is `258`, not `16`.

No union bound over the paper's martingale net belongs in the proposed QV integral
theorem.  Uniformity in the existing cut-net index comes directly from the common
event and T584's eventual statement.

## Moment route: producers and consumers

The repository's route (A-prime) replaces optional stopping by weighted Gaussian
moments.

- `APrimeQVRateTime.measurable_qvAt_on_window` proves joint Borel measurability of
  the actual normalized QV rate.
- `APrimeQVRateTime.intervalIntegrable_rateNormW_qvAt` proves time integrability of
  its weighted `L^p` rate for every fixed measurable weight in `[0,1]`.
- `APrimeQVGlobalPoly.eventually_qvAt_le_poly`, with `Keta=1`, gives the all-sample
  envelope

  ```text
  2^21 * N^(2D+16).
  ```

  Together with T579's `highProb_commonEvent`, this pays the complement of the
  literal common event by an arbitrary fixed `N^(-beta)` after `p` and `beta>0`
  have been fixed.
- `MomentDuhamel.weightedMinkowski_of_deriv_le` consumes an already established
  derivative bound, drift/cross integrability, and QV-rate integrability.  It does
  not produce any of those bounds.
- `Hierarchy/Step2Moment.lean`'s `Step2Moment.stochDom_timeIcc_of_holder` and
  the net theorems in `Hierarchy/Step2MomentStep.lean` consume uniform fixed-time
  moments plus a deterministic modulus.  Their `hev`/`hmom` inputs are not
  consequences of a pointwise QV bound.
- `Gauss/LoopLipschitz.lean` supplies the deterministic time modulus used by the
  repository's continuous-induction net (with separate event-restricted variants).
  It does not produce a martingale or the fixed-time moment input.  Its stronger
  Lipschitz route also requires a positive left endpoint; the event-based Holder
  route is the one that covers windows beginning at zero.
- `APrimeAssembly.weightedMoment_of_widened_family` turns the completed endpoint
  family `hfamily` into `WeightedMoment`; `aprimeSlot_of_widened_family` then builds
  `APrimeSlot'`.  Hence either `hfamily` or `APrimeSlot'` is downstream of the
  missing moment calculation and cannot be assumed in proving it.

Thus QV measurability and finite-size integrability are already available.  The
actual missing producer is a general-moving, support-compatible weighted
generator/Minkowski estimate which combines the QV integral with drift, full cross
term, initial datum, and one-step arithmetic to yield

```text
forall delta>0, delta<=delta0, forall p,
  exists C>0, eventually forall active k,
    integral widenedW * |cutTrunc ... J(v_k)|^(2p)
      <= C * N^((delta/2)*p).
```

For `p=0` this endpoint moment statement is trivial and should be discharged
separately.  Every QV-root or Minkowski argument requires `p>=1`.

## Exact boundary and measurability audit

T584 correctly requires `1 <= k`.  At `k=0`,

```text
v_0 = cutNetPt s mesh N 0 = s_N,
```

but `qvAt ... s_N s_N s_N omega` is an instantaneous QV rate and need not be
zero.  The exact zero appears only after integration:

```text
integral from s_N to v_0 of g(u) du = 0.
```

This follows from `cutNetPt_zero` and `intervalIntegral.integral_same`; it does
not use T584 and must not be advertised as a pointwise QV theorem.  The first-cell
files `APrimeFirstCellCrossQVCombinedBudget.zero_prefix_integrals` and
`APrimeFirstCellDeltaMinkowskiConsumer.minkowskiBoundAt_k_zero` already implement
this pattern.

For positive cells, the common event is measurable by T579.  The widened weight is
measurable by unfolding `widenedW` and using `measurable_prefixSoftW`; it lies in
`[0,1]`.  Joint QV measurability and a fixed-size uniform QV bound then give both
sample integrability of

```text
w(omega) * |qvAt(u,omega)|^p
```

and time interval integrability of

```text
g(u) = rateNormW (P d) w p (qvAt(u)).
```

Using only the event-cut rate `qvRateEvolvedOn` would not suffice for the generator,
whose QV term is uncut.  The honest route is the favorable-event weighted estimate
plus the global-polynomial payment on the event complement.

## Recommended dispatch: exact widened-weight QV integral budget

Add a new module such as
`RBM1D/Gauss/APrimeGeneralMovingQVNormBudget.lean`, importing T584,
`APrimeQVRateTime`, `APrimeQVGlobalPoly`, and the neutral weighted event-split
helper.  Define, for the target mesh,

```text
v(N,k) = cutNetPt s (targetMesh D) N k,

w(delta,p,N,k,omega) =
  widenedW (canonicalR s t (targetMesh D)) 1
    (APrimeGeneralMovingDetFields.J E D s)
    s t (targetMesh D) delta p N k omega,

Qexact(N,k,a,u) =
  (driftScale(d,E,D,N,a,s_N,v(N,k))^(-1) *
    rootProfile(B,E,N,u,v(N,k),D,
      sourceEll(s,zetaSrc,N),
      generalMovingBlockCap(E,s,tauG,delta,N,u),
      sourceC4(E,s,zetaSrc,N,u),
      W_N^(-1),a))^2,

g(delta,p,N,k,a,u) =
  rateNormW (P d) w(delta,p,N,k) p
    (qvAt(d,E,D,N,sigPM,a,s_N,v(N,k),u)).
```

The main theorem should have this quantifier order and conclusion:

```lean
theorem eventually_widened_qv_integral_le_exact_profile
    {E D c : Real} {s t : Nat -> Real}
    (hE : |E| < 2) (hD : 60 <= D)
    (hs0 : forall N, 0 <= s N)
    (hst : forall N, s N <= t N)
    (ht1 : forall N, t N < 1)
    (hc : 0 < c)
    (hreg : Cond272Reg B E s t c)
    (hB : BoundsCore (Gauss.sample d) E s)
    {zetaSrc zetaCtr tauG delta : Real}
    (hzetaSrc : 0 < zetaSrc)
    (hzetaCtr : 0 < zetaCtr)
    (htauG : 0 < tauG)
    (hdelta : 0 < delta)
    (hroom : tauG + 2 * delta + 2 / 15 < 1)
    (p : Nat) (hp : 1 <= p)
    (beta : Real) (hbeta : 0 < beta) :
    ∀ᶠ N : Nat in atTop, forall k : Nat,
      k <= cutNetTop s t (targetMesh D) N ->
      forall a : LoopArg (d.L N) 2,
        IntervalIntegrable (g delta p N k a) volume (s N) (v N k) /\
        IntervalIntegrable (Qexact delta N k a) volume (s N) (v N k) /\
        integral u in (s N)..(v N k), g delta p N k a u <=
          integral u in (s N)..(v N k), Qexact delta N k a u +
            (v N k - s N) * (N : Real)^(-beta)
```

Names of local abbreviations may be adjusted, but the order must not change:
geometry first, independent event losses next, then `delta,p,beta`, then eventual
`N`, and only then `k,a`.  The event itself must remain exactly T579's
`commonEvent E D s t zetaSrc zetaCtr tauG N`, independent of
`delta,p,beta,k,a`.

For `k>0`, split on `w omega=0`; the zero branch is algebraic, while the positive
branch invokes T584 on the same `omega`.  Apply
`weighted_norm_le_of_weighted_event` with exponent `p`.  The all-sample envelope
is exactly `2^21*N^(2D+16)` before its fixed constant/power is absorbed into the
high-probability complement payment.  This gives

```text
g(u) <= Qexact(u) + N^(-beta).
```

The exact profile is a deterministic continuous (hence interval-integrable)
function on the closed window: all denominators are positive there, and the near
indicator depends only on `v,a`, not on `u`.  Integrating gives the displayed
length factor.  Since `0 <= v-s <= 1`, a later corollary may replace the final term
by `N^(-beta)`, but the first theorem should retain the exact interval length.

For `k=0`, prove the complete conclusion by `v=s` and equal-endpoint integration;
do not invoke T584 and do not assert pointwise vanishing.  This one theorem therefore
has the exact all-`k` shape needed by later net-uniform consumers.

## Powers retained by the dispatch

The theorem must keep T584's literal profile, rather than silently replacing it by
paper notation.  With

```text
A_u = W*ell_u*eta_u,  r_u = ell_u/ell_s,
R_u = eta_s/eta_u,    R_v = eta_s/eta_v,
Jbar = 1 + N^tauG *
  (9*exp(sqrt 3)*(4*exp 1+2)*N^(2*delta)*R_u^4 + 2),
```

the pre-square-root rows are

```text
Near = 4*N^zetaSrc*eta_u^(-1)*cNear2*r_u^5 + 2*W^(-1),
Q2   = 16*eta_u^(-1)*cFar2*Jbar^2*N^(zetaSrc/2)
         *r_u^(3/2)*A_u^(-1/2),
Q3   = 1152*eta_u^(-1)*Jbar^3*A_u^(-1),
Qsp  = 32*W*L*W^(-D)*Jbar^3.
```

The endpoint kernel retains `256*exp(3)*W^(-D)`, and normalization retains the
exact root factor `R_u^(-2) R_v^(-2)`.  The QV bound is the square of the whole
root sum; it is not a termwise sum of squares.  The mesh density is eventually
`N^(4D+18)`.  T585's possible deterministic absorption may later bound these rows,
but it is not a premise of this first exact integral theorem.

## Acyclicity and nondegeneracy

The dispatch depends only on T579/T584, fixed-size QV regularity/global bounds,
the event-split inequality, and deterministic integration.  It must not import or
assume `hfamily`, `WeightedMoment`, `APrimeHyp`, `APrimeSlot'`, continuous induction,
or a final A-prime conclusion.

T584's `positive_first_cell_profile_hypotheses_witness` supplies a genuine same-event
positive cell at `E=0`, `D=60`, `k=1`, `p=1`, with

```text
zetaSrc = zetaCtr = tauG = delta = 1/100,
```

and positive widened support.  Taking any `beta>0` therefore leaves a nondegenerate
instance of the proposed theorem.  The `k=0` branch is a boundary identity, not the
only resident of the statement.

## What remains after this dispatch

The proposed theorem supplies exactly the widened-weight QV integral.  It does not
by itself feed `weightedMinkowski_of_deriv_le`, because the current differentiable
weight is the larger smooth weight.  The next genuine design decision is therefore
one of:

1. strengthen the common-support/profile theorem to the positive support of the
   actual smooth weight and build the general-moving smooth-weight drift/cross/QV
   generator package; or
2. construct a differentiable localization whose support is contained in the
   T584 widened support and which still dominates the endpoint widened moment.

No such support bridge or complete general-moving derivative/moment producer was
found in the inspected interfaces.  Until it is supplied, neither `hfamily` nor
`APrimeSlot'` is an honest premise for closing the continuous induction.

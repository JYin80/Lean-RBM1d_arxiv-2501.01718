# T724 — real and imaginary parts of the uniform permutation Fourier mean

## Statements and argument

For every `W ≥ 2`, arbitrary `x χ : Fin W → ℂ`, and `∑ i, χ i = 0`, the module proves

```text
(∑ π : Equiv.Perm (Fin W), (permutationFourierSum W x χ π).re)
  / (Fintype.card (Equiv.Perm (Fin W)) : ℝ) = 0
(∑ π : Equiv.Perm (Fin W), (permutationFourierSum W x χ π).im)
  / (Fintype.card (Equiv.Perm (Fin W)) : ℝ) = 0.
```

The summand is the same T684 quantity, with its `W⁻¹` normalization inside `permutationFourierSum`; the denominator outside is the full cardinality of `Equiv.Perm (Fin W)`, cast to `ℝ`. Each proof starts from T684's complex mean-zero identity, uses nonvanishing of the permutation cardinality to recover the zero complex counting sum, and applies `Complex.re_sum` or `Complex.im_sum` before forming the real scalar mean. No bound on `x` is assumed.

The `W = 2` witness theorem reuses T684's explicit example `x=(2,-2)`, `χ=(1,-1)`. Both are nonconstant, `∑χ=0`, and the normalized Fourier value at the identity is `2 ≠ 0`; the real and imaginary uniform counting means both vanish. Thus the component conclusions describe cancellation across permutations, not pointwise vanishing. As in T684, `W=0,1` are outside the theorem's domain.

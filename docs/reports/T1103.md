`RBM1D/Gauss/APrimeGeneralMovingHighQK1Good.lean` proves the good-event
contribution to T999's literal selected-order expectation at `k=1`. Under the
same general-moving `BoundsCore` and `Cond272Reg` inputs as T1097, it retains
the requested quantifiers and final rate:

```text
for each δ₀, each 0 < δ ≤ δ₀, and each p ≥ 1,
  choose q ≥ p and C > 0;
  eventually in N, if 1 ≤ cutNetTop ... N, then
    ∫_{good N} widenedW(order q, k=1) ·
      |cutTrunc(N^(2δ), jSnorm(cutNetPt ... 1))|^(2q) dP

```

The proof takes the `q=p` initial high-moment estimate from
`APrimeGeneralMovingHighQK0Repair.eventually_selected_high_k0`, so no new
moment assumption or fixed-parameter substitution is used. The constant and
order are chosen before the eventual `N`. The active-cell condition is retained
explicitly in the consequent.

## Preflight and endpoint comparison

The `k=1` point belongs to `[s_N,t_N]` whenever it is active, by
`netFinset_subset_Icc`; hence both it and `s_N` are below one. On the literal
good event, `APrimeGeneralMovingLoopModulus.eventually_jSnorm_modulus` bounds
their `jSnorm` difference by
`N^(2D+7) |cutNetPt(s,mesh,N,1)-s_N|^(1/2)`. The target mesh is eventually
`N^(4D+18)`, and the point displacement is `1/mesh_N`, so the product is
`N^-2`. The cutoff map is globally Lipschitz with constant `19/4`: its profile
`x ↦ cutChi(x)x` has derivative bounded by `19/4`, and scaling by the positive
cutoff level preserves that Lipschitz constant. Thus the two truncated values
differ by at most `(19/4)N^-2` on `good N`.

The order-`q` widened weight is nonnegative and at most one. Pointwise on the
good event, the endpoint cutoff is therefore bounded after taking its `2q`
power by
`2^(2q) (initialCutoff^(2q) + ((19/4)N^-2)^(2q))`. The first term integrates
using the exact T1097 initial-law bound; the second is at most the fixed
constant `(19/4)^(2q)`. Since `δq/2>0`, eventually `1≤N^(δq/2)`, so this error
is absorbed into the **same** requested final rate and does not weaken it.
Both truncated powers are integrable on the finite Gaussian probability space
by their deterministic cutoff envelope. No integrability premise is added.

### Modulus exponent correction

The ticket's proposed intermediate `N^-7` modulus difference is not the
uniform general-moving consequence of the available sources. T468's
`APrimeSlotFields.eventually_modulus_jSnorm_event` has coefficient
`N^(2+2D)`, but requires one fixed terminal `t₀<1` with `t_N≤t₀`; that
hypothesis is absent from the arbitrary `Cond272Reg` moving-window theorem.
The applicable general-moving producer T475 instead has coefficient
`N^(2D+7)`. With the specified mesh `N^(4D+18)`, this gives `N^-2`:
`(2D+7) - (4D+18)/2 = -2`. The requested `-7` would result from combining
the mesh with T468's fixed-terminal coefficient, which is not valid for the
general-moving statement. The proof uses the actual general-moving producer;
the `N^-2` error still yields the exact original `N^(δq/2)` endpoint bound.

## Nondegeneracy and paper scope

`positive_length_k1_good_witness` projects T995's
`scheduled_positive_cell_witness`. On one concrete `Dims.exampleGrow`
Gaussian model it supplies `E=0`, `D=60`, the same positive `c`, moving
`s,t`, `Cond272Reg`, `BoundsCore`, and an eventually active first positive
cell with a resident in the literal norm-good event. This verifies joint
satisfiability of the assumptions and good-event regime. The witness does not
specialize or replace the universal conditional theorem.

I checked only `paper/250520-YinJun-v2.pdf`. Formula (2.69) is the initial
bound represented by `BoundsCore`; (5.21) and (5.44) concern the integrated
loop hierarchy and stopped quadratic variation, respectively, and do not
directly assert this static widened-prefix moment. This result proves only the
good-event contribution at `k=1`; it does not prove the complementary event,
the complete T999 expectation, an all-`k` family, `hfamily`, or A′.

# T1319 — corrected-loss literal N1 drift-slot fit

## Result and scope

The new standalone module proves the conditional all-cell N1 drift-slot arrow
for a fixed order `p` and the literal actual smooth weight at loss `lambda`.
Assume that, eventually in `N`, uniformly over all active cells `k ≥ 1` and
all outputs `a`, the integrated **actual drift plus full positive-time cross
budget** is at most

```text
C N^(4 * (lambda / 1000)) ratR(E,s,N,v_k)^(-2).
```

Then, eventually and uniformly over every `k ≤ cutNetTop` and every `a`, the
two integrands are interval-integrable on `[s N, v_k]`, and their literal
closed-cell Minkowski sum satisfies the exact drift input of
`APrimeInit.coordinate_integral_of_small_slots`. The implication keeps
`lambda`, `p`, and `C` fixed before the eventual cutoff. It proves no producer
for the displayed integral premise.

The integrability conclusions are proved from the existing literal actual
drift and positive-time cross-budget integrability producers. No integrability
assumption is hidden in the conditional integral premise.

The Minkowski consumer uses a closed-cell cross budget which is zero at time
zero. The public identity
`closedCellCrossBudget_eq_positiveTimeCrossBudget` proves that it agrees
pointwise with the full positive-time cross budget, including at `u = 0`.
For `k = 0`, `cutNetPt_zero` gives `v_0 = s N`; both integrals vanish, so the
conclusion covers the degenerate cell without imposing the quantitative
premise there.

For the active cells put `x = N^(lambda/8)` and `R = ratR(E,s,N,v_k)`. The
accepted `driftTerm_ge_near` lower bound, with
`slotXi' x = x^(1/4)` and `slotQ R = R^2/2`, gives

```text
driftTerm / R^4 ≥ x^(5/4) / (2 Im(mE E))
                 = N^(5 lambda / 32) / (2 Im(mE E)).
```

The assumed upper bound is `C N^(4 lambda/1000) R^(-2)`. Since `R ≥ 1`, its
last factor is at most one. The exponent gap
`5 lambda/32 - 4 lambda/1000 > 0` absorbs the fixed coefficient
`2 Im(mE E) C` eventually. The resulting lower bound is the precise
`APrimeInit.coordinate_integral_of_small_slots` drift slot, including its
`/ R^4` normalization.

The module also proves
`nondegenerate_positive_cell_witness`: at `E = 0`, `D = 60`, there is an
eventually positive-length window, an active first cell, a common-event sample,
and a positive literal actual weight for a positive admissible loss. This
witness establishes nonvacuity of the Gaussian geometry and weight hypotheses;
it does not establish the conditional integrated drift-plus-cross premise.

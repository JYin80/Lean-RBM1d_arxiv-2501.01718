# T369 — whole-window sharp common-event preflight

## Verdict

**No closed, noncircular whole-window actual Gaussian sharp event is presently justified.** There is a mathematically coherent *conditional* construction: make one high-probability source event independent of the smooth weight, and derive the sharp early and current rates only on its intersection with the cutoff transition. The general-window source and support-to-running-time arrows required for that construction have not been produced. In particular, one cannot turn T348/T366's same-sample **one-time** witness into a `∀r∈[s,v]` event or turn T345's fixed-time fluctuation averaging into one continuum event by swapping quantifiers.

The paper's (5.43)–(5.47) uses a stop and a time net. The A′ smooth-weight route is an alternative formalization. Its Good event must be formed from genuine pre-A′ Step 1/entry/modulus sources. It must not contain the desired `jSnorm≺1`, weighted moments, or an all-sample sharp `jG` cap. Sharp QV estimates are required only on `Good_N∩T_k`; T361's global product envelope separately pays `Good_Nᶜ`.

## Same-sample witness and what it does not prove

T348 `exists_positive_time_firstCell_witness` produces, eventually, one positive `h_N=N^-248`, one Gaussian sample in a **measurable high-probability common event** `norm∩raw4∩raw6∩goodSetFlow`, an active `k=2` weight equal to one for every `p`, and `SourceEvent` at `h_N`. Its source level is `ellSource=(2N^ζ)^(-1/5)<1`; the `2` is required by T334. T366's `eventually_jG_le_N_of_norm` has the implication `‖Xmat_Nω‖≤N ⇒ jG(h_N,ω)≤N` for that same concrete model. The T348 sample satisfies its antecedent, so these two theorems combine on **one sample** at `h_N`, without intersecting separately nonempty events. T363 gives the sharper calculation `jG(h_N)≤1+64N^-186≤N`; T366 currently exports the final `≤N` statement. T334's other scale/log conditions are eventual on this first-cell family.

This is an honest positive-time anti-vacuity check for a single application of T334. It does not give `SourceEvent`, `jG≤N`, or the sharp evolved `qvAt` profile for *all* `r` even on that first cell. The witness lies on the cutoff plateau `c_2=1`; it does not prove `Good∩T_2` contains a transition sample. T361's event inequality does not require that transition intersection to be nonempty. General all-prefix success remains the output of a later weighted-moment/first-failure argument, never a source premise.

## Quantifiers, net loss, and probability order

A usable source theorem has the form `∀M>0, ∀ᶠN, P_d(Good_Nᶜ)≤N^-M` with one measurable `Good_N` whose membership asserts `∀r∈[s_N,t_N]`, all required loop labels, and all entry pairs. A `sup_r P(failure at r)≤N^-M` statement does not imply that event. T345 `fixedTimeFAStatement_proved` is parameterized by a deterministic time sequence; its eventual threshold may depend on that sequence. Instantiating it separately at each N-dependent net point and interchanging `∀ᶠN` with a polynomial `∀j` is unjustified without a finite-N moment estimate uniform in the net index.

If such a uniform estimate is produced, take a deterministic net of at most `N^B` points and at most `N^Cidx` labels (for endpoint two-loop labels `Cidx=2` suffices from `L_N²≤N²`; a crude enumeration of length-six labels costs up to `6` instead). Per-point failure `≤N^-M0` then costs at most `N^(B+Cidx-M0)`. Choose `M0>Dprob+B+Cidx+2` before taking eventual `N`, and prove an actual Hölder-half modulus `C N^K|r-r_j|^(1/2)` for every relevant observable/conditional average. A mesh spacing `≤N^-B` needs `B>2(K+λ)` to make its error `≤N^-λ`, with margin for ratios/denominators. The current code does not certify the common `K` for the support-to-running `jS` cap and averaged law. A fixed-time `StochDom` without the uniform finite-N estimate cannot be union-bounded this way.

For T361 fix the requested tolerance and integer `p≥1`, obtain the deterministic polynomial product exponent `Bprod`, then choose the Good failure exponent `M>2p(Bprod+κ)` (and the net/label union loss above), then eventual `N`. This gives the complement cost `Eall ρ^(1/(2p))≤C N^(Bprod-M/(2p))`. The global polynomial product envelope is itself still missing (T352/T360); a finite-N compactness bound is insufficient. No `M` is selected before `p` or required uniformly over all `p` at one finite `N`.

## Reroute

The dependency architecture has no inherent exponent contradiction on a support-local event, but the **first unproved actual general-window leaf** is a noncircular all-time Step 1 raw4/raw6 and entry/local-law event at arbitrary admissible windows, with the exact numerical source multipliers. The existing unconditional Gaussian construction covers the first cell; later-cell `step1Hyp_gauss_grid_pos` assumes higher Step 2 inputs and cannot be cited as an independent pre-A′ event. Next, the support-to-running modulus and pointwise T334 profile translation must be supplied; the averaged-law net upgrade is an additional drift/moment dependency.

Therefore **no disjoint Lean ticket asserting the whole-window sharp common event is ready**. Reroute first to an independent later-window Step 1/entry source (or explicitly restrict scope to the first cell), then audit a uniform finite-N time modulus and support-local `jG` cap. Once those sources exist, a disjoint assembly theorem can define one `Good_N` from them and instantiate T333/T334/T361. This report assumes neither A′ nor a separately nonempty intersection.

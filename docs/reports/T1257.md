## Mathematical preflight

I checked the accepted T1245/T1247 sources and reports, along with the T1205
integrability result and T995 schedule. The exact pointwise inputs are:

```text
normGoodCrossBudget(u)
  ≤ (15/4) crossProfile(u) + (15/8) N^(-1) u^(-1/2),

crossProfile(u)
  ≤ K N^(13δ/640) etaT(E,s_N)^(-1) ratR(E,s_N,N,v)^(-2) u^(-1/2),
```

where `K` is T1245's fixed positive `crossProfilePointwiseConst`. T1247 is
the exact p=1 norm-good reduction, including the T995 actual smooth weight,
strict transition indicator, canonical smoothing, output, and Gaussian law.
Its conditional event estimate does not assume that the common event and
transition have a resident or positive intersection probability.

For active `k=1`, the target mesh is eventually `N^(4D+18)`, so
`v-s_N = N^(-(4D+18))`. The exact integral
`∫_[s_N,v] u^(-1/2) du = 2(√v-√s_N)` is at most
`2N^(-(2D+9))`. From `Cond272Reg`, its scale margin gives
`N^c ≤ W ell(t_N) etaT(E,t_N)` eventually. The dimension bound
`W L ≤ N`, `ell(t_N) ≤ L`, `etaT(E,t_N) ≤ 1`, and monotonicity in time imply
`etaT(E,s_N)^(-1) ≤ N`. Thus the two integrated terms have exactly the
requested powers:

```text
(15/2) K N^(13δ/640 - 2D - 8) ratR(E,s_N,N,v)^(-2)
  + (15/4) N^(-2D - 10).
```

At `u=0`, the literal budget is zero by its totalized definition
(`1/sqrt(0)` contributes zero after the coefficient division by zero), and
the singular majorant is also zero. T1205 supplies interval integrability
of the exact budget including that endpoint. No favorable-event occupancy
condition was added.

I checked the prescribed PDF directly, formulas (5.21), (5.24), and
(5.40)–(5.43), on printed pp. 54–58. Formula (5.21) is the stopped
integrated hierarchy; (5.24) is its martingale moment estimate;
(5.40)–(5.42) bound the terms inserted into that hierarchy; and (5.43)
defines the stopping time. This theorem is only the smooth-prefix Lean
auxiliary above, not any of those stopped-process conclusions.

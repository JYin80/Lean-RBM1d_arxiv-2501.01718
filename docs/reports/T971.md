## Proof structure

The accepted strict-margin producer fixes one permutation and its actual encoded
Fourier-quantile sample before all times and physical indices. Its strict
all-time entry bound yields the existing finite-coordinate cylinder inside the
literal `goodSetFlow` event. The same center has `Xmat ≠ 0` and vanishes at all
zero-variance Gaussian coordinates.

For the centered event, the exact `Eblk` trace identity and the full
block-diagonal resolvent bridge from T961 give, at each physical block, the true
charge trace `qπ(u)−i`. The false charge is its complex conjugate at the same
sample, so both charge norms agree. The zero-mode quadrature bound is
`‖qπ(u)−i‖ ≤ 8√2/W_N`. For each fixed `ζ>0`, eventually
`4√2 < N^ζ`, hence

```text
8√2/W_N < N^ζ · (2/W_N).
```

The actual control extension satisfies `qExt(0,0,1/2,N,u) ≥ 1/W_N` throughout
the interval, so this gives a uniform strict margin below the literal
`N^ζ·(2 qExt)` threshold for both charges and every block.

Joint continuity of the true centered trace in `(ω,u)` holds for `u<1`: the
flow matrix and spectral parameter vary continuously, the resolvent inverse is
continuous while the spectral parameter remains in the upper half-plane, and
the finite trace and norm preserve continuity. The false-charge norm equals the
true-charge norm. Compactness of `[0,1/2]` and finiteness of the block indices
therefore give a second finite-coordinate cylinder inside the literal
`centeredEvent`. Taking the union of the two finite coordinate sets and
intersecting their open coordinate neighborhoods gives a single cylinder
inside the joint event.

On active coordinates this cylinder contains a nonempty open set in the finite
Gaussian product. On zero-variance coordinates it fixes the value to zero,
which agrees with the encoded Hermitian band-supported center. The proved
`support_cylinder_pos` lemma gives this hybrid cylinder strictly positive mass
under the actual product law `P Dims.exampleGrow`; measure monotonicity yields
positive mass of the literal intersection.

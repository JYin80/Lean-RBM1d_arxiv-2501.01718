## Delivered declarations

### Source moment row

`twoCoord_initial_source_momNorm_le` proves that for arbitrary bulk energy, moving window,
`Cond272Reg`, and `BoundsCore (sample d) E s`, for every `epsilon > 0` and integer `P >= 1`
there is `C > 0` such that, eventually in `N`, simultaneously for every two-loop charge and
label,

```text
momNorm P_gauss (2*P) (||lkT(s_N,q,b)||)
  <= C * (N^(epsilon/4) * scale(E,N,s_N)^(-2)).
```

The constant is independent of `N`, `q`, and `b`.  The direct analytic input is
`hB.LmK 2`.  `momNormDom_of_stochDom` performs the reverse moment bridge.  Its side conditions
are discharged for the actual Gaussian observable using `continuous_lkT_omega`,
`integrable_lkT_pow`, `norm_gloop_le_det`, `exists_norm_Kval_le_upto_one`, `flow_crude`, and
`eventually_etaT_inv_le_sq_window`.  No full `MomentDuhamel.Hyp` record is assumed.

### Transported ratio row

`twoCoord_initial_momNorm_le_ratio` proves, with the same quantifier order, eventually in `N`,
for every

```text
v in netFinset s t (meshK 21 (1/2)) N
q : LoopData (d.L N) 2,
```

the literal estimate

```text
scale(E,N,v)^2
  * momNorm P_gauss (2*P)
      (||Uker^{xi(q.charge)}_{s_N,v} lkT(s_N,q.charge)(q.label)||)

<= C * N^(epsilon/4)
     * [scale(E,N,v)^2
        * ((1-s_N)/(1-v))^2
        * scale(E,N,s_N)^(-2)].
```

Thus the checked powers are:

- moment order `2 * P`;
- small polynomial loss `N ^ (epsilon / 4)`;
- normalized endpoint factor `scale(E,N,v) ^ 2`;
- exactly two kernel coordinates, hence `((1-s_N)/(1-v)) ^ 2`;
- the `BoundsCore.LmK 2` source scale `scale(E,N,s_N) ^ (-2)`.

The proof uses `norm_xiOf_mSigma` to show that every charge coordinate has modulus one and
`momNorm_Uker_apply_le` for the two-coordinate propagator.  `netFinset_subset_Icc` supplies
`s_N <= v <= t_N`; no positive-time condition `s_N < v` is introduced.  The exact ratio is
kept as the primary interface, rather than packaging T602's optional weaker
`scale(E,N,s_N)^(1/2)` corollary.

# T840 — centered next-image martingale difference

## Result and proof route

`RBM1D/Gauss/PermutationNextImageCentered.lean` defines
`unusedImageAverage W k f` by averaging `f` over the unused images and proves
`uniformPerm_nextImage_centered_condExp` for every `W`, `k : Fin W`, and
`f : Fin W → ℝ`:

```lean
(uniformPerm W)[(fun ρ => f (ρ k) - unusedImageAverage W k f ρ) |
  prefixSigma W k.val] =ᵐ[uniformPerm W] (fun _ => 0)
```

The definition agrees pointwise with the nested `unusedImages` sum divided by
the real cast of `W-k.val`. For prefix measurability, the proof expands the
sum as a finite sum of indicators of unused-image membership and applies the
actual producer `measurableSet_unusedImage_prefixSigma`. The average is
integrable because the permutation space is finite. It then uses
`condExp_sub`, `condExp_of_stronglyMeasurable`, and T833's
`uniformPerm_nextImage_weighted_condExp`; the algebraic cancellation gives
zero. The target centered conclusion is not assumed.

## Boundaries and nondegeneracy

The denominator is positive for every valid `k`, by `k.isLt`. There is no
`Fin 0` index, as recorded by the imported boundary producer. At `W=1`, the
new pointwise lemma `uniformPerm_nextImage_centered_one_pointwise` proves the
fluctuation is identically zero. For a general last valid index, the new
pointwise lemma `uniformPerm_nextImage_centered_last_pointwise` proves the
unused-image set is exactly `{π k}` and the fluctuation again vanishes.

The width-two witness takes `k=0` and `f(0)=0`, `f(1)=1`. It proves the
average is `1/2`, both the identity and transposition singleton atoms have
positive mass, and the centered values there are respectively `-1/2` and
`1/2`. Thus the mean-zero conclusion has a genuine nonconstant instance.
There are no new hypotheses requiring a separate satisfiability witness.

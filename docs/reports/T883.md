### Planned finite-W construction

For `W ≥ 1`, define `gamma j` at each `j ≤ W` by applying
`semicircleQuantile` to `(j:ℝ)/W`, and `lambda j` for `j < W` at
`((j:ℝ)+1/2)/W`. First prove these levels lie in `[0,1]`; the positive
denominator follows from `1 ≤ W`. Then the existing strict monotonicity gives
`gamma j < lambda j < gamma (j+1)` and strict increase of consecutive
boundaries. The endpoint equations identify `gamma 0 = -2` and `gamma W = 2`.
For each `j < W`, the accepted Ioc increment and the CDF equations reduce the
cell mass to `ENNReal.ofReal (1/W)`.

Concrete satisfiability checks are available without eventual quantifiers:
`W=1` has the midpoint level `1/2` and cell mass `1`; `W=2` has midpoint
levels `1/4` and `3/4` and two cells of mass `1/2`. Reflection is optional and
will be attempted only after the required cells and witnesses compile.

No API beyond the inspected Mathlib CDF/Stieltjes and intermediate-value
interfaces appears necessary. There is no paper-delta: the module formalizes
only equal-mass cells for the accepted normalized density measure.

`semicircleQuantileCell_witness_two` verify nondegenerate W=1 and W=2 cells,
including their midpoint ordering and masses 1 and 1/2, respectively.

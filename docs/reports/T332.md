## Exact remaining step for arbitrary deterministic `Ψ`

The existing `firstCellFlucGain_from_localLaw` is specialized to `E=0`, the
first-cell window, and `firstCellPsi`. It cannot be instantiated with an
arbitrary fixed-time `Ψ`. The next producer must choose, **after** the
stochastic-domination tolerance `τ`, a threshold such as

`δ_θ(N)=min(1/4, (N+4)^θ·max(Ψ_N,(N+4)^(-2)))`,

with `0<θ<min(a/4,τ/16)`, and prove the following for each fixed `p`:

1. The local law and the margin `N^(θ/2)Ψ_N≤δ_θ(N)` imply
   `HighProb (goodSetFlow d E u u δ_θ)`. This requires the polynomial
   `η_u⁻¹` and `Ψ⁻¹` estimates in
   `highProb_goodSetFlow_of_localLaw`.
2. `PolyLo δ_θ`, `PolyHi (η_u⁻¹+1)`, eventual
   `8(2p)δ_θ≤1`, and eventual
   `2 minorDiffC(2p)(2δ_θ)+2δ_θ≤1` feed
   `fixedMoment_gain_of_goodSetFlow`.
3. `W⁻¹≤(4δ_θ)²` and `4δ_θ≤1` feed the actual row/block
   `UniformWeight` moment consumer. Its budget is at most `C_pδ_θ`.
4. `δ_θ²≤(N+4)^(2θ)Ψ²` eventually; absorb the exponent by choosing
   `θ` after `τ`, then apply the fixed-moment Markov criterion. The
   threshold must be a positive function for every `N`; the floor handles
   the finite exceptional prefix without adding an assumption on `Ψ`.

No mathematical counterexample to this chain was found. The missing item is
this general threshold producer and its arithmetic, not a new stochastic
hypothesis. The exact target remains open until it is compiled.

## Mathematical preflight and producer match

The exact claim is the conjunction of three disjoint index cases, with the
same `hbase` premise passed unchanged to both imported producers:

- `k=0`: the restricted integral is at most the full integral by
  `integral_mono_measure`, since the integrand is nonnegative and integrable.
  The integrand retains its literal `k=0` widened weight and cutoff; no
  stronger premise or replacement integrand is introduced.
- `k=1`: `eventually_k1_good_of_exact_k0` from T1153/T1154 has the same
  hypotheses and exact integral fields; its active-cell premise follows from
  the all-active hypothesis.
- `2≤k≤N^4`: `eventually_early_good_of_exact_k0` from T1157/T1158 has the
  same hypotheses and exact integral fields; the active-index premise is
  passed through unchanged.

Intersect the three eventual sets (and `N≥1`, for the `k=0` integrability
bound) once. If the producer constants are `Ck1` and `Cearly`, the new
constant is `C₁ = C₀ + Ck1 + Cearly`; it is positive and dominates each case.
Thus `q` is fixed before the eventual threshold, and neither the rate nor the
order changes at an index boundary. This is only a conditional norm-good
estimate on the early range; it is not a full expectation, all-range result,
`hfamily`, or A′ theorem.

The premise has a nondegenerate witness in the imported
`nondegenerate_fixed_order_k0_premise_witness`: for `0<δ≤1`, it supplies one
`q≥1`, positive `C₀`, the same moving Gaussian model and window, the exact
full `k=0` premise, and eventually an actual sample in the literal norm-good
set with `2≤cutNetTop`. The k2 activity and sample belong to T1157's
T1117/T995-backed positive-length witness. This verifies joint satisfiability
with an active `k=2` cell.

## Exact source package

`pointwise_source_package` is pathwise on one
`APrimeGeneralMovingCommonSources.commonEvent` resident and one
`u : TimeIcc s t N`.  It returns simultaneously:

```text
L3(b) <= N^zetaSrc * r_u^2 * A_u^(-2),

|eGpm(a1,a2)|
  <= (4 * N^zetaCtr * r_u) * (ell_u * eta_u)^(-1) * sum_b L3(b),

column block average <= flowDelta(d,E,t,N) + W^(-1),
row block average    <= flowDelta(d,E,t,N) + W^(-1).
```

Here `r_u = ell_u / ell_s` and `A_u = B.scale E N u = W ell_u eta_u`.
The coefficient `4` is literal: T523 contributes
`N^zetaCtr * (2 * qExt)`, `qExt = r_u * A_u^(-1)` on the moving window,
and `EGDef.norm_eGpm_le` contributes its outer factor `2W`.  No loss is
identified with another loss.

The two block averages are the generic declarations
`APrimeFirstCellEGFar.goodEvent_column_block_average` and
`goodEvent_row_block_average`, applied to the literal T575 component
`goodSetFlow d E s t (flowDelta d E t)` of T579's carrier.  Thus their
coefficient is exactly `flowDelta + W^(-1)`, not a first-cell threshold.

(SHA-256 `bd6f7e32bee30a28202e147742b28e88e85bdd6a691ae8fa89e3aedae22bbb43`),

#130 and #136). Thus the PASS verifies the exact **T615 source summand** and
its integration, not a verbatim proof of paper (5.41), (5.42), or the paper's
stopped argument. Formula (5.39) concerns initial data and (5.42) quadratic
variation; neither is used as a hidden premise here. T1003's phrase
“(5.41) is the transported linear drift row” should be read as orientation,
not formula identity.

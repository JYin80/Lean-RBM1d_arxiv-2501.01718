- If it is zero, nonnegativity of the actual smooth weight closes the goal.

  the actual weight on its order-independent plateau, including the active,
  inactive, `N < 2`, and empty-prefix branches.  The conclusion follows from
  `APrimeWeight.widenedW_le_one`.

No order-zero claim is made.

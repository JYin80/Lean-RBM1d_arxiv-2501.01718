# T747 — monotonicity of actual permutation prefix cells

## Scope and files

Added `RBM1D/Gauss/PermutationPrefixCellMono.lean`, importing only the accepted
T711 producer `PermutationPrefixFiltrationCore`. This is a finite support result
for the actual-prefix cells, with no conditional distribution, first-cell event,
shared event, `P2` estimate, or paper closure. The paper's (2.3)–(2.4) are
Green-function local-law estimates; this ticket establishes only a combinatorial
filtration-support fact used downstream and does not claim either estimate.

No root import was added. No new assumptions were introduced beyond the explicit
ordering hypothesis `k ≤ l` in the general monotonicity statements.

## Results

* `prefixCell_subset_of_le`: for all `W k l : ℕ` and `π : PermΩ W`, if `k ≤ l`,
  then `prefixCell W l π ⊆ prefixCell W k π`. Membership at every coordinate
  below `k` follows from membership at every coordinate below `l`.
* `prefixCell_card_antitone`: the corresponding finite cardinalities are
  antitone, by `Finset.card_le_card`.
* `prefixCell_zero_eq_univ`: at reveal time zero, the cell is `Finset.univ`.
* `prefixCell_eq_singleton_after_width`: when `W ≤ l`, the cell at `l` is
  `{π}`, including `W = 0`.
* `prefixCell_zero_width_eq`: all cells at width zero are equal, even when the
  reveal times and reference labels differ; this uses the subsingleton
  permutation type on `Fin 0`.
* `prefixCell_two_id_strict_shrink`: at width two, the cell for the identity
  shrinks from reveal time zero to one. The identity remains in the later cell,
  while the transposition belongs to the earlier cell and is absent from the
  later cell, witnessing strict inclusion with a concrete nonconstant support.

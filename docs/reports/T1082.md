## Claim and source

`RBM.norm_edgeKer_unit_phase_le_one` states that for every finite ring `ZMod L` with `L ≥ 3`, real `0 ≤ s ≤ t < 1`, complex `ξ` with `‖ξ‖ = 1`, and every pair of entries `a,b`,

```text
‖edgeKer L ξ (s : ℂ) (t : ℂ) a b‖
  ≤ (edgeKer L 1 (s : ℂ) (t : ℂ) a b).re.
```

`RBM.edgeKer_one_entry_re_nonneg` proves the right side nonnegative under the same time and size conditions. I inspected the actual `paper/250520-YinJun-v2.pdf`, printed page 53, (5.17)–(5.18), including a page rendering. Its factor is `(1-sξSᴮ)(1-tξSᴮ)⁻¹`, and (5.18) is `I-(s-t)ξ Θᴮ_{tξ}Sᴮ`. The local `Kernel.lean` defines exactly this factor and proves `edgeKer_eq = I-(s-t)ξ(SᴮΘᴮ_{tξ})`; the factor order agrees by the already proved commutation of `Sᴮ` and `Θᴮ`. The crucial sign is `t-s ≥ 0`.

This is a supplementary comparison for (5.17)–(5.18), not a claim that the paper itself states the comparison there. The proof is stronger in phase generality than the particular paper charges, and explicitly requires the time interval in the ticket.

## Independent mathematical check

1. `SB` has real, nonnegative entries: `1/3` on `{0,1,-1}` and zero elsewhere. Every matrix power therefore has real, nonnegative entries, including power zero. This uses only a finite sum over `ZMod L` and covers `L=3` without assuming the support points are distinct for smaller rings.
2. For `0 ≤ t < 1`, the Neumann expansion of `Theta L (tξ)` converges entrywise since `‖tξ‖=t<1`. Each complex term is `(tξ)^k(SB^k)_{ab}`; its norm equals the corresponding nonnegative real term `t^k(SB^k)_{ab}`. Thus the triangle inequality gives `‖Θ_{tξ}(a,b)‖ ≤ Θ_t(a,b)`, where the latter is a nonnegative real number.
3. Multiplying by `SB` preserves the comparison entrywise: finite convolution has nonnegative real weights. The code proves both nonnegativity and realness of `(SB*Theta_t)_{ab}` rather than assuming either.
4. From (5.18), the entry is `1[a=b]+(t-s)ξ(SB*Theta_{tξ})_{ab}`. For off-diagonal entries the identity contribution is zero; for diagonal entries `|1+v|≤1+|v|`. Since `t-s≥0` and `‖ξ‖=1`, both cases are bounded by the real unit-phase entry `1[a=b]+(t-s)(SB*Theta_t)_{ab}`. The proof treats `s=t`, `s=t=0`, and arbitrary finite `L≥3` without an exceptional division.

For a concrete nondegenerate check, choose `L=3`, `s=0`, `t=1/2`, `ξ=i`, `a=0`, `b=1`. Here `SB=J/3` and `Theta(z)=I+[z/(1-z)]J/3`. The left entry is `(-1+2i)/15`, of norm `√5/15>0`, while the right entry is `1/3`; the diagonal entries are `(14+2i)/15` and `4/3`. All hypotheses hold and the comparison is substantive.

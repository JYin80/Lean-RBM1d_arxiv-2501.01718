# T686 — nonempty finite upper-semicontinuous supremum

## Files and result

- Added `RBM1D/Gauss/FiniteSupUpperSemicontinuous.lean`, with Mathlib-only imports and no project dependency.
- `RBM.Gauss.upperSemicontinuous_finset_sup'` states that for any topological space `α`, nonempty `s : Finset ι`, and real-valued family `f : ι → α → ℝ`, upper semicontinuity of every `f i` for `i ∈ s` implies upper semicontinuity of the exact function `x ↦ s.sup' hs (fun i => f i x)`. It keeps the explicit nonempty witness `hs` and the finite maximum; it introduces no empty-set default.
- `RBM.Gauss.upperSemicontinuous_twoFunction_witness` uses the two members of `Bool` with `f true(u)=u` and `f false(u)=-u`. It proves the resulting `Finset.univ.sup'` is upper semicontinuous, equals `|u|` for every real `u`, and has different values at `u=0` and `u=1`. Thus the assumptions have a nonconstant, nonvacuous realization.

## Proof

The first theorem uses `Finset.Nonempty.cons_induction`, `Finset.sup'_singleton`, `Finset.sup'_cons`, and Mathlib's `UpperSemicontinuous.sup`. At each induction step it restricts the hypothesis `∀ i ∈ s` to the selected element and the remaining nonempty finset. The example uses continuity of `u` and `-u`, followed by `abs_eq_max_neg` for the exact two-element maximum.

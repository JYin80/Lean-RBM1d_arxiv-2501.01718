## Formal statements and argument

The module is [GreenSpectralAlphaTail.lean](../../RBM1D/Flow/GreenSpectralAlphaTail.lean). It reuses blockM_eq from Universality.lean, the eigenbasis completeness identity sum_sq_norm_eigenvectorBasis, and the real block-profile representation and column sum. No T1397 spectral expansion is reproved.

For N=LW, y=(a₀,β), and M_{y,α}=blockM, sum_norm_blockM_le proves
\[
\sum_\alpha |M_{y,\alpha}| \le 2N.
\]
Indeed, blockM_eq writes M_{y,α}=N\sum_x |u_\alpha(x)|^2(S_{xy}-N^{-1}). Triangle inequality, nonnegativity and the column sum \(\sum_x S_{xy}=1\), together with eigenbasis completeness \(\sum_\alpha |u_\alpha(x)|^2=1\), reduce the estimate to \(N\sum_x(S_{xy}+N^{-1})=2N\).

The interval is exactly
\[
J=[-2+\kappa/2,\;2-\kappa/2],\qquad \delta=\kappa/4.
\]
The pole theorem assumes \(\kappa>0\), \(|E|\le 2-\kappa\), \(|\operatorname{Re}z-E|\le C_0/N\), \(C_0/N\le\kappa/4\), and \(\operatorname{Im}z>0\). For every eigenvalue outside J, the real-part separation is at least \(\kappa/4\); the upper-half-plane condition also ensures that z is not an eigenvalue. Thus
\[
|p_\alpha(z)|^2 \le \delta^{-2}
\quad(\lambda_\alpha\notin J).
\]
spectral_alpha_outside_bulk_tail_le combines this pointwise estimate with the aggregate above to prove
\[
\sum_{\lambda_\alpha\notin J}|p_\alpha(z)|^2|M_{y,\alpha}|
\le 2N/\delta^2.
\]
Here “outside” and “inside” are represented by the exact complement and membership of spectralBulkInterval κ.

blockM_bulk_restricted_max_le states the maximum implication pointwise: if D ≥ 0 and, for every \(\lambda_\alpha\in J\), all site masses obey \(|u_\alpha(x)|^2\le D/N\), then \(|M_{y,\alpha}|\le 2D\) for every such alpha and every block site y. The factor \(2D\) follows from the same profile variation bound. There is no corresponding conclusion outside J.

The statements carry \(3\le L\), positive W (NeZero W), positive matrix size, Hermiticity, and the positive imaginary part where the spectral parameter is used. The profile column sum and eigenbasis completeness are compiled source lemmas. No N=0 or empty-spectrum case is admitted.

## Paper fidelity and same-sample witness

The manuscript's (2.10) supplies a delocalization estimate for eigenvalues in its applicable energy window; it does not justify the unrestricted alpha maximum displayed in (2.32). The new maximum implication is explicitly restricted to J. The aggregate tail is the deterministic input relevant to the outside-J part of the (2.31) spectral bookkeeping. These clauses do not establish stochastic domination or (2.29), (2.30), (2.32), or (2.33).

A same-sample support witness makes the hypotheses nondegenerate. Take W=1, L=N≥100, a path adjacency matrix on the first N−1 sites, and an isolated last site with diagonal entry 10. The path eigenvalues are \(2\cos(\pi k/N)\), and its normalized sine eigenvectors have every squared coordinate at most \(2/N\). With \(\kappa=1\), E=0, z=i, and \(C_0=1\), the interval J=[−3/2,3/2] contains path eigenvalues and the isolated eigenvalue 10 lies outside it. For the isolated eigenvector and the last site,
\[
M_{y,\alpha}=N(1/3-1/N)=N/3-1,
\]
so the tail is nonempty and its profile value grows with N. The same sample has
\[
R_z^2=N^{-1}\sum_\alpha|\lambda_\alpha-i|^2\le 5+96/N<6.
\]
All used path and diagonal coordinates lie in the positive-variance support of the W=1 Gaussian carrier; the matrix has simple spectrum, so the bulk mass, isolated tail value, and moment bound persist on a sufficiently small neighborhood. This is a support/satisfiability witness only, not a high-probability estimate or an OU event.

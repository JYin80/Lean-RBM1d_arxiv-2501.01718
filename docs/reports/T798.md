## Literal target and quantifier ledger

For fixed `c>0`, the paper assumes `N=WL` and `W≥N^(1/2+c)` (2.2). For fixed small `κ,τ>0`, large `D>0`, and `z=E+iη` with `|E|≤2−κ`, `N^(−1+τ)≤η≤1`, Theorem 2.3 asserts, for all sufficiently large `N`, separately:

| Formula | Single failure event whose complement is asserted to have probability `>1−N^(−D)` | Exact threshold |
|---|---|---|
| (2.3) | `∃ x,y∈ℤ_N`, entrywise resolvent error exceeds threshold | `W^τ (W ℓ(z) η)^(−1/2)` |
| (2.4) | `∃ a∈ℤ_L`, normalized block diagonal sum error exceeds threshold | `W^τ (W ℓ(z) η)^(−1)` |

Here `G(z)=(H−z)⁻¹`, `m(z)=m_sc(z)`, and **paper** `ℓ(z)=min(η^(−1/2),L)+1` is (2.1). Each formula has its own event, uniform over the displayed finite spatial indices. The paper's Definition 2.1 stochastic domination puts the union over its parameter set inside the probability. Its proof uses the flow law (2.64) for (2.3), the rank-one loop estimate (2.60) with `K_{t,+,a}=m^(E)` for (2.4), and the distributional transfer (2.65); the flow time satisfies `0≤t≤1−N^(−1+τ)` and `η_t=(1−t) Im m^(E)`, with `η_t ≍ η` and `ℓ_t ≍ ℓ(z)` (printed pp. 21–22). The two denominators must remain different; averaging (2.3) alone loses the extra square-root gain required by (2.4).

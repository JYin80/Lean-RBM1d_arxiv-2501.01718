## Why the available probability statements do not combine

T579 `highProb_commonEvent` gives, for each fixed `A>0`, an eventual bound
`P(commonEvent_Nᶜ) ≤ N^(−A)`. T1065 proves only
`0 < P(transition_N)` eventually. Its proof obtains positivity from an open
finite-coordinate cylinder around the T1051 scalar-ray sample, using
`support_cylinder_pos`; it exports no lower bound for that cylinder's mass or
for `P(transition_N)`.

The required mass argument would need `P(transition_N) > P(commonEvent_Nᶜ)`.
Qualitative positivity of the left side does not imply this comparison against
a super-polynomially small but otherwise unquantified complement. For example,
the abstract bounds `P(C_Nᶜ)=e^(−√N)` and `P(T_N)=e^(−N)>0` satisfy the stated
types of bounds while allowing `T_N ⊆ C_Nᶜ`. The `HighProb` quantifiers do not
give a threshold uniform in an exponent that varies with `N`.

The direct support route also remains incomplete. T1065's compatible sample
is certified to lie in `transition`, not in T579's `rawCarrier`; its open
cylinder is contained in `transition` only. The accepted local
`measCore`-retention lemmas require a cylinder already contained in
`rawCarrier ∩ transition`. T995 separately supplies a same-sample
`commonEvent` resident at the positive cell `k=1` with positive actual smooth
weight. That is not the strict transition at `k=2`, and it gives no link to
T1051/T1065's scalar-ray sample. Combining those residents would not preserve
the sample.

No checked producer supplies either (i) a quantitative lower bound on the
exact T1065 transition mass that beats the T579 common-event complement, or
(ii) a zero-variance-compatible center with strict margins for every literal
raw-carrier condition and the `k=2` transition, yielding a cylinder inside
`rawCarrier ∩ transition`. Thus the requested same-sample inhabitance is not
established. This conclusion does not assert emptiness or refute the smooth
weight route.

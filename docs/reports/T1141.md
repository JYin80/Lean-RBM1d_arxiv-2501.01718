## Mathematical preflight and composition

I checked the authorized PDF `paper/250520-YinJun-v2.pdf` at (5.43)–(5.44), and independently checked the accepted T1117/T1118 and T1115/T1116 sources and reports. Formula (5.43) defines a stopping time from `J*`; (5.44) bounds the quadratic variation of a stopped martingale by integrating the near- and far-field profile up to `τ=T∧t`. The present theorem is a static selected-high-order Gaussian moment estimate in the Lean T999 transfer route. It does not claim to prove or replace the paper's stopped-process argument or its (5.44) QV bound.

For each fixed `δ,p`, choose `pHigh = max(p, ⌈32/(3δ)⌉₊)`. Apply T1117 with `pHigh`; its returned `q` satisfies `q ≥ pHigh`, so `q ≥ p` and `32 ≤ 3δq`. Apply T1115's fixed-order complement estimate at this exact returned `q`. Thus the good- and bad-event estimates concern the same order and integrand. T1117 is used only for active `2 ≤ k ≤ N^4`; T1115 supplies the complementary-event bound for every active `k ≥ 2`. Intersecting their eventual sets with `N ≥ 1` preserves a single threshold uniform in `k`.

For each such `N,k`, active mesh membership gives `cutNetPt(k) ∈ [s_N,t_N]` and `t_N<1`. The existing `jSnorm_nonneg` therefore applies at the actual target endpoint. `widenedW` is measurable and lies in `[0,1]`; the truncated endpoint is measurable and has the deterministic `2N^(2δ)` cap. `Step2Bootstrap.integrable_weight_mul` proves integrability of the full integrand. The measurable good event and this integrability justify `integral_add_compl`; adding the good contribution `Cgood N^(δq/2)` and the complement contribution `2^(2q)N^(δq/2)` yields the claimed bound with `C=Cgood+2^(2q)>0`. The same `δ,q,N,k,ω` occur on both sides of the partition; no union bound or mesh-cardinality factor is introduced.

**Verdict: incomplete, with a narrow first-cell route.** The current T296 theorem `Gauss.psiF_stochDom_etaT_inv_of_drift_inputs''` takes `MomentDuhamel.Hyp X E s t n`, `Cond272`, `LKDecayQuant.FlowInputs`, `flowXiLK_1 ≺ 1`, and `xiRhs_{n+2} ≺ 1`, and concludes `psiF_{n+2} ≺ etaT_u⁻¹`. Here `n` is T296's offset: the first Lemma 5.14 length is **m=2, hence n=0** in T296.

The four clauses of `Gauss.Lemma514Premises X E s t m Λ Φ` give `XiL_{2m+2} ≺ Λ`, `XiLK_k ≺ Φ` for `1≤k<m`, each quadratic term `XiLK_k XiLK_{m-k+2}/A_u ≺ Φ` for `2≤k≤m`, and `XiL_{m+1} ≺ Φ`. They do not assert `Φ ≺ 1`. `SumZeroDyn.xiRhs_stochDom` consequently gives `xiRhs_m ≺ (2m−1)Φ`, exactly `2n+3` when `m=n+2`. For example, an allowed control `Φ_N=N^β` with fixed `β>0` leaves a genuine `N^β` factor. Taking the pointwise `Φ` from the *different* numerical parameter `Phi` inside `LKDecayQuant.FlowInputs` would also be a type/meaning error.

## Minimal length, scales, and quantifiers

By the actual `SumZeroDyn.xiRhs` definition,

```text
xiRhs_2 = XiLK_1 + XiLK_2²/A_u + XiL_3.
```

Thus `Lemma514Premises` at `m=2` gives three `≺Φ` terms, hence `≺3Φ`, not `≺1`. T288's one-point consistency example with `A_N=(N+1)²`, `XiLK_2=N+1`, `Λ=Φ=1` shows that its quadratic clause alone does not yield `XiLK_2≺1`. The proposed `Lemma514 2 → Step45.xiLK_le_one_of_hyp → XiLK_2≺1 → Lemma514 2` route is circular; `Step45.xiLK_le_one_of_hyp` explicitly consumes `Lemma514 2`.

There is a sound, narrower first-cell calculation from **Step 1/2 data**, without that cycle. On `Dims.exampleGrow`, `E=0`, `s=firstCellS τ'=0`, and `t=firstCellT τ'`, the latter is eventually `1/2` because `W_N→∞` and `τ'>0`. On this interval, `η_u=1-u∈[1/2,1]`, `ell_u=(1-u)^{-1/2}∈[1,√2]` (as `L_N≥3`), `R=ell_t/ell_s=√2`, and `A_u=W_N√(1-u)∈[W_N/√2,W_N]`. The growing witness has `W_N≈N^{3/4}`, so `A_u≈N^{3/4}` and `η_u⁻¹≤2`; these are asymptotic comparisons, not equalities for finite `N`. `Step2PP.flow_xiL_apriori_le'` gives `XiL_3≺R²=2` from `AprioriFlow`. `StepGlue.flow_xiLK_two_le` gives `XiLK_2≺(η_s/η_u)^4≤16` from `AprioriDecayAll`. `StepGlue.flow_hs1` gives `XiLK_1≺1` from `Eq45Flow` and the two-loop `L` bound; on this first cell `AprioriFlow` supplies that bound because `R≤√2`. Therefore `XiLK_2²/A_u≺W_N⁻¹≈N^{-3/4}` and **`xiRhs_2≺1` follows conditionally from those same-window Step 1/2 inputs**. The current repository has the component declarations, not this final first-cell composition. In particular `AprioriDecayAll` must remain an explicit input until produced; T288 and `Step2PP` identify why its strong two-loop form cannot be silently read off a weaker moment bootstrap.

For general fixed `m=n+2`, the paper's (5.77) and the Lean finite sums retain the `(2m−1)Φ` main RHS. T290 bounds the additive `xiSum_m` by `C_m N^{3m+1}` on every sample, and chooses its error decay exponent `D₁=D+(3m+1)(2m+2)+1` (at `m=2`, `D+43`); this absorbs **only the additive error**. T289's independent F-decay uses an input exponent `D₀=D+2m+7` (at `m=2`, `D+11`). In T290's main branch the radius `N^θ` costs a fixed `N^{(n+4)θ}`; choosing `θ` after a requested stochastic tolerance can absorb that cost but cannot absorb a fixed `Φ_N=N^β`. On a general `Cond272` window, `η_u⁻¹≤C_E N²`; the coarse resulting scale would be `N^{2+β+o(1)}` when `Φ=N^β`, whereas T296 asks for `η_u⁻¹` itself. The `≺` statements quantify over each fixed length and each `τ,D>0`, with `∀ᶠ N`; the error exponent may be chosen after fixed `m,τ,D`, but `m` or `Φ` cannot be made to depend on `N` to erase this factor.

## Next theorem and exact remaining estimate

The narrowest positive target is a **first-cell `xiRhs_2≺1` and `flowXiLK_1≺1` composition** from the explicitly listed same-window `AprioriFlow`, `AprioriDecayAll`, `Eq45Flow`, and local-law hypotheses, with `s=0`, `t=firstCellT τ'`. It should carry a nonempty high-probability event or a compiled joint witness, not infer joint satisfiability from unrelated witnesses. Its mathematics is the three-term calculation above; no new kernel estimate is needed. It still cannot instantiate T296 until the unprimed `FlowInputs` producer is completed or a carefully proved floored variant is used.

For arbitrary fixed `m≥3`, the more viable route is to prove a projected-drift estimate carrying the **actual `Φ` factor**, `psiF_m ≺ eta_u⁻¹ Φ` (with explicit eventual positivity/growth conditions needed by the moment bridge), and carry it through the `Λ^{1/2}+Φ` budget of Lemma 5.14. The exact missing implication in the current T296 signature is `xiRhs_m≺(2m−1)Φ ⇒ xiRhs_m≺1`; it is unavailable without `Φ≺1` or a separate stronger model estimate. T290's polynomial envelope does not imply it. This is a preflight recommendation, not a proved revised theorem.

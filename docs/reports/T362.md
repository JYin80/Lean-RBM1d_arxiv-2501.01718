## Exact multiplier calculation

Write `A=W ell_u eta_u`, `r=ell_u/ell_s`, and let `n` be the size parameter.
Suppose the actual centered one-loop bound used in `norm_eGpm_le` is

`c≤C₁ n^ζ₁ r/A`, with `C₁≥0`, `0≤ζ₁≤1`.

Then `norm_eGpm_le` has prefactor

`2Wc≤2C₁ n^ζ₁ r/(ell_u eta_u)`.

Thus the `κ₁` in a near-field `eG_near_le` invocation is
`κ₁=2C₁ n^ζ₁ r`, not the coefficient-one `κ₁=r` in the existing reduced
interface. Also retain the honest length-three input

`L3(b)≤C₃ n^ζ₃ r² A^-2`, with `C₃≥0`, `ζ₃≥0`.

The near principal term obtained from `sum_le_split_one` is exactly

`2C₁C₃ n^(ζ₁+ζ₃) eta_u^-1 r³ cNear(W,ell_u) T(d)`,

where `cNear=(2(log W)^3+2/ell_u)exp((log W)^(3/4))` as in `Lemma57`.
It is mathematically invalid to drop either source loss and claim the literal
coefficient-one reduced near term from these premises.

For a near output pair, set
`ρ=eta_u^-1 jG T(ellStarStar−ellStar)`. T350 gives `L3(b)≤ρ` on the exact
abstract `h554` far set, with `ρ≥0`. The remaining term is

`κ₁(ell_u eta_u)^-1 Lρ`
`=2C₁ n^ζ₁ [rL/(ell_u eta_u)] [eta_u^-1 jG T(F)]`.

This is definitionally the T357 residual with `(C,N,ζ,J)=(C₁,n,ζ₁,jG)`.
Under T357's explicit caps `jG≤n`, `A,L,eta_u^-1≤n`, `n≤W²`,
`ell_u,r≥1`, `D=60`, `(4D)²≤log W`, and the eventual fixed-`C₁`
condition `4C₁≤W`, the near-supported remainder is at most
`W^-1 eta_u^-1 r³ T(d)`. Hence the honest near conclusion is the sum of
the preceding principal term and this `W^-1` term, both times `T(d)`.

This audit concerns only the near branch. A full actual (5.35) also needs
multiplier-aware far estimates, including the two (5.57) orientations; the
coefficient-one `EGDef.eGpm_le_reduced` cannot simply be reused with the
stochastic source multipliers.

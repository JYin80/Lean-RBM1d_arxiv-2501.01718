## Scope and preflight

The prescribed paper formula (4.12) bounds a weighted conditional-expectation fluctuation by the random, unsmoothed loop maximum. It does not assume a smooth proxy or a reciprocal proxy. This ticket's result is therefore an abstract finite-cube calculus statement only; it proves no actual Gaussian `Pr`, `Yr`, stochastic-domination, or time assertion.

The two defects in T1422 are repaired in the corrected target: vertexwise lower comparison of the smooth norm with `Γ` controls the reciprocal scale, while either direct `ℓq` shifted-face bounds or a controlled factor `(card ι)^(1/q)` controls the vector dimension. Face-difference assumptions must apply to every shifted face. An order-zero upper bound remains separate. These hypotheses do not require any individual coordinate to be positive.

For fixed `1 ≤ r ≤ M`, T1424's finite-calculus argument gives the target bound, for `s ∈ {1,-1}`,

\[
 |\Delta_{[r]} F_{q,s}(a)| \le \Gamma^{s+r/2}
 \sum_{\pi\in\Pi(r)} h_{|\pi|}q^{|\pi|-1}\rho^{s-|\pi|}
 \prod_{B\in\pi} B_{|B|},
 \qquad h_k=\sum_{\sigma\in\Pi(k)}|\sigma|!,
 \]

where `F_{q,1}=smoothMax q`, `F_{q,-1}=(smoothMax q)⁻¹`, and one may take `ρ=2⁻ᴹ min(c,1)` from the vertex lower bound `smoothMax q (aᵛ) ≥ cΓ`. Thus the powers are `Γ^(1+r/2)` and `Γ^(-1+r/2)`, and the `q` cost is at most `q^(r-1)` times the displayed product of shifted-face losses. Coordinatewise inputs yield `B_j=K A_j` when `(card ι)^(1/q)≤K`; the accepted `rpow_card_le_exp_one` supplies `K=e` under `log(card ι)≤q`.

## Proof route and checks

The paper source was checked directly in `paper/250520-YinJun-v2.pdf`. Formula (4.9) supplies the rank-one principal-minor deletion identity used by `gEnt_sub_insert_rankOne`; formula (4.12) is the later weighted fluctuation estimate for which this first face is only a prerequisite. This report makes no claim to prove (4.12) or its other inputs.

The missing analytic step was formalized without a full-matrix comparison for minor block norms. For each principal minor, the proof forms the Hermitian principal submatrix and applies `im_green_apply_eq_mul_sum_normSq` to its resolvent at `zt 0 u` and its conjugate. On the first cell, `etaT 0 u ≥ 1/2`, and the imaginary diagonal entry is bounded by the resolvent norm, giving each retained minor column square sum at most `4`. The zero embedding contributes zero on deleted rows, so the full original-index column sum has the same bound. Restricting to one row block and summing over its `W` columns gives the raw minor block square sum `≤ 4W`.

On the same T1444 event, its two normalized endpoint bounds are `≤ 20 N^loss Γ`. Multiplying by `W` gives raw endpoint sums `≤ 20 W N^loss Γ`. Cauchy–Schwarz over the ordered `Fin W × Fin W` block coordinates then bounds each of the two rank-one cross sums, for `S` and for `insert κ S`, by `40 √W · W · N^loss Γ`. The pivot inverse is at most `2` by `MinorGoodLe`. Applying

`|‖x‖² - ‖y‖²| ≤ (‖x‖ + ‖y‖) ‖x-y‖`

and summing the two cross terms yields raw face sum `≤ 160 √W · W · N^loss Γ`. The squared-loop normalization is exactly `W⁻²`, leaving `160 (√W/W) N^loss Γ`. The same event gives `W⁻¹ ≤ 4Γ`, hence `√W/W ≤ 2√Γ` and the stated constant `320` with exponent `3/2`. There is no extra bandwidth factor.

The finite block sum is proved directly in the loop's order: `a` indexes the first/row coordinate and `c` the second/column coordinate. The proof does not pass through `Lre`, so it does not silently reverse this ordered pair. Every offset in the original `Fin W × Fin W` sum remains present; deleted coordinates are zero through `gEnt`'s embedding. `MinorGoodLe` and the endpoint bounds use the same `ω` and `u`, and the accepted T1444 actual witness supplies its own variable-loss `flowNetEvent`, rather than reusing an older fixed-threshold sample.

The threshold is `firstCellMinorEndpointDelta loss N = N^β firstCellPsi N`, with `β=min(loss/8,1/16)`. Since `firstCellPsi ≤ N^(-1/4)`, for each fixed loss and budget, eventually `δ≤N^(-3/16)`, `δ≤1/4`, and `8Mδ≤1`; these are combined with the T1444 witness only after fixing loss and `M`.

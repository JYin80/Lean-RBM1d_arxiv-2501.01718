## Exact bound and exponent gap

The new definition `supportConstant` is

\[
 C_*=144e^{\sqrt3}(16e^2+1)+3.
\]

The intermediate theorem proves, for `δ,ξ>0`,

\[
 jG_r\le C_*N^{2\delta+\xi}
\]

on the existing `good τ' ξ N` event and positive literal active weight. It does not require `2δ+ξ<1`; that condition belonged only to T380's final absorption into `N`.

For the specified `δ=ξ=1/100`, the exponent is `3/100`. The exact positive gap is

\[
 \frac18-\frac3{100}=\frac{19}{200}>0.
\]

The proof uses `N^(19/200)→∞`, so eventually `C_*≤N^(19/200)`, and combines real powers exactly to obtain

\[
 C_*N^{3/100}\le N^{19/200+3/100}=N^{1/8}.
\]

The normalized support threshold, interpolation error, `R_r⁴` restoration, and first-cell entry source are unchanged from T380.

## Quantifiers and same-event witness

For every fixed `τ'>0`, the stronger support theorem holds eventually in `N`, uniformly over all

- `N0≤N`, moment orders `p≥1`, smoothing orders `m≥1`;
- active prefixes `1≤k≤cutNetTop`;
- samples `ω∈good τ' (1/100) N` with positive weight at cutoff exponent `1/100`;
- running times `r∈[0,u_k]`.

The eventual size threshold is independent of these indices and samples. Positive support remains a premise. The theorem asserts no global cap on Good alone.

`exampleGrow_eighth_support_witness` has no hypotheses. It produces `τ'>0`, measurability of the existing event at every size, its `HighProb` property, and eventually a sample in that same event with

- `h_N=mesh_N⁻¹>0` in the actual first cell;
- active `k=2`;
- literal plateau weight one for every natural `p`, with `N0=2` and `m=N`;
- `jG_r≤N^(1/8)` for every `r∈[0,u_2]`.

The event still carries T380's norm, floored entry, matching `flowDelta` good-set and `jG/jS` comparison components. The witness uses the existing same-event plateau theorem and the stronger support theorem at `p=1`. It does not choose a sample from a separate unrelated event.

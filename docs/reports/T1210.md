# T1210 — independent final audit of T1209

## Verdict: PASS on T1209's negative conclusion; the proposed all-active exclusion is refuted

For the literal `E=0`, `D=60` T995 first-cell schedule, the statement that every norm-good sample avoids `APrimeCrossJointSplit.transition` at every active target-mesh index is false. T1209's same-sample argument gives, for arbitrarily large even `N`, an active `k>N^10` and a sample with `‖Xmat N ω‖≤N` in the **strict** transition set. I independently checked the producers and the discrete first-crossing step below. This sustains T1209's mathematical-preflight **FAIL**; it is not a Lean proof of a positive cross budget or a contradiction of the paper.

## Exact claim and producer checks

Fix `τ'=c=1`, `δ=1/200`. T995 allows `0<δ≤min(1,c/100)` and defines `δw=deltaWeight δ=1/20000`. In `APrimeSmoothWeightActual`, the threshold is

```text
Θ_N = 8 (exp 1)^2 N^(2δw) = 8 (exp 1)^2 N^(1/10000).
```

`APrimeGeneralMovingMesh.targetMesh 60 N=N^258` for `N>0`. The first-cell producers give `s_N=0` and eventually `t_N=1/2`. On even `N`, put `M=N^258`; then `cutNetTop=⌊M/2⌋=M/2`, `j_H=M/8` is an integer, and `k_H=j_H+1≤cutNetTop`. Also `k_0=N^10` is active and `k_0<k_H` for sufficiently large even `N`. The unchanged canonical order is `m=cutNetTop·q_N+1`, where `q_N=card(LoopArg (L_N) 2)≥1`. These are the exact T995 mesh and T1101 smoothing order, not the older transition mesh or a substitute order.

Take `ω_N=APrimeSmoothTransition.scalarSample Dims.exampleGrow (4√2)`. The source identity `Xmat_scalarSample` says `Xmat N ω_N=(4√2)I`. In the matrix operator norm used by the producers, `‖Xmat N ω_N‖=4√2≤N` eventually. This is a concrete inhabitant of T1201's literal norm-good event at the same `N`; no common-event assertion is needed. Since `cutNetPt s targetMesh N j_H=1/8` and `2/√(1/8)=4√2`, the hypotheses of `jSnorm_transition_lower` hold (`0<h≤1/8`, `2≤D`). Thus, writing `J_j=jSnorm(cutNetPt ... j,ω_N)` and `P_k=prefixSample(...,k,m,ω_N)`, the accepted stored-node comparison gives

```text
J_(j_H) ≥ W_N/64,             P_(k_H) ≥ J_(j_H).
```

`Dims.bandwidth_grow` supplies `W_N≥N^(5/8)` eventually. The exact threshold inequality, also proved as `eventually_large_threshold` in the T1051 producer, is `W_N>1024(exp 1)^2 N^(1/10000)` eventually. Hence `P_(k_H)>2Θ_N`. At the low endpoint T1101's accepted theorem gives `P_(k_0)/Θ_N≤3/(8 exp 1)<1` on this very sample. This uses its stated `k≤N^10` range only; the later indices are handled by a different argument.

## Independent no-jump check

For every adjacent `j,j+1` in `[0,cutNetTop]`, both node times are in the first cell. `APrimeSlotFields.eventually_modulus_jSnorm_event`, the all-pairs producer used in T1101, therefore applies on the norm event and gives

```text
|J_(j+1)-J_j| ≤ N^(2+2·60) (1/M)^(1/2) = N^122 N^-129 = N^-7.
```

This is a bound on adjacent increments throughout the cell. It is not T1101's early-time bound on `J_j` itself. The separately discussed T475 `N^127` coefficient would yield `N^-2` here and is unnecessary.

Let `f_j` be the actual smooth-JS node value. The source comparisons `jSnorm_le_smoothJS` and `smoothJS_le_card_mul_jSnorm_add` give, at all stored nodes,

```text
J_j ≤ f_j ≤ A_N (J_j+N^-10),   A_N=q_N^(1/(2m)).
```

By `softMax`'s literal even-power definition, `P_k=(Σ_{j<k} f_j^(2m))^(1/(2m))`. It is monotone in `k`, dominates each stored `f_j`, and satisfies

```text
P_k ≤ 2^(1/(2m)) max(P_(k-1),f_(k-1)).
```

Let `k_*` be the least `k∈(k_0,k_H]` with `P_k>Θ_N`. It exists by the two endpoint bounds. Then `P_(k_*-1)≤Θ_N` and, because `k_*-2<k_*-1`, `J_(k_*-2)≤f_(k_*-2)≤P_(k_*-1)≤Θ_N`. The adjacent modulus yields `J_(k_*-1)≤Θ_N+N^-7`. Consequently,

```text
P_(k_*)/Θ_N ≤ 2^(1/(2m)) max(1, A_N[1+(N^-7+N^-10)/Θ_N]).
```

Here `m≥cutNetTop·q_N`, `q_N≥1`, and `log q_N≤q_N`; hence `log A_N≤1/(2cutNetTop)→0`. Also `m→∞` and `Θ_N≥8(exp 1)^2`, so the right side tends to `1` along the even subsequence. It is strictly below `2` eventually. By the definition of `k_*`, `1<P_(k_*)/Θ_N<2`. Thus `ω_N` belongs to the literal `transition` set at an active `k_*>N^10`. All factors, indices, and samples are the same through this argument. The explicit sample and positive-length cell show nondegeneracy; an arbitrarily large even subsequence suffices to refute an eventual universal exclusion.

## Joint-rate and paper scope

The T1201 consumer uses `APrimeCrossJointSplit.jointRate`: the strict-transition indicator multiplied by `prefixGradient` and the square root of `APrimeDriftTimeFamily.qvAt`, then restricted to the same norm-good event for its moment budget. The counterexample proves that the **indicator** does not vanish identically on norm-good samples. It does **not** prove either remaining factor nonzero at `ω_N`, positive norm-good probability of this far-index transition, or nonzero `jointRate`; those would require separate evidence. It decisively invalidates the proposed *transition-exclusion route* to exact norm-good `jointRate` vanishing.

I checked only the authorized `paper/250520-YinJun-v2.pdf`: (5.21) and (5.24) concern the stopped hierarchy/martingale bound, while (5.43) defines a first-crossing stopping time for `J*` and (5.44)–(5.46) estimate the stopped QV and time-net terms. The Lean smooth-prefix transition is an internal replacement event. Nothing here refutes the paper's stopped-process argument or supplies the missing small norm-good N1/A′ bound.

## Compiled result

`APrimeQVEndpoint.sqrt_evolvedQV_le_endpoint` is a pointwise bound at `(u,ω)` for the **evolved** two-loop rate with `σ=(+,-)`. It has the endpoint indicator `|a₁-a₂|≤6ℓ*_v`, the fourth power of `η_u/η_v` after squaring, and the sharp `(J*)²` contribution in the far coefficient. The T262 far coefficient also contains its separate `(J*)³` term. The exponentially small spill from the near band is absorbed into an explicit `256e³ W^{-D} T_{v,D}` far term under deterministic conditions `A_u≥1` and `log W≥(4D)²`. `endpoint_scale_witness` certifies these added conditions with positive duration (`u=0<v=1/2`, `W=e¹⁶`, `D=1`). `quadVar_le_four_terms` converts the square-root estimate into a sum-shaped quadratic-variation estimate.

Proof chain:

1. `sqrt_quadVar_Uker_le` uses `Gauss.sqrt_wsum_sum_le` (weighted ℓ² Minkowski). Only diagonal `quadVar(lk_b)` enters.
2. `sqrt_quadVar_Uker_le_diagShape` applies T262's `EarlyQVRate.quadVar_lkFun_le_ee_sym` at every input loop. Step-1 bounds remain pointwise hypotheses at `(u,ω)`; there is no `∀ω` size assumption.
3. `weightedKernel_tail_le` and `weightedKernel_supp_far_le` turn the existing `U` estimates into **absolute-kernel** convolution estimates. A phase choice at each input realizes the absolute sum as the norm of a single `Uker` output. `weightedKernel_near_tail_far_le` enlarges the input support from `4ℓ*_u` to the endpoint `6ℓ*_v` band.
4. `exp_neg_half_ellStar_le_rpow_neg`, `tailT_zero_le_two`, and `nearLeak_le_farTail` absorb the near-band spill into the far tail. `quadVar_ukerObsT_eq_evolved_loopObs` identifies the proved rate with the quadratic variation of the Duhamel model's actual evolved `(L-K)` observable; the deterministic primitive `K` drops from its matrix derivative.

The earlier exact doubled-tensor bridge and `ratio_witness` remain in the file, but the endpoint proof uses only diagonal rates.

## Exact absorbed rate

```lean

  coefficientConstant * (Real.sqrt 2 + 1) *
    (N : Real) ^ (2 * alpha) * xRate v ^ (-(2 : Real))
```

```lean
eventually_integral_endpointCoefficient_le_absorbedRate

    (hdelta : 0 < delta) (hdelta100 : delta <= 1/100) (alpha : Real) :
  ∀ᶠ N in atTop, ∀ k, k <= cutNetTop ... N ->
```

literal coefficient.  The integral claim is deterministic and does not
assert measurability or integrability of the random moment norm.

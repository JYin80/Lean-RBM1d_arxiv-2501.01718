# T327 — smooth-prefix covariance-gradient feasibility audit

## Verdict

The regularized finite-prefix gradient is mathematically viable, with no inverse-`ε` loss and no *polynomial* cardinality loss after choosing the two soft-max orders. A literal bound by the largest coordinate gradient with coefficient `1` is false: the exact coefficient is `k^(1/q₀) card(LoopArg)^(1/q₁)`, where `q₀,q₁` are the outer and inner even powers. The narrower, cardinality-free statement uses mixed finite `ℓ^q` norms rather than a maximum. T320's `e` is valid only after cardinality calibration, not as a raw contraction constant.

No sharp `WeightC1`, (S5), (G), or A′ instance follows from T314's fixed-time `C¹` theorem alone. The first missing Lean producer is a covariance-gradient theorem for the **sample-coordinate, mixed-time** smoothed prefix. The general-window stochastic closure also still lacks the pre-A′ uniform fluctuation-averaging input (4.5) identified in T320. This is a read-only audit, not a compiled proof.

## Exact gradient and normalization

Fix `N`, `k`, net points `u_j=cutNetPt s mesh N j` for `j<k`, `ε>0`, inner order `m≥1`, outer order `r≥1`, and `q₁=2m`, `q₀=2r`. Put `R_j=ratR E s N u_j`, `T_{j,a}=tT (band d) E N D u_j (zdist(a₀-a₁))>0`, and

`F_{j,a}(M)=coordFun d E N u_j a M`,

`b_{j,a}(X)=sqrt(|F_{j,a}(sqrt(u_j) X)|²+ε²)/T_{j,a}`,

`S_j(X)=(1+(Σ_a b_{j,a}(X)^q₁)^(1/q₁))/R_j⁴`,

`S_k(X)=(Σ_{j<k} S_j(X)^q₀)^(1/q₀)` for `k>0`, and `S_0=0`.

Here `S_j` is exactly T314's `smoothJSMatrix` evaluated along `Hflow`; the `+1` and endpoint factor `R_j⁻⁴` are essential. All `S_j>0`; hence the outer root is `C¹` for nonempty prefix. With `||DG||_g²=Σ_{q∈usedCoord} gvar_q |∂_qG|²`, define the nonnegative actual coordinate rate

`K_{j,a}(X)=sqrt(u_j) sqrt(quadVar d N (F_{j,a}) (Hflow d N u_j X)) / (T_{j,a} R_j⁴)`.

The scalar derivative of `sqrt(|z|²+ε²)` is `Re(conj(z) Dz)/sqrt(|z|²+ε²)`; its coefficient has modulus at most `1`. Thus no `ε⁻¹` occurs in the first derivative, including at `z=0`. Chain rule, weighted Minkowski, and finite Hölder give the precise usable inequalities

`||D_X S_k||_g ≤ (Σ_{j<k} (Σ_a K_{j,a}(X)^q₁)^(q₀/q₁))^(1/q₀)`

`≤ k^(1/q₀) card(LoopArg(d.L N,2))^(1/q₁) max_{j<k,a} K_{j,a}(X)`.

The first line is free of an explicit cardinality factor. The second cannot generally have coefficient `1`: for two identical positive regularized scalar coordinates `b_i(x)=sqrt(x²+ε²)` and `q₁=2`, the derivative of their `ℓ²` norm is `sqrt(2)` times either coordinate derivative at `x>0`. This is an algebraic obstruction to a universal factor-one maximum lemma; it is not claimed as a counterexample to a special RBM loop identity. Choosing `q₀,q₁` so each displayed cardinality power is at most `e` makes the total at most `e²`, independent of `N`. A single flattened smoothing could use one calibrated factor, but that is a different surrogate from T314's nested one.

The factor `sqrt(u_j)` is exact because `Hflow(u_j,X)=sqrt(u_j) X`. It must not be included again if `F_{j,a}` is first normalized by `T_{j,a}R_j⁴`; conversely it must not be dropped when applying a same-matrix QV bound to the base Gaussian coordinates. The early QV is `quadVar(F_{j,a})(Hflow(u_j,X))`, controlled by T262/T286's unevolved `qvShape`, while the running-time moment uses T292/T300's **evolved** `qvAt`.

## Weight, cross term, and event split

Let `χ=cutChi`, `Θ>0`, `φ=χ(S_k/Θ)`, and `w=φ^(2p)` for `p≥1`. The chain rule and `|χ'|≤15/8` yield

`||D_X w||_g ≤ (2p)(15/8) Θ⁻¹ φ^(2p−1) ||D_X S_k||_g`.

Writing `Y_u` for the normalized evolved coordinate and `Q_cur(u,X)=qvAt(u,X)`, the derivative of `|Y_u|^(2p)` supplies the second `2p`. Therefore the pointwise (S5) integrand has the rate-product form

`crossAbsSum(u,X) ≤ (2p)²(15/8) Θ⁻¹ φ^(2p−1)|Y_u|^(2p−1) K_early(X) sqrt(Q_cur(u,X))`,

where `K_early` is the mixed norm in the first gradient display (or its calibrated maximum upper bound). The derivative vanishes off the transition band of `χ`, so its indicator can be retained. `crossPart_le_integral` then contributes `1/(2sqrt(u))` for `u>0`. Hölder with exponents `2p/(2p−1)` and `2p` leaves the **single joint norm**

`||K_early sqrt(Q_cur(u))||_{L^(2p)(P)}`

multiplied by the weighted moment to power `(2p−1)/(2p)`. One cannot replace this by a norm of `sqrt(Q_cur)` without a global deterministic bound on `K_early`.

For a common measurable Good event, the correct split bounds that joint norm by its Good part plus its complement. On Good intersected with the cutoff support, the prefix cap and the uniform-time Step 1, averaged-law, entry, and loop estimates bound both rates. On Good's complement, a global polynomial finite-`N` resolvent/floor envelope `Env_prod` gives `Env_prod P(Goodᶜ)^(1/(2p))`. The exceptional probability exponent is chosen after fixed `p` and the envelope degree. The existing `APrimeBadSplit.crossPart_le_of_S5_event` splits only the **current** `sqrt(qvRateEvolved)` under a deterministic global (S5) constant; it cannot directly implement this two-rate split. Replacing `K_early` by a crude global `C_N` there is valid algebraically but does not preserve the sharp `N` gain. A new joint-rate event-split lemma or a suitably reformulated cross consumer is required.

T314's sandwich gives `jSnorm(u_j)≤S_j`. If `φ>0`, then `S_k<2Θ`, hence every earlier hard `jSnorm(u_j)<2Θ`; the ordinary time-modulus step propagates this to the prefix interval on its own norm/modulus event. Conversely, on a narrow hard-prefix event, T314's upper sandwich gives `S_j≤[1+c₁(max_a |F|/T+ε/Tmin)]/R_j⁴`; calibrating both soft-max orders and taking `ε/Tmin` below the threshold slack makes `φ=1`. This is a deterministic implication, not a claim that the support has high probability by itself. High probability and eventual nonemptiness of **Good intersect narrow prefix** must come from the sequential weighted-moment/net induction; separate nonemptiness of Good and support is insufficient. A nontrivial derivative witness needs a positive earlier net time and a transition-region sample. The empty prefix, and the first-cell prefix containing only `u₀=0`, have zero derivative.

## Time endpoint and full QV budget

The one-step Duhamel integral for fixed prefix `k` runs from the window start to the target net point, so it may have `u_j>u`. The ratio `sqrt(u_j)/sqrt(u)` cannot be canceled throughout that integral. At `u=0`, the Stein bound is invoked only for `u>0`; its isolated endpoint has zero Lebesgue measure. On a first window with terminal cap `v≤1/2`, `η_u`, resolvent derivatives, and the full fixed-`N` rate envelopes are bounded near zero, while `∫₀ᵛ u^(−1/2)du=2sqrt(v)`. For `k=0`, `w=1` and the cross term is zero. When `s=0` and the terminal point exceeds `1/2`, the integration must be split there while retaining the same endpoint normalization. For later windows `s>0`, `u⁻¹` integration is finite. No logarithm `log(v/0)` is allowed.

The running QV is not the near term alone. `APrimeQVEndpoint.diagNearRate` is `2η_u⁻¹ cNear2(W,ℓ_u)(ℓ_u/ℓ_s)^5`; `diagFarRate` has both `J² A_u sqrt(Smax)` and `J³ A_u⁻¹`, plus the `WL W⁻ᴰ J³` tail; `diagResidual=2WLρ`. The evolved endpoint bridge also carries the fixed `T_vR⁴` reciprocal, the near spatial indicator, and the `W⁻ᴰ` kernel leakage. Under T320's conditional common-event profile, with widened `J≤C N^(2δ+τ)(η_s/η_u)^4`, the far current-QV rows are `N^(4δ+2τ+ν)R^(19/4)/A_v^(1/2)` and `N^(6δ+3τ+ν)R^8/A_v`; the early gradient has its own near/far/residual shape. With the **separate** actual scale hypotheses `A_v≥N^c` and `A_v≥R^30`, the checks are `(4δ+2τ+ν)/c+19/120<1/2` and `(6δ+3τ+ν)/c+8/30<1`. For T320's `δ=c/1000`, `τ=δ/16`, `ν=δ/100`, the respective strict margins are `0.337531…` and `0.727135…`. These are conditional rate checks, not suppliers of the common Good event or the missing (4.5); no product-scale condition is used. The bare residual requires the primed near-only treatment or an explicit separate bound, and cannot be dropped from `qvAt` by assertion.

It uses `APrimeSmoothPrefix.smoothJSMatrix_flow` and `contDiff_smoothJSMatrix`, `Gauss.Hflow`, the weighted Minkowski/Hölder pattern of `Gauss.sqrt_quadVar_softMax_le`, and the actual `Gauss.quadVar` coordinate identity. Direct substitution of the raw `F_{j,a}` into `sqrt_quadVar_softMax_le` is invalid: that theorem evaluates all functions at one matrix, while the prefix uses different `Hflow(u_j,X)`. It may be reused by first pulling every function back to the common base matrix `X`, encoding each positive regularized modulus as a real-valued complex function, and proving its QV chain bound with the exact `sqrt(u_j)` factor. Those pullback and regularization lemmas, followed by the outer-prefix application, are the missing interface; this is not a mathematical obstruction to reuse of T265.

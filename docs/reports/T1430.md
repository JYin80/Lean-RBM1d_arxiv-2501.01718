## Scope and direct-proof check

The requested theorem is the dimension-free, all-order estimate (A) from
`docs/reports/T1424.md` §3.2: for finite nonempty `ι`, even `q ≥ max(2, 4M)`,
`1 ≤ k ≤ M`, `s ∈ {1, -1}`, `x ≠ 0` with zero coordinates permitted, and arbitrary
directions `v₁, …, vₖ`, bound the `k`-th real multilinear derivative of
`F_q(x)^s`, `F_q(x) = (Σᵢ xᵢ^q)^(1/q)`, by

```text
h_k * q^(k - 1) * F_q(x)^(s - k) * ∏ⱼ ‖vⱼ‖_q,
h_k = Σ_{σ ∈ Π(k)} |σ|!.
```

smooth-maximum results only; the latter has a coordinatewise lower-bound
premise. It has no direct all-order result with the requested zero-coordinate
domain. Mathlib's `Analysis/Calculus/ContDiff/FaaDiBruno.lean` supplies the
ordered-partition composition formula, but not the dimension-free estimate for
the `q`-power sum composed with the reciprocal/power map. No mathematical
counterexample was found: the coefficient and exponent calculation is the one
in T1424 §3.2. The blocker reached here is the missing Lean derivation, not a
refutation of (A).

## Implemented public interface

The source currently defines `RBM.Gauss.smoothNorm q x` using
`(Σᵢ |xᵢ|^q)^(1/q)` and proves:

- `smoothNorm_eq_smoothMax`: identifies this value with accepted
  `Cutoff.smoothMax` on nonnegative vectors.
- `smoothNorm_eq_evenPowerSum`: identifies it with the polynomial power-sum
  expression for even `q`.
- `smoothNorm_pos_of_ne_zero`: the value is positive at any nonzero vector,
  including vectors with zero coordinates.
- `smoothNormWitnessTwo_ne_zero` and `smoothNormWitnessTwo_value`: in `Fin 2`,
  `x = (1,1)` is nonzero and has value `2^(1/q)`.
- `smoothNormWitnessZeroCoordinate_ne_zero`,
  `smoothNormWitnessZeroCoordinate_first/second`, and
  `smoothNormWitnessZeroCoordinate_value`: `x = (1,0)` is nonzero, its second
  coordinate is zero, and its value is `1` for positive `q`.

These statements establish the common norm notation, the smooth-maximum
identification, and the simultaneous nonzero/zero-coordinate domain checks.
They do **not** establish the derivative estimate, its reciprocal derivative,
the all-order smoothness needed to state it, or the `M = 0` case. The smallest
remaining arrow is the full bound (A), derived from derivatives of the even
polynomial power sum, dimension-free generalized Hölder, and the ordered
partition composition formula. This ticket is therefore INCOMPLETE; no
first- or second-order substitute is presented as completion.

## Independent endpoint arithmetic and active range

The public T475 modulus is

```text
|jSnorm(v,ω)-jSnorm(w,ω)| ≤ N^(2D+7)|v-w|^(1/2)
```

for `v,w∈[s_N,t_N]` and `ω∈good N`. The actual mesh satisfies
`M_N=targetMesh D N=N^(4D+18)` eventually, while
`cutNetPt s M N k=s_N+k/M_N` and
`cutNetTop s t M N=⌊(t_N-s_N)M_N⌋₊`. Hence at an active index,

```text
|jSnorm(cutNetPt k)-jSnorm(s_N)| ≤ N^-2 √k.
```

At `k=1` this is T1103's `N^-2`. For `k≤N^4` it is at most `1`, exactly
T1117's early-index calculation. With the global `19/4` Lipschitz constant
of `cutTrunc`, the naive initial-moment comparison has a `2q`-power error
bounded by a fixed multiple of `N^(-4q) k^q`. To pay this term at the
requested `N^(δq/2)` rate using a constant independent of `N,k`, this route
needs approximately `k≲N^(4+δ/2)`. It cannot cover the full active range
under the given window hypotheses. This is an upper-bound limitation, **not**
a lower bound for the true random endpoint difference.

The range is genuinely much longer than `N^4` in an admissible model. In
T995's nondegenerate first-cell witness, `E=0`, `D=60`, `s_N=0` and
`t_N=1/2` eventually, with `Cond272Reg` and `BoundsCore` jointly satisfied.
There `M_N=N^258` and `cutNetTop=⌊N^258/2⌋`, so active far indices exist
through the mesh scale. At such indices T475's endpoint-to-initial upper
bound can be of order `N^127`; no uniform `O(1)` error follows. Even
`k=N^5` is active eventually and gives only `N^(1/2)` from this modulus.

The weight's active branch is the `2q` power of a soft-max cutoff over
`Finset.range k` at threshold `2e N^(2δ)`. If it is nonzero, the public
soft-max support lemma bounds each earlier mesh value by `4e N^(2δ)`.
The final mesh step costs at most `N^-2`, but this leaves a deterministic
endpoint scale `O(N^(2δ))`; its `2q` power has order `N^(4δq)`, above the
target `N^(δq/2)` for every `δ>0`. The truncation cap gives the same scale.
This support statement alone has no tail probability or moment gain.

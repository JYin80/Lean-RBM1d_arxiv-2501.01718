## Formal result and satisfiability

`eventually_positiveTimeCrossBudget_le_normGood_add_normBad` states that for
each fixed `p ≥ 1`, one eventual threshold in `N` works uniformly over all
active `k`, outputs `a`, and `r > 0` in the closed cell, with

```text
positiveTimeCrossBudget E D deltaWeight s t N k p a r
  ≤ normGoodCrossBudget E D deltaWeight s t N k p a r
      + (15p/8) N⁻¹ / √r.
```

The good term is exactly the T1089 coefficient times the `2p` Gaussian moment
norm of the literal T1089 `jointRate` restricted by
`Set.indicator (APrimeGeneralMovingGoodMesh.good N)`; this is T1197's norm-good
event.
The rate retains the strict transition indicator, canonical smoothing order,
target mesh, moving endpoint, sample, and `Step2.sigPM` charge. The eventual
quantifier precedes the uniform index/time quantifiers, as required.

No new mathematical assumptions were introduced. The inherited witness
`nondegenerate_active_k2_witness` realizes `E=0`, `D=60`, `c=1/2`,
`s=0`, `t=firstCellT 1`, and positive scheduled `deltaWeight`; eventually it
has an active `k=2` cell with positive endpoint and a sample in the literal
strict transition event. The theorem does not claim positive mass or a
positive value for either restricted moment.

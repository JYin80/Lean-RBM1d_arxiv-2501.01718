# T359 — actual smooth-weight covariance cross-rate interface audit

## Verdict

The joint covariance estimate is mathematically valid as a **conditional** estimate for each fixed endpoint, prefix and integer `p ≥ 1`. The current Lean interface does not close its sharp actual-model application. `APrimeBadSplit.crossPart_le_of_S5_event` requires one favorable constant in its S5 bound on **every** sample and splits only the evolved QV. The actual favorable prefix gradient is known only on a pre-A′ event intersected with the cutoff transition. Substituting its good-event value as that global constant is invalid. A joint-product event split is needed. This audit proves no A′ moment or general-window rate producer.

Paper (5.43)–(5.47) uses a stopping time, integrated martingale estimate, time net, and removal of the stop. The smooth-weight Gaussian cross term is an A′ interface intended to replace that stopping argument; it is not a term displayed literally in (5.43)–(5.47). The uncut evolved covariance rate entering it is the (5.42) object.

## Exact variables and same-coordinate contraction

Fix `N`, one net endpoint `v=u_k`, one label `(σ,a)`, and the **entire** prefix of indices `j<k`. In the active piecewise branch `k≤cutNetTop` and `N₀≤N`, freeze the actual T333 matrix cutoff

`c_k(ω)=cutChi(S_k(Xmat_N(ω))/Θ_N)`, `Θ_N=8e² N^(2δ)` (the compiled T333 value),

and put `w_k=c_k^(2p)` and `wD_k(q,ω)=D(weightMatrix_k)(Xmat_N(ω))[B_q]`. Outside that branch the actual weight is the constant 1, its derivative and cross term vanish, and no identity with `c_k^(2p)` is asserted. T333 proves `WeightC1` for the precise piecewise derivative, including finite dependence and boundedness. The prefix samples are all obtained from the same base `Xmat_N(ω)`, at their separate times `u_j`; their covariance gradients are not independent. T333's `sqrt_quadVar_prefixMatrix_le_exp_max` contracts the **same** `usedCoord d N`, `gvar` and `Bmat` that occur in the Gaussian generator, with exact factors `sqrt(u_j)`, `R_j^-4`, `T_j^-1`. There is no factor `sqrt(r)` for the running integration time `r`.

At running `r`, set `Ψ₁(r,M)=APrimeDriftTimeFamily.coordAt(d,E,D,N,σ,a,s,v,r,M)` and `Ψ(r,M)=momentAt(...,r,M)`. Then

`Y_r(ω)=‖Ψ₁(r,Hflow_r(ω))‖`, `Q_r(ω)=APrimeDriftTimeFamily.qvAt(...,r,ω)`

with `Q_r=qvRateEvolved(d,N,Ψ₁,r,ω)=quadVar(d,N,Ψ₁(r),Hflow_r(ω))` definitionally (`qvAt_eq_evolved`). This is the **uncut, full evolved** rate. The endpoint denominator `driftScale=T_(v,D)(a)(η_s/η_v)^4` is already inside `coordAt` and therefore inside `Q_r`; it must not be inserted again. The same current coordinate derivative `coordD1(d,N,Ψ(r),Hflow_r,q)` occurs in `crossAbsSum` and `crossPart`. A near-only rate, an unevolved `APrimeModel.qvRate`, or a Good-indicated rate is a different object.

Let `T_k={ω : 1<S_k(Xmat_Nω)/Θ_N<2}`, `B_k=√quadVar(S_k)/Θ_N` (the covariance gradient seminorm divided by `Θ_N`), `Z_r=1_Tk B_k √Q_r`, and `φ_r=E[|c_kY_r|^(2p)]`. For `p≥1`, the chain rule gives

`|wD_k(q)| ≤ 2p c_k^(2p-1) |cutChiD(S_k/Θ_N)| |D_qS_k|/Θ_N`.

`cutChiD` vanishes also at the transition endpoints 1 and 2; `|cutChiD|≤15/8`. The derivative of `|Ψ₁|^(2p)` has covariance norm at most `2p |Y_r|^(2p-1)√Q_r`, including `Y_r=0`. Thus Cauchy–Schwarz over the **same q** yields on every sample

`crossAbsSum(d,N,Ψ,wD_k,r,ω) ≤ (2p)^2(15/8)|c_kY_r|^(2p-1) Z_r(ω)`.  (S5-joint)

This remains true off `T_k`, where `wD_k=0`, without claiming any favorable `B_k` bound there. `k=0` gives `S_0=0`, `c_0=1`, and zero cross term. An inactive piecewise branch also has constant weight 1 and zero cross term.

## Hölder and the event split

For `r>0`, `crossPart` has the coefficient `1/(2√r)`. The integral triangle inequality and Hölder with conjugate exponents `2p/(2p-1)` and `2p` give

`|crossPart(r)| ≤ (2p²)(15/8) r^-1/2 φ_r^((2p-1)/(2p)) ‖Z_r‖_(2p)`.  (1)

In the active branch `wscale(w_k,p,Y_r)=c_kY_r` because `0≤c_k≤1` and `p≥1`; hence its `2p` moment is exactly `φ_r`. In the inactive branch the cross term is zero and its moment weight is 1. Measurability and integrability of `Z_r^(2p)` and the product are required for a Lean Hölder invocation. At fixed `N` they follow from finite-coordinate continuity and finite-size resolvent bounds; a **polynomial in N** bound needs a separate explicit proof. Using `WeightC1.bddD` alone gives a finite constant with no controlled N dependence.

Let one measurable pre-A′ `Good_N` be fixed for the whole window, all prefixes and labels. Suppose, for the present `r`, on `Good_N∩T_k` one has `B_k≤b_N` and `Q_r≤q_N(r)`, while on **all** samples `B_k√Q_r≤E_N(r)`. Assume these bounds are nonnegative and `P_d(Good_N^c)≤ρ_N`. Then

`‖Z_r‖_(2p)^(2p) ≤ (b_N√q_N(r))^(2p) + E_N(r)^(2p)ρ_N`, so

`‖Z_r‖_(2p) ≤ b_N√q_N(r)+E_N(r)ρ_N^(1/(2p))`.  (2)

The good part uses `P(Good_N∩T_k)≤1`; the bad part uses `T_k∩Good_N^c⊆Good_N^c`. There is no conditioning and no division by the probability of the transition. In particular, `Good_N∩T_k` may be empty without invalidating (2), but a proposed *sharp* conclusion cannot obtain its nonvacuity from that observation.

If `E_N(r)≤C N^B h(r)` and `ρ_N≤N^-M`, the bad term in (1) is at most `C_p N^(B-M/(2p)) r^-1/2 h(r) φ_r^α`, `α=(2p-1)/(2p)`. This is harmless only after proving `∫_s^v r^-1/2 h(r)dr` has the required bound and choosing `M>2p(B+κ)` for the desired `N^-κ` margin. For a uniform finite-size envelope `h=1`, the time integral is `2(√v-√s)`. The fixed prefix factors `√u_j` must not cancel `r^-1/2`; the latter is integrable at zero. At `r=0`, the real formal `crossPart` definition evaluates its reciprocal coefficient as zero, but its generator derivation is used only for `r>0` and then extended across zero by integration. No pointwise positive-time inequality is inferred from the formal value at zero.

For `p=0`, `w_k=c_k^0=1` and `wD_k=0`; the test moment `|Y|^0` is constant, so the covariance cross term is zero. The `wscale` root and Hölder exponent in (1) must **not** be used at `p=0`. For `p=1`, the conjugate pair is 2 and 2 and all powers in (S5-joint) are defined at `Y=0`.

Fix requested tolerance, then the integer `p≥1`; choose the polynomial envelope exponent `B` from actual deterministic scales, then choose the high-probability failure exponent `M`, and finally take eventual `N`. For polynomially many endpoints and labels, one common event must be quantified over every `r∈[s,v]`, every preceding `u_j`, and every label before the finite union is paid. An event separately constructed for each `r` does not justify a uniform integral or a single cutoff induction. No bound uniform in all `p` at one fixed N is asserted.

## Existing interface and missing producers

`APrimeBadSplit.crossPart_le_of_S5_event` assumes

`∀ω, crossAbsSum(ω) ≤ √r Cs |wscale(w,p,Y_r)(ω)|^(2p-1) √Q_r(ω)`

with a deterministic global `Cs`; its event hypotheses only bound `Q_r`. To make this true from T333, `Cs` must pay the **all-sample** early `B_k/√r`, which is a crude polynomial factor and loses the favorable good-event power. Taking the favorable `b_N` as `Cs` is false on `Good_N^c∩T_k`. The other T280c lemma `crossPart_le_of_event` is an L¹ split of `crossAbsSum` and similarly does not retain the moment power in (1). Neither is a direct instance of (1)–(2).

T333 supplies the deterministic prefix-gradient contraction and `WeightC1`; T351 supplies comparison with the old weight. T334 supplies conditional full evolved `qvAt` bounds from actual length-four/six and `jG` inputs. T344 integrates a deterministic envelope. These do **not** supply the needed general-window common event with a sharp early prefix full-QV bound and a sharp current evolved `qvAt` bound at every running time on that same sample. The first missing stochastic producer for this cross application is that uniform pre-A′ common source event (including the actual Step 1 loop inputs, entry/J support and numerical multipliers). T352 identifies the corresponding missing current full-QV event-to-profile bridge. Separately, its audit notes that T294 only gives a finite-size all-sample QV bound, not the polynomial `E_N` required to pay the complement. The pre-A′ averaged law (4.5) is also needed for the drift part of the eventual moment argument; it cannot be silently substituted into this covariance calculation.

There is no event/dependency cycle in (S5-joint)–(2): the weight is frozen before the moment argument; `Good_N` must be built from Step 1/local-law/entry data, not from the desired A′ weighted moments, `JSNormDom`, or a sharp support event inferred from the same induction. `APrimeFirstCellCommon.exists_nonempty_firstCell_common` already proves an actual positive-probability common first-cell event, and `eventually_active_weight_two_one` puts its norm-event samples on the plateau for `k=2`. This establishes nonempty useful support there, but says nothing about the general-window common event. The currently uncommitted `APrimeSmoothTransition` gives scalar-sample identities and a plateau example; it does not yet prove a nonzero-derivative transition point on `Good_N`. Such intersection is not required by (2), but a globally nonconstant cutoff claim must have its own witness.
